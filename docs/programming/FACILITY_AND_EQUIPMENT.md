# Vortex facility and equipment reference

**Owner-provided planning update · September 13, 2026**

Apply this reference when reviewing previously written programs and designing all future Vortex training blocks, on-ramp sessions and workouts. The owner confirmed the running lanes and equipment below and authorized incorporating any of these tools. This update supersedes conflicting earlier assumptions about available running length or whether these equipment categories are available.

## Running space

- **Three running lanes, each 12 metres long.** These are three separate lanes, not a continuous 36-metre route.
- Plan the movement and controlled stopping within the available 12 metres unless additional usable run-off is established. A 12-metre lane does not automatically permit a 12-metre sprint followed by extra stopping distance.
- Lane widths, space beyond the lanes, return paths, waiting areas and adjacent training space have not been specified. Include them in the actual layout rather than inheriting earlier diagrams.
- Select starts, short accelerations, braking or direction tasks that fit the chosen setup. Where a running objective cannot fit, explicitly change the objective and choose an appropriate jump, throw or other available task. Short accelerations, shuttles and throws do not count as upright maximal-speed running.
- Timing-gate placement, queues, returns and equipment setup must fit the same layout and existing session clock.

Equipment availability expands the choices; it does not require every tool in every session. Choose the exercise from its purpose and the athlete's demonstrated level, retain productive anchors, and include instruction, loading, setup, measurement and recovery inside the booked time. Confirm the attachment/anchor, target surface, station fit and quantity needed for the selected variation. Wall-ball availability alone does not specify a throwing wall; barbell availability alone does not specify racks or benches.

Preserve the eight tenets and **Prepare & Access → Explosiveness → Strength → separate Body Control / Tumbling**, with the established 90/60-minute athletic booking and separate 30-minute tumbling allocation. Timing gates and force plates support selected observations; they do not create an extra testing block or require extra attempts.
