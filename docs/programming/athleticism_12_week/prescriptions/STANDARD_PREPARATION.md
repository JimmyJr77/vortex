Full preparation is **12 minutes of base + 3 minutes for exactly two session-specific tasks = 15 minutes**. Compact preparation is **7 + 3 = 10 minutes**. The two target tasks belong to the session exemplar and are not prescribed here. The four pillars, 90/60-minute athletic booking and separately programmed 30-minute tumbling allocation remain intact.

These are warm-up observations, not age-based overload or automatic competency passes.

## Physical delivery and clock assumptions

Three columns of five distinct 3 m × 3 m personal bays: 15 independent working spaces. Five athletes share a lane assignment, never an overlapping crawl corridor.

Unobstructed peripheral coach circulation/access/egress and separately verified start, runoff and return corridors are additional to the 9 m × 15 m bay rectangle. The marked rectangle is not the total facility footprint.

Standing paths remain in personal bays. Short forward passes are at most 1.6 m; backward/lateral spans 1 m or less except 1.5 m carioca. Individually check full hand-walk/plank footprint before use.

All bays face one end. Complete a pass and stop before ordinary walking return within that same bay. No crossing between athletes or concurrent opposite-direction traffic inside a bay.

Familiar shared practice: one coach scans A/B and one scans B/C with clear oblique views. Three-wave teaching/checks: both coaches watch one active lane and split five athletes 2/3; other lanes stand. Clear views of all active feet/trunks are required.

The active lane finishes standing, then the next lane starts on cue. The 2-second change (3 seconds for floor teaching) is a cue change, not relocation. Athletes stay in their own bays.

Flat markers, bottles outside active rectangles, clean dry nonslip surface. No partner support, unstable props or unverified anchor is assumed.

Mark/inspect space before class. Readiness/restriction information must be available. First demonstration includes stop cue and immediate comfort check. Unknown restrictions or inability to follow safe directions require individual resolution, never assumed clearance.

Row time = demonstration + waves × individual execution envelope + explicit wave handovers + reset/transition. The individual envelope includes stated steps, pauses and returns. Shared practice means separate bays, not 15 times one athlete dose.

Execution envelopes are estimates for the stated variant, not speed commands. If safe learning/movement needs longer, use contingency, remove optional work or revise the booking. Never rush to validate arithmetic.

Base ends at 12:00 full or 7:00 compact with athletes standing in bays. Reserve exactly 180 seconds for exactly two parent-owned target tasks. The parent exemplar defines the split and charges bay-to-start gathering, demonstrations, observed holds, rehearsal, exit and return within those 180 seconds. Use the parent-defined allocation; do not double-charge gathering or hide it before the base ends.

UNVERIFIED: measured space, body fit, sightlines, surface, shared-floor schedule and target corridors have not been observed. Arithmetic does not certify physical feasibility.

The marked bay rectangle is **9 m × 15 m before peripheral access and separate target-task corridors**. This is a conditional requirement, not an observed facility fact. One athlete occupies each bay. Five athletes never crawl simultaneously through one overlapping short corridor.
