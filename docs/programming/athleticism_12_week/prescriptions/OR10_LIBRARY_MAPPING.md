## The goblet choice preserves an existing movement

Migration447 restores exact front-loaded squat identities and links related legacy sources 167, 415, 461, 464, 748, 1255, 1295 and 1699; these are [provenance relationships](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/backend/migrations/447_coaching_front_loaded_squat_identity_and_family_completion.sql:275), not a fabricated single current ID. The selected DB variant differs from KB horns-or-bell support, medicine-ball support and heel-elevated variants. Barbell front rack, two independent front racks, unilateral rack and sumo stance remain separate exact choices. Equipment relationships require renewed exact grip/load review; equal kilograms do not create equivalence.

The source [capacity-strength profile](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/backend/migrations/447_coaching_front_loaded_squat_identity_and_family_completion.sql:495) uses 2–4 sets, 5–12 reps, RPE 5–8 or 2–5 repetitions in reserve, controlled or explicitly slow eccentric work, 60–180 s recovery and technical stopping. Its 35 s setup, 5 s per rep and 25 s transition estimates are not a full first-teaching group clock. OR-10 explicitly selects one small set, at least five good repetitions in reserve, 3-1-1 tempo and 90 s minimum recovery. Actual age does not assign kilograms.

The [source entry requirement](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/backend/migrations/447_coaching_front_loaded_squat_identity_and_family_completion.sql:345) is pain-free owned bodyweight squat, ability to pick up and hold the selected object, and stop-signal understanding. The first selected-DB pickup and support can be observed within counted handling before loaded reps. It does not demand a previous successful complete loaded squat. Source setup rehearsal is fulfilled through coach demonstration, familiar evidence where applicable, and the counted handling/first repetitions; no extra athlete test is added.

For the chosen DB, both palms support opposite sides under its upper head, fingers comfortably around the edges, shaft vertical and lower head clear of the trunk. An individually fitting elevated cradle supports the lower head at pickup/parking and prevents rolling; it carries **no weight during the squat repetitions**. The source requires exact two-hand center-chest support and controlled pickup/set-down but does not dictate this head shape, grip angle or parking height. A DB/cradle that cannot fit this declared context is unavailable for that athlete. No floor pickup, overhead lift, improvised KB or extra deep setup squat is presumed.
