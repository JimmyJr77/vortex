## Source69 A-March and basic rhythm teaching

Exact candidate UUID: `69000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002`. The definition is review/quarantined with no approvals created. Historical sources878/924 are provenance, not new approved options. [Contract](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/scripts/data/canonical-research/contracts/a-march.v1.json:6), [variant and profiles](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/scripts/data/canonical-research/contracts/a-march.v1.json:29).

Travel forward on a clear level lane using deliberate alternating **no-flight** steps. Each thigh rises toward a declared comfortable landmark while the opposite foot supports; the next foot returns close beneath the body with reciprocal opposite arm/leg action. Finish in a controlled walk or stable stop in the safe finish zone, then reset. One cycle means **one step each side**. Source contact count2 refers to walking contacts, not two jump landings; intentional flight/plyometric landing events are zero.

Prerequisites are comfortable pain-free walking, brief controlled single-support exchange, understood pattern/count/stop/exit and fitting lane/session demands. There is no universal thigh height or cadence. Stationary marching, running/skipping, wall support, resistance, overhead reaching, obstacles or a projection transition are not this exact identity.
