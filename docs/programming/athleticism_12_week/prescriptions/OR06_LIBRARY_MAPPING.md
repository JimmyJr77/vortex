## FIRST-FLIGHT-TEACH: proposed instructional contract

The [complete draft card](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/docs/programming/athleticism_12_week/prescriptions/proposals/or06_first_flight_teaching_candidate.json) follows the existing [canonical card JSON schema](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/docs/workout-generator/schemas/exercise-card.schema.json) and includes identity, requirements, anatomy, why layer, coaching execution, equipment/environment, support, dose/scaling, load/fatigue, pairing, safety, media status and review gates. Difficulty scores and live IDs are null. The profile's fit score is explicitly a draft author judgment, not calibration, human approval or a probability of athlete eligibility. No media URL is fabricated. Review must decide whether the proposal belongs under the existing CMJ owner as an instructional profile/variant; a separate canonical exercise is not presumed.

**Action:** start stationary with comfortable bilateral feet and arms beside the trunk. Use one small shallow continuous countermovement and small natural arm swing, then a small in-place bilateral takeoff. Land on both feet in the same personal area, absorb through comfortable ankle/knee/hip movement, hold for 2 seconds, then stand/reset. No height/distance quota, overhead arm target, approach, static-start pause, repeated bounce, rebound, external load or assistance. The root's 8-second envelope is up to 2 s action + 2 s hold + 4 s stand/reset; it is a planning allowance, never a forced cadence.

**Instruction entry:** actual comfortable grounded lowering, ready-position hold and bilateral rise; comfortable intended unloaded arm range; understood action/stop/reset; qualified direct supervision; actual suitable surface, landing/fall/overhead space and recent-impact budget. Grounded evidence may be prior/direct equivalent or observed in the counted P2 action. No previous successful flight is required. The first takeoff and landing are observed in the first counted E1 attempt. The athlete may remain grounded, decline or stop; P2 completion never forces a jump or establishes jump competence.

**Root context:** P2 stays grounded: 3 s lower + 2 s hold + 1 s stand + 2 s reset. It is not a preparatory flight or rapid snap-down pass. All three ages' first-flight D route has two standard or one compressed single-attempt set; L has zero flight and its own grounded prescription. Minimum recovery is 60 s, with planned round spacing about 5–6 minutes. Each actual takeoff/landing, including faults, is counted; an attempted but grounded action has zero flight. No hidden screening or make-up attempt is added.

**Space:** 15 personal 3 × 3 m planning bays, centered first takeoff and nominal 1.5 m clear radius, plus separate peripheral coach circulation. Only one first-flight athlete acts at a time with two qualified coaches. Actual body fit, surface, fall/landing/overhead space and sightlines must be checked. Reconfigure or omit when the actual requirement exceeds the bay; 3 m is not a universal safety guarantee.

**Result boundary:** record actual flight/nonflight action, assistance or cue needs, landing/hold quality, first fault, dose and response. Instruction eligibility and a few attempts are not automatic same-day forward220, rebound, maximal jump or main JUMP qualification. The first-flight branch retains the actual unloaded hinge variant, setup and recent dose.
