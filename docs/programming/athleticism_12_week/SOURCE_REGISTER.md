4. Earlier programs provide reusable formatting, candidate concepts and historical context. Their different weekly calendars, warm-ups and phase structures do not override the current brief.
5. Verified primary external material can support general methodology. Vortex adapts it to youth, irregular attendance, time and tumbling. Source marketing is not evidence of guaranteed results or endorsement of this program.
