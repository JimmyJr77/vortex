## Primary decision and observable target

OR-11 should connect the actually owned straight stop to the early-announced ordinary walking exit with a shorter deliberate pause, while retaining the approach, exit direction/angle, output and Strength demand. The proposed single progression for individual authoring is **the full 2 s stopped pause → a 1 s stopped pause**, only when the corresponding 2 s task is actually repeatable and current readiness supports the change. This is a Vortex teaching decision for review, not a quoted source prescription or an automatic age/week progression.

The athlete still reaches an organized bilateral stop, then opens with the exit-side foot and walks the retained 2 m route at the retained 45° angle, with feet/body turning together. Keep the separate quiet finish. Look for controlled approach absorption, the actual stopped position, orderly opening steps, balance and a finish that does not require an uncontrolled recovery step. Do not impose a fixed braking-foot quota or pretend ordinary walking contacts are known constants. Record the actual approach, direction, pause used, movement/finish quality, cue needs and response.

The first counted 1 s attempt does not require a prior successful 1 s attempt. It does require actual repeatability of the corresponding full-pause task and current component suitability. If that prior evidence is missing or emerging, retain the 2 s teaching task, independently eligible terminal stop, position work or quiet standing. If control falls after a changed attempt, restore the suitable held/segmented task or stop; the fault consumes its opportunity. A successful reduced-pause attempt is useful evidence, not instant repeatability or reactive-cut permission.

The historical map mixes “reduce” with “removal of the pause” and “known plant.” Reconcile those fields explicitly during OR-11 authoring. A 1 s stopped pause does not remove the stop, and the ordinary opening-step turn is not a fixed-foot pivot. Preserve the map's intended controlled connection without silently substituting immediate cutting. Main BRAKE entry must name the actual observed stopped/stepped variant; it does not unlock every exercise in the BRAKE family. OR-13 and OR-20 retain their separate later roles.

## Preserve actual history and a useful full-body visit

Read actual OR-03/07 or direct-equivalent approach, stop, side-specific exit and static stance records, plus the most recent OR-06–10/direct Strength and response records. Session dates are opportunities, not prerequisites. A shorter pause on one repeatable side cannot manufacture repeatability on the other side. Use the allowed direction prefix at the actual smaller count; missing directions create no repayment work.

The retained static stance is the knee/unilateral role for the proposed lesson. Preserve its own actual hold seconds on each lead, support and range. OR-10's dynamic goblet work does not overwrite static-stance history, just as static stance does not unload or overwrite the compatible bilateral squat. A quiet bilateral task has no split entries. Track overlapping fatigue separately from these identities.

Hip, push, pull and brace complement the primary lesson without another load/handling or support-complexity progression. Carry the most recent actual compatible variation, count, range, setup, load and response. A first OR-06 DB set is still distinct from familiar loaded handling; later performed bodyweight hip work governs over older loaded records. Current smaller L/compressed doses persist when a new booking is D/standard. A first controlled observation does not erase familiar bodyweight work or grant familiar loaded row handling.

Resolve each secondary role's actual entry policy and time before writing its instructions. Independently suitable counted small bodyweight/high-support instruction is different from a retained familiar check; neither a small dose nor primary movement success qualifies unfamiliar loaded handling. Missing one role should preserve independently useful other work, with the missing instruction arranged within an appropriate existing budget or the affected role deferred. Push, row and brace readiness are separate; the breathing alternative requires its own supported floor setup and simultaneous bilateral arm action.

Ages 12–14 remain the reference. All three age bands need complete standard D/L and compressed D/L prescriptions, with actual within-age readiness overrides. A qualified younger athlete does not lose an owned task because of age, and an older newcomer does not receive the changed pause or a new load by default.

## Count preparation and first instruction honestly

Preserve the complete current instructional base and exactly two targeted tasks. The authoring direction is to retain the owned stance/finish rehearsal and straight walking-stop rehearsal, then place the first complete selected stop–pause–walking-exit task inside the existing main opportunity total. This requires an explicit amendment to the outline's preparation phrase about a whole blended exit, not an unrecorded additional rehearsal.

If the first main opportunity is represented as E0, it consumes the first assigned opportunity; E1 contains only the remainder. Do not require an extra whole 2 s exit before the first eligible 1 s exit when the athlete already has the relevant repeatable historical task and current component suitability. That would increase work for a one-opportunity history merely to satisfy a screen. Conversely, a narrow P2 walking stop establishes only its actual observed components, not a jog-stop, complete exit, opposite side or reduced-pause pass. Record retained full-pause and first/retained changed-pause opportunities separately.

The final athletic window remains recovery, explanation and actual recording, with no shuttle/chase repayment or exhaustion finisher. The separate tumbling coach receives actual braking/stance, lower-body, support/grip and attention/response information. Precise separately approved tumbling content remains unresolved and is not assigned zero workload.

## Required individual-authoring checks

Keep the standard athletic 0–15/15–45/45–75/75–90 and compressed 0–10/10–35/35–55/55–60 clocks. Use the current 15-athlete, three-lane, two-coach planning assumptions without claiming actual facility verification. Recompute lane clearance, announced-direction geometry, possible overlapping returners, incoming staging, personal-bay alternatives and coach sightlines. A shorter pause does not authorize faster athlete releases, shorter recovery or more work. Slower real travel/instruction requires delayed starts and removal of later opportunities.

Before finalizing OR-11, its author/checker/reviews must establish:

- A precise changed-pause movement/context and exact retained/first-instruction alternatives, with corresponding approach and each actual direction gated independently. No previous whole changed task is required merely to teach the first eligible attempt.
- A complete dose and purpose for every exercise/set in all three ages/four modes, including counted preparations, handling, side changes, finite main opportunities and actual smaller holds/repetitions.
- Five purposeful Strength roles or an explicitly justified different arrangement, with no accidental second knee set caused by treating static stance and dynamic squat as interchangeable.
- Current source/profile/method status and complete schema proposals only where the existing library needs them; no fabricated approval, kg, media or calibration.
- First whole-task/E0 inclusion, direction prefixes, faults that consume opportunities, preserved recovery and required reductions when real setup/traffic takes longer.
- Separate approach, ordinary walking exit, return travel, actual unknown natural contacts, static hold/entry units, dynamic repetitions and upper-body handling. Planned work is not an athlete result.
- Actual prior → current → next links to OR-13 and OR-20, with the unchanged OR-12/14/15 objectives and OR-15/19 first-flight amendment preserved.

Write the OR-11 amendment, complete author/export/mapping, daily anchor/workload ledgers and independent reviews before moving to OR-12. This source/stimulus brief does not complete OR-11, the remaining on-ramp, any additional main-block day or the full goal.
