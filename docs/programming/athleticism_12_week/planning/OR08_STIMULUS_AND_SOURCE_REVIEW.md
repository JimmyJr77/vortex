## Meaning of an intent-only decision

A modest change could mean **one slightly more purposeful but still comfortable build/relaxed-effort cue or ceiling** relative to the actual prior easy run, while keeping standing start, segment markers, ordinary arms, smooth rhythm, gradual runoff and recovery. This is a candidate interpretation for the author, not a selected RPE, pace, percentage, distance or dose. Do not convert the earlier estimated segment seconds into a faster completion target or a sprint test.

Keep opportunity count and protected route stable rather than simultaneously increasing effort, repetitions, distance and preparation complexity. A retained 15 m runoff is eligible only if it still accommodates the actual demand; if it does not, reduce/hold intent or explicitly redesign and revalidate the route. Do not shorten runoff, force a stop at the marker, or enlarge geometry while describing the change as intent alone. Physical facility fit and coach sightlines must be checked again; OR-04's three 35 m corridors, 2 m planning widths and separate one-way returns remain unverified inventory assumptions. OR-07's single 6×15 m diagonal fan and rear traffic layout cannot silently replace them. [OR-04 geometry](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/docs/programming/athleticism_12_week/instructional_on_ramp/week_01/or_04.json:10777), [OR-07 delivery amendment](/Users/jimmy_mac/Desktop/code/vortex/docs/programming/athleticism_12_week/instructional_on_ramp/DETAILED_OUTLINE_RECONCILIATION.json:49).

A dose or coaching-emphasis change does **not necessarily require a new exercise identity** under spec §9. A modest retained easy-run context may therefore remain EASY-BUILD-RUN-TEACH with explicit revised intent and renewed readiness/time/fatigue review. The exact existing owner/profile and current approval still need resolution.
