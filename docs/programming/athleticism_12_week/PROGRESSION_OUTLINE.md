## Week 1 — Continue and calibrate (P1)

Carry useful prior-block work into productive training; standardize observations without a beginner reset.

**Progression:** Retain established doses and variants; calibrate only uncertainty. **Review/recovery:** Comparable opening observations replace some work; actual prior-block history is required for individual continuation.

Pair a lead lift only with a task that does not share its limiting tissues or disrupt rest.
