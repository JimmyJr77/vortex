# OR-07 — Stop, pause and leave the announced direction

## Daily stimulus brief

Teach an ordinary walking exit in a direction announced before movement, after a complete controlled straight stop and balanced two-second pause. Retain the actually controlled approach. Supported static stance and small unloaded hip, push, pull and brace work follow. The new direction is the learning change; speed, angle, pause, support and strength load are not concurrently progressed.

**Success:** The athlete names the direction, controls the assigned straight stop, holds a balanced 2 s, opens with the exit-side foot and walks into the announced route without a planted-foot twist or rushed cut. Record stop, pause and each direction independently. A planned opportunity, ordinary side gate or supported hold never supplies a completed redirect result.

**Prior → current → next:** Actual OR03 or direct equivalent stopping/stance evidence and OR06 recent impact/hinge/grip response → OR07 announced paused walking exit with retained/reduced support work → OR11 reduces a pause only after repeatability at the retained approach/angle; OR13 checks movement and cue demands independently. Offered dates are not compulsory attendance or readiness evidence.

**SP-01, ages 9-11, 12-14, 15-18:** Easy walking march (1 × 60 s). Tempo: Natural walking rhythm, without a technical high-knee target. Effort: Easy and conversational; no maximal speed, height, stretch or fatigue target. Entry: Comfortable action, clear personal bay and ability to stop on cue. Counting: Time determines the dose; no repetition or contact count is predetermined. Contacts: No intentional flight; ordinary support/steps are not scored plyometric landings.

**SP-02, ages 9-11, 12-14, 15-18:** Two backward and two forward steps (4 × 2 s) → Settle (1 × 2 s). Tempo: 2 seconds per step; no rapid backpedal. Effort: Easy and conversational; no maximal speed, height, stretch or fatigue target. Entry: Comfortable action, clear personal bay and ability to stop on cue. Counting: One cycle is two backward steps followed by two forward return steps. Per-side values count backward steps only. Contacts: No intentional flight; ordinary support/steps are not scored plyometric landings.

### E1 — Stop, pause, then walk into the announced direction (Explosiveness)

**Mapping:** PAUSED-EXIT-TEACH new complete proposal; terminal-stop and stationary replacements retain their separate local mappings. **Set purpose / contribution:** Every listed attempt teaches or retains a complete controlled stop, balanced pause and ordinary walking exit in an early-announced direction. The first opportunity includes the first actual exit instruction; no separate test precedes it. Standard D uses three opportunities with a 2/1 direction split, compressed D and standard L use one per direction, compressed L one selected direction. Smaller recent count caps omit later opportunities.

**Setup and execution:** The coach names the athlete and left or right before release. Use the actual controlled walking 2 m or gentle-jog 5 m approach and original gradual braking/finish region. Establish a bilateral stop with soft knees and relaxed arms; hold a balanced 2 s before leaving. Unload and place the exit-side foot in a small opening step toward the announced 45-degree line; the other foot lifts/replants and follows as the body turns with the feet into ordinary walking for 2 m. Do not twist over a fixed planted foot, cross over, force a braking lead foot or accelerate. Finish quietly on two feet for 2 s, then walk outward to that side’s protected return on the coach’s instruction. Ordinary outward clearance is not another assessed exit. A failed stop means no attempted directional exit: keep the protected straight overrun clear, stop the affected sequence and record partial work. The 15 m straight lane end is not a compulsory braking deadline.

**Cues:** Name the direction first. Stop and balance. Hold two. Open with that side, let the other foot follow. Walk, finish, wait for clearance. **Common errors:** Leaving before balance, skipping the pause, a late cue, fixed-foot twisting, crossover, forced speed, rushed foot placement, uncontrolled exit/extra recovery step, claiming both directions from one side, or adding an attempt to correct a fault.

**Selection / order / pairing:** A full pause separates known braking from the new exit. The exact inspected sources describe terminal stops or immediate cuts; neither is renamed to claim approval for this teaching composite. Directional walking introduces orientation without simultaneously increasing approach output, angle, impact or strength load. Retained supported stance and small full-body support follow after adequate recovery.

**Metadata:** Method: segmented predictable instruction with full reset. Tenets: Body Control / Balance / Movement Intelligence integrated; Explosiveness component teaches braking and redirection prerequisites. No high-output cut, sprint or jump is prescribed; no performance gain or automatic sport transfer is asserted.
