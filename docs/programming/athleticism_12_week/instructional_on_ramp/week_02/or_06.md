## Daily stimulus brief

Develop a controllable landing and an understandable hinge decision without changing both demands at once. An athlete with a known flight or nonflight task retains it while considering a manageable hinge variant/hold. An athlete ready for first-flight instruction learns a small in-place bilateral takeoff and terminal hold while keeping unloaded hip work. The retained knee/push/pull/brace sets support full-body participation and respect the recent stopping, running and throwing lessons.

**Success:** The athlete performs the exact assigned flight/nonflight action, owns its comfortable finish and resets on cue. Separately, the hinge shows controlled hip-back motion and the athlete can explain why today uses bodyweight, a familiar pair or a first manageable implement lesson. Actual successful flight, extra steps, cue needs, grip/setup, load and response are recorded; planned attempts are not a pass.

**Prior → current → next:** Actual OR-02 landing/hip and more recent OR-03–05 response or direct equivalents → one named new learning demand today, with other anchors retained/reduced → OR-15 consolidates the actual landing and hinge record, then OR-19 verifies the independently eligible branch. Adjacent OR-07 still needs its own controlled stop/announced-exit checks. Use the preserved map together with the active detailed-outline amendments; attendance and age grant no prerequisite.
