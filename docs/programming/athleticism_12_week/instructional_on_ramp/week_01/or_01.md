## Daily stimulus brief

Teach the start/stop and group-flow contract while identifying eligible acceleration and basic full-body strength variations. Familiar or directly demonstrated competence can bypass instruction; unqualified behaviors receive focused coaching within time. The aim is a usable participation record, not fatigue or automatic graduation.

**Prior → current → next:** Direct intake/existing competence, with unknown evidence left unknown →OR-01 actual observed behavior and selected doses →OR-04/05 relevant conduct/running/upper-body opportunities and OR-10 next ACC/knee comparison. **SP-01, ages 9-11, 12-14, 15-18:** Easy walking march (1 × 60 s). Tempo: Natural walking rhythm, without a technical high-knee target. Effort: Easy and conversational; no maximal speed, height, stretch or fatigue target. Entry: Comfortable action, clear personal bay and ability to stop on cue. Counting: Time determines the dose; no repetition or contact count is predetermined. Contacts: No intentional flight; ordinary support/steps are not scored plyometric landings.

**SP-02, ages 9-11, 12-14, 15-18:** Two backward and two forward steps (4 × 2 s) → Settle (1 × 2 s). Tempo: 2 seconds per step; no rapid backpedal. Effort: Easy and conversational; no maximal speed, height, stretch or fatigue target. Entry: Comfortable action, clear personal bay and ability to stop on cue. Counting: One cycle is two backward steps followed by two forward return steps. Per-side values count backward steps only. Contacts: No intentional flight; ordinary support/steps are not scored plyometric landings.

**SP-03, ages 9-11, 12-14, 15-18:** Rightward step-close (1 × 4 s) → Leftward step-close (1 × 4 s) → Settle (1 × 2 s). Tempo: Slow walking steps; 4 seconds each direction. Contacts: No intentional flight; ordinary support/steps are not scored plyometric landings.
