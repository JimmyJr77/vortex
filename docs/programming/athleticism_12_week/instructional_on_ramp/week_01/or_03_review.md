# OR-03 written athletic session review

**Status: PASS for the written athletic design and numeric model.** This review concerns the individually authored athletic prescription. It does not establish current canonical approval, actual athlete readiness/completion, physical facility execution, or a completed separate tumbling program.

## Objective and continuity

The audited on-ramp map calls for a deliberate straight stop and a supported split stance before any redirect. [OR-03](or_03.md) preserves that objective: one walking preparation attempt, recovered individual stopping instruction, and a supported stance lesson followed by one familiar hinge, push, pull and brace set. The standardized base retains squat awareness. No cut, reactive cue, maximal sprint, jump series or conditioning finisher is introduced.

Actual OR-01 conduct and OR-02 bilateral-position observations, or direct equivalents, inform selection. A clean counted walking stop can establish that specific prerequisite, while pain-free easy-jog ability must be established independently. The jogging source does not require an already successful jog-stop. Mid-range split stance requires controlled descent and safe exit at its assigned depth; a shallow teaching hold is recorded separately. Unknown split entry uses bilateral orientation in preparation and the longer high-stance teaching block later, with the first listed entry counted inside its set.

OR-07 may add an announced exit only after retaining the actual controlled stop and checking the exit separately. OR-11 may later shorten a pause after repeatable stop-and-exit performance. Approach speed, zone geometry, support/depth and strength are not automatically advanced. Offered session numbers do not impose attendance or catch-up work.

## Source and exercise decisions

The [OR-03 library register](../../prescriptions/OR03_LIBRARY_MAPPING.md) distinguishes exact source155 jogging mechanics, the incomplete proposed walking variant, source212 supported mid-range isometric, dynamic split squat, and explicitly authored high-stance/quiet-standing teaching tasks. Local documentation keys are not live canonical identifiers. Familiar hinge, incline push, bench-supported row and fixed-arm heel-tap mechanics retain their previous mappings. Supported breathing and qualified suspension pulling replace their own role only.

Every default and conditional route includes all three ages and four booking/readiness modes. The small split holds are intentionally identical across ages; entry, depth and support depend on ability. D is three standard or two compressed single stopping attempts; L is two standard or one compressed walking/position attempt. Exact braking-contact counts remain unknown until observed. Zero intentional jump events does not mean zero jogging impact.
