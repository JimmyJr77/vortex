# OR-04 — Find upright rhythm and understand the running corridor

## Daily stimulus brief

Teach comfortable upright running rhythm and recognition of build, finish, runoff and return boundaries. Count one complete selected-corridor walk before subsequent eligible easy runs. Keep five familiar strength roles equally visible without a new loading emphasis.

**Success:** The athlete recognizes the selected running finish and protected runoff, remains inside the corridor, uses its outside return and waits for release. An eligible run builds smoothly and retains comfortable rhythm through its upright segment before gradual slowing. Record the actual route and first fault; walking/marching/short starts do not establish an upright-running result.

**Prior → current → next:** Actual OR-01 corridor/participation or direct equivalent →OR-04 counted route walk and eligible easy rhythm task →OR-08 may change gradual intent alone at the retained route →OR-12 retains route/output while teaching rest and return responsibility. OR-02/03 actual strength, braking, stance and sport exposure inform readiness; these are offered links, not required attendance.
