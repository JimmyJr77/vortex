The eight philosophy tenets remain unchanged. Every lesson preserves Prepare & Access → Explosiveness → Strength and the separate Body Control/Tumbling interface. Movement intelligence and resilience are expressed through the actual position, landing, braking, rhythm, waiting, handling and recovery tasks. No additional mandatory component or exhaustion finisher was introduced.

Statuses remain **unobserved, needs instruction, emerging, repeatable, modified or restricted** according to actual evidence. No number of attended sessions changes a status automatically. Ages 12–14 are the reference; every age uses its explicit prescription and the same requirement for actual independent prerequisites. An older newcomer may need instruction while an experienced younger athlete can retain a qualified variation.

## Attendance and numeric audit

The [weekly checker](../../planning/check_on_ramp_week_01.py) evaluates all **31 nonempty attendance subsets**, all three ages, every mixed standard/compressed and D/L assignment on attended days, and two explicitly hypothetical starting hip histories: **18,744 paths**, saved as **186 per-pattern/age/history summaries** in the [attendance/workload ledger](week_01_attendance_workload_ledger.json). The [check results](week_01_check_results.json) bind the audit to current daily-check and ledger provenance rather than trusting a status label alone.

The hypothetical history state represents **hip set count only**. A hypothetical completed and tolerated OR-01 one-set dose cannot become OR-02’s two-set hinge. Missing OR-01 with unknown hip history also selects the one-set hold. A compatible documented two-set history can retain that option; it never obliges two sets. Current actual variation, repetitions, load, range, response and other domain readiness still need their own evidence.

The original exemplar ledger did not include OR-01’s fully prescribed walking replacement. This weekly audit resolves **72 walking choices** from that existing prescription, keeping its 5 m target and 5 m walking exit separate from running metres. The historical daily scenario count remains unchanged. Partial mixtures and deferred work require actual recording; scalar minima/maxima are not a claim that all route extremes occur together.

OR-01’s return model is now explicit in its standalone export: three athletes per wave means **25 seconds between uses of each active lane**, not 75. Walking clears in 15 seconds and running in 20; each has a separate 40-second return allowance. All 32 mixtures across the five waves produce at most two returners per segregated path and up to 20 seconds of overlapping return travel. Dedicated one-way paths, no shared merge, adequate spacing and coach sightlines must be inspected. If unavailable, delay releases and remove later opportunities. Same-athlete repeat rounds remain at least 240 seconds apart. This is a conditional logistics check, not an observed facility result.

Every attendance subset leaves missing-domain instruction visible. A once-weekly visitor is not locked into that weekday’s first lesson forever: use actual records to arrange the needed module or a targeted check, while retaining mastered domains. Missing a session creates no repayment work. Even attending all five leaves main entry dependent on the exact observed branch and current readiness.

## Workload and recovery judgment

The model keeps unlike demands separate: OR-01 starts versus walking and runoff; OR-02 flight events and bilateral landing contacts; OR-03 walking/jogging approaches and stance holds; OR-04 short starts versus easy build/upright target metres, counted orientation walking and runout; OR-05 throws, static holds, floor handling, unloaded reaches and carrying. A retrieval-distance cap is labeled as a cap, not an actual carried distance. Ordinary walking/return contacts and actual athlete responses remain unknown rather than zero.

Across full five-visit, fully performed eligible scenarios, the reference-age model spans **25–30 strength station sets**. That figure neither compares the stress of a press, supported row, split hold and breathing substitution nor establishes that five D sessions suit an athlete. The ledger also preserves known pressing repetitions, bench-row repetitions per side, bilateral suspension-row repetitions, heel contacts per side and supported breath cycles. Actual load, support height, range, sport demand and response still determine the meaning of those counts.

OR-02/03/04 place different learning demands on the lower body; their low doses and rest do not prove recovery. Carry forward actual landing/braking/running fatigue, coordination changes and sport commitments. OR-05 removes intentional running/jumping from its main lesson, but floor handling, retrieval, repeated preparation and familiar lower-body strength still contribute work. Recent response may select a lighter route, fewer completed actions or deferral.

Upper-body/support work recurs throughout the week. OR-05 adds throwing and handling before pressing/pulling, so grip, wrist and shoulder response matters even with long between-attempt recovery and only one set per role. Breathing alternatives still have their own supported-position/reach gate. The final windows add zero physical sets. The separate tumbling coach must account for the actual accumulated support, limb, impact and attention demands; a handoff cannot certify that additional workload.
