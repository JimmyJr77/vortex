## What this session teaches

The athlete practices an eligible low forward jump with a two-second terminal hold, or a separately qualified nonflight position task. Main attempts receive individual observation: one athlete across the whole group acts at a time. The hip hinge receives eight minutes of standard Strength teaching or six minutes compressed. Knee, push, pull and brace each retain one small set. This gives the new landing and hip distinction attention without adding a second jump family, heavier resistance or a physical finisher.

Preparation retains the reviewed instructional base and concluding hinge/squat checks. Exactly two targeted tasks follow: a counted hip-position rehearsal and one already eligible finish rehearsal. The jump prerequisite exists before the scheduled flight attempt; neither attendance nor a slow squat grants it. When that evidence is unknown, the session supplies a separately named grounded route. An unqualified rapid snap-down uses a slow partial squat/hold teaching adaptation, with zero flight and no snap-down or jump pass.
