## Attendance and entry decisions

**Calendar:** Four weeks of five offerings organize teaching availability. An athlete progresses from demonstrated work, not from elapsed weeks, age, or number of visits.

**Direct entry:** Before bypass, verify universal participation domains and the exact simple variations needed by the current main session. Accept recent documented evidence with current readiness checks. Unknown is not passed. No forced retesting of every mastered domain.

**Partial entry:** Admit a documented suitable main variation while targeting remaining gaps only when supervision and that session permit. If a missing domain prevents safe participation, retain supervised instruction for that task. Do not place the athlete in unsupported independent stations.

**Lower frequency:** At each actual visit prioritize the missing domain needed next, retain brief familiar full-body/explosive practice, and carry the unresolved modules forward without adding missed workload. Fixed-weekday attendees may be directed to a relevant module/check within their visit; never wait for the same lesson to recur on the calendar.

**Frequent attendance:** Use low-demand learning and retained pattern practice between substantive exposures. Count sport and tumbling as well as gym work. Even an instructional session may add fatigue; reduce attendance when the whole schedule lacks recovery.

**Recording:** For every observed domain record athlete, date, offering ID, exact observed variation, setup/conditions, cue/support needed, status, readiness and actual completed work once prescribed. Status vocabulary: unobserved, needs_instruction, emerging, repeatable, modified, restricted. Record coach and next action. Outline plans are never actual results.

**Pass rule:** A qualified coach observes repeatable execution at the intended simple variation across separated opportunities with required control, cue understanding and recovery behavior. A single lucky effort or self-report is insufficient. Exact exercise-level criteria await canonical mapping; no universal numeric test is invented.

**Main handoff:** Record the matched current main session ID, eligible variation, most recent actual instructional exposure, unresolved restrictions, appropriate starting dose once prescribed, and next conditional exposure. Main calendar week never grants a progressed dose. Main-family links below identify eligible demand, not an exact unverified Day number.
