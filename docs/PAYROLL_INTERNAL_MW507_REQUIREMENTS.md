The certificate must preserve name, address, SSN, county, withholding-rate selection, exemption count, additional amount, exact exemption claim lines, signature under the printed perjury declaration and date. Keep all instructions and worksheet pages. The rate selections are single, married rate (including surviving spouse/head of household), and married but single rate. Do not infer the worksheet tax-return filing group from the withholding-rate selection.

The worksheet uses the printed AGI-dependent exemption values, separate additional aged-dependent and age/blindness exemptions, employee-estimated eligible line-c amounts, and floors line-e dollars divided by $3,200. Claimed line 1 cannot exceed a supplied worksheet line f. Further eligibility guidance and required-worksheet completion must be exposed in the employee flow.

Line 3 requires both prior-year and current-year no-liability attestations and the effective year. Renewal is due February 15 of the next year. A claim with expected wages above $200 weekly requires submission to Maryland. The signature date and historical applicability must be retained; an exemption Boolean alone cannot prove eligibility or current validity.

Line 4 lists DC, VA and WV and the printed abode certification. Pennsylvania uses line 5 for state exemption; local exemption requires the separate line 6 or 7 basis and EXEMPT on line 4 as instructed. Do not select a DC/VA/WV domicile checkbox for Pennsylvania. The WV abode exception in the instructions needs additional workflow treatment; never manufacture a false certification.

Military-spouse line 8 requires a state of legal residence and the stated eligibility certification. The form requires MW507M and a spousal military-ID attachment. A rendered line 8 does not prove those attachments or legal eligibility have been reviewed.

Employer review must cover suspected incorrectness, more than ten exemptions, nonresidence, military claims, expected wages for no-liability claims, revocations and required approval of replacement certificates. Additional withholding remains subject to the existing employer agreement workflow. Rendered claims must not silently enable unsupported state/local calculations.
