# Employer funding must not depend on employee deferral enrollment

An operational omission was found while preparing pre-approval employer compensation integration: payroll candidate discovery considered signed employee elections and eligible employee-deferral reviews, but neither exists for every person who may be owed an employer contribution. A nonelective plan could therefore be omitted before the employer-processing safeguard was reached.

Candidate discovery now includes each employee in the requested workplace payroll for plans with applicable employer matching, nonelective, combined, or unresolved terms. It chooses the latest revision effective on or before the payment date. A future revision cannot erase an earlier obligation. An applicable explicit NONE revision does not add employer candidates. Existing election/deferral candidates remain independently included and UNION removes duplicate employee-plan pairs. The shared discovery also feeds off-cycle retirement review.

Assumption: absent participant evidence is not proof of no employer obligation. Candidate inclusion means the plan must be evaluated; it does not establish eligibility, enroll the employee, or authorize a deduction. Until the complete employer-funding producer is integrated, these candidates receive the existing payroll review blockers. Even an ineligible participant will need the future employer eligibility/calculation path to establish that no contribution is due; this change does not claim that path is finished.

Verification: actual isolated database/API tests start with an approvable employee payroll, add a nonelective plan with no employee election or deferral review, observe the blocking preview, and verify an attempted approval returns 409 with the run still in REVIEW. Additional checks cover off-cycle review, cross-workplace/empty/unknown employee lists, future terms, effective NONE terms, and matching/combined/unresolved plans. The existing regular payroll contribution, approval ledger, finalization and employee decline test also passed.
