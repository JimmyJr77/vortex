# Carrier payment integration: verified boundary and remaining work

Reviewed September 11, 2026. This records the next missing execution path; carrier payments are not enabled. The provider adapter described below is implemented; internal reviewed destination setup is now implemented; execution coordination remains unfinished.

## Existing code boundary

- `backend/payroll/paymentConnection.js` retains encrypted, employer-scoped Modern Treasury funding configuration and verification history. Its internal reader supports a specifically retained connection revision.
- `backend/payroll/modernTreasuryPayments.js` builds employee ACH PPD instructions with payroll-run and employee identities. Its account reader explicitly requires an individual recipient. Those contracts cannot represent a carrier invoice payment without changing their meaning.
- Carrier invoice accounting now retains allocations, premium journals, reversals and correction reconciliations. It has no carrier payee destination, payment instruction, bank-settlement allocation or remittance receipt. QuickBooks journal confirmation is not evidence that the carrier was paid.

## Provider facts checked

Modern Treasury describes external accounts as counterparty-owned accounts, distinguishes individual and business parties, and returns account verification status and payment-specific routing details. The carrier destination path must bind the selected business and ACH destination to reviewed carrier instructions; verification alone does not establish that the carrier authorized that destination. [External account contract](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/platform/reference/external-account-object)

The provider documents CCD for business recipients and PPD for individual recipients. Carrier payments therefore need their own reviewed business-payment contract, independent of employee payroll elections and identifiers. [SEC codes](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/payments/docs/customize-sec-codes)

Payment orders support integer minor-unit amounts, direction, destination, effective date, external identity and remittance-advice configuration. A created order starts a payment lifecycle; it does not prove bank settlement. [Payment-order contract](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/platform/reference/payment-order-object)

External identifiers are immutable and can identify a resource during retrieval; a duplicate external identifier is rejected. Idempotency response caching is limited to 24 hours and scoped to the API key. Durable carrier instruction identity and retained send/recovery evidence must survive both cache expiry and credential replacement. [External IDs](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/platform/reference/external-ids), [idempotent requests](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/platform/reference/idempotent-requests)
