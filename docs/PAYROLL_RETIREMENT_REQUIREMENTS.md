# Internal retirement workflow requirements

This is the next implementation scope, not a completed feature or evidence that Vortex sponsors a particular retirement plan. The original payroll goal includes all applicable retirement deductions, reporting and remittance. Standard 401(k) support is the first implementation slice; other applicable plan types and exceptions remain requirements rather than being classified as not applicable automatically.

## Verified rules and implementation consequences

- The 2026 ordinary elective-deferral limit is $24,500. The general age-50 catch-up limit is $8,000; the higher limit for participants attaining ages 60–63 is $11,250. Plan permission and individual eligibility must be retained separately from statutory ceilings. A later tax year cannot reuse these values silently. [IRS 2026 limit announcement](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500), [IRS catch-up rules](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-catch-up-contributions).
- For 2026, the Roth catch-up prior-year wage threshold is $150,000. Review the applicable plan-sponsor wage basis and whether the threshold is exceeded, not merely current salary or estimated household income. Eligibility and plan Roth features matter; do not convert an employee's pretax election without the applicable retained plan/election basis. [IRS catch-up rules](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-catch-up-contributions), [IRS 2025-49 bulletin](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-49_IRB).
- Ordinary employee pretax 401(k) deferrals reduce federal income-tax wages, but remain in Social Security and Medicare wages. Roth elective deferrals remain in federal income-tax wages too. Preserve deduction type independently of generic pretax/posttax totals and verify state/employer-tax handling before integration. [IRS contribution withholding table](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plan-faqs-regarding-contributions-are-retirement-plan-contributions-subject-to-withholding-for-fica-medicare-or-federal-income-tax), [IRS 401(k) participant overview](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/401k-resource-guide-plan-participants-401k-plan-overview).
- W-2 reporting must retain distinct plan/type amounts; ordinary 401(k) elective deferrals use code D and designated Roth 401(k) contributions use code AA. Participation review is separate from the contribution totals. Existing employer-only box-13 support does not cover employee deferrals. [IRS W-2/W-3 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw2w3), [IRS retirement reporting errors](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/common-errors-on-form-w-2-codes-for-retirement-plans).
- Contribution remittance must account for the earliest reasonably segregable date. The seven-business-day small-plan safe harbor and the outside monthly limit must not become a universal permission to hold deductions until those dates. Retain actual employer/provider timing, participant-count applicability and deposit evidence. [DOL contribution fact sheet](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/resource-center/fact-sheets/employee-contributions.pdf).

## End-to-end acceptance scope

| Requirement | Evidence required before completion |
| --- | --- |
| Employer plan setup | Internal dated plan/provider configuration, plan document/reference, plan type, eligibility, eligible compensation, employer contribution formula, Roth/catch-up features, applicable enrollment rules and independently verified destination. No invented adopted policy. |
| Employee eligibility | Admin-reviewed effective eligibility, annual catch-up eligibility and relevant prior-year wage evidence; rehire/termination and plan changes preserve history. |
| Employee elections | Employee portal presents actual terms, signed percentage/fixed-amount pretax/Roth election, effective date, change/withdrawal history and acceptance state. Required automatic enrollment treatment must be implemented if applicable. |
| Annual sources | Retained verified opening balances and other-employer/other-plan deferrals where aggregation applies. Unknown amounts cannot be treated as zero. Corrected sources invalidate unused previews. |
| Calculation | Integer-cent calculations distinguish ordinary/catch-up, pretax/Roth, employee/employer amounts and plan/statutory limits. Tests cover age/year boundaries, limit exhaustion, variable compensation, insufficient pay, reversals and concurrent payroll. |
| Wage/tax integration | Independent federal, FICA, Medicare, FUTA and state wage-basis assertions; existing health-benefit deduction treatment cannot be reused indiscriminately. |
| Payroll review | Employee-level itemization, exact source/election binding, signed authorization where required, reconciliation of existing finalized and pending deductions, and no double reservation across simultaneous runs. |
| Statements and history | Employees can see deducted amounts, election basis, year-to-date totals and contribution delivery status without private admin/provider information. |
| Provider remittance | Employer/provider account verification, employee account mapping, contribution allocation, explicit authorized scheduling, committed idempotent claims, retries only with affirmative non-send evidence and query-only uncertain-outcome recovery. |
| Timeliness | Automatic deadline preparation and alerts use the verified employer/provider rule, business calendar and actual withheld dates. Provider acceptance is not trust receipt or participant allocation. |
| Bank/provider reconciliation | Exact bank settlement and participant allocation receipts, partial/returned/rejected contributions, corrected files and shortage/excess handling. Funds are not marked remitted solely from a UI confirmation. |
| QuickBooks | Separate employee contribution liabilities and employer expense/liability treatment, verified accounts/company, posting claims, recovery and correction journals. |
| Year-end | W-2 code D/AA totals and box-13 participation bind finalized evidence; imported corrections and excess-deferral treatment retain their own reviewed source. Applicable 403(b), SIMPLE, 457 and other plan codes remain open until implemented. |
| Admin exception resolution | Internal queues for missing eligibility, insufficient pay, stale elections, missing external balances, unmatched provider records and late/returned remittances, with dated resolution history and employee-visible responses. |
| Production verification | Configured employer/plan/provider access, tested integration contracts and a full employee-to-admin-to-provider-to-accounting rehearsal. Synthetic tests do not prove real plan enrollment or remittance. |

## Implementation sequence

1. Add typed, dated retirement plan and participant eligibility/election evidence; implement reviewed 2026 limits and opening balances without enabling payroll deductions prematurely.
2. Integrate typed deferrals and employer contributions into payroll wage bases, reservations, statements and annual reporting together.
3. Add contribution batches, provider transmission/recovery, allocation/bank evidence and QuickBooks journals.
4. Finish applicable special-plan, correction, excess and termination cases; run the full acceptance matrix above.

The existing 654-test payroll/email baseline predates this retirement scope. No retirement deductions, remittances or W-2 changes have been enabled by this requirements document.

## Proposed per-pay allocation and typed wage treatment

`retirementContributionCalculation.js` now computes proposed employee deferrals from an applicable signed election, exact plan/annual fingerprints, explicit compensation categories and ledger inputs. Percentage elections use integer arithmetic; fixed-per-regular-pay elections skip off-cycle runs. Included compensation respects the reviewed first-compensation-limit treatment. Ordinary and permitted catch-up amounts are separate. Limits allocate proportionally with largest fractional remainders; a tied cent goes to pretax. This is an implementation assumption to bind to reviewed processing terms before activation, not a statutory allocation rule. Mandatory Roth catch-up never converts an elected pretax amount. Catch-up requires explicit caller-supplied authorization and valid plan treatment. Insufficient available pay raises a review error. The caller still must calculate available pay after taxes/other deductions and derive all ledger/reservation inputs from scoped records.

`retirement401kTaxWages.js` separates gross, income-tax, bonus and unemployment/FICA wages for standard 2026 Maryland-resident employee deferrals. The withholding engine accepts this explicit typed input while retaining its rejection of generic unverified pretax deductions. Regular/annual-bonus allocation must be supplied and reconcile to the corresponding wages. Pretax employee deferrals reduce federal income-tax wages; Roth does not, and both remain in FICA/FUTA wages. [IRS withholding treatment](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plan-faqs-regarding-contributions-are-retirement-plan-contributions-subject-to-withholding-for-fica-medicare-or-federal-income-tax), [IRS 401(k) overview](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc424). Maryland withholding generally follows federal wage exclusions for this ordinary treatment. [2026 Maryland withholding guide](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/instructions/withholding/2026/withholding-guide.pdf). The Maryland employer guide expressly includes elective employee 401(k) deferrals in unemployment taxable wages. [Maryland employer guide, elective-contribution table](https://www.labor.maryland.gov/employment/empguide/empguide.pdf). These helpers do not cover state pension pickup contributions or other plan types.
