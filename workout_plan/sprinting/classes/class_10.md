# Sprinting — Class 10 of 12

**Primary effort:** Initiate forward force from a compact two-foot base, then support prompt free-leg recovery and nearby subsequent contacts. Practice the upright component separately in stationary space.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified coaching. Reviewed Classes 1–9, start/upright and strength anchors, workload, all drill contingencies, equipment and feedback. **Actual attendance, execution, loads and recovery remain unknown.** Five default variants have specific task differences; the paired wall switch deliberately recurs. Strength doses hold, with no inferred tolerance or automatic intensity increase.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Today's starts are straight ahead; upright running drills and targeted strength are supplemental gym work. No actual maximum-velocity running or forced upright transition within the short start is prescribed.

**Quality marker within E1:** Record first stepping foot, setup, attempted/controlled starts, forward push without a backward step, body control and stopping. Observe only the four scheduled attempts. Keep this static bent-over record separate from falling or crouched-start scores.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 receives the main performance practice. Other entries isolate forward projection, individual reception, upright recovery, inclined support exchange and light resisted thigh force. Use modest jump distances/heights and quick clean intent; do not turn the drills into conditioning.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bent-Over Sprint Start — parallel feet, first two steps** | **2 × 2 starts**, one with each first stepping foot per pair. Toes at **1 m**; at most **2 driving strikes or 3 m of drive**, ending by **4 m**. Progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between starts; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main task:** create the first push from a still hand-free hip hinge, then step forward into individual support. Feet parallel near hip width, knees softly bent, hips slightly back, trunk inclined with a comfortable spine and arms free. Fit the whole starting body inside **0–2 m**. Settle **2 s**, push and bring the selected foot through; no backward step, preliminary hop or fall from tall standing. Let the posture rise naturally rather than forcing an early upright position. First pair is controlled-fast to establish stopping; second is faster only while control remains. |
| 2 | **Split-Stance Broad Jump to Split-Stance Stick — same lead** | **1 × 2 per lead leg**, natural dip/arms, modest forward distance; **2 s landing hold**. **20 s between same-lead reps; 60 s between leads.** | Preserve an offset base through forward projection and reception, complementing E1's parallel origin. Both feet contribute and leave the floor; receive with the same leg forward on separate hip-width tracks. Keep the split modest, front foot supported and rear forefoot stable; no scissor exchange or long lunge. Reduce distance/offset for twisting, uneven reception or rear-foot collapse. |
| 3 | **Two-Foot Vertical Jump to Single-Leg Stick** | **1 × 2 per receiving leg**, bodyweight, natural arms and modest height; hold each receiver **2 s**, then set the free foot down gently. **20 s between same-leg reps; 60 s between legs.** | Separate a shared two-foot push from one-leg support, without forward travel. Use a small countermovement with parallel feet; both feet leave before the free leg lifts clear. Bring the selected receiving foot beneath the pelvis, land softly and control the knee/hip without an extra hop. Do not chase height. Use the two-foot replacement if the single-leg receiver cannot be qualified. |
| 4 | **Stationary Alternating Step-Over Run — both legs cycle** | **1 × 8 alternating running contacts**, four/foot, low flight; finish with **one gentle free-foot set-down**. About 5 s including the finish; contacts govern. | Couple compact heel/thigh recovery to modest lower-leg release on both sides before the foot returns nearby. Begin on one foot with the other thigh comfortably raised; alternate supports with natural opposing arms. Let the knee open a little after recovery without a high kick, long backswing or deliberate backward claw. Unlike Class 6's asymmetric drill, both legs use the fuller pattern. Reduce range/speed for reaching, drift or a broken rhythm. |
| 5 | **Wall Drill Double Switch — paired exchanges** | **1 × 2 pairs per initially raised knee**. Each pair = **2 quick switches without an intermediate hold**, then **2 s hold** and a controlled free-foot set-down. **20 s between pairs; 45 s between starting sides.** | **Intentional Class 8 repeat:** retain two consecutive leg exchanges while the forward body line stays controlled, directly supporting the next-contact task after E1. Both hands press a verified immovable wall; switch supports twice and finish with the original knee raised. Keep hips level, elbows steady and feet near their starting positions. Eight fast contacts plus four controlled set-downs. Step out of the lean for recovery; shorten lean/range for sliding or sagging. |
| 6 | **Standing Banded Knee Drive — opposite foot planted** | **1 × 3 per moving leg**, very light suitable closed foot-loop band; quick lift / **1 s finish / 2 s controlled return**. **10 s between reps; 45 s between legs**. | Express rapid thigh recovery against light resistance while the other leg supports the upright body. Start with both feet flat near hip width and a secure loop around the shoe midfeet; the support foot stays flat and still as the moving knee lifts through a comfortable range. The band must oppose the lift. Return the moving foot gently and relax between reps. No added knee extension, hop, torso lean or heavy grinding lift. Reduce tension/range for a slowed drive, pelvic movement or slipping band. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Instruction/setup may occupy recovery, counted once. Relax out of held positions between efforts and extend rest when force, focus or control has not returned. No extra contacts fill waits.

**Room and equipment:** Use one protected **10 m lane**, with one athlete moving and the lane closed during return. E1 begins at 1 m and stops by 9 m: **8 m maximum outward travel per start**, including at most 3 m of driving. Verify the whole starting footprint, traction, stopping, lane width, waiting positions and return route. E2/E3 need clear receiving and overshoot space; E4–E6 stay in place. A movable rack/box is not a wall. The older 12 m facility note remains unresolved; no additional run-out is assumed.

**Band qualification:** Use only an intact closed loop intended and sized for the foot setup, securely seated across shoe midfeet, within its stated stretch limit. Check support-foot traction and verify that lifting stretches the band against the moving foot; do not improvise a knot, external anchor or extra loop. Actual loop type/length/tension, wall suitability, surface, equipment sizes, staff and quantities remain unconfirmed. If a fast controlled standing lift cannot be qualified, use the floor replacement before delivery; do not add tension to force a stronger stimulus.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** If the parallel hinged launch is unproductive, use **Two-Point Standing Start instead**, four starts, front toe at 1 m, rear foot within the protected starting area, same drive/stop limits and rests. This repeats an earlier family. If stopping is insufficient, use one driving step at lower intent for remaining attempts; stop if still uncontrolled. No extra trials.
- **E2:** If split reception is unsuitable, use **Staggered-Stance Broad Jump to Parallel Two-Foot Stick instead**, same four jumps/rests/holds; Class 6 recurrence. If the offset takeoff itself is unproductive, use **Parallel Broad Jump to Stick instead**, same total/rests, natural dip/arms and modest distance; Class 3 recurrence. Choose one replacement, not both.
- **E3:** If single-leg reception is not controlled, use **Bilateral Countermovement Jump to Stick instead**, **2 × 2**, natural arms, modest height and 2 s holds; **20 s between reps, 60 s between sets**. This repeats an earlier fallback family. Four bilateral receivers replace four unilateral receivers; the four separate free-foot set-downs are removed.
- **E4:** If lower-leg release creates persistent reaching, use **Stationary A-Run instead**, eight contacts and one controlled finish set-down; earlier family. If flight is unsuitable, use **Fast A-March instead**, eight alternating controlled foot returns, one foot always down and no extra running finish set-down. No full step-over bout is added afterward.
- **E5:** For lost paired rhythm, use **Isolated Wall Single Switches instead**, eight total, four/foot: hold 2 s, set the free foot down and release the lean for **10 s** before resetting; alternate the initial raised knee. Eight fast contacts plus eight controlled set-downs. Without a qualified wall, use **High-Plank Isolated Single-Switch Knee Drives instead**, eight total: hands beneath shoulders, forward foot hovering, exchange once so only the extending foot lands, hold 2 s, lower knees and rest 10 s before resetting; alternate the initial forward knee. Keep the trunk quiet. These are Class 2 families; no paired work is added.
- **E6:** If the band, traction or standing support cannot be qualified, use **Supine Unresisted Knee Drive, opposite heel grounded, instead**, **1 × 3/leg**, quick knee lift / 1 s finish / 2 s controlled lower, 10 s between reps and 45 s between legs. Keep the nonmoving leg comfortably long, heel grounded and pelvis quiet; gently return the moving heel to the floor. This repeats Class 3's fallback with different resistance/support and avoids adding another standing march. Stop for persistent arching or discomfort after reducing range.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts count as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; persistent faults despite adjustment end that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait, insecure band, lost control or lane intrusion stops the affected work immediately. Log actual substitutions, set-downs, extra contacts, stopping, omissions and reasons. Incomplete delivery remains incomplete; no make-up attempts, added tests or extra slots.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Keep these small strengthening doses below pre-fatiguing levels.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, supporting the initial push, individual receiver and nearby subsequent contacts. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for pelvic control during individual reception and free-thigh recovery. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range for pelvic rolling or a forward-drifting thigh. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; keep the work easy rather than adding resistance to create a burn.
