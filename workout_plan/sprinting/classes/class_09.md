# Sprinting — Class 9 of 12

**Primary effort:** Maintain upright support while recovering a compact free leg and returning the foot near the hip, with quick force expression and little unnecessary trunk movement.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified coaching. ## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main rapid coordination task. E2 uses knee/hip-driven vertical rebound; E3 uses low ankle-led recoil. E4–E6 supply distinct nonflight placement, limb-timing and hip-force jobs. Keep intent quick and clean, without chasing height, cadence or fatigue.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stationary High-Knee/Butt-Kick Run — coupled recovery** | **2 × 6 alternating running contacts**, three/foot/bout; **90 s between bouts**. Low flight, bodyweight. Start on opposite support legs; finish each bout with **one gentle free-foot set-down**. | **Main task:** fold the recovering knee while the thigh rises, then return the foot near its standing position. Begin on one foot with the other knee comfortably raised and heel folded beneath the thigh. Exchange supports with natural opposing arms and no extra bounce; allow a compact recovery on each side. Keep the pelvis tall, without forcing a hip-height knee or heel-to-butt contact. This is one integrated running pattern, not separate high-knee and butt-kick sets. Reduce height/speed for reaching, trailing thigh, arching or drift. |
| 2 | **Repeated Vertical Jump — two consecutive jumps, final stick** | **2 × 2 consecutive jumps**, bodyweight, natural arms; **120 s between two-jump bouts**. Modest height, no approach or external drop. | Accept the first vertical receiver and reuse it for another upward leg push. Start with parallel feet and a comfortable knee/hip dip; jump twice without a deliberate pause between them, then hold the final bilateral landing **2 s**. Keep the knees tracking and the torso controlled. Do not take the second jump if the first receiver collapses, twists or needs a deep recovery squat. |
| 3 | **Split-Stance Pogo — same lead retained** | **1 × 2 low hops per lead leg**, four hops total; **60 s between leads**. Bodyweight; final landing held **2 s**. | Keep upright alignment through ankle-led recoil with front/rear support offset. Use a small comfortable split with hip-width spacing, both forefeet contributing, knees soft and nearly unchanged. Both feet leave and return to their own positions together; no scissor exchange, travel, deep split-squat dip or forced toe-only finish. Let the heels settle for the final hold. Reduce offset/amplitude for uneven contacts or trunk pitching. |
| 4 | **Standing Straight-Leg Downstroke — other foot planted** | **1 × 3 per moving leg**, bodyweight; controlled **1 s low forward lift**, quick return near the support foot, then settle. **10 s between reps; 45 s between legs**. | Place a longer recovering leg near the hip through a small hip-driven downstroke, without a full running cycle or support-leg hop. Keep the moving knee softly extended, lift the foot only a little forward/off the floor, then bring it down beside its starting position. The other foot stays planted. Make a precise light contact, not a stomp or backward scrape. Reduce lift/range for pelvic tipping, a reaching contact or hamstring discomfort. |
| 5 | **Seated Alternating Sprint Action — knee lift with opposing arms** | **1 × 6 alternating knee drives**, three/leg; quick lift / **1 s finish / 2 s foot return**. **10 s between reps**, feet down and arms relaxed during resets. Bodyweight. | Couple the rising thigh to opposing arm action while the seat supports body mass. Sit tall on a stable fitted seat, feet initially flat and knees comfortably bent. Lift one thigh a few centimetres while the opposite arm moves forward and the same-side arm moves back; lower the foot gently and reset before alternating. No rocking, hand push, shrug or independent extra arm swings. Reduce the range/speed for lost timing or torso movement. |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Bodyweight Hip Hinge — rapid return** | **1 × 2 per support leg**, bodyweight; **2 s shallow hinge, quick controlled rise, 1 s tall hold**, then gentle free-foot set-down. **10 s between reps; 45 s between legs**. | Express hip extension over one planted foot without another jump landing. With a soft support knee, move the hips back as the trunk inclines slightly and the free leg follows behind; keep the hip points level without opening sideways. Return briskly to tall alignment without throwing the back leg, snapping the spine or adding a knee drive. Use a shallow range and light contact on verified stable hand support if needed. If the return becomes slow balance work, use the floor replacement. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Instruction/setup may occupy recovery, counted once. Relax out of held positions between efforts. Extend rest if force, attention or control has not returned; no extra contacts fill the waiting time.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** If the coupled fold cannot stay controlled, use **Stationary A-Run instead**, same two six-contact bouts, rest and finish set-downs; comfortably bent-knee exchanges, without forcing the tighter heel fold. This repeats Class 4. If flight itself is unsuitable, use **Fast A-March instead**, **2 × 6 alternating returning-foot placements**, one foot always down and 90 s rest; no extra running finish set-downs. Record the actual pattern rather than assuming the default occurred.
- **E2:** If the rebound receiver is not controlled, use **Isolated Bilateral Countermovement Jumps to Stick instead**, **2 × 2**, natural arms, modest height and 2 s holds; **20 s between reps, 120 s between sets**. This repeats an earlier fallback family. A failed first receiver ends its bout; the omitted second jump is not made up.
- **E3:** If the split base disrupts ankle recoil, use **Bilateral Ankle Pogo in parallel stance instead**, **1 × 4**, low flight, soft knees and 2 s final hold. This repeats Class 1's family and remains different from E2's larger knee/hip-driven jumps.
- **E4:** If the long-leg return cannot be controlled after reducing range, use **Standing Single-Leg Cycle and Strike instead**, **1 × 3/moving leg**, 10 s resets and 45 s between legs. Keep the support foot planted; fold the free heel/thigh into a compact cycle, allow a modest lower-leg release and return the foot beside its support. This repeats Class 3. No hopping, high kick or backward claw.
- **E5:** A stable fitted box may replace the bench. If suitable seating is unavailable or the coupled action cannot stay controlled, use **Standing Arm Action Drill instead**, **1 × 6 opposing arm exchanges**, quick change / 1 s controlled finish, 10 s relaxed reset between reps; feet grounded, knees soft and torso quiet. This repeats Class 4's fallback and removes the six knee lifts/foot returns; no marching is added.
- **E6:** If a shallow hinge with available support is unproductive, use **Single-Leg Glute Bridge with rapid rise instead**, **1 × 2/leg**, quick rise / 1 s level-pelvis hold / 2 s lower, 10 s between reps and 45 s between legs. Keep the working foot planted and the opposite thigh comfortably raised; reduce range for twist/cramp. If single-leg control is also unsuitable, use **Bilateral Glute Bridge with rapid rise instead**, **1 × 4**, same tempo and 10 s between reps. Both are disclosed Class 4 families. Stop if pelvic control cannot be restored.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts count as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; persistent faults despite adjustment end that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait or lost body/implement control ends it immediately. Record actual substitutions, controlled set-downs, extra contacts, omitted work and reasons. No make-up attempts, added tests or extra slots; incomplete delivery stays incomplete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Keep these small strengthening doses below pre-fatiguing levels.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, supporting the foot used for upright support, recoil and nearby placement. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for pelvic control as the free thigh recovers over a support leg. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range for pelvic rolling or a forward-drifting thigh. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; keep the work easy rather than adding resistance to create a burn.
