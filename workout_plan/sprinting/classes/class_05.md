# Sprinting — Class 5 of 12

**Primary effort:** Support the body through one leg while recovering the other thigh quickly, with a stable pelvis and compact foot return. This is supplemental gym work for upright running mechanics.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique, under qualified coaching. Classes 1–4, workload and drill history, audits, equipment assumptions and available feedback were reviewed. **Actual completion, loads and recovery remain unknown.** New drill demands are conditional on current competence; no athlete has been recorded as earning an intensity increase. Strength doses stay at Class 4's reduced levels.

**Framework:** The curriculum retains `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Today emphasizes the latter through stationary support work. There are **no starts or actual maximum-velocity runs**. Gym placement cues are not literal descriptions of full-speed touchdown geometry, and these drills do not establish a sprint-speed result.

**Quality marker within E1:** For each target leg, record the three attempted cycles, controlled cycles, compact heel recovery, thigh lift without backward lean, support-side pelvic control and any extra contact. Use only the listed attempts. This asymmetric running rhythm has its own observation record, separate from Class 4's alternating A-run.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main rapid coordination task. Complete exercises in order. Brief supporting doses serve different parts of the same support/recovery effort; speed must remain clean and unforced.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stationary Single-Leg High-Knee Run** | **1 × 3 cycles per target leg**; **90 s between target legs**. One cycle = target-foot running contact plus opposite-foot running contact: **12 total contacts**, 6/foot. | **Main task:** repeatedly recover one thigh while the other leg uses a low, compact running step. Begin supported on the opposite leg with target thigh comfortably raised. Exchange onto the target foot, then onto the opposite foot as the target heel folds beneath the hip and its thigh rises again. Repeat this asymmetric rhythm without a full cycle on the low-step leg. Use small flight, natural arms and nearby foot contacts; do not hop twice on one foot. Finish the third cycle under control. Lower knee height/speed for long backswing, drift, reaching or backward lean. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Vertical Jump to Two-Foot Landing** | **1 × 2 per takeoff leg**, bodyweight, natural arms; forceful push at modest height. **20 s between same-leg reps; 60 s between legs.** | Express each leg's upward push while the free thigh rises, then share reception across both feet. Use a small countermovement on the takeoff leg; jump upward and bring both feet beneath the body for a **2 s held landing**. This changes Class 4's same-leg receiver rather than demanding greater height. Reduce height for pelvic rotation, wide/asymmetric reception or an extra hop. |
| 3 | **Single-Leg Knee-Drive Pogo — stationary, same support leg** | **1 × 2 hops per support leg**, bodyweight; very low flight. **60 s between legs.** | Couple a quick ankle push on the same leg with a pulse of the opposite thigh. Pop the free thigh forward as the support ankle pushes; let the free leg lower toward a low hover during recoil without touching the floor, then repeat. Keep the support knee soft and the torso tall; the free foot does not become the next support as it does in E1. Hold the final same-leg landing **2 s**, then set the free foot down. Reduce thigh range/amplitude if the two actions cannot stay coordinated. |
| 4 | **Stationary B-Skip — controlled lower-leg release** | **1 × 2 left–right pairs** = **4 step-hop actions**, two per raised-thigh side. Each action has one support-leg hop landing and one raised-foot transfer contact. | Add a controlled lower-leg release to the thigh recovery while preserving the skip rhythm. Lift a thigh during a small hop on the opposite foot; allow the raised knee to open modestly, then bring that foot down near its standing position and transfer support. Alternate sides without traveling. This is not a high straight-leg kick or a backward claw. Reduce knee opening/range for a reaching contact, trunk lean or disrupted rhythm. Finish after the fourth transfer; no extra skip. |
| 5 | **Reverse Lunge to Knee Drive — rapid ascent, no flight** | **1 × 3 per working/front leg**, bodyweight; **2 s lower, quick rise, 1 s tall finish**. **10 s reset between reps; 45 s between legs.** | Drive through a planted front foot while the stepping leg returns forward into thigh recovery. Step the free foot back a comfortable distance, lower into a controlled lunge, then push predominantly through the front leg and bring the rear knee forward/up. The front foot stays flat and planted throughout; no jump. Return the free foot beside it before the next rep. Shorten step/depth for rear-foot propulsion, knee collapse, pelvic hitching or a slow/unstable rise. |
| 6 | **Supine Alternating Hip Switch — both heels off the floor** | **1 × 4 opposing leg exchanges**, bodyweight; **quick switch, 1 s settled finish**, then **10 s reset between exchanges**. Each exchange moves both legs once; two returns to each starting configuration. | Practice rapid opposing thigh motion while the pelvis has floor support, removing the landing task. Lie with one hip/knee comfortably flexed and the other leg reaching diagonally away with a soft knee and heel clear of the floor. Exchange the leg positions through a short controlled range; do not fully straighten the reaching knee or force the thigh to the chest. Keep the pelvis quiet and breathe. Bring both feet down during resets, then gently re-establish the next starting configuration. Shorten the reach/bend the knees more for arching, breath-holding or slowing; stop if control cannot be restored. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Resets are recovery rather than extra holds. Setup/instruction can use rest time; count actual elapsed time once. Extend rest if force, focus or movement control has not returned.

**Room and equipment:** All defaults are stationary or use one backward lunge step within a clear floor station. Verify enough width and rear clearance for that step and any unplanned landing adjustment. Keep weights and waiting athletes outside the movement area. No runway, wall, low platform, band anchor, throwing clearance or device is required. The supplied three separate 10 m lanes do not establish group capacity or a continuous runway; the older 12 m facility note remains unresolved. DB ranges, fitted bench/box, surface and quantities still require on-site checks for the strength work.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** Use **Stationary Single-Leg High-Knee March instead of the airborne run** if the rhythm cannot be controlled: same three cycles/target leg and rest, quick target-leg recovery with one foot always down. Twelve nonflight placements replace twelve running contacts; record the change.
- **E2:** Use **Bilateral Countermovement Jump to Stick instead of single-leg takeoff** if the push cannot be controlled: **1 × 4**, modest height, natural arms, 2 s held landing, 20 s between reps. This repeats an earlier optional jump family for readiness; all four landings remain bilateral.
- **E3:** Use **Single-Leg Pogo without the thigh pulse instead of Knee-Drive Pogo** if coordination is the limitation: same two hops/leg and rest. This explicitly repeats the Class 2 family at a lower dose. If unilateral reception itself is unsuitable, use **Bilateral Ankle Pogo instead**, **1 × 4**, very low flight and a 2 s final hold; not both replacements.
- **E4:** Use **Stationary A-Skip instead of B-Skip** if the release produces persistent reaching: same two left–right pairs, four hop landings and four transfer contacts. This repeats Class 1's family and removes the lower-leg release job; do not label it a successful B-skip.

**Quality rules:** Attempted work counts even when faulty. Adjust the next scheduled rep; recurring faults despite adjustment end that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait or loss of control ends the affected exercise immediately. Record omissions, actual replacements, extra settling/exit contacts and reasons. No make-up work or added exercise slots. If neither the default nor its regression is productive, actual delivery is incomplete rather than recorded as fourteen finished exercises.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Retain these small strengthening doses without pre-fatiguing the loaded work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases as the working arch gently rises, supporting the foot repeatedly used for E1/E3. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for holding pelvic position over one support leg as the other moves. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range if the pelvis rolls or the thigh drifts forward. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; do not add resistance to create a burn.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging loads/leverage leaving approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve (RIR)**. Keep Class 4's working doses and preparation rules. Actual prior loads are unknown: qualify today's load rather than assuming an increase or a previously completed set. No forced reps, maximal tests or automatic restoration of reduced volume.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **1 × 5 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. **60 s between legs.** | Retain loaded lead-leg extension from a stable base to support the leg-force task rehearsed rapidly in E5. Both feet stay on the floor; modest whole-trunk inclination without rounding. Reduce load/depth for knee collapse, pelvic rotation or rear-leg hopping. The reduced single round limits overlap with the bodyweight lunge. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. | Retain hip-extension strength through a flexed-hip range, complementing free-thigh recovery and ankle-oriented work. Soft knees, bells close; stop depth before spine/pelvic compensation. Reduce range/load as needed. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Load thigh lifting separately from the light rapid exchanges. Secure a DB on the distal thigh above the knee with both hands; lift the knee a few centimetres without hand assistance or backward lean. Start on a fitted stable seat with foot supported and knee near a right angle. Reduce load/range for pinching, tipping or a shifting implement. |
| 4 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 complete cycles**, approximately 2–3 clean cycles in reserve. One cycle = **4 small alternating heel steps out + 4 back**, about **8–10 s**. | Strengthen knee-flexor support as the knee opens/returns, complementing E4's controlled release and the hip hinge. Keep the pelvis raised, knees soft at the far endpoint and return to the original heel positions. Shorten steps for sag/cramp; stop if control cannot be restored. This is capacity work, not high-speed swing simulation. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, floor range** | **1 × 10**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. | Retain near-straight-knee plantarflexion force for repeated upright support. DBs at sides, both forefeet loaded, heels return to floor. Reduce load for lost heel height, outward roll or bounce; do not lock the knees forcibly. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **2 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. **90 s between sets.** | Maintain ankle-force work with the knee bent, complementing P5 and soft-knee support during the drills. Secure weights on thighs above the knees; forefeet stay down and heel range stays repeatable. Reduce load for knee straightening, rolling or bounce. |

**Load-preparation sets, separate from working sets:** P1 **1 × 4/leg** bodyweight/light (30 s between sides, 60 s before work); P2 **1 × 5 easy then 1 × 3 moderate** (60 s between, 90 s before work); P3 **1 × 3/leg light** (30 s between, 45 s before work); P4 **1 short-lever cycle** (45 s before work); P5 **1 × 5 easy** (45 s before work); P6 **1 × 5 easy** (60 s before work). If P5 balance limits productive loading, use **supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between, 45 s before work. Verify stable hand support and log laterality. A fitted stable box may replace the bench for P3/P6.
