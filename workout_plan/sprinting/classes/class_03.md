# Sprinting — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Direct each leg's push into forward projection and transfer to the next support without pelvic rotation or reaching. Pair that work with rapid free-leg recovery and targeted strength.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified coaching. The saved Classes 1–2, map, ledger, drill history, audits, equipment assumptions and available feedback were reviewed. **No actual completion or recovery observations are reported.** This is a conditional prescription, not evidence that the athletes earned greater intensity. The six default explosive tasks differ from earlier defaults; familiar strength lifts continue with less overlapping hinge/walkout work.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. This class uses straight-line starts and supplemental gym work. It does **not** contain actual maximum-velocity running or establish a top-speed result.

**Quality marker within E1:** For each scheduled start, record front leg/grounded hand, whether hand release and leg drive produce a direct forward launch, any backward rock or rotation, whether the opening steps reach conspicuously ahead, and stopping control. Log attempted/valid starts and specific faults; no extra trials. A three-point start has its own record and is not numerically comparable to standing or half-kneeling starts.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task. Complete the exercises in order, with quick, clean intent and modest supporting doses. Rapid force expression does not require every exercise to include flight.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Three-Point Start Acceleration — first two steps** | **2 × 2 starts**, one with each front leg per pair. Front toes at **1 m**; fit the entire starting body inside **0–2 m**. At most **2 driving strikes or 3 m of drive**, ending by **4 m**; progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between starts; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main launch task:** coordinate release of hand support with the legs' forward push. Use staggered feet and the hand opposite the front leg on the floor; choose comfortable spacing that allows both feet to push without a cramped crouch. Hold still briefly, then release and drive without twisting. First pair uses controlled fast intent to establish stopping; second pair uses the fastest clean intent that preserves it. No blocks or maximal-load test. |
| 2 | **Rapid Step-Up to Knee Drive — no flight** | **1 × 3 per working leg**, bodyweight; rapid rise, **1 s tall finish, 2 s controlled lower**. **10 s reset between reps; 45 s between legs.** | Make the elevated leg supply most of the upward push while the other thigh recovers. Whole working foot on a stable low box; front knee below hip level at the start. Rise quickly without bouncing off the floor leg, hopping off the box or lifting the working heel. The free knee rises naturally as the body comes up; return that foot gently to the floor, keeping the working foot on the box. Lower the box height for pelvic hitching or loss of forceful ascent. |
| 3 | **Forward Bound to Opposite-Leg Stick** | **1 × 2 per takeoff leg**; modest distance, natural arms. **20 s between same-takeoff reps; 45 s between legs.** | Transfer a forward push from one leg into controlled support on the other, rather than receiving every projection on two feet. From one-leg support, use a small dip, bound forward and hold the opposite-leg landing **2 s** before setting the free foot down. Keep the pelvis facing forward; shorten distance for reaching, trunk rotation or an unstable receiver. No linked bounds. |
| 4 | **Broad Jump to Stick — countermovement and arm swing** | **2 × 2**; natural arms, one smooth dip and immediate forward jump. **20 s between reps; 90 s between sets.** | Integrate arm action and a dynamic leg drive into horizontal projection. This differs from Class 1's paused, hands-on-hips jump: there is no held squat or deliberate interruption of the dip. Land on both feet and hold **2 s**; select a distance that permits control rather than chasing a record. |
| 5 | **Standing Single-Leg Cycle and Strike** | **1 × 3 cycles per moving leg**. One compact recovery cycle followed by a quick target-foot strike; **10 s reset between reps, 45 s between legs**. | Rehearse returning the free foot beneath the pelvis while the other leg provides stable support. Keep the support foot planted. Recover the moving heel beneath the hip, bring the thigh forward through a comfortable range, let the lower leg unfold without kicking, then place the foot quickly beside the support foot. No running travel or hopping on the support leg. Reduce cycle height/range for leaning, a long backswing or an ahead-of-body strike. A rep is one complete recovery-and-strike cycle. |
| 6 | **Supine Banded Knee Drive — opposite heel grounded** | **1 × 3 per moving leg**, very light band. **Quick knee drive, 1 s finish, 2 s controlled lower**; **10 s reset between reps, 45 s between legs**. | Express rapid thigh recovery against light elastic resistance while the pelvis is supported, complementing E5's placement task. Lie with both legs comfortably long and heels on the floor, a suitable closed band around both shoe midfeet. Keep the nonmoving leg long, heel grounded and foot secure; quickly bend/lift the moving knee toward the torso as the band stretches, then lower under control. Stop the lift before pelvic movement or the band's stretch limit. The grounded heel must not slide or lift. The band must oppose the knee drive, not assist it; do not start with the opposite thigh raised. Use lighter resistance/shorter range for a slowed drive, rolling band or loss of pelvic control. |

**Recovery between exercises:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Resets are recovery, not extra isometric holds. Instruction/setup may occupy these intervals; count actual elapsed time once. Extend recovery if force, focus or movement quality has not returned.

**Space and equipment:** Use one protected **10 m lane** for starts: front toes at 1 m, whole starting body inside 0–2 m, driving ends by 4 m, progressively stop by 9 m, leave the final metre clear. One athlete moves at a time; close the lane during the walking return and verify separate coach/waiting positions. The footprint does not prove an individual can stop within it. E3/E4 use a modest selected landing distance within a clear 3 m area with further empty space beyond; no approaching run. E2/E5/E6 are stationary. Boxes, benches and bands are listed equipment, but low-box height, closed-loop band type/length, surface, quantities and clearances are not confirmed. No wall, band anchor, sled, rack, throw or long running lane is required by the defaults.

**Replacements and regressions — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** If hand support is uncomfortable or the stance cannot be controlled, use **Two-Point Standing Start instead of Three-Point Start**, same four starts and boundaries. This deliberately reuses an earlier family for readiness. If stopping space is insufficient, use one driving step at lower intent for remaining attempts; stop if that still fails. Do not add attempts.
- **E2:** If no suitably low stable box is available, use **Split Squat Jump to Stick, same lead, instead of Rapid Step-Up**, **1 × 2 per lead leg**, bodyweight, modest height, two-second finish, 20 s between same-lead reps and 45 s between leads. This repeats Class 2's task for equipment feasibility and adds four split-stance jump landings in place of six nonflight rises. No unstable stacked plates or improvised platform.
- **E3:** If opposite-leg reception is not qualified, use **Single-Leg Broad Jump to Two-foot Landing instead of the opposite-leg bound**, same 1 × 2/takeoff leg and rests, shorter controlled distance. This repeats Class 1's reception option; record the changed landing type. Do not demand a one-leg finish to preserve novelty.
- **E6:** Use only an intact closed band suitable for the foot-loop setup and within its stated stretch limit; do not tie an improvised loop or use an unverified external anchor. If no suitable band is available, use **Unresisted Supine Knee Drive instead of the banded version**, same count, controlled lowering, rapid drive and rests. Its resistance is different and must be logged; no claim of equal force demand.

**Quality rules:** Attempted work counts even when faulty. Cue or reduce demand before the next scheduled rep; repeated loss of the same criterion despite adjustment ends that exercise. Pain, a slip, changed gait, an insecure band or loss of control ends the affected task immediately. Log omitted work and actual substitutions; do not add make-up reps or other exercises. If an athlete cannot perform a default or its stated replacement productively, actual delivery is incomplete rather than falsely recorded as fourteen finished entries.

**Final sequence correction:** All E1 defaults and standing/one-step replacements use the 1 m front-toe origin and entire starting footprint inside 0–2 m. Drive at most two strikes/3 m to line 4, progressively stop by line 9 and keep the final metre clear. Maximum outward travel is 8 m/start; this is a boundary from the toe line, not an exact body trajectory. The revised layout requires fresh stopping qualification; prior 0 m-layout scores are not matched comparisons. Counts/rests hold; additional setup or recovery extends the allowance.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. These retained bodyweight strengthening doses support the foot base and lateral hip without tiring the athlete before the primary lifts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, supporting the foot used to push and the opposite foot receiving the bound. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing or rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for keeping the pelvis level/directed forward during step-up, bound reception and the planted-leg cycle. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range if the pelvis rolls or the thigh moves forward. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; do not increase resistance to create a burn.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Select a challenging load or leverage leaving approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve (RIR)**. A prior prescribed weight is not known: qualify today's load rather than assuming any earlier set was performed. No forced repetitions, maximal tests or automatic load increase. P2 drops from two working sets to one and P4 from four cycles to three to limit overlap with new leg-specific work; the other prescriptions stay consistent.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 5 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. **60 s between legs; 120 s between rounds.** | **Main strength anchor:** load lead-leg knee/hip extension in a split base while preserving pelvis direction. Both feet remain on the floor; modest whole-trunk inclination without rounding. Reduce depth/load for knee collapse, rotation or rear-leg hopping. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, controlled strong ascent. | Retain challenging hip-extension strength from flexed hips, with one working set because forward bounds and rapid step-ups already add new posterior-chain work. Soft knees, bells close; stop depth before pelvis/spine compensation. Reduce range/load if needed. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Build force for lifting the thigh through a stable seated range, complementary to E6's light rapid drive from a longer-leg starting position. Secure a DB across the distal thigh above the knee with both hands; lift the knee a few centimetres without hand assistance or leaning, then return the foot gently. Use a stable fitted seat with foot supported and knee initially near a right angle. Reduce load/range for pinching, tipping or shifting load. |
| 4 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 complete cycles**, approximately **2–3 clean cycles in reserve**. One cycle = **4 small alternating heel steps out + 4 back**, about **8–10 s**. | Retain knee-flexor support as the knee opens/returns while reducing the total lever-loading dose. Keep pelvis raised and knees soft at the far endpoint; return to original heel positions. Shorten steps for sag/cramp; do not lengthen them just to compensate for the lower rep count. Stop if control cannot be restored. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, floor range** | **1 × 10**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. | Strengthen straight-knee plantarflexion for the pushing/receiving foot without adding rebound contacts. DBs at sides, both forefeet loaded, heels return to floor; reduce load for lost height, outward roll or bouncing. Knees stay nearly straight, not forcibly locked. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **2 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. **90 s between sets.** | Retain plantarflexion force with the knees bent for initial support and the low launch, complementing P5's knee position. Secure weights on thighs above the knees, forefeet down, heels moving through repeatable floor range. Reduce load for knee straightening, foot roll or bounce. |

**Load-preparation sets, separate from working sets:** P1 **1 × 4/leg** bodyweight/light (30 s between sides, 60 s before work); P2 **1 × 5 easy then 1 × 3 moderate** (60 s between, 90 s before work); P3 **1 × 3/leg light** (30 s between, 45 s before work); P4 **1 short-lever cycle** (45 s before work); P5 **1 × 5 easy** (45 s before work); P6 **1 × 5 easy** (60 s before work). Reduced RDL working volume does not remove its load-preparation requirement. Add small ramps only in the listed movement when needed and record them; this is not a new preparation sequence.

**Between primary exercises:** **120 s after P1 and P2; 90 s after P3, P4 and P5**, before the next preparation set. Setup/instruction can occupy recovery; no extra required rest after final P6. If P5 balance prevents productive loading, use **supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between, 45 s before work. Verify a stable hand support and log laterality. A suitably fitted stable box may replace the bench for P3/P6; seat/load dimensions remain unconfirmed.

**Timing and recovery:** Plan **74–84 minutes after Access & Prepare 1** for prompt individual equipment access: about 32 min explosive work, 5–6 min light strengthening and 37 min primary work, including instruction, ramps, rests and transitions. Group queues, extra teaching/ramps or longer recovery can extend it. Preparation duration and the actual booking/group/staff are unknown, so full-session duration is unresolved. A 60-minute complete booking cannot fit this version. Treat it as a demanding lower-body exposure; review actual sport, sprinting, gym, other classes and separate tumbling. Nonconsecutive demanding exposures, often around 48–72 hours apart, are only a planning starting point; actual recovery and the wider schedule govern.
