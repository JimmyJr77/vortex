# Sprinting — Class 7 of 12

**Primary effort:** Initiate a strong forward push from a settled position, expressing hip/leg force without a preceding approach or repeated rebound. Support the push with a stable body line and rapid free-thigh recovery.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique, under qualified coaching. Reviewed Classes 1–6, start/strength anchors, workload and all default/contingency drill history, audits, equipment assumptions and feedback. **Actual attendance, execution, loads and recovery remain unknown.** Five default tasks introduce distinct support or initiation demands; E6 deliberately retains the dead-stop swing because its hip-drive job fits today. Strength doses hold. No progression is earned merely because six classes are saved.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Today's application emphasizes straight-line starting force; hip, knee-flexor and ankle strength also support upright running capacity. No actual maximum-velocity running occurs.

**Quality marker within E1:** Record seat/foot setup, first stepping foot, controlled versus attempted starts, backward rocking/hand assistance, direct rise-and-forward push and stopping control. Use only the four scheduled attempts. Seated starts have their own record, not a score comparable to the earlier start origins.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main performance task. E2/E3 supply modest forward projections; E4/E5/E6 express rapid force without jump landings. The wall pulse is a force-onset task, not external movement power or an endurance hold.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Sprint Start — first two steps** | **2 × 2 starts**, one with each first stepping foot per pair. Start with feet at the **1 m line**; at most **2 driving strikes or 3 m of drive**, ending by the **4 m line**. Progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between starts; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main task:** transfer support from the seat to the feet and initiate forward travel without backward rocking. Sit at the front of a stable fitted bench/box, both feet flat near hip width, knees comfortably bent and trunk slightly inclined forward. Settle for **2 s**, then push up/forward through the legs and take the selected first step; arms assist naturally but hands do not push the seat. First pair uses controlled fast intent to establish stopping; second uses the fastest clean intent that preserves it. No jump off the seat. |
| 2 | **Seated Broad Jump to Two-Foot Stick** | **2 × 2**, bodyweight; **2 s still seated start**, natural arm drive. **20 s between reps; 90 s between sets.** | Express bilateral forward projection after seated support, separating the force task from E1's sequential running steps. Use a fitted stable seat, feet flat and trunk slightly forward before the pause. Push off both feet and jump a modest distance forward; land on both feet and hold **2 s**. No backward rock, preliminary rise/re-dip, hand push or jump onto a box. Reduce distance for twisting or poor reception. |
| 3 | **Paused Single-Leg Broad Jump to Two-Foot Landing** | **1 × 2 per takeoff leg**; hold a comfortable shallow single-leg dip **2 s**, then jump without a re-dip. Natural arm drive from a settled position. **20 s between same-leg reps; 60 s between legs.** | Initiate forward impulse through one leg from a controlled loaded position; the two-foot receiver shares landing demand. Keep the pelvis facing forward during the pause, push forward and hold the bilateral landing **2 s**. The pause changes Class 1's dynamic takeoff task; it does not make the tissues purely concentric. Shorten dip/distance for instability, re-dipping or a reaching receiver. |
| 4 | **Split-Stance Wall Drive — rapid force pulses** | **1 × 3 pulses per front leg**. From quiet pressure, increase leg-driven force quickly for **1 s**, then relax. Firm submaximal effort, about **RPE 6–7/10**; **20 s between pulses, 60 s between sides**. | Rehearse rapid force onset through the legs while the body line remains fixed. With both palms on a verified immovable wall near shoulder height, take a comfortable split stance and shallow forward lean. Front foot stays fully planted, rear forefoot supports; quickly push the floor back through the front leg while the hands transmit pressure to the wall. No arm pressing motion, heel pop, rocking, breath-holding or maximal strain. Step out of the lean during recovery. Reduce pressure/lean for lost position; no measured rate-of-force-development claim is made. |
| 5 | **Wall Knee Drive — repeated same moving leg** | **1 × 3 per moving leg**, bodyweight; **quick knee lift / 1 s finish / 2 s controlled foot return**. **10 s reset between reps; 45 s between legs.** | Keep the stance leg transmitting pressure while the other thigh recovers rapidly. Start with both feet on the floor in a comfortable two-hand wall lean. Keep the support foot planted as the target knee lifts forward, then return that foot gently beside its starting position. Repeat the same moving leg; do not swap supports explosively or hop. Reduce range/speed for sag, leaning from the waist or a lifted/sliding support heel. |
| 6 | **Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing — two hands, one float per reset** | **2 × 3 singles**; **20 s after each parked rep within a set, 90 s between sets**. Light-to-moderate technically qualified bell; crisp drive and controlled park. | **Intentional retained drill:** independently initiate hip drive on each rep, directly matching today's settled-force theme. With the bell parked just ahead within comfortable reach, hinge with a stable spine, hike back once, drive to a tall finish and let the bell float **no higher than chest level**. Guide it back through the hinge and park under control. Feet stay planted; no arm lift, backward lean or release. Reduce load for a slow/unstable rep. Each single still contains a backswing; this is not a claim of zero eccentric loading. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Instruction/setup can occupy rest; count actual elapsed time once. Release held positions between efforts and extend recovery if force, attention or control has not returned.

**Room and setup:** The seat and rearward foot extent must fit inside the first metre of one protected **10 m lane**, with feet at 1 m and the entire starting body inside 0–2 m. The seated start therefore has **8 m outward travel** to the 9 m stopping line, including at most 3 m of drive; the final metre stays clear. Verify actual seat footprint, traction and stability before using this layout. As with the corrected earlier starts, use the first pair's controlled intent and the stated regression. One athlete moves at a time; close the lane during return and verify coach/waiting positions. E2/E3 use modest distances with clear landing and overshoot space; the seat remains behind the takeoff feet. Wall tasks require a verified immovable non-slip wall; no movable box/rack is assumed suitable. Keep the full bell path separate. Seat/DB/KB sizes, wall suitability, floor, staff and quantities are unknown. The older 12 m facility note remains unresolved; use the tighter 10 m footprint.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** If the seat cannot fit securely or the rise is unproductive, use **Two-Point Standing Start instead of seated start**, four starts, front toes at **1 m**, entire starting body inside **0–2 m**, drive at most two steps/3 m to line 4 m, stop by 9 m. This repeats an earlier family and retains the 8 m/start outward bound. If stopping is insufficient in either layout, use one driving step at lower intent for remaining attempts; stop if still uncontrolled. No extra trials.
- **E2:** Without a suitable seat or controlled seated takeoff, use **Paused Broad Jump to Stick instead**, **2 × 2**, comfortable dip held 2 s, hands on hips, no re-dip, modest distance and 2 s landing hold; same rests. This repeats Class 1's task.
- **E3:** If the single-leg pause is unstable, use **Paused Staggered-Stance Broad Jump to Two-Foot Stick instead**, two attempts per front-leg configuration, small offset, both feet pushing, 2 s pause/landing hold and the same rests. Related broad-jump family; do not label it a completed unilateral takeoff.
- **E4:** If the wall or posture cannot be qualified, use **Paused Squat Jump to Stick instead of wall pulses**, **1 × 4**, hands on hips, comfortable dip held 2 s, no re-dip, modest upward jump, 2 s bilateral landing hold and 20 s between reps. This repeats an earlier fallback family and adds four landings instead of six force pulses.
- **E5:** If no suitable wall is available, use **Supine Unresisted Knee Drive, opposite heel grounded, instead**: **1 × 3/leg**, quick lift, 1 s finish, 2 s lower, 10 s resets and 45 s between legs. Keep the other leg comfortably long with heel grounded and pelvis quiet. This repeats Class 3's unresisted fallback family with different body support.
- **E6:** If swing competence, bell or clearance is unsuitable, use **Bilateral Glute Bridge with rapid rise instead of dead-stop swing**, **1 × 6**, feet planted, quick hip rise, 1 s top, 2 s lower, 10 s between reps. Stop at a level pelvis without lumbar arch. This repeats Class 4's rapid bilateral bridge fallback; no added jump landings and no claim of equivalent resistance.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts count as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; persistent faults despite adjustment end the affected task. Pain, slipping, changed gait or lost body/implement control ends it immediately. Record actual substitutions, extra contacts, slowing steps, omitted work and reasons. No make-up attempts or extra exercise slots; incomplete delivery remains incomplete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Retain these small strengthening doses without pre-fatiguing the loaded work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases as the working arch gently rises, supporting the foot repeatedly used for E1/E3. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for holding pelvic position over one support leg as the other moves. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range if the pelvis rolls or the thigh drifts forward. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; do not add resistance to create a burn.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging loads/leverage leaving approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve (RIR)**. Keep Class 6's working doses and preparation rules. Actual prior loads are unknown: qualify today's load rather than assuming an increase or a previously completed set. No forced reps, maximal tests or automatic restoration of reduced volume.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **1 × 5 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. **60 s between legs.** | Retain loaded lead-leg extension from a stable base for the seat-to-stance push and unilateral projection. Both feet stay on the floor; modest whole-trunk inclination without rounding. Reduce load/depth for knee collapse, pelvic rotation or rear-leg hopping. The reduced single round limits overlap with starts and horizontal jumps. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. | Retain hip-extension strength through a flexed-hip range, complementing E6’s brief rapid hinge with a controlled loaded range. Soft knees, bells close; stop depth before spine/pelvic compensation. Reduce range/load as needed. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Strengthen the thigh lift used for free-leg recovery in E1/E5. Secure a DB on the distal thigh above the knee with both hands; lift the knee a few centimetres without hand assistance or backward lean. Start on a fitted stable seat with foot supported and knee near a right angle. Reduce load/range for pinching, tipping or a shifting implement. |
| 4 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 complete cycles**, approximately 2–3 clean cycles in reserve. One cycle = **4 small alternating heel steps out + 4 back**, about **8–10 s**. | Strengthen knee-flexor support as the knee opens/returns, supporting the takeoff/receiving legs alongside the hip hinge. Keep the pelvis raised, knees soft at the far endpoint and return to the original heel positions. Shorten steps for sag/cramp; stop if control cannot be restored. This is capacity work, not high-speed swing simulation. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, floor range** | **1 × 10**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. | Retain near-straight-knee plantarflexion force as the driving leg lengthens during E1's push and forward takeoff. DBs at sides, both forefeet loaded, heels return to floor. Reduce load for lost heel height, outward roll or bounce; do not lock the knees forcibly. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **2 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. **90 s between sets.** | Maintain ankle-force capacity with the knee bent for the seated/paused push before leg extension, complementing P5. Secure weights on thighs above the knees; forefeet stay down and heel range stays repeatable. Reduce load for knee straightening, rolling or bounce. |

**Load-preparation sets, separate from working sets:** P1 **1 × 4/leg** bodyweight/light (30 s between sides, 60 s before work); P2 **1 × 5 easy then 1 × 3 moderate** (60 s between, 90 s before work); P3 **1 × 3/leg light** (30 s between, 45 s before work); P4 **1 short-lever cycle** (45 s before work); P5 **1 × 5 easy** (45 s before work); P6 **1 × 5 easy** (60 s before work). Additional small ramps, if needed, stay within the listed movement and are logged; no additional preparation sequence is invented.

**Between primary exercises:** **120 s after P1/P2; 90 s after P3/P4/P5**, before the next preparation set. Setup may occupy recovery; no extra required rest after final P6. If P5 balance limits productive loading, use **supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between, 45 s before work. Verify stable hand support and log laterality. A fitted stable box may replace the bench for P3/P6.

**Progression note:** Seated support and a paused unilateral takeoff change how forward force is initiated. Brief wall pulses add a rapid-force-onset task without a landing; the same-leg wall drive adds free-thigh recovery. Dead-stop swings recur deliberately because their existing initiation job is useful and another loaded hinge would add complexity without a clearer benefit. All strength doses hold. No heavier load, greater distance or earned tolerance is inferred.
