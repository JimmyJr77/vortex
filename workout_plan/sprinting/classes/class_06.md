# Sprinting — Class 6 of 12

**Primary effort:** Organize the first forward push and the next support exchange while controlling the body line. Combine short start practice with gym support for upright foot return and rapid hip/ankle force.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique, under qualified coaching. Reviewed Classes 1–5, strength anchors, workload/drill history including replacements, audits, equipment assumptions and feedback. **Actual attendance, execution, loads and recovery remain unknown.** This class consolidates related demands through distinct task variants; no improvement or earned intensity increase is assumed. Strength doses remain at Class 5 levels.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. The start application is straight-line; upright work is supplemental gym practice. No actual maximum-velocity running is possible in this prescription, and stationary placement cues are not literal full-speed touchdown geometry.

**Quality marker within E1:** Record first stepping foot, whether the lean stays organized, whether the first recovery step catches/supports the moving body without a long reach or waist fold, attempted/controlled starts and stopping control. Observe only the four prescribed attempts. Falling starts are a new protocol, not directly comparable to standing, half-kneeling or three-point start scores.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main performance task. Work in order; quick clean intent and full recovery take priority over distance, height or cadence. The upright drill has a smaller dose because starts return today.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Falling Start — first two steps** | **2 × 2 starts**, one with each first stepping foot per pair. Front toes at **1 m**; fit the entire starting body inside **0–2 m**. At most **2 driving strikes or 3 m of drive**, ending by **4 m**; progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between starts; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main task:** turn a controlled forward whole-body lean into the first supporting push and next step. Start with feet parallel near hip width, arms relaxed; let the body tip forward from the ankles without folding at the waist. Initiate the selected stepping foot early enough to stay in control; do not wait for a near-fall or chase a maximum lean. First pair is controlled fast to establish stopping; second pair uses the fastest clean intent that preserves it. No jump into the start. |
| 2 | **Staggered-Stance Broad Jump to Parallel Two-Foot Stick** | **1 × 2 per starting front leg**, bodyweight, natural arms. **20 s between same-front-leg reps; 45 s between sides.** | Coordinate a forward impulse from both legs in an offset base, complementing the start's first-step push. Set feet about hip width apart with a small fore-aft offset; use one controlled dip, push through both feet and jump forward a modest distance. Bring feet approximately parallel for a **2 s held landing**. Do not turn it into a one-leg bound or a split-stance receiver. Reduce offset/distance for twisting or a visibly one-sided push. |
| 3 | **Wall-Lean Single-Leg Pogo** | **1 × 2 low hops per support leg**, bodyweight; **60 s between legs**. Keep the free thigh comfortably raised and still; controlled two-foot reset after the final same-leg landing. | Express brief ankle recoil while preserving a forward body line against firm hand support. Use both palms on an immovable non-slip wall near shoulder height and a comfortable shallow whole-body lean. Make very small same-leg hops in place; support knee stays soft, hands remain fixed and free leg does not pulse or exchange. Reduce lean/amplitude for hand/foot slip, pelvic sag or drifting contacts. This requires a verified wall; use the stated replacement if unavailable. |
| 4 | **Split Jump — single scissor exchange to stick** | **1 × 2 per starting front leg**, bodyweight, modest height. **20 s between same-start reps; 45 s between starting sides.** | Coordinate rapid opposing thigh movement with a new support configuration. From a comfortable split stance, dip slightly, jump upward and exchange the feet once in the air; land with the opposite foot forward and hold **2 s**. Reset to the named starting stance before the next rep. Feet stay on separate hip-width tracks, not a tightrope; no repeated bouncing or second exchange. Lower height/range for rotation, crossed feet or an unstable receiver. |
| 5 | **Stationary Single-Leg Step-Over Run** | **1 × 2 cycles per target leg**, bodyweight; **75 s between target legs**. One target-foot contact + one low opposite-foot contact = one cycle: **8 total running contacts**, 4/foot. | Bring the controlled lower-leg release from earlier skipping into an asymmetric running exchange. Recover the target heel beneath the hip, lift that thigh comfortably, let its lower leg open modestly, then return the foot near its normal standing position as the opposite leg uses a low running step. Use small flight and natural arms; no kick, backward claw or literal obstacle to clear. Lower the release range/cadence for reaching, leaning or extra hops. Finish after the second cycle. |
| 6 | **Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing — two hands, one float per reset** | **2 × 3 singles**; **20 s after each parked rep within a set, 90 s between sets**. Choose a light-to-moderate bell that permits a crisp hip drive and controlled park; no effort to grind or reach a fixed height. | Re-establish hip loading and a rapid drive for each initiation rather than relying on a continuous swing rhythm. With the bell parked just ahead within comfortable reach, hinge with a stable spine, grip with both hands, hike it back once, drive the hips to a tall finish and let the bell float **no higher than chest level**. Guide its return through the hinge and park it under control before resetting. Feet remain planted; no arm lift, backward lean or release. Reduce bell size/load for a slow drive or unstable hike/park. This is a parked-single variant of the earlier swing family, not a swing without any backswing. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Setup/instruction may occupy recovery; count actual elapsed time once. Rest outside held stances where possible and extend recovery if movement quality or attention has not returned.

**Space and equipment:** Use one protected **10 m lane** for starts: front toes at 1 m, whole starting body inside 0–2 m, driving ends by 4 m, progressively stop by 9 m, final metre clear. One athlete at a time; close the lane during walking return and verify waiting/coach positions. The lane length does not establish individual stopping ability. E2 uses a modest selected distance inside a clear 3 m area with further empty space beyond. E3–E6 need separate clear stationary footprints; protect the entire bell path, landing areas and returns from crossing traffic. A wall is not confirmed equipment: verify that it is immovable, non-slip and clear, or use E3's floor replacement. KBs/DBs/benches are listed, but loads, dimensions, surface and quantities remain unknown. No movable box substitutes for a wall and no stacked plates are needed. The separate 12 m facility note remains unresolved; do not combine three lanes into a runway.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** Use **Two-Point Standing Start instead of Falling Start** if the lean/recovery step is not controlled, keeping four starts and the same boundaries. This repeats an earlier family. If stopping space is insufficient, use one driving step at lower intent for the remaining attempts; stop the task if that still cannot be controlled. No extra trials.
- **E2:** Use **Parallel-Stance Broad Jump to Stick instead of staggered takeoff** if both legs cannot contribute without twisting: **1 × 4**, natural dip/arms, modest distance, 2 s hold and 20 s between reps. This repeats Class 3's family.
- **E3:** Use **Upright Single-Leg Pogo instead of Wall-Lean Pogo** if the wall or wall-pressure posture is unsuitable: same two hops/leg, 60 s between legs and a controlled two-foot reset. This repeats an earlier family. If same-leg reception is unsuitable, use **Bilateral Ankle Pogo instead**, **1 × 4** low hops; choose one replacement, not both.
- **E4:** Use **Split Squat Jump to Stick, same lead, instead of the scissor exchange** if switching disrupts reception: same two/starting lead and rests, no airborne leg swap. This explicitly repeats Class 2's task.
- **E5:** Use **Stationary Single-Leg High-Knee Run instead of Step-Over Run** if release causes reaching: same two cycles/target and rest, compact bent-knee recovery. This repeats Class 5's family at a smaller dose.
- **E6:** If swing competence, a suitable bell or clear bell path is unavailable, use **Paused Squat Jump to Stick instead of the dead-stop swing**, **2 × 2**, hands on hips, comfortable squat held 2 s without a re-dip, modest upward jump and 2 s bilateral landing hold. Rest **20 s between reps, 90 s between sets**. This repeats the earlier squat-jump fallback family and adds four bilateral landings in place of six nonflight swings.

**Quality rules:** Attempted/failed work counts as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; repeated faults despite adjustment end the exercise. Pain, slipping, changed gait or lost implement/body control ends the affected task immediately. Record actual replacements, extra contacts, stopping steps, omitted work and reasons. No make-up reps or extra exercise slots. Incomplete delivery stays incomplete in the record.

**Final sequence correction:** All E1 defaults and standing/one-step replacements use the 1 m front-toe origin and entire starting footprint inside 0–2 m. Drive at most two strikes/3 m to line 4, progressively stop by line 9 and keep the final metre clear. Maximum outward travel is 8 m/start; this is a boundary from the toe line, not an exact body trajectory. The revised layout requires fresh stopping qualification; prior 0 m-layout scores are not matched comparisons. Counts/rests hold; additional setup or recovery extends the allowance.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Retain these small strengthening doses without pre-fatiguing the loaded work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases as the working arch gently rises, supporting the foot repeatedly used for E1/E3. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for holding pelvic position over one support leg as the other moves. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range if the pelvis rolls or the thigh drifts forward. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; do not add resistance to create a burn.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging loads/leverage leaving approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve (RIR)**. Keep Class 5's working doses and preparation rules. Actual prior loads are unknown: qualify today's load rather than assuming an increase or a previously completed set. No forced reps, maximal tests or automatic restoration of reduced volume.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **1 × 5 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. **60 s between legs.** | Retain loaded lead-leg extension from a stable base for the starting push and staggered projection. Both feet stay on the floor; modest whole-trunk inclination without rounding. Reduce load/depth for knee collapse, pelvic rotation or rear-leg hopping. The reduced single round limits overlap with starts and split-stance jumps. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. | Retain hip-extension strength through a flexed-hip range, complementing E6’s brief rapid hinge with a controlled loaded range. Soft knees, bells close; stop depth before spine/pelvic compensation. Reduce range/load as needed. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Load thigh lifting separately from the light rapid exchanges. Secure a DB on the distal thigh above the knee with both hands; lift the knee a few centimetres without hand assistance or backward lean. Start on a fitted stable seat with foot supported and knee near a right angle. Reduce load/range for pinching, tipping or a shifting implement. |
| 4 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 complete cycles**, approximately 2–3 clean cycles in reserve. One cycle = **4 small alternating heel steps out + 4 back**, about **8–10 s**. | Strengthen knee-flexor support as the knee opens/returns, complementing E5's controlled foot return and the hip hinge. Keep the pelvis raised, knees soft at the far endpoint and return to the original heel positions. Shorten steps for sag/cramp; stop if control cannot be restored. This is capacity work, not high-speed swing simulation. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, floor range** | **1 × 10**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. | Retain near-straight-knee plantarflexion force for repeated upright support. DBs at sides, both forefeet loaded, heels return to floor. Reduce load for lost heel height, outward roll or bounce; do not lock the knees forcibly. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **2 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. **90 s between sets.** | Maintain ankle-force work with the knee bent, complementing P5 and soft-knee support during the drills. Secure weights on thighs above the knees; forefeet stay down and heel range stays repeatable. Reduce load for knee straightening, rolling or bounce. |
