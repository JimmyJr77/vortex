# Sprinting — Class 8 of 12

**Primary effort:** Leave a stable crouched start with consecutive forward pushes, recovering the free leg promptly while maintaining a useful support line.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified coaching. Reviewed Classes 1–7, strength/start anchors, workload, default/contingency drill history, equipment and feedback. **Actual attendance, execution, loads and recovery remain unknown.** This class changes support, reception and exchange tasks; it does not award greater intensity or volume from the class number. No directional-start need is established, so the application stays straight ahead.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Starts are the main task; stationary recovery and retained hip/knee-flexor/ankle strength also support upright running mechanics. No actual maximum-velocity running occurs.

**Quality marker within E1:** Record front-leg choice, setup, coordinated hand release, whether the first two steps continue forward without a stumble or abrupt upward pop, and stopping control. Use only the four scheduled attempts. Keep this four-point record separate from earlier start types.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 receives the main performance practice. The other entries isolate forward re-push, split reception, linked leg exchange, knee recovery and hip-led release. Use modest jump distance and brief clean bouts; speed never overrides control.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Four-Point Crouch Start — first two steps, no blocks** | **2 × 2 starts**, one with each leg forward per pair. Front toes at **1 m**; at most **2 driving strikes or 3 m of drive**, ending by **4 m**. Progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between starts; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main task:** coordinate release from two-hand support into successive leg pushes. Use a comfortable staggered crouch, both hands shoulder-width on the floor just ahead of the front foot, shoulders near the hands, knees clear and spine/head comfortably aligned. Fit the entire starting stance inside **0–2 m**. Settle **2 s**, push through both feet and release both hands into natural opposing arm action; no preliminary hop or forced head-down posture. First pair is controlled-fast to establish stopping; use faster intent in the second only while control remains. |
| 2 | **Repeated Broad Jump — two consecutive jumps, final stick** | **2 × 2 consecutive jumps**, bodyweight, natural arms; **120 s between two-jump bouts**. Modest distances, no approach. | Accept the first forward landing and use it for another forward push without a deliberate pause. Start with parallel feet and a comfortable dip; make two small broad jumps, then hold the second landing **2 s**. Keep both landings aligned and the first receiver responsive without rushing a collapsed position. If the first landing is unstable or needs a deep recovery squat, do not take the second jump. |
| 3 | **Broad Jump to Split-Stance Stick — parallel takeoff** | **1 × 2 per receiving lead leg** = four jumps. Natural dip/arms, modest distance; hold **2 s**. **20 s between same-lead reps; 60 s between leads.** | Project from a parallel two-foot push and accept force in a small front–back split with both feet sharing support. This changes the receiving demand after forward force, not the running stride itself. Land softly with hips facing forward, front foot flat and rear forefoot supported; avoid a long lunge, narrow tightrope stance or crossed feet. Reduce distance/split length for twisting or uneven reception. |
| 4 | **Wall Drill Double Switch — paired exchanges** | **1 × 2 pairs per initially raised knee**. Each pair = **2 quick switches without an intermediate hold**, then **2 s hold** and a controlled free-foot set-down. **20 s between pairs; 45 s between starting sides.** | Keep an inclined support line through two successive leg exchanges, rather than stopping after each strike. Both hands press a verified immovable wall; begin with one knee comfortably lifted. Switch supports twice, finishing with the original knee raised; keep the hips level, steps near their starting positions and elbows steady. Eight fast foot contacts plus four controlled set-downs in total. Step out of the lean for recovery. Shorten the range/lean if feet slide or the torso sags. |
| 5 | **Stationary Butt-Kick Run — heel recovery with slight thigh lift** | **1 × 8 alternating running contacts**, four/foot, bodyweight and low flight; finish with **one gentle free-foot set-down**. About 5 s including the finish; contacts govern. | Fold the recovering knee promptly while the thigh comes slightly forward, complementing the forward thigh exchange in E1/E4. Stay tall and in place; recover the heel underneath the hip without forcing it to touch the buttock or leaving the thigh trailing behind. Use natural opposing arms and nearby foot placement. Reduce heel height/speed for back arch, reaching, drift or an exaggerated backward kick. |
| 6 | **Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — standing, two hands** | **2 × 2 throws**, light enough for a crisp hip-led release and secure grip; **30 s after retrieval between throws, 90 s between sets after retrieval**. No catch, step or intentional jump. | Transfer a leg/hip push through the trunk into a forward external release, retaining hip-drive intent without another jump receiver. Face forward with parallel feet behind the **1 m release line**, ball in both hands below the hips. Use a controlled hinge/knee bend, then extend and scoop forward underhand; arms transmit the drive without a separate overhead heave, twist or backward lean. Finish balanced behind the line. Aim for a modest floor landing around **line 3–4 m**, within the qualified containment layout below. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. E6's final retrieval comes before its transition. Instruction/setup may use recovery time, counted once. Extend recovery if speed, attention or control has not returned. Do not fill waits with extra contacts.

**Room and equipment:** Use one protected **10 m lane**, one athlete moving at a time, closed during return. E1's rear foot and hands must fit within 0–2 m with the front toe at 1 m; this leaves an **8 m outward bound** to the 9 m stop line, including the driving portion. Verify floor traction, hand comfort, stance fit and stopping. Use modest E2/E3 distances with clear landing/overshoot space. Wall suitability, lane width, staff and group positions remain unconfirmed; a movable rack or box is not a wall substitute.

**Toss containment:** Use an intact, appropriately sized, light, floor-rated **low-rebound ball** only when the actual layout can contain its entire flight, bounce and roll **before line 9 and inside lane width**, leaving the final metre clear. Confirm overhead clearance and floor suitability. The 3–4 m landing target is not proof of containment. If ball behavior or available space cannot be qualified, use E6's non-throwing replacement. When qualified, the first pair uses controlled-fast intent; the second may be faster only while contained. Close the lane, wait until the ball stops, then retrieve on the coach's signal; no person catches or blocks it. Do not use an excessively heavy ball just to shorten travel. The separate 12 m facility note remains unresolved; no extra run-out is assumed.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** For unsuitable hand support/crouch, use **Two-Point Standing Start instead**, four starts with the front toe still at 1 m, same drive/stop limits and rests. This repeats an earlier family. If stopping is insufficient, use one driving step at lower intent for remaining attempts; stop if still uncontrolled. No extra trials.
- **E2:** If a linked rebound cannot stay controlled, use **Isolated Broad Jumps to Two-Foot Stick instead**, **2 × 2**, natural dip/arms, modest distance and 2 s landing holds; **20 s between jumps, 120 s between sets**. This repeats Class 3's family. A failed first receiver ends that bout; record its omitted second jump rather than making it up.
- **E3:** If a split receiver is unsuitable, use **Broad Jump to Parallel Two-Foot Stick instead**, four jumps, natural dip/arms, 2 s holds, 20 s between each pair's reps and 60 s between pairs. **If E2 is also using isolated broad jumps**, use **Split Squat Jump to Stick, same lead, instead in E3**: **1 × 2/front leg**, both feet in a modest split base, small upward jump, same lead retained and 2 s held reception; 20 s between reps and 60 s between legs. This disclosed Class 2 recurrence avoids duplicate exercise slots. If neither receiver is controlled, stop E3.
- **E4:** For lost linked rhythm, use **Isolated Wall Single Switches instead**: eight total, four/foot, 2 s hold after each, then set the free foot down and release the lean for **10 s** before resetting. Alternate the initial raised knee; eight fast contacts plus eight controlled set-downs. If no suitable wall exists, use **High-Plank Isolated Single-Switch Knee Drives instead**, eight total: hands beneath shoulders, one knee drawn forward with that foot hovering, exchange once so only the extending foot lands, hold 2 s, then lower the knees and rest 10 s before resetting. Keep the trunk quiet; alternate the initial forward knee. These repeat Class 2's default/fallback families. No double-switch pairs are added.
- **E5:** If flight disrupts control, use **Standing Alternating Heel-Recovery March instead**, **1 × 4/leg**. Lift the thigh slightly and fold the knee quickly, then lower the foot under control for **2 s** before changing legs. Keep the support foot planted; eight controlled returning-foot placements, no hops and no extra running finish.
- **E6:** Without a qualified toss layout/ball, use **Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing instead**, **2 × 2 parked singles**, 20 s between singles and 90 s between sets, technically qualified light-to-moderate bell. Hinge, hike once, drive to a tall finish with chest-height ceiling and park under control; no arm lift, backward lean or foot flight. This repeats Classes 6–7. If bell/skill/clearance is also unsuitable, use **Bilateral Glute Bridge with rapid rise instead**, **1 × 4**, quick rise / 1 s level-pelvis hold / 2 s lower, 10 s between reps; a disclosed earlier fallback. Stop for lumbar arch or cramp that persists after shortening range.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts count as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; persistent faults despite adjustment end that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait, lost body/implement control or lane intrusion ends the affected task immediately. Record substitutions, set-downs, extra contacts, omitted work and actual stopping/retrieval. Incomplete delivery stays incomplete; no make-up attempts or extra slots.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, supporting the foot used for repeated forward push and reception. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for pelvic control while support changes in starts, wall switches and split reception.
