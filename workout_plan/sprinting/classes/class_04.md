# Sprinting — Class 4 of 12

**Primary effort:** Keep the pelvis and trunk organized over the supporting leg while the other leg returns quickly to a nearby foot placement. Train upright support and rapid limb action within a stationary gym footprint.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified coaching. Classes 1–3, their workload and drill history, equipment assumptions and available feedback were reviewed. **Actual attendance, execution and recovery remain unknown.** Today's new drills are conditional entry prescriptions, not earned increases in intensity. The six defaults have distinct tasks; familiar strength lifts recur with one fewer split-squat working round.

**Framework:** This category-wide curriculum retains `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Today's emphasis is gym support for the latter. There are **no starts or actual maximum-velocity runs in this class**. Stationary placement cues do not describe the exact touchdown geometry of full-speed running, and drill scores do not establish sprint speed.

**Quality marker within E1:** Record attempted and controlled contacts by foot, visible forward/backward drift, pelvic drop, backward trunk lean and conspicuous foot reaching. Observe only the prescribed contacts; no extra trials. A controlled contact preserves the upright position and compact exchange without an extra settling hop. Compare only repetitions of this same A-run protocol, not earlier skips or low dribbles.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main coordination/rapid-force task. Work in order, with quick clean intent and small supporting doses. E4–E6 intentionally express speed without jump landings; they are not maximal-load efforts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stationary A-Run — alternating high-knee exchanges** | **2 × 6 alternating foot contacts**, 3/foot/set; **90 s between sets**. Bodyweight; quick rhythm without chasing cadence. Start the sets on opposite support legs. | **Main task:** alternate a bent-knee thigh recovery and rapid support exchange while staying tall. Begin on one foot with the other knee comfortably raised; make small airborne exchanges, landing the incoming foot close to its normal standing position as the opposite thigh lifts. No extra bounce between exchanges, forced hip-height target, backward lean or deliberate clawing. Finish on contact six, stabilize, then set the other foot down. Reduce knee height/cadence for drift, reaching or loss of posture. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Vertical Jump to Same-Leg Stick** | **1 × 2 per leg**, bodyweight, natural arms; modest height with forceful takeoff. **20 s between same-leg reps; 45 s between legs.** | Produce an upward push and receive it over the same foot, separating vertical support from the earlier forward bounds. Use one small countermovement, jump upward and hold the same-leg landing **2 s** before the free foot touches down. Keep the pelvis facing forward and knee tracking with the foot. Lower height for reaching, rotation or a second hop; do not add a rebound. |
| 3 | **Straight-Leg Scissor Bounce in Place** | **1 × 6 alternating landing contacts**, 3/foot; bodyweight, very low flight. | Repeatedly exchange support through the ankles with the knees nearly straight, unlike E1's bent-knee recovery and E2's larger leg drive. Start on one foot; use a tiny forward/backward leg exchange in flight and receive the next foot near the standing position. Knees stay soft, not locked; no high kicks, long reach, double bounce or forward travel. Finish contact six under control, then set the other foot down. Reduce amplitude/speed for hard contacts or lost rhythm. |
| 4 | **Low Box Hip Switch — same free-foot tap and return** | **1 × 3 per moving leg**; bodyweight. Quick floor tap/return, **1 s balanced finish**; **10 s reset between reps, 45 s between legs**. | Maintain a level pelvis as the free foot moves between a raised-knee position and a lower contact surface. Keep the entire support foot on a stable platform approximately **10 cm high**; begin with the other thigh comfortably raised. Quickly lower that free foot to a light floor touch beside the platform and return the knee, allowing a small support-knee bend as needed. Switch the arms with the free leg. The support foot stays on the platform: this is not a jumping box exchange or a step-up. Reduce knee range/speed for a hip hitch, forceful floor push or trunk tilt. |
| 5 | **Arm Action Drill — seated fast exchanges** | **2 × 4 continuous arm exchanges**, bodyweight; **75 s between sets**. One rep switches both arms from their opposing positions to the other positions; 8 total exchanges. | Practice quick opposing arm action while the torso stays organized, without adding another ankle-impact task. Sit tall on a fitted stable bench, feet on the floor, no backrest assistance. Move relaxed bent arms forward/backward from the shoulders within a comfortable sprint-like range. Hands do not cross the trunk or strike the seat; avoid shrugging or exaggerated rocking. Use the fastest relaxed exchanges that preserve posture; reduce speed/range if the trunk supplies the movement. This isolates arm action, not complete arm–leg timing. |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Glute Bridge — rapid hip drive** | **1 × 3 per working leg**, bodyweight; **quick rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. **10 s reset between reps; 45 s between legs**. | Express hip-extension force rapidly as the hip approaches neutral, supporting the upright position without another landing. Lie with the working foot flat, knee bent; hold the free thigh comfortably toward the torso. Drive the pelvis up quickly until the working thigh and trunk align, then lower under control. Keep the pelvis level, heel down and ribs settled; no lumbar arch, foot launch or maximal bridge height. Shorten the rise for twisting or cramp; if a single-leg drive cannot remain crisp, use the stated replacement. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Step out of held positions during resets. Instruction/setup can use rest time; count actual elapsed time once. Extend recovery for incomplete recovery of force, attention or control.

**Space and equipment:** All defaults are stationary, with a clear flat non-slip floor area and clearance for unplanned steps; no run-up or sprint finish. Keep boxes, weights and waiting athletes outside the landing area. A lane can contain the stations sequentially; its length does not establish usable width or simultaneous group capacity. Boxes/benches and DBs are listed equipment, but a suitable approximately 10 cm platform, fitted seat, surface and quantities are unconfirmed. No stacked plates, band anchor, throwing clearance, rack or device is assumed.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** Use **Fast A-March instead of airborne A-Run** if the athlete cannot control the exchanges: same 2 × 6 alternating placements, 90 s rest, quick foot return without flight or extra bounce. This changes the loading and rhythm; log it as marching, not running.
- **E2:** Use **Bilateral Countermovement Jump to Stick instead of the single-leg jump** if single-leg reception is not qualified: **1 × 4**, modest height, 2 s hold and 20 s between reps. This deliberately reuses the Class 1 optional jump family for readiness.
- **E3:** Use **Alternating Straight-Leg Quick Steps instead of scissor bounces** if flight cannot remain small and controlled: **1 × 6 alternating placements**, knees softly extended and one foot always down. Keep quick placement intent; it is a nonflight replacement, not an equivalent elastic dose.
- **E4:** If no suitably low stable platform is available or its height cannot be managed, use **Standing Single-Leg Cycle and Strike instead of Low Box Hip Switch**, **1 × 3/moving leg**, 10 s resets and 45 s between legs. Keep the support foot planted, recover the free heel/thigh in a compact cycle, then quickly place that foot beside the support foot. This explicitly repeats Class 3 E5; no claim of unique novelty or equal pelvic demand.
- **E5:** A fitted stable box may replace the bench. If neither permits secure seating and arm clearance, use **Standing Arm Action Drill instead of the seated version**, same count/rest, parallel feet grounded and knees soft. Record the changed support condition.
- **E6:** Use **Bilateral Glute Bridge with rapid rise instead of the single-leg version** for persistent twisting or an unproductive unilateral rise: **1 × 6**, same tempo, 10 s between reps. Keep both feet planted; reduced per-leg demand is intentional.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts count as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; repeated loss of the same criterion despite adjustment ends that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait or loss of control ends the affected exercise immediately. Record omitted work, extra exit steps and actual replacements; no make-up attempts or extra exercises. If neither a default nor its replacement is productive, delivery is incomplete rather than recorded as fourteen completed entries.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. These familiar doses support the foot base and lateral hip without pre-fatiguing the primary work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**; both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, reinforcing the foot used for nearby upright contacts. Keep toes long; lower effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for controlling pelvic drop over one support leg. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso and toes forward; reduce range if the pelvis rolls or the thigh moves forward. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation. Do not add resistance to create a burn.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Select a challenging load/leverage leaving approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve (RIR)**. Qualify today's load; previous prescriptions do not establish weights used or tolerance. All six strength identities and their tempo rules remain familiar. **P1 falls from two working rounds to one; P2 and P4 retain Class 3's reductions.** No automatic increase in load, range or lever length accompanies lower volume.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **1 × 5 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. **60 s between legs.** | Retain the leg-force anchor for supporting the body on a primarily loaded leg. Both feet remain on the floor; modest whole-trunk inclination without rounding. Keep the prior implement/technique conditions; reduce depth/load for knee collapse, rotation or rear-leg hopping. One round limits overlap with today's unilateral contacts. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong controlled ascent. | Load hip extension through a flexed-hip range, complementing E6's rapid bodyweight drive near neutral. Soft knees, bells close; stop depth before pelvis/spine compensation. Reduce range/load if needed. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Strengthen the thigh lift used in E1/E4 with a stable pelvis. Secure a DB on the distal thigh above the knee with both hands; lift the knee a few centimetres without hand assistance or leaning. Use a fitted stable seat, foot supported and knee initially near a right angle. Reduce load/range for pinching, tipping or shifting weight. |
| 4 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 complete cycles**, approximately 2–3 clean cycles in reserve. One cycle = **4 small alternating heel steps out + 4 back**, about **8–10 s**. | Load knee-flexor support as the knee opens/returns while keeping the pelvis raised. This changing knee lever distinguishes it from E6's rapid hip rise. Keep knees soft at the far endpoint and return to the original heel positions. Shorten steps for sag/cramp; stop if control cannot be restored. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, floor range** | **1 × 10**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. | Strengthen plantarflexion with nearly straight knees for the upright support and long-leg bounce. DBs at sides, both forefeet loaded, heels return to floor; reduce load for lost height, outward roll or bouncing. Do not lock the knees forcibly. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **2 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. **90 s between sets.** | Retain ankle-force work with the knee bent, complementing P5 and the soft-knee support required during landings. Secure weights on thighs above the knees; forefeet stay down and heels use repeatable floor range. Reduce load for knee straightening, foot roll or bounce. Neither calf lift exactly reproduces sprint contact. |

**Load-preparation sets, separate from working work:** P1 **1 × 4/leg** bodyweight/light (30 s between sides, 60 s before work); P2 **1 × 5 easy then 1 × 3 moderate** (60 s between, 90 s before work); P3 **1 × 3/leg light** (30 s between, 45 s before work); P4 **1 short-lever cycle** (45 s before work); P5 **1 × 5 easy** (45 s before work); P6 **1 × 5 easy** (60 s before work). Keep these preparations despite reduced working volume. Additional small ramps, if needed, stay within the listed exercise and are logged.

**Between primary exercises:** **120 s after P1/P2; 90 s after P3/P4/P5**, before the next preparation set. Setup may occupy recovery; no extra required rest after final P6. If P5 balance prevents productive loading, use **supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between legs, 45 s before work. Verify stable hand support and log laterality. A fitted stable box may replace a bench for P3/P6.

**Timing and recovery:** Allow **64–74 minutes after Access & Prepare 1** with prompt individual equipment access: about 25 min explosive work, 5–6 min light work and 33 min primary work, including teaching, rests, preparation sets and transitions. Actual group queues, extra teaching/ramps or recovery can extend this. Preparation duration, booking, group size and staffing remain unknown; full-session time is unresolved. A 60-minute complete booking cannot accommodate this version. Review actual sport, sprinting, other gym/classes and separate tumbling before scheduling. Nonconsecutive demanding lower-body exposures, often roughly 48–72 hours apart, are only a planning starting point; individual response and the full schedule govern.

**Progression note:** The map now shifts from launch/forward transfer to upright support. New drill identities are not proof of improved technique or tolerance. Split-squat work drops by one round; RDL/walkout reductions and calf doses remain. Future load, contact or lever changes require actual controlled execution, target reserve and recovery.
