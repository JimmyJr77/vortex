# Sprinting — Class 11 of 12

**Primary effort:** Initiate a clean forward push on one clear signal, then organize individual support and limb recovery without accumulating long drill bouts.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified coaching. Reviewed Classes 1–10, anchors, workload, all drill contingencies, equipment and feedback. **Actual attendance, execution, loads and recovery remain unknown.** Four proposed task variants are paired with two openly retained drill families. Soleus working volume is reduced; other strengthening doses hold. This is a conditional prescription, not a claim that ten exposures earned progression.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. The signal has one known forward response; it does not create a direction-choice agility task. Upright work stays stationary and supplemental. No actual maximum-velocity running is prescribed.

**Quality marker within E1:** Record assigned front foot, cue opportunity, whether movement began before/after the cue, forward push, body control and stopping for the four scheduled opportunities. A coach observation is not a measured reaction time. Keep this record separate from earlier self-start scores.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 receives the main performance practice. Supporting work separates same-leg forward reception, externally loaded projection, a resettable upright exchange, free-leg placement and rapid opposing arms. Keep distances, heights and bout lengths modest; full resets preserve quality.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split-Stance Auditory Sprint Start — first two steps** | **2 × 2 starts**, one/front leg per pair; four cue opportunities total. Front toes **1 m**, whole starting body inside **0–2 m**. At most **2 driving strikes or 3 m drive**, ending by **4 m**; progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between opportunities; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main task:** preserve a useful staggered push while waiting for an external start signal. Use a comfortable two-point stagger and forward whole-body inclination, hands free. After the athlete settles, the coach waits an unannounced **2–4 s**, then says one clear, non-startling **“go”** from a verified position outside the lane. Push straight forward with natural opposing arms; no rocking, backward step or guessed launch. First pair is controlled-fast to establish stopping; faster intent in the second requires control. Do not force an upright transition in two steps. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Broad Jump to Same-Leg Stick** | **1 × 2 per takeoff/receiving leg**, bodyweight, modest distance, natural dip/arms; hold **2 s**, then gently set the free foot down. **20 s between same-leg reps; 60 s between legs.** | Link one leg's forward push to controlled reception on that same leg, supporting individual force acceptance after a start. Use a small hip/knee dip, project forward and bring the receiving foot beneath the body; no linked second jump or distance record. Keep the pelvis facing forward and avoid reaching or an extra hop. Shorten distance for unstable reception; use the shared receiver below if this demand cannot be qualified. |
| 3 | **Split-Stance Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — no step** | **1 × 2 per lead leg**, light suitable ball; **30 s after retrieval between throws, 90 s after retrieval between leads**. Four throws; no catch, jump or step. | Express leg/hip drive into a forward external release while retaining the asymmetric base used for E1. Both feet contribute: front foot flat, rear forefoot supported, hip-width tracks and modest stagger. Hold the ball below the hips in two hands; use a small hinge/knee bend, then extend and scoop forward underhand without twisting or an arm-only heave. Keep feet behind the **1 m line**, whole stance inside the lane, and finish balanced. Aim for a modest floor landing around **3–4 m**, only within the qualified containment layout below. Reduce ball mass/range for a slow release, rotation or a recovery step. |
| 4 | **Standing A-Switch to Stick — isolated support exchange** | **1 × 2 per initially raised knee**; one small airborne exchange per rep, **2 s receiving hold**, then gentle free-foot set-down. **20 s between reps; 60 s between starting sides.** Four exchanges total. | Quickly exchange thigh positions into upright individual support, then stabilize the new position before another attempt. Begin tall on one foot, opposite knee comfortably raised. Make only enough upward push to exchange legs once; place the previously raised foot near its standing position while the original support knee rises. Natural opposing arms, low flight and no extra bounce. Unlike a continuous A-run, every exchange finishes and resets. Reduce knee height/flight for drift, reaching or trunk motion; use the nonflight replacement if control is unsuitable. |
| 5 | **Standing Single-Leg Cycle and Strike** | **1 × 3 cycles per moving leg**. One compact recovery cycle followed by a quick target-foot strike; **10 s reset between reps, 45 s between legs**. | **Intentional Class 3 repeat:** retain a full free-leg recovery/placement task with the support foot planted, adding no flight exchanges after E4. Recover the heel beneath the hip, lift the thigh comfortably, allow modest knee opening, then return the foot quickly beside the support foot. No kick, backward claw, long backswing, hop or deliberate hard stamp. Reduce cycle range for reaching or pelvic motion. One complete cycle is one rep. |
| 6 | **Standing Arm Action Drill — fast opposing exchanges** | **2 × 4 continuous arm exchanges**, bodyweight; **75 s between sets**. One exchange reverses both arms' opposing positions; eight total. Allow about 4 s/bout including the finish. | **Intentional earlier fallback recurrence:** practice quick opposing arm motion for initiation and upright limb timing without another foot-contact task. Parallel feet stay grounded, knees soft and trunk quiet. Move relaxed bent arms from the shoulders through a comfortable sprint-like range; hands do not cross the trunk. Use fast relaxed intent, then relax both arms. Reduce speed/range for shrugging, rocking or back arch. This isolates arms; it does not replace integrated arm–leg practice in E1/E4. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Setup/teaching may occupy recovery, counted once. Relax held positions between attempts and extend recovery if force, focus or control has not returned. No work fills waiting time.

**Cue and lane:** The coach confirms the cue is accessible before delivery. Use one protected **10 m lane**, one moving athlete, final metre clear; close the lane during the return. Four starts give at most **32 m outward start/slowing travel**, including at most 12 m drive. A premature start uses one opportunity: ease down inside the same route, log it and take full recovery; do not abruptly freeze or add a penalty start. A missed cue consumes its opportunity and is recorded as no start. Repeated anticipation or unclear/unusable cues triggers the self-start replacement for remaining opportunities. No extra trial or timed-reaction block. Confirm lane width, whole starting footprint, coach/waiting positions, traction and return route. The separate 12 m facility note remains unresolved; use ten metres.

**Throw qualification:** E3 uses the same closed lane after E1 has finished. Use only an intact light ball rated for the floor and with sufficiently low rebound. Verify grip, stance, flight, bounce and roll containment for the actual ball/surface before delivery. Keep all people outside the throw corridor, including lateral rebound space; the ball must settle by **8 m**, leaving **8–10 m clear**. Retrieve only after it settles and the coach releases the lane; full prescribed rest begins after retrieval. Listed balls alone do not prove containment. If this cannot be established, use the nonthrowing replacement; no test throws are added. Other exercises wait until retrieval and lane clearance are complete.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** Use **Self-Started Two-Point Start instead** for remaining opportunities if cue access/anticipation cannot be managed; same front-toe line, limits, dose and rests. This is an earlier movement family. If stopping is insufficient, use one driving step at lower intent; stop if still uncontrolled. No additional attempts replace false/missed starts.
- **E2:** Use **Single-Leg Broad Jump to Two-Foot Landing instead**, same four jumps, rests and 2 s holds, if unilateral reception is unsuitable; Class 1 recurrence, removing the four separate free-foot set-downs. If unilateral takeoff itself cannot be qualified, use **Bilateral Broad Jump to Stick instead**, **2 × 2**, modest dip/arms, 20 s between reps and 60 s between sets. Choose one replacement, not both.
- **E3:** If only split-base control is unsuitable, use **Parallel Forward Scoop Toss instead**, **2 × 2**, same ball/containment, 30 s after retrieval between throws and 90 s between sets; Class 8 recurrence. If throwing cannot be qualified, use **Dead-Stop Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing instead**, **2 × 2 parked singles**, 20 s between reps and 90 s between sets; technically qualified light-to-moderate bell, one hike/hip-driven float no higher than chest level, controlled park, feet planted. Verify bell sweep/grip/space. If that setup is also unsuitable, use **Rapid Bilateral Glute Bridge instead**, **1 × 4**, quick hip rise / 1 s neutral finish / 2 s lower, **10 s between reps**; heels planted, no back arch. These are earlier families; choose one path only.
- **E4:** Use **Nonflight A-Step Exchange to Hold instead**, same four reps, holds and rests. From one-foot support, lower the raised foot beside its standing position before lifting the other knee; one foot always remains down. Hold 2 s, then set the free foot down. Four support-transfer placements plus four finish set-downs replace four flight receivers plus four set-downs. E5 keeps one support foot planted throughout and has a separate full-cycle job.
- **E5:** Use **Standing Straight-Leg Downstroke instead**, **1 × 3/leg**, controlled 1 s low forward lift with a soft knee, quick light return beside the support foot; 10 s resets and 45 s between legs. This repeats Class 9 and removes the full recovery cycle. Reduce range for reaching or discomfort.
- **E6:** Use **Seated Arm Action Drill instead**, same eight exchanges/rest, on a fitted stable bench/box with feet grounded and arm clearance; Class 4 recurrence. Stop if rapid relaxed action cannot be maintained after reducing range/speed.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts count as exposure. Adjust the next scheduled attempt; persistent faults despite adjustment end that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait, lost control, cue distress or lane intrusion stops the affected work. Record actual defaults/replacements, contacts, set-downs, outside work, omissions and reasons. Incomplete delivery stays incomplete; no make-up attempts or extra exercise slots.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Keep these small strengthening doses below pre-fatiguing levels.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, supporting E1’s push and E2/E4’s individual receivers. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for pelvic control during same-leg forward reception and upright exchange. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range for pelvic rolling or a forward-drifting thigh. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; keep the work easy rather than adding resistance to create a burn.

Additional small ramps, if needed, stay within the listed movement and are logged; no additional preparation sequence is invented.

**Between primary exercises:** **120 s after P1/P2; 90 s after P3/P4/P5**, before the next preparation set. Setup may occupy recovery; no extra required rest after final P6. If P5 balance limits productive loading, use **supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between, 45 s before work. Verify stable hand support and log laterality. A fitted stable box may replace the bench for P3/P6.

Extra setup, queues, ramps or recovery extend this. Preparation duration, booking, staff/group and full-session fit remain unknown; a 60-minute complete booking cannot fit. Same-leg forward reception, a split-base external release and isolated upright exchange provide three further related task variants. The full-cycle drill and standing arm-action family recur openly to retain direct recovery/timing practice without further flight bouts. P6 falls from 2 × 8 to 1 × 8; other strength doses hold. This planned consolidation does not establish reduced actual fatigue or earned readiness for same-leg broad reception.
