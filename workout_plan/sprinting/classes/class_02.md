# Sprinting — Class 2 of 12

**Primary effort:** Push out of a low start position while recovering the opposite thigh, keeping the pelvis directed into the lane. Support that push with rapid leg extension, then a small dose of upright ankle support and compact foot recovery.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique under qualified coaching. Class 1, its workload, drill history, assumptions and feedback record were reviewed. **Class 1 execution and response remain unknown.** Class 2 is a conditional new prescription, not an earned load increase. All six default explosive drills differ meaningfully from Class 1; the eight strengthening exercises retain useful roles and doses.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Today uses a straight-line start application. Upright work is supplemental gym practice; this session does **not** include actual maximum-velocity running.

**Quality marker within E1:** For each scheduled start, note whether the athlete leaves the low position without rocking backward, folding at the waist, twisting away from the lead leg or taking a separate upright step before driving forward. Record controlled stopping separately. Log lead leg, attempted/valid starts and specific faults. This task establishes its own observation record; do not compare its score or distance directly with Class 1's standing start. No extra trials.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Perform in order. E1 is the main performance task. All six drills use rapid intent, with amplitude and speed constrained by clean execution; no effort is a conditioning interval.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Half-Kneeling Sprint Start — first two steps** | **2 × 2 starts**, one with each front leg per pair. Front toes at **1 m**; fit the entire starting body inside **0–2 m**. At most **2 driving strikes or 3 m of drive**, ending by **4 m**; progressively slow and stop by **9 m**. **75 s between starts; 120 s between pairs.** | **Main low-start task:** create forward motion from a flexed lead hip/knee while bringing the rear thigh through. Front foot flat, rear knee comfortably supported on the floor and rear toes tucked; establish a modest forward torso lean without rounding, pause briefly, then push into the lane. Both legs may contribute; this is not a test of isolated lead-leg force. First pair uses controlled fast intent to establish the route; second pair uses the fastest clean intent that preserves stopping control. |
| 2 | **Wall Drill Fast Switch — isolated single exchanges** | **2 × 4 total alternating switches** = 2/leg/set, **8 switches total**. After each rapid exchange, hold the new position **2 s**; reset **10 s between switches**, **60 s between sets**. | Rehearse rapid reciprocal thigh action while external support constrains the forward-leaning body line. Hands on a **verified solid wall**, feet far enough back for a comfortable whole-body lean; one thigh forward, stance leg long but not locked. Exchange legs quickly, placing the new stance foot beneath the hip line without stamping or folding. Reduce knee height/lean for pelvic rotation or heavy contact. |
| 3 | **Seated Vertical Jump to Floor Landing** | **2 × 2**, bodyweight, natural arms. Settle **2 s** on a stable, fitted bench/box with feet flat; jump without a backward rock. **20 s between reps; 90 s between sets.** | Express rapid knee/hip extension after supported sitting, reducing the contribution of a preceding dip. This supplies a bilateral force-production task from flexed legs, separate from the traveling start and split-stance jump. Sit near the edge, torso already inclined over the feet; jump mainly upward and only enough forward to clear the support. Land on both feet and hold **2 s**. Reduce height for a noisy/unstable finish. |
| 4 | **Split Squat Jump to Stick — keep the same lead leg** | **1 × 2 per lead leg**; bodyweight, shallow dip and modest height. **20 s between same-lead reps; 45 s before changing lead.** | Express rapid extension from an asymmetric base without asking the athlete to alternate legs in flight. Both feet leave the floor and return to their original split relationship; finish with controlled weight through both feet for **2 s**. Stay aligned into the lane; reduce depth/height for scissoring, twisting or rear-foot collapse. This is one jump at a time, not continuous jumping lunges. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Pogo — low amplitude, final same-leg hold** | **1 × 3 hops per leg**, **45 s between legs**. Two quick rebounds, then hold the third same-leg landing **2 s** before gently setting the other foot down. | Introduce a very small repeated same-leg ankle-support demand, closer to unilateral support than Class 1's bilateral pogo. Stay tall, free foot clear, minimal knee dip; prioritize controlled recoil over height. Lower amplitude or use the replacement below if the athlete cannot keep the pelvis/foot organized. |
| 6 | **Stationary Low Dribble Run** | **2 × 6 alternating foot strikes** = 3/foot/set, **12 fast contacts total**; approximately 3–4 s per set. **60 s between sets.** | Coordinate compact ankle-to-low-shin foot recovery with brief alternating upright contacts. Make small circular foot paths and place each foot close beneath the pelvis; relaxed opposite-arm action, no high-knee lift or forward reaching. This uses running exchanges rather than A-skip step-hop pairs. Slow cadence/shorten the cycle if posture or placement degrades; end the bout rather than extending it. |

**Switch reset:** After E2's two-second position hold, step out of the wall lean for the ten-second reset; resume from that ending leg position for the next alternating exchange. The floor replacement rests the knees during resets. Do not turn the resets into prolonged isometric work.

**Between exercises:** Allow **90 s after E1, E2, E3 and E4**, then **60 s after E5 and E6** before the next task (including the transition to S1). Instruction and setup may occupy these intervals; count elapsed time once. Extend recovery when breathing, focus or movement quality has not recovered.

**Start and station layout:** Use one protected **10 m lane**: front toes at 1 m, whole starting body inside 0–2 m, driving ends by 4 m, progressively slow to a stop by 9 m, last metre clear as reserve. These boundaries do not establish that every athlete can stop there. One athlete moves; keep the lane closed during the walking return and verify waiting/coach positions outside it. No joined lanes, hard stop at line 4 m, long acceleration or flying sprint. E3/E4 use a cleared floor area with room around the landing, clear of bench edges and waiting athletes. E5/E6 are stationary. Do not place the wall station or equipment inside the active start/run-off path.

**One-for-one replacements and regressions:**

- **E1:** If the first prescribed attempts show insufficient stopping control, use **one driving step instead of two** for remaining attempts at lower intent; stop the task if control still fails. If kneeling is uncomfortable, **use a low two-point standing start instead of the half-kneeling start**, same 2 × 2 schedule and boundaries. This is an explicitly justified recurrence of the Class 1 start family, not a claim of a unique drill; record the substitution and do not add starts.
- **E2:** A suitable wall is **not confirmed by the inventory**. If unavailable, **use High-Plank Single-Switch Knee Drive instead of Wall Drill Fast Switch**, same 2 × 4 exchanges, two-second holds, ten-second resets and sixty-second set rest. Hands on the floor below shoulders, one knee drawn forward with foot hovering and the other leg extended on its toes; rapidly exchange legs and place only the new rear foot down. Keep the trunk still; briefly rest the knees during resets. This retains rapid hip exchange with trunk control but changes support and is not equivalent to inclined sprint stance. End for wrist/shoulder discomfort or loss of trunk control.
- **E3:** Choose a stable seat height permitting flat feet and knees near a right angle. If no suitable seat exists, **use Squat Jump to Stick instead of Seated Vertical Jump**, 2 × 2, two-second standing squat pause, natural arms, no re-dip, same rests and two-foot finish. This removes seat support; log the change. Do not improvise unstable stacked seats or add a hurdle.
- **E5:** If repeated same-leg contacts are not qualified, **use Ankle Pogo in Place instead of Single-Leg Pogo**, **1 × 6 low bilateral hops**, holding the last landing two seconds. This is an intentional recurrence of Class 1's drill family for readiness, not novelty. The nominal landing-event count remains six but laterality changes.

**Stop rules:** Every attempted jump/contact/start counts as actual exposure, including faulty attempts. Cue or reduce demand before the next scheduled rep; repeated loss of the same criterion despite adjustment ends that exercise. Stop the affected task immediately for pain, a slip, changed gait or lost control. Do not add make-up attempts or fill omitted work with unlisted exercises. If a default or its documented replacement cannot be performed productively, record incomplete actual work.

**Final sequence correction:** All E1 defaults and standing/one-step replacements use the 1 m front-toe origin and entire starting footprint inside 0–2 m. Drive at most two strikes/3 m to line 4, progressively stop by line 9 and keep the final metre clear. Maximum outward travel is 8 m/start; this is a boundary from the toe line, not an exact body trajectory. The revised layout requires fresh stopping qualification; prior 0 m-layout scores are not matched comparisons. Counts/rests hold; additional setup or recovery extends the allowance.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep effort easy, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, well short of fatigue. These familiar strengthening tasks support the day's asymmetric push and unilateral ankle work without increasing pre-lift fatigue.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**. Both feet supported; **5 s release between holds, 20 s between feet**. | Control the working foot's arch while keeping heel and both forefoot bases supported, providing a stable base for start and pogo force. Keep toes long; reduce effort or **use seated foot doming instead of standing** for toe clawing or rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for keeping the pelvis directed forward over the support leg. Bottom knee bent, top leg in line with torso, toes forward; lift only while the pelvis stays stacked. Reduce range for rolling or thigh drift. |

Allow **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation sets. No burn, grinding or testing to fatigue; stop for pain or persistent compensation.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Keep Class 1's **working-set prescriptions**, not an assumed previously used weight. Qualify today's load or leverage to leave approximately **2–3 technically sound reps in reserve (RIR)**. Missing feedback authorizes no automatic increase in load, range or sets. No maximal tests or forced reps.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 5 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, strong ascent. **60 s between legs; 120 s between rounds.** | **Main strength anchor:** load lead-leg knee/hip extension from the split base used to leave the low start. Both feet stay on the floor; a modest whole-trunk forward inclination is allowed without rounding. Reduce load/depth for pelvic rotation, knee collapse or rear-leg hopping. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6**, 2–3 RIR; **2 s down**, controlled strong ascent. **120 s between sets.** | Strengthen hip extension with flexed hips and lengthened hamstrings, complementing P1's knee-extension contribution to the start. Soft knees, bells close; stop depth before pelvic/spinal compensation. Reduce range/load when needed. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Load the thigh-recovery action rehearsed rapidly in E1/E2 while removing the need to balance. Sit near a stable bench edge, foot supported and knee near a right angle; secure a DB across the distal thigh above the knee with both hands. Lift the knee a few centimetres without pulling with the hands or leaning back; return the foot gently. Reduce load/range for tipping, pinching or implement movement. |
| 4 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 4 complete cycles**, **2–3 clean cycles in reserve**. Each cycle = **4 alternating small heel steps out + 4 back**, about **8–10 s**. | Load knee-flexor support while the knee opens and returns, supplementing the hip hinge rather than adding a third hip-extension lift. Keep the pelvis raised; walk only to a controlled soft-knee position, then return to the original heel marks. Choose step length for the reserve target; shorten for sagging or cramp, stop if unresolved. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, floor range** | **1 × 10**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**, heels return to floor. | Develop straight-knee ankle force capacity supporting push-off and the new unilateral rebound task. Hold DBs at sides, rise through both forefeet without outward roll, bounce or forced knee lock. Reduce load for lost heel height. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **2 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. **90 s between sets.** | Load plantarflexion with flexed knees for the low start/support demand, complementing P5's knee position. Keep knees near a right angle, weights secured on thighs above the knees, forefeet planted and heel travel repeatable. Reduce load for knee straightening, foot roll or bouncing. |

**Load-preparation sets, separate from work:** P1 **1 × 4/leg** bodyweight/light, 30 s between sides and 60 s before work; P2 **1 × 5 easy then 1 × 3 moderate**, 60 s between and 90 s before work; P3 **1 × 3/leg light**, 30 s between and 45 s before work; P4 **1 short-lever cycle**, 45 s before work; P5 **1 × 5 easy**, 45 s before work; P6 **1 × 5 easy**, 60 s before work. Additional small ramps may be used only in listed movements when necessary to select a qualified load; record their work/time. These sets do not replace Access & Prepare 1.

**Rest between primary exercises:** **120 s after P1 and P2; 90 s after P3, P4 and P5**, before the next preparation set. Count equipment changes and instruction inside recovery where possible; no mandatory extra interval after final P6. If P5 balance prevents a challenging set, **use supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between sides and 45 s before work. A stable hand support must be verified. Log changed laterality. A fitted stable box may replace the bench for P3/P6; neither seat dimensions nor DB weight ranges are confirmed.

**Timing and scheduling:** Budget **75–85 minutes after the existing preparation** for an athlete with prompt equipment access, including coaching/setup, resets, rests, ramps and transitions. Model: approximately 30 min explosive, 6 min light strength and 39 min primary strength. Group queues, extra teaching/ramps or recovery add time. Preparation duration and actual booking remain unknown: full-class time is unresolved; a 60-minute whole-class window does not fit this version. Do not shorten required recovery to force a timetable. Treat as demanding lower-body work; review actual sport, sprint, gym, other classes and separate tumbling. A nonconsecutive demanding exposure, often around 48–72 hours later, is only a planning starting point; readiness and the wider schedule decide.

**Progression note:** Four low starts replace six standing starts; no sprint distance increases. The new tasks isolate different start/support demands, not a claim that the athlete earned harder training. Single-leg pogo has only three landings per side. Strength doses remain unchanged while loads are qualified anew. Class 3 should refine leg-specific push contribution with distinct drills after reviewing actual response; retain useful strength measures. Compare a drill only with its own matched protocol, never with a different start or jump as an improvement test.

**Audit summary:** Exact 6/2/6 and seven-category mapping preserved. Default explosive workload is **4 starts, 14 jump/hop landings, 8 supported switch strikes and 12 low-dribble contacts**; no throws, swings or top-speed runs. Compared with Class 1, fewer landings do not make a same-leg rebound equivalent to a bilateral pogo or skip. The limited new same-leg dose, unchanged strength sets and full recovery address that uncertainty. Solid-wall availability, seat fit, lane width/clearance, athlete response, outside workload, staffing and full-class time remain conditional.
