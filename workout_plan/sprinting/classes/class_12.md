# Sprinting — Class 12 of 12

**Primary effort:** Maintain upright support while the legs recover promptly and the feet return nearby; finish with repeatable observations that can guide the next training decision.

**Baseline and review:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique and qualified coaching. Reviewed Classes 1–11, their anchors, workload, default/contingency history and available feedback. **Actual attendance, execution, loads and recovery remain unknown.** Two proposed variants add specific force-expression tasks; four earlier drills recur openly. Strength doses hold at Class 11 levels. No final-class test, earned progression or automatic restoration of volume is assumed.

**Framework:** `Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)` and `Sprinting > Maximum velocity`. Today emphasizes gym support for upright mechanics, with a small forward ankle-force task linking back to acceleration. No start sprint or actual maximum-velocity run is prescribed today.

**Quality marker within E1:** Within the twelve scheduled contacts, record nearby placement, upright pelvic/trunk control, clean alternating rhythm, exact pattern, completed contacts and finish set-downs. Compare with Class 4 only if actual observations and setup/intent match; otherwise establish today's record. A gym-drill score is not sprint speed.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main upright coordination/rapid-force task. The remaining jobs separate upward force from a settled leg, forward ankle recoil, full free-leg placement, opposing thigh timing and rapid hip extension. The three final drills add no flight contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stationary A-Run — alternating high-knee exchanges** | **2 × 6 alternating foot contacts**, 3/foot/set; **90 s between sets**. Bodyweight; quick rhythm without chasing cadence. Start the sets on opposite support legs. | **Main task; intentional Class 4 repeat:** alternate compact bent-knee recovery and rapid support while staying tall. Begin on one foot, other knee comfortably raised; use small airborne exchanges and natural opposing arms. Place each foot near its standing position without extra bounce, forced knee height, backward lean or clawing. After contact six, stabilize and gently set the free foot down: **two finish set-downs total**. Reduce knee height/cadence for drift, reaching or lost posture. |
| 2 | **Paused Single-Leg Vertical Jump to Two-Foot Landing** | **1 × 2 per takeoff leg**. Hold a shallow single-leg dip **2 s**, then jump without re-dipping; natural arm drive from a settled position. Modest height; bilateral receiver held **2 s**. **20 s between same-leg reps; 60 s between legs.** | Express upward leg force from a settled unilateral position while sharing the receiver across both feet. This changes Class 5's dynamic takeoff rather than adding a harder landing. Keep the pelvis forward and the dip comfortable; leave on one foot, receive softly on both beneath the body. Reduce depth/height for wobbling, a hidden re-dip, reaching or twisting. The pause does not eliminate all elastic contribution. |
| 3 | **Low Forward Pogos — two hops, final stick** | **2 × 2 small consecutive bilateral hops**, bodyweight; **90 s between bouts**. Soft knees with minimal knee bend; final receiver held **2 s**. Total forward travel **no more than 1 m per bout**. | Retain a tall body while the ankles contribute a small forward push and reuse the first contact, linking ankle support to propulsion. Start with parallel feet at line **1 m**, finish by line **2 m**, and keep the entire **0–4 m area** clear for setup and any adjustment. Both feet leave/land together; no deep squat, long bound or chase for distance. Do not take the second hop if the first receiver is unstable or requires a deep recovery bend. |
| 4 | **Standing Single-Leg Cycle and Strike** | **1 × 3 cycles per moving leg**. One compact recovery cycle followed by a quick target-foot strike; **10 s reset between reps, 45 s between legs**. | **Intentional Classes 3/11 repeat:** isolate full free-leg recovery and nearby placement while the other foot stays planted. Recover the heel beneath the hip, lift the thigh comfortably, allow modest knee opening, then return the foot lightly beside the support foot. No kick, long backswing, backward scrape, hop or hard stamp. Reduce cycle height/range for reaching or pelvic movement. |
| 5 | **Supine Alternating Hip Switch — both heels off the floor** | **1 × 4 opposing leg exchanges**, bodyweight; quick switch / **1 s settled finish**, **10 s reset between exchanges**. Each exchange moves both legs once. Alternate the initial configuration so each finish occurs twice. | **Intentional Class 5 repeat:** practice rapid opposing thigh movement with the pelvis supported and no receiving impact. One hip/knee is comfortably flexed, the other leg reaches diagonally away with a soft knee and heel clear. Exchange through a short range without fully straightening the reaching knee or forcing the thigh to the chest. Keep the pelvis quiet and breathe. Gently lower **both feet after each rep** and relax before re-establishing the next setup: **eight controlled set-downs**, separate from the four exchanges. Shorten reach/bend knees more for arching or slowed motion. |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Glute Bridge — rapid hip drive** | **1 × 3 per working leg**, bodyweight; **quick rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower**. **10 s reset between reps; 45 s between legs**. | **Intentional Class 4 repeat:** express hip extension toward neutral without another landing, supporting the hip position used in upright stance. Working foot flat, knee bent, free thigh comfortably raised; drive the pelvis up until working thigh and trunk align, then lower. Keep heel down, pelvis level and ribs settled; no lumbar arch or foot launch. Lower the free foot and relax during each reset: **six controlled free-foot set-downs**. Shorten rise for twist/cramp; use the shared-support replacement if the drive becomes slow balance work. |

**Recovery:** **90 s after E1–E4; 60 s after E5–E6**, including the transition to S1. Teaching/setup may occupy recovery, counted once. Relax positions during resets and extend recovery if force, attention or control has not returned. No conditioning work fills waits.

**Space and delivery:** E3 uses at most 1 m of small forward travel per bout, with protected setup/overshoot inside one ten-metre lane; walk back only after the coach clears the area. Other explosive tasks stay stationary. Qualify surface, usable width, clear receiving/return space and waiting positions. No external drop, hurdle, wall, band, ball, runway extension or device is required. DB/bench fit and quantities remain unconfirmed for strength. Three lanes do not establish simultaneous group capacity. The separate 12 m facility record remains unresolved; use the tighter ten-metre footprint.

**Replacements — use instead of, never in addition:**

- **E1:** Use **Fast A-March instead**, **2 × 6 alternating placements**, 90 s rest; one foot always remains down. Twelve controlled returns replace the running contacts, with no extra running finish set-downs. This repeats an earlier fallback.
- **E2:** If the pause disrupts a useful push, use **Dynamic Single-Leg Vertical Jump to Two-Foot Landing instead**, same four jumps, rests and receivers; Class 5 recurrence. If unilateral takeoff cannot be qualified, use **Bilateral Paused Squat Jump to Stick instead**, **2 × 2**, shallow 2 s pause, no re-dip, natural arms, modest height/2 s receiver hold, 20 s between reps and 60 s between sets. Choose one path only.
- **E3:** If forward drift is unproductive, use **Low Bilateral Pogos in Place instead**, same two bouts of two and rest; earlier family. If the immediate second hop cannot be controlled, use **Isolated Low Bilateral Ankle Hops instead**, **2 × 2**, 2 s held receivers, **20 s between reps; 90 s between sets**. No second hop follows a failed receiver and no omitted attempt is made up. Minimal knee excursion keeps the ankle task distinct from E2's knee/hip push.
- **E4:** Use **Standing Straight-Leg Downstroke instead**, **1 × 3/leg**, 1 s low forward lift with a soft knee, quick light return beside the planted support foot, 10 s resets and 45 s between legs; Class 9 recurrence.
- **E5:** Use **Supine Unresisted Knee Drive, opposite heel grounded, instead**, **1 × 2/leg**, quick lift / 1 s finish / 2 s lower, **10 s between every rep**, including the side change. Four knee drives/four moving-heel returns replace four two-leg exchanges/eight foot set-downs. Keep the opposite leg comfortably long and pelvis quiet; earlier family.
- **E6:** Use **Rapid Bilateral Glute Bridge instead**, **1 × 6**, same quick rise/1 s top/2 s lower and 10 s between reps; both feet stay planted. This replaces the unilateral rises and removes six separate free-foot set-downs. Earlier fallback; no extra bridge set.

**Quality rules:** Failed attempts are exposure. Adjust the next scheduled rep; persistent faults despite adjustment end that task. Pain, slipping, changed gait, lost control or lane intrusion stops affected work. Log actual identities, side doses, contacts/set-downs, extra setup, omissions and reasons. Incomplete delivery remains incomplete. No make-up attempts or added tests.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Easy effort, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**. Keep these small strengthening doses below pre-fatiguing levels.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot**, both feet supported. **5 s release between holds; 20 s between feet.** | Maintain heel and both forefoot bases while gently raising the working arch, supporting the nearby contacts in E1/E4 and ankle recoil in E3. Keep toes long; reduce effort or use **seated foot doming instead of standing** for clawing/rolling. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction** | **1 × 6 per side**, bodyweight; **2 s up / 1 s hold / 2 s down**. **20 s between sides.** | Give the lateral hip a small strengthening dose for pelvic control during upright support and free-thigh recovery. Bottom knee bent, top leg aligned with torso, toes forward; reduce range for pelvic rolling or a forward-drifting thigh. |

Rest **45 s between exercises and 60 s after S2** before P1 preparation. Stop for pain or persistent compensation; keep the work easy rather than adding resistance to create a burn.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging loads/leverage leaving approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve (RIR)**. Hold Class 11’s working doses and preparation, including one soleus working set. Actual prior loads are unknown: qualify today's resistance instead of assuming an increase. No maximal tests, forced reps or automatic restoration of reduced volume.

| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 per leg**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s lift / 1 s hold / 2 s lower**. **45 s between legs.** | Strengthen thigh lifting for E1’s upright recovery and the free-leg tasks in E4/E5. | 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, bilateral DB loading** | **1 × 8**, 2–3 RIR; **1 s rise / 1 s top / 2 s lower / 1 s bottom**. | Maintain flexed-knee ankle-force capacity for E2’s softened receiver and the flexed-knee portion of stance, complementing P5. Reduce load for knee straightening, rolling or bounce. |

**Load-preparation sets, separate from working sets:** P1 **1 × 4/leg** bodyweight/light (30 s between sides, 60 s before work); P2 **1 × 5 easy then 1 × 3 moderate** (60 s between, 90 s before work); P3 **1 × 3/leg light** (30 s between, 45 s before work); P4 **1 short-lever cycle** (45 s before work); P5 **1 × 5 easy** (45 s before work); P6 **1 × 5 easy** (60 s before work). Additional small ramps, if needed, stay within the listed movement and are logged; no additional preparation sequence is invented.

**Between primary exercises:** **120 s after P1/P2; 90 s after P3/P4/P5**, before the next preparation set. Setup may occupy recovery; no extra required rest after final P6. If P5 balance limits productive loading, use **supported Single-Leg Calf Raise instead of bilateral P5**, **1 × 6/leg**, same tempo/RIR, 30 s between legs; preparation **1 × 3/leg easy**, 20 s between, 45 s before work. Verify stable hand support and log laterality. A fitted stable box may replace the bench for P3/P6.

**Timing and recovery:** Allow **65–75 minutes after Access & Prepare 1** with prompt individual equipment access: about 28–29 min explosiveness, 5–6 min light work and 31 min primary work, including teaching, resets, rest, load preparation and transitions. Extra queues, setup, ramps or recovery extend this. Preparation, booking, staff/group and complete-session fit remain unknown; a 60-minute complete booking cannot fit. Review actual sport, gym, sprinting, other focus classes and separate tumbling. Roughly 48–72 hours between demanding lower-body exposures is only a starting consideration governed by actual response and the full schedule.

**Progression note:** The paused unilateral vertical push and brief forward ankle recoil have distinct jobs. A-run, full-cycle placement, supine opposing exchanges and rapid bridge deliberately recur because extra airborne complexity, a loaded jump or a fresh throwing setup adds little to this final upright-support review. No novelty credit is claimed for them. All strength doses hold at Class 11 levels; do not restore reduced volume without actual clean work at target reserve and adequate recovery. The next training decision should use matched observations, actual outside workload and the still-unaddressed need for appropriately spaced real sprinting when a suitable runway is available.
