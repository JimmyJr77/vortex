## Counting conventions

- One jump/hop repetition is one takeoff-flight-landing event, including an unsuccessful attempt. A bilateral landing is one event involving two feet; it is not interchangeable with a unilateral hop or low pogo. Walks and stance changes are not hidden jumps.
- Class 1 E5 uses a session-specific count: one left–right pair includes two step-hop actions. Three pairs = six hops/landings (three on each foot) plus six support-transfer steps/contacts (three per foot). These transfer contacts are additional to the six hop landings. Do not log three pairs as three contacts or as the library's traveling A-skip dose.
- In Classes 1–3, 6–8 and 10–11, E1's first two foot strikes after leaving the start stance are driving contacts. Initial support/pushes and subsequent easing-down contacts are separate; total running contacts cannot be known without observing them. Running steps are not added to the jump-event subtotal. A stop is an episode, not a single impact.
- For unilateral strength, a written set **round** includes both sides; a **bout** is each separately executed side. Bilateral simultaneous work is one bout. A bridge-walkout **cycle** is four alternating heel steps out and four back; a step is not a full repetition.
- Preparation sets, work sets, static holds and ballistic releases are separate. We do not calculate a combined jump-equivalent score or tonnage without actual loads. No workload is labeled safe solely because of a count.
- Totals exclude unknown Access & Prepare 1, walking returns and other training. Log changed/failed attempts, partial cycles, extra preparation sets and unscheduled athlete demonstrations as actual exposure when reported.

- **E6 hands-on-hips CMJ to stick instead of KB swing:** six added bilateral jump landings; zero swings if fully substituted. - **P5 supported single-leg calf instead of bilateral:** 1 × 6/leg means 2 working bouts / 12 reps instead of 1 bout / 10 reps. Primary totals become **13 bouts / 72 dynamic reps plus 4 cycles**. Preparation 3/leg replaces 5 bilateral: preparation becomes **10 bouts / 33 dynamic reps plus 1 cycle**. Add about 1 minute for side changes. Support must be stable and verified; do not use a loose implement as a handhold.
- Seated short-foot, a fitted stable box instead of the bench, lower amplitude/distance, lighter load and shorter walkout step length retain their slot and nominal count. Record their altered conditions. Stopped work is incomplete actual work, never a zero-dose prescription passed off as a full class.
