## Daily stimulus brief

1. **Result:** Maintain upright support while recovering a compact free leg and returning the foot near the hip.
2. **Demand:** Coordinate hip/knee folding, vertical force reuse, ankle recoil and controlled hip extension without drift or excessive trunk movement.
3. **Anchor:** Two six-contact bouts of stationary high-knee/butt-kick running. Observe the coupled fold, foot placement and upright position within these attempts.
4. **Supporting jobs:** Whole-leg vertical rebound; ankle-led recoil from a split base; a long-leg downstroke with the other foot planted; opposing arm/thigh timing with seated support; rapid unilateral hip return without a landing. Retained strengthening supports these same jobs.
5. **Constraints:** Existing Access & Prepare 1, exact 6/2/6, baseline ages 12–14 with established technique and qualified coaching, no top-speed runway, unknown actual response and no automatic escalation. New variants must earn their place beyond a different name.

## Replacement effects and duplication check

- E1 simpler A-run preserves 12 running contacts plus two finish set-downs and repeats Class 4. Its nonflight march version gives 12 returning-foot placements without the two extra running finish set-downs. Neither is recorded as the completed coupled default.
- E2 isolated bilateral countermovement jumps keep four landings, remove immediate rebounds and repeat an earlier fallback family. Add two 20 s between-rep rests; allow about one extra minute.
- E3 parallel bilateral pogo keeps four low landings but removes the offset, repeating Class 1's family. It stays mechanically distinct from E2's knee/hip-driven jumps even if both regress.
- E4 compact single-leg cycle-and-strike preserves six target-foot returns but changes the recovery path; explicit Class 3 recurrence. E5's no-seat option is six standing arm exchanges, feet grounded, with no knee lifts or foot returns; explicit Class 4 fallback family. This avoids duplicating E1's marching replacement in E5.
- E6 unilateral rapid bridge gives four hip rises, two/side, and removes four standing free-foot reset placements. Bilateral rapid bridge gives four rises with shared support. Both are disclosed Class 4 families and different from P4's knee-lever walkout. Record actual setup contacts and changed resistance/support.
- Supported unilateral P5 changes work to **10 bouts / 56 dynamic reps + 3 cycles**, preparation to **10 bouts / 33 reps + 1 cycle**, about one additional minute. Verify the support and log laterality.
