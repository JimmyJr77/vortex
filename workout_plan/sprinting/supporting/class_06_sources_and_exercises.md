# Class 6 — sources and exercise identities

Reviewed 2026-09-13. Sources inform particular mechanics and exercise choices; no source validates this exact session, youth dosage or guaranteed sprint-speed improvement.

## Coaching and evidence basis

- [Overtime Athletes: Falling Starts](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/falling-starts/) describes a whole-body lean leading into initial running steps. Class 6 uses an earlier controlled recovery step, four attempts and the established short driving/stopping limits. Near-fall cues, toe stabbing, aerodynamic explanations and guaranteed outcomes are not adopted.
- [Overtime Athletes: Five Explosive Exercises](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/5-explosive-exercises/) describes staggered broad takeoff and a two-foot finish. Class 6 uses modest distance/offset and both front-leg configurations. Source claims of immediate sprint improvements and maximal distances are not imported.
- [Overtime Athletes: Top Speed Training Progression](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/use-this-top-speed-training-progression/) describes a step-over recovery between high-knee work and fuller cycling. Class 6 adapts the release to stationary asymmetric running, two cycles per target. A literal obstacle, aggressive clawing and injury-prevention guarantees are excluded.
- [StrongFirst: The Kur for Your Kettlebell Swing and Clean](https://www.strongfirst.com/the-kur-for-your-kettlebell-swing-and-clean/) discusses dead-swing initiation, controlled hikes and a conservatively selected implement. Only that parked-single concept informs E6. The article's long practice sessions, random selection, weekly frequency and loading examples are not used. The six singles, chest ceiling and fallback are authored here; each single still contains a backswing.
- Prior [source register](sources_and_exercises.md) and [Class 5 notes](class_05_sources_and_exercises.md) retain the ground-force/hip/ankle-function and supervised technical-loading rationale. Wall recoil and scissor exchange have mechanical component jobs, not demonstrated exact sprint-transfer outcomes. No new measurement, injury diagnosis or actual performance is claimed.
