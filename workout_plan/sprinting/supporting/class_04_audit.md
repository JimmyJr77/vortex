## Daily stimulus brief

1. **Result:** Maintain an upright trunk/pelvis while rapidly organizing the next nearby foot placement and supporting the body over one leg.
2. **Demand:** Separate support-leg force from free-leg recovery; avoid drift, reaching and excessive pelvic movement during brief rapid exchanges.
3. **Anchor:** Two six-contact stationary A-runs. Record attempted/controlled contacts, drift, lean and pelvic control within those contacts; this is a new protocol, not a speed test.
4. **Supporting jobs:** Single-leg vertical push/reception; alternating long-leg ankle rebound; foot return across unequal surface heights with a stable support foot; rapid arm action with limited torso compensation; rapid hip extension toward neutral. Strength retains split-leg extension, a flexed-hip hinge, thigh lift, changing knee-lever work and two knee-position ankle-force tasks. Light foot/lateral-hip strengthening supports the same upright demand.
5. **Constraints:** Exact 6/2/6, ages 12–14 baseline competence and supervision, no reported prior execution, unique-drill preference, gym footprint and unconfirmed platform/seat dimensions. No actual maximum-velocity practice can be supplied by this room.
