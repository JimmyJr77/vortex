## Coaching sources and limits

| Source | Contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [OTA — Offseason Football Linear Speed Workout](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/offseason-football-linear-speed-workout/) | Names bent-over starts and includes a hip-hinge initiation emphasis. | The still parallel-foot, hand-free setup and four short starts are our explicit variant. Its warm-up, longer routes and six-reps-each-side dose are not copied. No dominant-leg assumption. |
| [OTA — Advanced Linear Speed Drills](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/advanced-speed/) | Distinguishes step-over running from a one-sided dead-leg pattern and treats these as mechanics rehearsal. | Our version alternates both legs in place for eight contacts. No traveling bound, fixed mastery timeline, deliberate claw or injury-prevention claim is imported. If actual previous technique already matched, record overlap. |
| [OTA — NFL Combine Prep Training](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/maximize-your-speed-nfl-combine-prep-training-for-the-40-yard-dash/) | Names single/double/triple wall switches with a focus on posture and quality. | Class 10 intentionally repeats Class 8's four double-switch pairs. No novelty credit or automatic extension to longer runs. |
| [OTA — Three Drills to Get Faster Instantly](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/3-drills-to-get-faster-instantly/) | Provides a resisted hip-recovery concept in its banded hip-drive example. | Our foot-loop standing drive has no external anchor or extra knee-extension action. Its band setup, six reps and quick-lift/controlled-return dose are authored here; the source's warm-up, volume and immediate-speed claims are not imported. |

Horizontal projection and individual force-acceptance families are already documented in the earlier OTA source registers. E2's same-lead forward receiver and E3's two-foot takeoff/single-foot receiver are explicit locally authored variants, not claimed exact OTA demonstrations. Neither is a literal simulation of a running contact. General youth-supervision and hip/ankle rationale remains in the [initial source register](sources_and_exercises.md); it does not establish readiness or equal loading between variants.
