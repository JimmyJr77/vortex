## Daily stimulus brief

1. **Result:** Initiate forward force from a compact hand-free base, then support prompt free-leg recovery and nearby subsequent contacts.
2. **Demand:** Transfer from a two-foot starting base to individual support while retaining body alignment; practice upright recovery separately within stationary space.
3. **Anchor:** Four static bent-over starts, observing first stepping foot, forward push without a backward step, body position and stopping. Use the scheduled attempts only.
4. **Supporting jobs:** Forward projection while retaining a split receiver; shared takeoff into one-leg reception; alternating upright step-over recovery; two linked inclined exchanges; brief resisted thigh recovery over a planted support foot. Retained strength supports these same demands.
