**Reviewed 2026-09-13.** The user's Sprinting target, seven-category framework, exact 6/2/6 and existing Access & Prepare 1 govern. These are locally dosed supplemental gym tasks; no source validates the whole session or establishes an athlete response.

| Source | Use and limit |
|---|---|
| [OTA — Soccer Speed Drills for Acceleration and Max Velocity](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/soccer-speed-drills/) | Provides horizontal/vertical force and start/upright drill families, including tempo changes in unilateral jumps. | [Initial source register](sources_and_exercises.md) | Preserves primary hip/ankle biomechanics and supervised youth-strength guidance, plus earlier OTA inspiration. It supports intended jobs, not the efficacy/readiness of this exact fourteen-entry prescription. |

The local CSV contains **Forward / Backward Pogos / forward-backward-pogos** and **Line Pogo Forward-Back / line-pogo-forward-back**. Neither is an exact binding for forward-only two-hop bouts; do not copy their identifiers as an exact variant. No exact paused unilateral vertical-floor-jump binding was found in the inspected local names. Both E2/E3 are proposed additions/variants. Existing family identity records cover the other entries; no invented IDs, live publication claim or database edit.

Novelty was considered against a linked same-leg hop, a loaded jump and another throwing setup. Those add reception, resistance or equipment demands without a better final-review job. Two proposed variants plus four declared repetitions are more defensible here; this is not a claim of six new base movements. Replacement families recur openly and occupy their own slots only.

The complete sequence contains many related variants, including A-run/coupled-recovery overlap, and cannot honestly be advertised as 72 mechanically unique drills. See the [full-sequence audit](full_sequence_audit.md). All actual use remains unknown. Appropriate real sprinting on a suitable runway remains necessary to assess and practice top-speed running; gym observations alone do not demonstrate it.
