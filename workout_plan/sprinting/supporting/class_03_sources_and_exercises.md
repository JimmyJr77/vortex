## Additional style and local evidence

[Overtime Athletes — Step-Up Variations for Explosive Athletes](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/the-best-step-ups-exercise-variations-for-explosive-athletes/) distinguishes controlled strengthening and rapid step-up tasks. It informs E2's leg-drive/free-thigh job. Our nonflight six-rep adaptation avoids the article's more demanding launches and their elevated landings; its outcome promises are not adopted.

[Overtime Athletes — Sprint Mechanics for Maximum Speed: Volume 2](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/sprint-mechanics-for-maximum-speed/) discusses foot recovery, placement and single-leg cycling, informing E5's isolated cycle/strike job. The stationary planted-support execution, three repetitions per leg and absence of a subsequent sprint are declared adaptations. The source favors repetition of useful drills; the user's later preference for distinct drills governs this curriculum's selection. Neither source validates this fourteen-entry class.

Local names/keys were checked in [`docs/exercise-difficulty-review.csv`](../../../docs/exercise-difficulty-review.csv). Selected generated records clarify identity boundaries. This is repository evidence, not confirmation of live approval, publishing or athlete eligibility. No IDs or library records are created by this plan.

The inspected [straight-leg-bound/march research record](../../../scripts/data/canonical-research/generated/straight-leg-bound-march.v1.json) flags a conflation between no-flight marching and flight-based bounding. That ambiguous card was not used as a convenient binding or exercise slot. E5 defines its own nontraveling, planted-support cycle and fast strike precisely.

**Replacements:** Standing two-point start, same-lead split jump and single-leg broad to two-foot landing deliberately recur from prior classes only under the stated comfort/equipment/receiving-control conditions. Unresisted supine knee drive removes external resistance while retaining the same rapid-drive task. Supported unilateral calf and fitted-seat/short-foot changes keep the earlier declared rules. Record actual identity and altered contact types; none is additional work.

**Unknowns after review:** Actual attendance, technique, loads, soreness/recovery, outside sport/gym/tumbling, preparation content/time, lane width and actual 10 m/12 m discrepancy, low-box/seat dimensions, closed-band suitability, group/staff/booking. No test equipment is assumed necessary, and no top-speed result is inferred from any gym drill.
