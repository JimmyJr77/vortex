# Class 7 — sources and exercise identities

Reviewed 2026-09-13. Exact doses, seated lane geometry, floor adaptations and replacements are authored here. Sources inform components, not validation of this complete youth session or guaranteed sprint transfer.

## Evidence and coaching inspiration

- [Overtime Athletes: Three Single-Leg Plyometric Exercises](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/3-single-leg-plyometric-exercises-for-more-bounce/) discusses forceful seated jump initiation without rocking and permits removing a receiving box. Class 7 uses the broader settled-support idea; its bilateral seated forward jump, seated start and short layout are local adaptations, not exercises or doses copied from that article.
- [Overtime Athletes: Isometric Training](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/isometrics-training-for-athletes-build-elite-strength-and-joint-resilience/) distinguishes force against an immovable object from sustained position holds. Class 7 chooses brief submaximal rapid-onset pulses; it does not import the article's long maximal efforts, therapeutic claims or claims that such work lacks joint stress.
- [Balshaw et al., 2016, Journal of Applied Physiology](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/japplphysiol.00091.2016) compared brief explosive and sustained isometric knee-extension training in healthy young men. This supports distinguishing force-onset intent from a prolonged hold. It did not test six submaximal wall pulses in ages 12–14 or sprint outcomes. The current entry dose/posture is a programming inference, not that study's protocol, and no force-onset measurement is available here.
- [Overtime Athletes: Five Explosive Exercises](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/5-explosive-exercises/) informs the single-leg forward-push/two-foot-reception family. The two-second unilateral pause is an authored constraint. A pause reduces preceding motion but does not prove all muscle/tendon action is purely concentric.
- The retained swing uses the previously reviewed [StrongFirst parked-swing guidance](https://www.strongfirst.com/the-kur-for-your-kettlebell-swing-and-clean/). Its recurrence is explicit; no new source is claimed to justify changing the name or load. Earlier hip/ankle/knee-flexor strength rationales and youth loading limits remain in the [initial source register](sources_and_exercises.md) and [Class 6 notes](class_06_sources_and_exercises.md).

The THIRST seated-broad-jump search result supplied name-level coaching evidence, but its full page timed out; no unseen execution instruction or outcome is attributed to it. Seated-start mechanics are locally authored. No inaccessible material is treated as reviewed evidence.
