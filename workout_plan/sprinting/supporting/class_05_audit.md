## Daily stimulus brief

1. **Result:** Recover one thigh quickly while keeping the supporting leg and pelvis organized for the next contact.
2. **Demand:** Distinguish the supporting leg's push from free-leg motion; keep a compact recovery without reaching, pelvic hitching or excess trunk movement.
3. **Primary task:** Stationary single-leg high-knee run, three cycles per target leg. Observe controlled cycles and extra contacts within the scheduled work, with a new drill-specific record.
4. **Supporting jobs:** Unilateral upward push with shared reception; same-leg ankle recoil coupled to the opposite thigh; modest lower-leg release in a skip rhythm; rapid front-leg extension as a trailing leg recovers; opposing thigh exchanges without landings. Light foot/lateral-hip work and six retained strength roles support those demands.
5. **Constraints:** Exact 6/2/6, baseline ages 12–14 with established technique and supervision, no reported response, unique-drill preference, no maximum-velocity runway, unconfirmed group/booking and equipment dimensions. The session is gym supplementation, not a complete top-speed exposure.

## Exposure and recovery accounting

Default running: **12 contacts**, six/foot. E2: **4 single-leg takeoffs and bilateral landings**, two takeoffs/leg. E3: **4 same-leg pogo landings**, two/leg. E4: **4 support-hop landings** plus **4 raised-foot transfer contacts**, two/foot in each role. Jump/hop total: **12 events, four bilateral and eight unilateral landings; 16 jump/hop foot impacts**. Running contacts and skip transfers are additional, different contact types.

E5: **6 nonflight lunge rises**, three/front leg; each includes one rear-foot floor placement and one returning free-foot set-down, **12 controlled foot placements** in total. E6: **4 simultaneous opposing exchanges**, both legs move on each exchange; eight limb excursions are not eight exercise reps. Gentle setup/reset/exit foot contacts are additional incidental contacts. Starts, sprint distance, throws, swings, external drops and actual maximum-velocity running are zero.

Class 4 had 12 running contacts, 10 unilateral jump/hop events and six low-box taps. Class 5 retains 12 running contacts but prescribes 12 jump/hop events with shared reception in four of them. The extra two events and changed types are disclosed, not presented as a neutral load swap or earned impact progression. Pogo dose is reduced and no height/distance target is increased. On-site competence and actual recovery govern use of the defaults or regressions; small counts do not establish readiness.

Light work remains **2 rounds / 4 bouts / 12 dynamic reps + 30 foot-seconds**. Primary remains **7 rounds / 9 bouts / 54 dynamic reps + 3 walkout cycles** (24 heel steps). Preparation remains **7 rounds / 9 bouts / 32 dynamic reps + 1 short-lever cycle** (8 heel steps). A round includes both sides; a bout is one separately performed side. Unknown Access & Prepare 1 and outside sport/gym/tumbling are excluded, not assumed zero. No performed cumulative total can be calculated.

Rest separates brief fast tasks from light strengthening and loaded work. Keep the light work at RPE 3–4 and primary work at 2–3 technical RIR. Extend recovery or stop affected tasks when quality does not return; do not shorten rests to fit a clock or add make-up attempts. Future changes need actual execution, technical reserve and subsequent recovery. Retaining a written dose does not establish that it was previously performed or tolerated.

## Replacement effects

- E1 march substitutes 12 nonflight placements for 12 airborne running contacts.
- E2 bilateral takeoff keeps four bilateral landings/eight landing foot impacts but removes four unilateral takeoffs. It repeats the earlier optional jump family.
- E3 plain same-leg pogo keeps four unilateral landings with no thigh pulse; the family recurs at a lower dose. Bilateral pogo instead keeps four events but changes them to bilateral: with other defaults, jump/hop total becomes **8 bilateral + 4 unilateral events = 20 foot impacts**. Choose one replacement, not both.
- E4 A-skip preserves four hop landings/four transfer contacts but removes the release task and repeats the Class 1 family. No B-skip performance is recorded when A-skip was used.
- E5 shorter step/depth and E6 shorter reach/more knee bend scale the existing exercise; they do not add entries. Log actual execution range, failed/partial work and extra foot placements.
- Supported unilateral P5 changes working totals to **10 bouts / 56 dynamic reps + 3 cycles**, preparation to **10 bouts / 33 reps + 1 cycle**. A fitted box can replace a bench without creating another exercise; actual support/seat conditions affect comparisons.
