# Force Absorption & Elastic Rebound — complete 12-class curriculum

**Twelve individually finalized classes · Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique.**

Every class preserves the exact **6 explosive / 2 light stabilization-resilience / 6 challenging primary-strength** structure and references the existing **Access & Prepare 1** without creating it. All athlete completion and response remain unknown. This is a progressive written curriculum with readiness gates and hold routes, not evidence that prescribed training occurred.

Use each class’s readiness gate before selecting its listed or replacement route. Do not automatically advance height, distance, sequence length, volume or load with the class number. Primary working loads target about 2–3 technical repetitions in reserve; light work stays easy. Classes are exposures, not consecutive days or weeks.

Sections 2–4 generally require about **71–85 minutes** with prompt equipment access. The existing preparation sequence, equipment queues and extra recovery are additional. A complete session duration cannot be confirmed until those facts are known.
