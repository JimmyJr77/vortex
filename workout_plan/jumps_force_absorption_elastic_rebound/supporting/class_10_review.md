## Stimulus and selection

The limiting factor is preserving organized reversal as a sequence extends from three jumps to four. E3 is the sole candidate volume/sequence change. E2 is reduced to four low pogos to offset two extra vertical landing events. E1 keeps terminal braking, E4 a separately qualified small forward rebound, E5 lateral stopping and E6 unilateral ankle rhythm. The same light and challenging strength roles support the receiving mechanics without an added strength progression.

**Selection findings and revisions:** The audit rejected adding a third set or turning the sequence into a timed rebound test. Two short four-jump sets provide the desired continuity demand while preserving full recovery and a stop rule for the fourth landing. It also rejected retaining Class 9’s conditional height step automatically. The smaller pogo entry remains real, purposeful work rather than a zero-dose slot.

**Readiness decision:** Use the four-jump route only after actual 2 × 3 execution at the chosen fixed amplitude shows 4/4 organized transitions and 2/2 final holds, acceptable subsequent response and current readiness. A height merely prescribed in Class 9 is not qualified. Without evidence, use two sets of three instead. E4 forward rebounds remain independently gated by actual forward-rebound control and recovery; otherwise use the held-forward replacement. Hold strength load and all distances.
