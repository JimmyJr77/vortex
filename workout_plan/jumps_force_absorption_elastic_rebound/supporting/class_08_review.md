## Stimulus and selection

The main result is retaining organized lateral reversal while also keeping a small familiar forward and vertical rebound dose. E3 is the primary directional marker, E4 the stable vertical anchor and E5 the separately gated forward pattern. E1 retains terminal stopping, E2 bilateral ankle rhythm and E6 a small unilateral ankle task. The foot/medial-hip and six primary-strength jobs remain relevant to receiving the same contacts; their doses stay fixed.

**Selection findings and revisions:** The audit distinguished a mixed-direction session from a simple lower contact count: each direction needs its own prior evidence. It retained held-landing replacements instead of assuming the athlete can complete both directions, and added 40 seconds to the upper timing calculation because fully reset holds can take longer than connected pairs. Strength and light work retain their distinct jobs without an added conditioning or balance block.

**Readiness decision:** Use E3 connected lateral bounds only with an actual controlled prior lateral-return exposure and acceptable response; use E5 connected broad jumps only with actual controlled prior forward-rebound practice and acceptable response. Both require current readiness. Qualification in one direction does not qualify the other. Missing evidence selects the corresponding held-landing replacement independently. Keep amplitude, displacement and all strength loads stable; this is consolidation, not another increase.
