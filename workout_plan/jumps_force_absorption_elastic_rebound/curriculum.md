## Exact seven-category framework

### Protracted running

- Endurance
- sustained pace
- pacing control

### Sprinting

- Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)
- Maximum velocity

### Jumps

- Vertical
- Horizontal
- Lateral
- Force absorption
- Elastic rebound

### Agility

- Deceleration
- Change of direction/reacceleration
- Reactive agility and anticipation

### Object control & receiving

- Tracking
- Catching/receiving
- Controlling/manipulating objects

### Upper-body force generation

- Pushing
- Pulling
- Straight-line projection (such as chest passes)

### Rotational explosiveness

- Upper-body emphasis (rotational throws, punches, and swings)
- Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)

Strength, stability, explosiveness and resilience support these performance categories. Body control/tumbling remains separate.

## Design decisions recorded after Class 1

- Start from self-generated landings and short connected jumps. E3 receives three sets while supporting jump entries remain small. This is competent-athlete practice with a readiness condition, not a mandated beginner reset.
- Keep held landing, ankle rebound and multi-joint rebound jobs distinct. Forward and lateral stopping tasks address incoming momentum outside the vertical anchor; the final unilateral pogo entry addresses one-leg ankle return.
- Challenge squat and hinge anchors with two working sets each. Use one set per side for split squat, lateral lunge and standing calf work; give bent-knee calf work two bilateral sets. This limits cumulative impact-plus-eccentric work while preserving all six primary-strength jobs.
- Replace generic trunk/accessory choices with foot and medial-hip strengthening directly related to the receiving base. Do not claim these exact drills prevent injury or independently improve jumping.
- Keep the first class free of external drop height and distance testing. Device data can be added only inside an existing anchor, with capabilities and repeatable measurement conditions verified; no extra test attempts are prescribed.
