## Class 1 prescribed exposure

One landing event is a return to the ground after one jump, hop or bound. A two-foot landing counts as one event, with two limb contacts; a one-foot landing counts as one event with one limb contact. Final settling landings count. The initial standing stance does not. These conventions describe exposure, not an equivalence of tissue load. Failed attempted efforts count; unperformed work is recorded separately.
