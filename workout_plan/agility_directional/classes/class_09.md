# Agility: Directional — Class 9 of 12

**Primary effort:** Prepare the next plant early while linking two planned changes of direction.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and demonstrated readiness for the selected movements. This is a conditional prescription; no athlete completion or improvement has been reported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** E2 is a two-corner Z route. A short W introduces alternating plants, a triangle changes gait between corners, a U route sustains a rounded return, diagonal bounds link forward-moving landings, and a grounded rotational release supplies force transfer without another cut.

**Performance marker within E2:** In E2, record whether braking begins before both corners and whether the second exit remains controlled; score each corner separately rather than treating a fast total time as success. Record attempts actually performed and valid attempts by side, entry intent and faults. Use scheduled attempts only. This drill is unique in the curriculum; its results are not a like-for-like time/distance comparison with a different class.

**Before delivery:** Only link corners when each incoming leg can be slowed under control. With unknown prior response, keep the short routes at moderate entry intent and do not race the second corner. Each attempt is the complete stated route. Review recent sport, gym, other Vortex classes and separately programmed tumbling. Missing feedback never proves tolerance. Use conservative entry speed and familiar controllable loading; do not raise approach speed, cut demand and strength load together.

## 1. Access & Prepare 1

Complete the **existing predetermined Access & Prepare 1**. Its content and duration are not supplied; neither is created or modified here. Include its actual exposures in the coach's full-session review.

## 2. Explosiveness

Use E1–E6 in order. Routes/directions are known before starting; this is directional practice, not reactive anticipation. E2 receives the main practice allocation. All takeoffs, pushes and exits have fast intent within demonstrated control; no maximum entry speed, maximal-distance landings or conditioning races. A continuous route or flight-to-exit is one drill, not a circuit of additional exercises.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **W Drill Forward Cuts — short three-leg route** | **1 × 1 complete route**. Run three 1 m diagonal legs that continue generally forward, changing between left and right diagonals at two cones; stop over 2 m after the last leg. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Look and prepare for the second plant before arriving at it. **Regress:** Lower speed on all three legs if the second plant becomes late; do not add routes to compensate. |
| E2 | **Z Route — Two Square Corners — proposed addition** | **1 × 1 each mirrored route**. Run 1.5 m, cut 90 degrees for 1 m, then cut 90 degrees the opposite way and run 1.5 m; stop over 2 m. Rest **120 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Main two-corner practice: retain a useful body position for the second plant after the first exit. **Regress:** Lower the middle-leg speed if the second corner is rushed or the plant foot reaches too wide. |
| E3 | **Triangle Shuffle-Sprint Drill** | **1 × 1 clockwise and 1 counterclockwise route**. On an equilateral triangle with 1.5 m sides, run the first leg, shuffle the second while facing the centre, then open and run the third; stop over 2 m beyond the start on the final heading. Rest **120 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Change gait while preparing two differently oriented plants. **Regress:** Reduce travel speed if the facing change causes crossed feet at a vertex. |
| E4 | **U-Drill Turn-and-Go** | **1 × 1 each rounded-turn direction**. Run 1.5 m, follow a semicircle of radius 0.5 m and run 1.5 m back on the parallel leg; stop over 2 m. Keep the arc controlled rather than making one hard pivot. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Sustain force around a rounded return when a discrete 180-degree plant is unnecessary. **Regress:** Lower speed around the tight arc if the athlete crosses feet or pivots abruptly. |
| E5 | **Forward Zigzag Bounds — two flights — proposed addition** | **1 × 1 two-bound route each starting leg**. Bound forward-left to the opposite leg, then forward-right to the original leg, or mirror; hold the second landing 2 s. Each flight no more than 0.4 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Redirect lateral force while continuing to gain forward position between plants. **Regress:** Shorten both flights if the first landing cannot support the second push. |
| E6 | **Split-Stance Rotational Scoop Slam — proposed addition** | **1 × 1 each rotation direction**. Hold a light slam ball near the rear hip in a split stance. Drive through the legs and rotate hips/trunk together to release low into the floor 1 m diagonally forward; allow the rear foot to unload and reorient. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Transfer force through a changing torso direction without adding a running plant or jump. **Regress:** Reduce load and rotation if the knee stays fixed while the trunk twists or the arms dominate. |

**Exact setup and counting:**

- **E1:** Mark a forward zigzag with 1 m legs and a clear final stopping line. Both left and right plant directions occur once in the route; no cone touching. Exposure per attempt: 2 discrete cuts and 3 braking episodes; both plant directions included.
- **E2:** Three legs form a Z-like offset route with parallel first/last legs. Mark each corner and reserve the last 3.5 m exit/stop; mirror one route at a time. Exposure per attempt: 2 cuts and 3 braking episodes per route.
- **E3:** Each route travels 4.5 m before the 2 m stop. Only the two intermediate vertices are cuts; the start/finish is a terminal stop, not a third turn. Clear the outgoing stop line beyond the start. Exposure per attempt: 2 cuts and 3 braking episodes per route; no added start-line turn.
- **E4:** Semicircle length about 1.57 m; total path 4.57 m plus 2 m stop. The parallel lanes are 1 m apart inside the cleared bay, not separate occupied facility lanes. Exposure per attempt: Continuous 180-degree heading change; 0 discrete cuts and 1 terminal braking episode counted.
- **E5:** Two small diagonal landing targets in a 3 m × 3 m clear area; no extra approach steps or third bound. Exposure per attempt: 2 unilateral landings, one each leg, per route.
- **E6:** Use a low-bounce ball and clear all impact/retrieval space. If unsuitable, use instead a stationary split-stance band rotational punch with a light band loop held by the rear hand close to the hip, 1 × 2 per side; remove two throws and log four grounded resisted efforts. Exposure per attempt: 1 throw, no intended athlete flight.

**Space and flow:** Use a measured 9 m × 6 m bay with one active route. All short legs, rounded returns and final 2 m stopping corridors fit its declared length; width and escape clearance require measurement. Ball work uses a cleared floor-impact lane and an appropriate low-bounce ball. Lengths include final stopping; do not connect separate lanes into a longer runway. Verify lane width and side clearance on site. One athlete uses the entire active route at a time; close intersecting/neighboring paths as needed. Coach stands outside the plant and exit corridors. Returns occur only after the active attempt finishes, with the next athlete held until the route is clear. For throws, clear the entire flight/impact/retrieval area, throw away from people, let the ball settle and retrieve on the coach's release; do not catch the rebound. If that clearance is unavailable, use the stated replacement within the same slot. Stationary tasks require a clear landing/fall area; smaller travel never proves an unknown width is adequate.

**Quality decisions:** First scheduled attempts calibrate entry intent and count in the dose. After a fault, recover and reduce speed, flight distance or range in the **same** drill on the next scheduled attempt; no make-up attempts. Do not rename a previous drill as a regression to satisfy the unique-drill rule. Stop the task for repeated overrun, loss of knee/foot/pelvis control despite regression, or immediately for pain, dizziness, slipping or an uncontrolled plant. If a required skill/space cannot be established, defer that drill and record incomplete actual work; do not invent successful completion or force the written counts.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

Light/bodyweight strengthening, **at least 5 clean reps in reserve** or an equally easy hold. No shaking or burning; these sets must leave the athlete ready to lift. Rest 30 s between sides, 45 s between these exercises, then 60 s before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Lateral Step-Down — low support, light** | **1 × 4 per stance leg**. Bodyweight first; a light dumbbell only if still easy. Lower **3 s**, lightly touch the free heel, return under control. Rest **30 s between legs; 45 s before S2**. | Control knee/pelvis alignment before repeated plants. Rehearse the plant leg controlling knee travel and pelvic position during weight acceptance. Use the lowest stable support that permits a level pelvis and whole stance-foot contact; use hand support if needed. Reduce step height/load for knee collapse or pelvic drop. |
| S2 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — self-anchored (proposed)** | **1 × 6 per foot**. Sit securely, use the other foot to hold a light loop; turn the working forefoot slightly outward against tension, 2 s return, keep knee still. | Maintain foot-edge control as the travel direction alternates. Use less tension/range for toe gripping or whole-leg rotation. |

## 4. Strength (Primary)

Use challenging, technically controlled working loads with **2–3 good repetitions in reserve**. Isometrics finish with about **5 s of clean holding capacity remaining**, without testing a maximum. Choose load from demonstrated ability and today's preparation sets, not age or a prior written load. Repeated lifts retain their technique/effort standard; recurring work is intentional strength progression, not a repeated explosive drill.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Goblet Squat** | **1 × 6**. Dumbbell held close; lower 3 s through owned range and stand decisively with both feet grounded. Rest 120 s between sets. | Shared knee/hip braking capacity without another high-balance task after linked cuts. Reduce load/depth if the trunk folds or heels lift. |
| P2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, bilateral; **2 s down**, purposeful hip extension; 2–3 RIR. Rest **120 s between working sets**. | Retain hip-hinge strength for repeated exits. Maintain a softly bent, nearly constant knee angle, weights close and a controlled back position; stop lowering at the owned range. |
| P3 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 6 per lunging leg**. step out, lower **2 s**, push back to the original stance; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs**. | Frontal acceptance for the alternating corners. Keep the lunging foot grounded and knee aligned; sit into that hip without rotating away. Reduce width, depth or load for loss of control. |
| P4 | **Glute Bridge Iso Hold — long-lever hamstring variant** | **1 × 20 s**, bilateral. Heels farther from the hips than a standard bridge, knees still bent; bodyweight or a securely hand-held sandbag over the pelvis to reach the holding-effort target. | Knee-flexor support with a small isometric dose. Try to pull the heels toward the body without sliding them; keep ribs down. Bring heels closer or remove load for cramp/lost height; stop for symptoms. |
| P5 | **Copenhagen Side Plank — short lever** | **1 × 15 s per supported top leg**. top knee/lower thigh on a stable bench, lower leg clear. Rest **60 s between sides**. Use bodyweight; if already controlled and too easy, secure a small dumbbell at the upper hip with the upper hand to reach the holding-effort target. | Adductor support through successive side changes. Keep shoulder comfortable and pelvis level. Use light lower-foot assistance if needed; stop for groin/shoulder pain or loss of position. |
| P6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **1 × 8**, bilateral; knees near 90°, forefeet on floor, **1 s at top, 2 s lowering, quiet bottom reset**; 2–3 RIR. Rest **90 s between sets**. | Bent-knee ankle force for low positions at both corners. Secure the weights above the knees, maintain knee angle and repeatable heel rise. Reduce load if heel height falls or bouncing replaces muscle force. |

**Preparation sets, separate from work:** P1: 2 × 4 light then intermediate; P2: 1 × 5 light; P3: 1 × 3 per side light; P4: 1 × 8 s easy; P5: 1 × 5 s per side assisted; P6: 1 × 5 light. These use the same listed exercises; they are not new slots. Allow 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s between preparation rounds, 90 s before the first compound working bout and 60 s for other lifts. Rest at least 90 s between primary exercises. Additional ramps must be logged and extend time; never rush the lift to fit the clock.

**Equipment-fit replacements:** If the step is unsuitable, use instead a hand-supported shallow single-leg squat, the same 1 × 4 per leg and easy effort. Use instead of the band, gentle hand resistance at the outer forefoot with the same easy reps if a loop cannot be secured. If no suitable stable Copenhagen support fits, use instead floor side-lying hip adduction, upper foot on the floor in front, 1 × 8 per lower leg at 2–3 RIR with 60 s between sides; add a small hand-secured dumbbell above the inner knee only if needed and comfortable. Its preparation is 1 × 4 easy reps per leg instead of the hold ramps. This proposed replacement adds 16 dynamic working reps and removes 30 s of working holds; preparation adds 8 reps and removes 10 s of holds. Bouts are unchanged; allow up to 2 extra minutes. For seated calf work, use a securely held sandbag instead of dumbbells when its shape and load fit better. All replacements **use instead of** the original slot, retain its working/preparation dose unless stated otherwise, and must meet the same technical effort target. Record changed implements/support before comparing a strength anchor. No unlisted rack, cable stack, sled, machine or throwing wall is assumed.

**Workload:** 7 locomotor attempts; 29.14 m declared movement paths and up to 14 m additional stopping (some approach paths already contain braking). 10 changes of travel direction; 17 locomotor braking episodes, which are **not foot-contact counts**. 0 bilateral and 4 unilateral flight landings; 2 throws. Keep intensity/flight types separate as described above. Light work: 2 rounds/4 side bouts, 20 reps plus 0 s holds. Primary: **6 rounds/8 bouts**, 32 reps plus 50 s holds. Preparation adds 9 bouts, 24 reps plus 18 s holds. Bilateral bouts count once; per-side work counts both sides. Access & Prepare 1, walking resets and outside training remain unquantified, not zero.

**Timing:** **58–70 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, before extra group queues: E1–E6 approximately 3, 5, 5, 5, 5, 4 minutes; light block 6; primary block 25. This includes attempts, reset/rest, demonstrations, ordinary setup and the stated preparation sets; the upper allowance covers load changes/extended recovery. Full duration remains unknown. Alternate athletes only when actual intervals supply their prescribed rest and the route clears. A coach must model the real class size, equipment quantities and booking; do not compress rests, delete slots or call a partial class complete to fit an assumed window.

**Scheduling:** This loads the legs through plants, landings and lifting. Roughly 48–72 hours before comparably demanding lower-body work is a starting planning interval, adjusted for actual recovery and the full sport/gym/tumbling schedule. A new drill does not mean recovered tissues. No observed response is yet available.

**Progression note:** Introduce linked corners by cutting route lengths and repetitions; the main Z receives two complete routes, four corner practices. Total discrete cuts rise to ten while unilateral landings and strength bouts fall; this is a conditional redistribution, not proof of readiness. Repeated strength anchors: Use one round per lift and a stable goblet squat instead of the supported single-leg hinge emphasis. RDL, lateral lunge, bridge, Copenhagen and soleus keep familiar execution/reserve. Calibrate the goblet load; do not raise other loads with the new route demand. Advance only one justified demand after reported control on both sides, intended effort and acceptable recovery; hold for unknown response, regress for loss of control or overload. Unique drills permit comparison of coaching criteria, not claims of improved test scores between different routes.
