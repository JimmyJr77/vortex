# Agility: Directional — Class 7 of 12

**Primary effort:** Brake for an obtuse exit and accept force after crossover and curved approaches.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and demonstrated readiness for the selected movements. This is a conditional prescription; no athlete completion or improvement has been reported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** E2 uses a planned 135-degree running cut. Low split-stance rebounds support staggered ankle force; a drop-step stop isolates a new receiving base, crossover and curved stops address different incoming paths, and a low rotational bound coordinates foot/hip orientation before landing.

**Performance marker within E2:** For E2, record early braking before the cone, a controlled plant and two steps along the 135-degree return diagonal; distinguish a controlled sharper cut from an uncontrolled pivot or loop. Record attempts actually performed and valid attempts by side, entry intent and faults. Use scheduled attempts only. This drill is unique in the curriculum; its results are not a like-for-like time/distance comparison with a different class.

**Before delivery:** An obtuse angle increases the redirection demand even with a short approach. Use lower entry intent until the athlete demonstrates the required positions; the first attempts count. No athlete response from Class 6 has been reported. Review recent sport, gym, other Vortex classes and separately programmed tumbling. Missing feedback never proves tolerance. Use conservative entry speed and familiar controllable loading; do not raise approach speed, cut demand and strength load together.

## 2. Explosiveness

Use E1–E6 in order. Routes/directions are known before starting; this is directional practice, not reactive anticipation. E2 receives the main practice allocation. All takeoffs, pushes and exits have fast intent within demonstrated control; no maximum entry speed, maximal-distance landings or conditioning races. A continuous route or flight-to-exit is one drill, not a circuit of additional exercises.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Split-Stance Pogo** | **1 × 2 low hops per lead stance**. Feet stay in a short staggered stance; make two very low ankle-driven rebounds, then reset and swap lead stance. Rest **45 s between attempts**; **60 s before the next exercise**. | Produce rapid ankle force from the unequal foot positions often present around a sharper plant. **Regress:** Reduce height/cadence if one knee collapses or the stance widens in flight. |
| E2 | **135-Degree Running Cut — proposed addition** | **2 × 1 per plant leg**. Use a controlled 1.5 m approach, brake before the cone and turn 135 degrees into a 1.5 m diagonal return; stop over 2 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Main early-braking practice when the exit demands substantial reversal of the incoming momentum. **Regress:** Lower entry intent first if the turn becomes a locked-foot pivot; retain the declared route rather than substituting a prior drill. |
| E3 | **Drop-Step Decel Cone Box** | **1 × 1 each opening direction**. From athletic stance, make one quick drop step into a cone-marked rear diagonal base within 0.5 m, reorient the other foot as it unloads and hold 2 s. No crossover or sprint. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Own the receiving base for a rear-diagonal redirection before a running exit is demanded. **Regress:** Use a smaller step if the knee twists or the stance becomes too wide. |
| E4 | **Crossover Run to Stick** | **1 × 1 each travel direction**. Travel laterally with crossover running steps for 2 m, then brake over 2 m into a balanced staggered stop and hold 2 s. Rest **75 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Control incoming momentum after crossed-leg running instead of only after a square shuffle. **Regress:** Shorten strides if legs tangle or the torso keeps rotating during the stop. |
| E5 | **Curved Run Decel** | **1 × 1 each curve direction**. Run a quarter-circle arc of 1.5 m radius at controlled speed, leave on its tangent and stop within 2 m; do not plant-cut at the arc end. Rest **75 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Reduce momentum after a curved entry while controlling lean and pelvis position. **Regress:** Lower arc speed if the athlete leans from the waist or steps across the feet. |
| E6 | **Rotational Bound to Stick** | **1 × 1 each rotation direction**. Push from one leg, make a low short bound within 0.4 m while rotating 90 degrees, land on the opposite leg and hold 2 s. Rest **75 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Align hips and landing foot in the air before accepting a redirected force. **Regress:** Reduce rotation/flight distance if the foot and pelvis arrive out of alignment; log the changed angle. |

**Exact setup and counting:**

- **E1:** Centre of lane with stable staggered base; no stance switch in flight. Exposure per attempt: 2 bilateral landings per stance attempt, 4 total.
- **E2:** Mark the return diagonal 45 degrees off the straight-back line. Clear the 3.5 m exit/stop, 2 m straight-ahead abort and side margins; mirror separately. Exposure per attempt: 1 cut and 2 braking episodes.
- **E3:** A 3 m × 3 m clear area; cones mark the final stance, not a multi-corner course. Exposure per attempt: 1 rapid weight-acceptance stop, 0 running cuts; 0.5 m ground travel ceiling.
- **E4:** Face obliquely across the lane, travel along its length from 2 to 4 and stop by 6; mirror from 8 toward 6 and stop by 4. Exposure per attempt: 1 terminal braking episode, no prescribed jump.
- **E5:** Arc travel is about 2.36 m; mark the radius and tangent stop within the bay. Keep inside/outside margins empty. Exposure per attempt: 1 terminal braking episode; curved heading change is continuous and not logged as a planted cut.
- **E6:** 3 m × 3 m clear landing area, no approach run and no height target; mirror takeoff/landing legs. Exposure per attempt: 1 unilateral landing per direction.

**Space and flow:** E2/E3/E5 require a measured 8 m × 6 m bay, with the return diagonal, curved route and tangent stop individually cleared. For the 135-degree task, the exit and stop head back diagonally, not into the incoming athlete or queue. Other work can follow a cleared lane axis. Lengths include final stopping; do not connect separate lanes into a longer runway. Verify lane width and side clearance on site. One athlete uses the entire active route at a time; close intersecting/neighboring paths as needed. Coach stands outside the plant and exit corridors. Returns occur only after the active attempt finishes, with the next athlete held until the route is clear. For throws, clear the entire flight/impact/retrieval area, throw away from people, let the ball settle and retrieve on the coach's release; do not catch the rebound. If that clearance is unavailable, use the stated replacement within the same slot. Stationary tasks require a clear landing/fall area; smaller travel never proves an unknown width is adequate.

**Quality decisions:** First scheduled attempts calibrate entry intent and count in the dose. After a fault, recover and reduce speed, flight distance or range in the **same** drill on the next scheduled attempt; no make-up attempts. Do not rename a previous drill as a regression to satisfy the unique-drill rule. Stop the task for repeated overrun, loss of knee/foot/pelvis control despite regression, or immediately for pain, dizziness, slipping or an uncontrolled plant. If a required skill/space cannot be established, defer that drill and record incomplete actual work; do not invent successful completion or force the written counts.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

Light/bodyweight strengthening, **at least 5 clean reps in reserve** or an equally easy hold. No shaking or burning; these sets must leave the athlete ready to lift. Rest 30 s between sides, 45 s between these exercises, then 60 s before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Hip Airplane — supported** | **1 × 4 per stance leg**. Bodyweight, hand support and slight hip hinge. Slowly open/close pelvis through a small owned arc without rolling the stance foot. | Control rotation of the pelvis over the planted leg in the obtuse exit. Reduce arc or use more hand support if knee/foot rotates uncontrollably. |
| S2 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — self-anchored (proposed)** | **1 × 6 per foot**. Sit securely, use the other foot to hold a light loop; turn the working forefoot slightly outward against tension, 2 s return, keep knee still. | Maintain foot-edge control during the obtuse plant without adding adductor pre-fatigue. Use less tension/range for toe gripping or whole-leg rotation. |

## 4. Strength (Primary)

Use challenging, technically controlled working loads with **2–3 good repetitions in reserve**. Isometrics finish with about **5 s of clean holding capacity remaining**, without testing a maximum. Choose load from demonstrated ability and today's preparation sets, not age or a prior written load. Repeated lifts retain their technique/effort standard; recurring work is intentional strength progression, not a repeated explosive drill.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 6 per lunging leg**. step out, lower **2 s**, push back to the original stance; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs**. | Frontal-plane loading supports the rear-diagonal push. Keep the lunging foot grounded and knee aligned; sit into that hip without rotating away. Reduce width, depth or load for loss of control. |
| P2 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells, both feet on floor** | **1 × 6 per lead leg**. **3 s down**, smooth forceful rise; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs, 120 s between rounds**. | Maintain knee/hip force through a lowered sharper-cut plant. Keep full front-foot pressure and knee travel aligned with the foot. Reduce load/range for heel lift, knee drift or trunk collapse. |
| P3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6**, bilateral; **2 s down**, purposeful hip extension; 2–3 RIR. Rest **120 s between working sets**. | Maintain hip-hinge capacity without increasing volume as entry shape changes. Maintain a softly bent, nearly constant knee angle, weights close and a controlled back position; stop lowering at the owned range. |
| P4 | **Glute Bridge Iso Hold — long-lever hamstring variant** | **1 × 20 s**, bilateral. Heels farther from the hips than a standard bridge, knees still bent; bodyweight or a securely hand-held sandbag over the pelvis to reach the holding-effort target. | Retain knee-flexor support for braking. | P5 | **Copenhagen Side Plank — short lever** | **1 × 15 s per supported top leg**. top knee/lower thigh on a stable bench, lower leg clear. Rest **60 s between sides**. | P6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **1 × 8**, bilateral; knees near 90°, forefeet on floor, **1 s at top, 2 s lowering, quiet bottom reset**; 2–3 RIR. Rest **90 s between sets**. | Bent-knee ankle force supports the low plant and redirected push. Secure the weights above the knees, maintain knee angle and repeatable heel rise. Reduce load if heel height falls or bouncing replaces muscle force. |

**Preparation sets, separate from work:** P1: 1 × 3 per side light; P2: 2 × 3 per leg, light then intermediate; P3: 1 × 5 light; P4: 1 × 8 s easy; P5: 1 × 5 s per side assisted; P6: 1 × 5 light. These use the same listed exercises; they are not new slots. Allow 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s between preparation rounds, 90 s before the first compound working bout and 60 s for other lifts. Rest at least 90 s between primary exercises. Additional ramps must be logged and extend time; never rush the lift to fit the clock.

**Equipment-fit replacements:** Use instead of the band, gentle hand resistance at the outer forefoot with the same easy reps if a loop cannot be secured. If no suitable stable Copenhagen support fits, use instead floor side-lying hip adduction, upper foot on the floor in front, 1 × 8 per lower leg at 2–3 RIR with 60 s between sides; add a small hand-secured dumbbell above the inner knee only if needed and comfortable. Its preparation is 1 × 4 easy reps per leg instead of the hold ramps. This proposed replacement adds 16 dynamic working reps and removes 30 s of working holds; preparation adds 8 reps and removes 10 s of holds. Bouts are unchanged; allow up to 2 extra minutes. For seated calf work, use a securely held sandbag instead of dumbbells when its shape and load fit better. All replacements **use instead of** the original slot, retain its working/preparation dose unless stated otherwise, and must meet the same technical effort target. Record changed implements/support before comparing a strength anchor. No unlisted rack, cable stack, sled, machine or throwing wall is assumed.

**Workload:** 10 locomotor attempts; 21.72 m declared movement paths and up to 16 m additional stopping (some approach paths already contain braking). 4 changes of travel direction; 14 locomotor braking episodes, which are **not foot-contact counts**. 4 bilateral and 2 unilateral flight landings; 0 throws. Keep intensity/flight types separate as described above. Light work: 2 rounds/4 side bouts, 20 reps plus 0 s holds. Primary: **6 rounds/9 bouts**, 38 reps plus 50 s holds. Preparation adds 11 bouts, 28 reps plus 18 s holds. Bilateral bouts count once; per-side work counts both sides. Access & Prepare 1, walking resets and outside training remain unquantified, not zero.

**Timing:** **62–74 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, before extra group queues: E1–E6 approximately 3, 8, 4, 4, 4, 4 minutes; light block 6; primary block 29. This includes attempts, reset/rest, demonstrations, ordinary setup and the stated preparation sets; the upper allowance covers load changes/extended recovery. Full duration remains unknown. Alternate athletes only when actual intervals supply their prescribed rest and the route clears. A coach must model the real class size, equipment quantities and booking; do not compress rests, delete slots or call a partial class complete to fit an assumed window.

**Scheduling:** This loads the legs through plants, landings and lifting. Roughly 48–72 hours before comparably demanding lower-body work is a starting planning interval, adjusted for actual recovery and the full sport/gym/tumbling schedule. A new drill does not mean recovered tissues. No observed response is yet available.

**Progression note:** Use a new exit angle and entry shapes with controlled speed; reduce true running cuts to four rather than increasing speed and cut count together. Keep rotational flight very small and conditional on alignment. Repeated strength anchors: Return to the reduced familiar Class 5 strength pattern: one round each, target reserve unchanged. No load increase is assumed from the written Class 6. Advance only one justified demand after reported control on both sides, intended effort and acceptable recovery; hold for unknown response, regress for loss of control or overload. Unique drills permit comparison of coaching criteria, not claims of improved test scores between different routes.

**Audit summary:** Counted curved heading change separately from planted direction changes. Limited the rotational bound to two total landings and replaced the extra adductor-light set with easy ankle eversion so Copenhagen work is not preceded by redundant adductor loading. Verified 6 explosive/2 light/6 primary entries, meaningful doses, unique explosive movement identities, separate side/flight/braking accounting and preserved recovery.
