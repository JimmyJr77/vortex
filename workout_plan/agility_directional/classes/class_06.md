# Agility: Directional — Class 6 of 12

**Primary effort:** Control backward momentum and choose a clean forward reversal or open-turn running exit.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and demonstrated readiness for the selected movements. This is a conditional prescription; no athlete completion or improvement has been reported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** E2 is a backpedal-to-forward reversal with facing unchanged. A backward stop isolates braking, sprint-to-backpedal reverses the gait order, a backward landing checks absorption, a drop-step initiation opens a new path, and an open turn converts backward gait into running along the same travel line.

**Performance marker within E2:** For E2, record a controlled backward brake, no heel-crossing or backward fall, and two forward exit steps on the return line. Distinguish this reversal from E6's same-travel-direction open turn. Record attempts actually performed and valid attempts by side, entry intent and faults. Use scheduled attempts only. This drill is unique in the curriculum; its results are not a like-for-like time/distance comparison with a different class.

**Before delivery:** The athlete must backpedal comfortably at short distance and see the coach's setup before moving. Keep backward speed low enough to stop inside the marked zone; no blind pursuit or external surprise cue. Review recent sport, gym, other Vortex classes and separately programmed tumbling. Missing feedback never proves tolerance. Use conservative entry speed and familiar controllable loading; do not raise approach speed, cut demand and strength load together.

## 1. Access & Prepare 1

Complete the **existing predetermined Access & Prepare 1**. Its content and duration are not supplied; neither is created or modified here. Include its actual exposures in the coach's full-session review.

## 2. Explosiveness

Use E1–E6 in order. Routes/directions are known before starting; this is directional practice, not reactive anticipation. E2 receives the main practice allocation. All takeoffs, pushes and exits have fast intent within demonstrated control; no maximum entry speed, maximal-distance landings or conditioning races. A continuous route or flight-to-exit is one drill, not a circuit of additional exercises.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Backpedal-to-Stick** | **1 × 2 attempts**. Backpedal 2 m, brake over 1.5 m and hold a balanced two-foot stance for 2 s. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Isolate stopping backward travel before asking for another exit. **Regress:** Shorten steps/speed if heels cross, torso leans back or vision leaves the coach. |
| E2 | **Backpedal-to-Forward Reversal — proposed addition** | **2 × 1 per first forward push leg**. Face toward 10 m; backpedal 2 m, brake before the turn line and drive forward 2 m without turning the torso; stop over 3 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Main reversal of backward momentum into a forward first step. **Regress:** Slow the backpedal if the final backward step reaches excessively or the restart needs a hop. |
| E3 | **Sprint-to-Backpedal Transition** | **1 × 1 each terminal lead leg**. Run forward 2 m, brake and backpedal 2 m along the return without spinning; stop within 1 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Organize the legs to push backward after forward braking. **Regress:** Reduce approach speed if the athlete turns instead of backpedaling or cannot stop within the return zone. |
| E4 | **Backward Two-Foot Jump to Stick — proposed addition** | **1 × 2 jumps**. Make a small backward two-foot jump within 0.3 m and hold the two-foot landing for 2 s; keep eyes forward. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Accept backward-directed force without allowing the torso to fall behind the feet. **Regress:** Shorten the flight if balance shifts behind the heels. |
| E5 | **Drop-Step Crossover Go** | **1 × 1 each opening side**. Make a small near-leg drop step to open the hips, then cross the far leg and accelerate 2 m; stop over 3 m. Rest **75 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Create room for the hips to turn before applying running force into a rearward exit. **Regress:** Make the opening step smaller if the athlete twists on the support leg. |
| E6 | **Backpedal-to-Sprint Turn — open, same travel heading** | **1 × 1 each opening side**. Backpedal 2 m, slow and open the hips through a half turn, then run another 2 m in the same direction of travel; stop over 2 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Convert a backward gait into a stronger running exit without falsely treating the body turn as a travel reversal. **Regress:** Reduce backpedal speed if feet tangle or the turn pivots on a locked knee. |

**Exact setup and counting:**

- **E1:** Face the 0 m end, start at 2, backpedal to 4 and stop by 5.5; keep the rest of the lane empty. Exposure per attempt: 1 terminal stop, no planned flight.
- **E2:** Start at 5 m, backpedal toward 3, reverse at 3, run to 5 and stop by 8. Keep 0–3 clear for an aborted reversal. Exposure per attempt: 1 travel reversal and 2 braking episodes.
- **E3:** Start at 2 m, run to 4, backpedal to 2 and stop by 1; keep 4–7 clear for an aborted transition. Exposure per attempt: 1 travel reversal, turn braking plus terminal stopping.
- **E4:** Centre of the clear lane; inspect the landing space before starting. Exposure per attempt: 1 bilateral landing.
- **E5:** Set the initial stance oblique to the lane so the declared drop-step/crossover exit runs down its long axis. Start at 2, accelerate to 4, stop by 7. Exposure per attempt: 1 stationary initiation and terminal stop, not an incoming-momentum cut.
- **E6:** Start at 3 m facing 0, backpedal to 5, open to face 10, run to 7 and stop by 9. Keep metre 9–10 clear. Exposure per attempt: 0 travel-direction changes; 1 gait-transition braking phase and 1 final stop.

**Space and flow:** All travel can follow one 10 m lane, mirrored one direction at a time. Keep its entire length clear, including the backward stopping corridor; verify width and side clearance. E5's stationary opening is oriented so the resulting run stays down the lane. Lengths include final stopping; do not connect separate lanes into a longer runway. Verify lane width and side clearance on site. One athlete uses the entire active route at a time; close intersecting/neighboring paths as needed. Coach stands outside the plant and exit corridors. Returns occur only after the active attempt finishes, with the next athlete held until the route is clear. For throws, clear the entire flight/impact/retrieval area, throw away from people, let the ball settle and retrieve on the coach's release; do not catch the rebound. If that clearance is unavailable, use the stated replacement within the same slot. Stationary tasks require a clear landing/fall area; smaller travel never proves an unknown width is adequate.

**Quality decisions:** First scheduled attempts calibrate entry intent and count in the dose. After a fault, recover and reduce speed, flight distance or range in the **same** drill on the next scheduled attempt; no make-up attempts. Do not rename a previous drill as a regression to satisfy the unique-drill rule. Stop the task for repeated overrun, loss of knee/foot/pelvis control despite regression, or immediately for pain, dizziness, slipping or an uncontrolled plant. If a required skill/space cannot be established, defer that drill and record incomplete actual work; do not invent successful completion or force the written counts.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

Light/bodyweight strengthening, **at least 5 clean reps in reserve** or an equally easy hold. No shaking or burning; these sets must leave the athlete ready to lift. Rest 30 s between sides, 45 s between these exercises, then 60 s before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Lateral Step-Down — low support, light** | **1 × 4 per stance leg**. Bodyweight first; a light dumbbell only if still easy. Lower **3 s**, lightly touch the free heel, return under control. Rest **30 s between legs; 45 s before S2**. | Keep the front knee and pelvis controlled as backward steps load the front leg. Rehearse the plant leg controlling knee travel and pelvic position during weight acceptance. Use the lowest stable support that permits a level pelvis and whole stance-foot contact; use hand support if needed. Reduce step height/load for knee collapse or pelvic drop. |
| S2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported standing hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot**. **5 s relaxed reset** between holds, **30 s between feet**, **60 s before P1**. Stand with most weight on the working foot and fingertip support. | Maintain a stable foot base when braking from a backward gait. Strengthen active arch control while maintaining heel, big-toe base and little-toe base contact for planting. Keep toes long; do not roll onto the outside edge. Use bilateral weight support instead if arch control needs toe-clawing or ankle rolling. |

## 4. Strength (Primary)

Use challenging, technically controlled working loads with **2–3 good repetitions in reserve**. Isometrics finish with about **5 s of clean holding capacity remaining**, without testing a maximum. Choose load from demonstrated ability and today's preparation sets, not age or a prior written load. Repeated lifts retain their technique/effort standard; recurring work is intentional strength progression, not a repeated explosive drill.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Reverse Lunge — two dumbbells** | **1 × 5 per returning front leg**. Step backward under control, lower 2 s and drive through the original front foot to return. Keep knee tracking with the foot. Rest 60 s between legs, 120 s between rounds. | Load the front leg while a controlled backward step changes the base, then drive back forward. Reduce step length/load for an uncontrolled backward reach or front-heel lift. |
| P2 | **Hip Thrust — dumbbell** | **1 × 8**. Upper back on a stable bench, dumbbell secured over pelvis; rise to hip extension without arching the back, lower 2 s. Rest 90 s before next exercise. | Support forceful hip extension through the forward restart after a backward stop. Reduce load/range for lumbar extension or hamstring cramp. |
| P3 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 6 per lunging leg**. step out, lower **2 s**, push back to the original stance; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs**. | Maintain frontal control for the open-turn and drop-step exits. Keep the lunging foot grounded and knee aligned; sit into that hip without rotating away. Reduce width, depth or load for loss of control. |
| P4 | **Glute Bridge Iso Hold — long-lever hamstring variant** | **1 × 20 s**, bilateral. Heels farther from the hips than a standard bridge, knees still bent; bodyweight or a securely hand-held sandbag over the pelvis to reach the holding-effort target. | Retain knee-flexor heel-dig support for braking. Try to pull the heels toward the body without sliding them; keep ribs down. Bring heels closer or remove load for cramp/lost height; stop for symptoms. |
| P5 | **Copenhagen Side Plank — short lever** | **1 × 15 s per supported top leg**. top knee/lower thigh on a stable bench, lower leg clear. Rest **60 s between sides**. Use bodyweight; if already controlled and too easy, secure a small dumbbell at the upper hip with the upper hand to reach the holding-effort target. | Adductor support controls pelvis height as the hips open. Keep shoulder comfortable and pelvis level. Use light lower-foot assistance if needed; stop for groin/shoulder pain or loss of position. |
| P6 | **Standing Calf Raise — supported dumbbell** | **1 × 8 per leg**. Nearly straight working knee, light hand support, 1 s at top and 2 s lowering to the floor. Rest 60 s between legs. | Supply plantarflexion force as the forward exit lengthens from the low braking stance. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel rise. |

**Preparation sets, separate from work:** P1: 2 × 3 per side, light then intermediate; P2: 1 × 5 light; P3: 1 × 3 per side light; P4: 1 × 8 s easy; P5: 1 × 5 s per side assisted; P6: 1 × 4 per leg light. These use the same listed exercises; they are not new slots. Allow 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s between preparation rounds, 90 s before the first compound working bout and 60 s for other lifts. Rest at least 90 s between primary exercises. Additional ramps must be logged and extend time; never rush the lift to fit the clock.

**Equipment-fit replacements:** If the step is unsuitable, use instead a hand-supported shallow single-leg squat, the same 1 × 4 per leg and easy effort. If bench geometry is unsuitable, use instead a loaded dynamic floor glute bridge at the same rep/effort and preparation dose. If no suitable stable Copenhagen support fits, use instead floor side-lying hip adduction, upper foot on the floor in front, 1 × 8 per lower leg at 2–3 RIR with 60 s between sides; add a small hand-secured dumbbell above the inner knee only if needed and comfortable. Its preparation is 1 × 4 easy reps per leg instead of the hold ramps. This proposed replacement adds 16 dynamic working reps and removes 30 s of working holds; preparation adds 8 reps and removes 10 s of holds. Bouts are unchanged; allow up to 2 extra minutes. All replacements **use instead of** the original slot, retain its working/preparation dose unless stated otherwise, and must meet the same technical effort target. Record changed implements/support before comparing a strength anchor. No unlisted rack, cable stack, sled, machine or throwing wall is assumed.

**Workload:** 12 locomotor attempts; 40 m declared movement paths and up to 27 m additional stopping (some approach paths already contain braking). 6 changes of travel direction; 20 locomotor braking episodes, which are **not foot-contact counts**. 2 bilateral and 0 unilateral flight landings; 0 throws. Keep intensity/flight types separate as described above. Light work: 2 rounds/4 side bouts, 8 reps plus 30 s holds. Primary: **6 rounds/10 bouts**, 46 reps plus 50 s holds. Preparation adds 12 bouts, 31 reps plus 18 s holds. Bilateral bouts count once; per-side work counts both sides. Access & Prepare 1, walking resets and outside training remain unquantified, not zero.

**Timing:** **67–79 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, before extra group queues: E1–E6 approximately 4, 8, 5, 4, 4, 5 minutes; light block 6; primary block 31. This includes attempts, reset/rest, demonstrations, ordinary setup and the stated preparation sets; the upper allowance covers load changes/extended recovery. Full duration remains unknown. Alternate athletes only when actual intervals supply their prescribed rest and the route clears. A coach must model the real class size, equipment quantities and booking; do not compress rests, delete slots or call a partial class complete to fit an assumed window.

**Scheduling:** This loads the legs through plants, landings and lifting. Roughly 48–72 hours before comparably demanding lower-body work is a starting planning interval, adjusted for actual recovery and the full sport/gym/tumbling schedule. A new drill does not mean recovered tissues. No observed response is yet available.

**Progression note:** Change gait demands while keeping entries at 2 m and all exits short. Only six true travel reversals are prescribed; body turns are not counted as extra cuts. New backward skill does not justify maximal speed. Repeated strength anchors: Keep lateral lunge, bridge and Copenhagen at their existing single-round doses. Introduce one round each of reverse lunge, hip thrust and supported standing calf work for backward-step loading and forward extension; calibrate loads with the listed ramps, not assumed prior ability. Advance only one justified demand after reported control on both sides, intended effort and acceptable recovery; hold for unknown response, regress for loss of control or overload. Unique drills permit comparison of coaching criteria, not claims of improved test scores between different routes.

Omitted a longer backward course and reduced new lift working rounds to avoid combining novelty with volume escalation.
