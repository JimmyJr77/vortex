# Agility: Directional — Class 1 of 12

**Primary effort:** Brake early enough to plant under control, then produce a forceful first exit step. Establish a planned forward-to-backward reversal and carry the same brake–push relationship into lateral movement.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision, and readiness for the selected movements. Class 1 begins this curriculum; it does not imply first-ever training. This is a finalized conditional prescription, not a record of work performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The main demand is reducing incoming momentum before the final plant, then directing force into the new path without a locked-foot twist. The **180-Degree Turn / Shuttle Cut (E2)** is the performance anchor. Other slots isolate forward braking, unilateral lateral absorption, lateral stopping, lateral force reversal, crossover exit mechanics, plant-position control, and the leg/ankle strength needed for those actions. All directions are known before starting; anticipation is outside today's target.

**Quality marker inside E2:** Record each attempt's plant leg, whether braking begins before the turn line, whether the athlete redirects without a recovery hop or uncontrolled knee/trunk movement, and whether the first two exit steps travel down the return path. Record valid attempts out of attempts performed **by plant leg**, plus faults and entry intent. Do not add test attempts or reward a faster entry that ruins the turn. These are coaching observations, not a validated score or evidence of improvement.

## 1. Access & Prepare 1

Complete the **existing predetermined Access & Prepare 1**. Its content and duration are not supplied here; do not create or substitute a preparation sequence. Review its actual lower-body work when assessing the complete day's exposure.

## 2. Explosiveness

Perform E1–E6 in order. Bodyweight throughout. Accelerations and exits have crisp, forceful intent; entry speed and bound distance stay within demonstrated braking/landing control. No maximal-speed approach, races, repeated shuttle conditioning, or forced one-step stops. Each listed travel task is one continuous start-to-finish movement. The walk back is recovery.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Sprint-to-Stick Deceleration — 3 m approach** | **1 × 2 attempts**; 3 m acceleration, up to 2 m braking, 2 s final two-foot hold. Alternate starting lead foot. Rest **60 s between attempts; 90 s before E2**. | Isolate reducing forward momentum before adding a turn. Lower through hips/knees over several braking steps; finish balanced with feet under control. Reduce entry intent if the stop exceeds its zone; stop the drill for repeated overrun or loss of alignment. |
| E2 | **180-Degree Turn / Shuttle Cut — 3 m in, 3 m out** | **3 × 1 per plant leg = 6 attempts**. Alternate right/left plant; rest **90 s after every attempt**, including between rounds and before E3. | **Main task:** coordinate early braking, a planted reversal, and immediate reacceleration. Start braking during the final 1.5 m of approach. Unload/reorient the feet and hips together; push back down the lane. Do not twist over a fixed knee or require all braking from one contact. Use the exact layout below. |
| E3 | **Lateral Bound to Stick — opposite-leg landing** | **1 × 2 per landing leg = 4 bounds**; comfortable distance, 2 s single-leg hold. Reset **30 s between bounds; 60 s before E4**. | Isolate one leg accepting lateral momentum after an explosive push from the other. Push sideways rather than chasing height; keep the landing foot, knee and pelvis controlled. Shorten distance for a free-foot touch, extra hop or trunk collapse; stop if this persists. |
| E4 | **Lateral Shuffle Decel Stick** | **1 × 1 each travel direction = 2 attempts**; 2 m brisk shuffle, up to 2 m stopping, 2 s hold. Rest **60 s between attempts; 90 s before E5**. | Rehearse lateral braking while retaining the original facing direction, without the demand of an immediate return. Push rather than click the feet together; slow before the finish. Lower entry speed for crossed feet, upright collapse or an unstable stop. |
| E5 | **Planned Lateral Shuffle Reversal** — proposed library addition | **2 × 1 each initial direction = 4 attempts**; 2 m out, one reversal, 2 m return, up to 2 m final stopping. Rest **90 s after every attempt**, including before E6. | Use the outer leg to redirect sideways force immediately while the chest keeps its original facing direction. This applies E4's braking to a moving return, distinct from E2's body turn. Begin braking before the reversal cone; do not bounce vertically, cross feet, or chain extra changes. |
| E6 | **Crossover Step and Go** | **1 × 2 each exit direction = 4 attempts**; 3 m acceleration, up to 3 m stopping. Rest **60 s between attempts; 90 s before stabilization**. | Isolate the first running exit from a side-facing stance. Open the hips as the far leg crosses toward the exit, then run down the lane; no preceding drop step or bound. Drive into the exit instead of standing tall or reaching across a fixed knee. Lower speed if feet tangle or the stop becomes rushed. |

**Layout, metres measured from one end of one 10 m lane:** All travel follows that lane's long axis. For lateral work the athlete faces across the lane, so sideways travel still uses its length. The coach declares which end corresponds to the athlete's right/left before each task.

- **E1:** start at 1, accelerate to 4, stop by 6; leave 6–10 clear.
- **E2:** start at 4, turn at 7, accelerate back to 4, stop by 1; leave 0–1 clear. Begin approach braking by 5.5. Keep 7–10 clear as an escape corridor: if unable to turn under control, continue straight into it and stop; abort the turn rather than force it. An abort counts as an attempted exposure.
- **E3:** begin near 5, bound along the lane within a comfortable distance no greater than 1 m; preserve at least 2 m clear beyond either landing target. This is a distance ceiling, not a target every athlete must reach.
- **E4:** start 3 → shuffle to 5 → stop by 7; mirror as 7 → 5 → 3.
- **E5:** start 4 → reverse at 6 → drive to 4 → stop by 2; mirror as 6 → 4 → 6 → 8. The reversal is a direction change, not a required static pause.
- **E6:** start 2 → accelerate to 5 → stop by 8; mirror as 8 → 5 → 2. Choose a controlled speed compatible with the final 3 m.

One active athlete per cleared movement zone. Lane width, side clearance, queue location and coach position still require on-site verification. Hold neighboring lanes clear if needed for a missed plant or lateral fall. Walk back only after the active attempt ends; release the next athlete after the lane and return are clear. Do not assume three simultaneous stations or a continuous 30 m runway.

**Quality decisions:** The first scheduled E1/E4 attempts establish today's entry intent; they count toward the dose. For E2/E5, use a slower approach within the same task if braking or redirection is inconsistent. Retain forceful exit intent only while control remains. After a quality fault, rest, lower the relevant demand and use the next scheduled attempt; do not add make-up attempts. Stop a task after a repeated fault despite regression, or immediately for pain, dizziness, slipping or an uncontrolled plant. Record the actual reduced dose; the written 6/2/6 architecture never obliges an athlete to complete unsuitable work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

Keep both tasks easy: bodyweight or a very light load, approximately **5 or more clean repetitions in reserve**, with no shaking, burning or pre-fatigue. These are controlled strengthening tasks.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Lateral Step-Down — low support, light** | **1 × 4 per stance leg**. Bodyweight first; a light dumbbell only if still easy. Lower **3 s**, lightly touch the free heel, return under control. Rest **30 s between legs; 45 s before S2**. | Rehearse the plant leg controlling knee travel and pelvic position during weight acceptance. Use the lowest stable support that permits a level pelvis and whole stance-foot contact; use hand support if needed. Reduce step height/load for knee collapse or pelvic drop. |
| S2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported standing hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot**; **5 s relaxed reset** between holds, **30 s between feet**, **60 s before P1**. Stand with most weight on the working foot and fingertip support. | Strengthen active arch control while maintaining heel, big-toe base and little-toe base contact for planting. Keep toes long; do not roll onto the outside edge. Use bilateral weight support instead if arch control needs toe-clawing or ankle rolling. |

## 4. Strength (Primary)

Working sets below are **challenging strength work**, separate from the light block. Use approximately **2–3 technically sound reps in reserve (RIR)**; for isometrics, select a position/load leaving about **5 s of clean holding capacity**, without testing a maximum. The coach checks the athlete's effort report against movement quality. Use controlled lowering and a purposeful ascent; no grinding, forced reps or fatigue circuits. A slower strength descent trains capacity; it does not imitate the speed of a cut.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells, both feet on floor** | **2 × 6 per lead leg**. **3 s down**, smooth forceful rise; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs, 120 s between rounds**. | Main strength investment in the lead leg's knee/hip force through a flexed plant position. Keep full front-foot pressure; allow controlled knee travel in line with the foot and return to the same stance. Reduce load/range for heel lift, knee drift or trunk collapse. |
| P2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6** bilateral; **2 s down**, purposeful hip extension; 2–3 RIR. Rest **120 s between working sets**. | Load hip extension from a flexed-hip position while the posterior thigh resists the hinge. This complements the more knee-dominant split squat and supports hip force in braking and exit steps. Maintain soft, nearly constant knee bend, weights close and a controlled back position; stop the descent at the owned range. |
| P3 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 6 per lunging leg**; step out, lower **2 s**, push back to the original stance; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs**. | Load frontal-plane acceptance and the push back from a lateral plant. Keep the lunging foot grounded and knee tracking over it; sit into that hip without rotating away. One set per side limits overlap with the explosive lateral work. Reduce step width/range/load if control is lost. |
| P4 | **Glute Bridge Iso Hold — long-lever hamstring variant** | **1 × 20 s** bilateral. Heels farther from the hips than a standard bridge, knees still bent; bodyweight or a securely hand-held sandbag over the pelvis to reach the holding-effort target. | Give the knee flexors a sustained heel-dig task while holding hip extension, distinct from P2's moving hip hinge. Try to pull the heels toward the body without allowing them to slide; keep ribs down. Bring heels closer/remove load for cramp or lost height; stop for symptoms. |
| P5 | **Copenhagen Side Plank — short lever** | **1 × 15 s per supported top leg**; top knee/lower thigh on a stable bench, lower leg clear. Rest **60 s between sides**. Use bodyweight; if already controlled and too easy, secure a small dumbbell at the upper hip with the upper hand to reach the holding-effort target. | Directly load adductor support and resistance to sideways pelvic drop at the plant. This isolates a supporting function that P3's whole-leg push does not. Keep shoulder comfortable and pelvis level. Use light lower-foot assistance instead if needed; stop for groin/shoulder pain or loss of position. |
| P6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **2 × 8** bilateral; knees near 90°, forefeet on floor, **1 s at top, 2 s lowering, quiet bottom reset**; 2–3 RIR. Rest **90 s between sets**. | Load ankle plantarflexion with bent knees for shin control and push-off from a lowered braking stance. Hold the dumbbells securely just above the knees; maintain knee angle and repeatable heel rise. Lower load if heel height shrinks or bouncing replaces muscle effort. |

**Preparation sets, before each corresponding exercise:** P1: **2 × 3 per leg** (bodyweight/light, then intermediate); P2: **1 × 5** light; P3: **1 × 3 per side** light; P4: **1 × 8 s** easy; P5: **1 × 5 s per side** assisted/easy; P6: **1 × 5** light. They are preparation for these same six exercises, not extra slots. Allow 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s between P1 preparation rounds, and 90 s from the last preparation bout to P1/P2 working work, 60 s for P3–P6. Add another same-exercise ramp only if required for load selection; log it and extend time. Rest at least **90 s between primary exercises**; setup may supply that rest when the athlete is actually recovering.

**Equipment-fit replacements:** All are **use instead of**, never extra work. If no suitably low step is available for S1, use a hand-supported shallow single-leg squat to the same controllable depth, 1 × 4 per leg at the same easy effort (proposed replacement). If no comfortable Copenhagen bench setup is available, use floor side-lying hip adduction, upper leg bent with its foot supported in front, **1 × 8 per lower leg, 2–3 RIR**, 60 s between sides; add a light hand-secured dumbbell above the inner knee only if needed and comfortable (proposed replacement). For that replacement, use **1 × 4 easy reps per leg instead of P5's preparation holds**. P6 may use a securely held sandbag instead of dumbbells. Verify bench stability and loading fit; do not improvise unstable plate stacks. Log all substitutions. If suitable resistance cannot produce challenging controlled work, that primary prescription remains unsuitable for that athlete until adapted.

**Timing and recovery:** Budget **78–90 minutes for the prescribed work after Access & Prepare 1**, for an individually paced athlete with equipment ready and no queue delays: about 34 minutes explosive work, 6 stabilization, 38 primary including ramps, rests, instruction and transitions, plus up to 12 minutes for load changes/extended recovery. Full class duration remains unknown because Access & Prepare 1 duration, class size, staffing and booking are unconfirmed. Groups may take longer; see the delivery audit. Do not shorten recovery to fit an assumed booking.

Treat this as a demanding lower-body exposure despite modest jump count. Review sport, gym, other Vortex classes and separately programmed tumbling; the saved Vertical Jumps prescription may overlap but is not evidence of completed work. Roughly 48–72 hours before comparable lower-body work is a planning starting point only, adjusted to actual loading and readiness. Do not progress through pain, persistent soreness, poor coordination or an unreviewed competition load.
