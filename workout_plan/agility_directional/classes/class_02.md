# Agility: Directional — Class 2 of 12

**Primary effort:** Compress into a lateral plant and drive from a shuffle into a running exit.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and demonstrated readiness for the selected movements. This is a conditional prescription; no athlete completion or improvement has been reported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** E2 links lateral braking to forward acceleration. Short elastic foot contacts, an open first step, two-foot lateral acceptance, one rebound return and a grounded lateral ball projection each isolate a different contributor. The strength focus is frontal-plane acceptance supported by hip and ankle force.

**Performance marker within E2:** On each shuffle-to-sprint attempt, note whether the athlete slows the lateral approach before opening the hips and sends the first two running steps down the exit, without an upright hop. Record attempts actually performed and valid attempts by side, entry intent and faults. Use scheduled attempts only. This drill is unique in the curriculum; its results are not a like-for-like time/distance comparison with a different class.

**Before delivery:** Confirm stable lateral landings and the ability to open the hips without twisting on a fixed knee. Use a low-amplitude rebound only when the first bound lands under control; otherwise shorten both bounds without adding a new drill. Review recent sport, gym, other Vortex classes and separately programmed tumbling. Missing feedback never proves tolerance. Use conservative entry speed and familiar controllable loading; do not raise approach speed, cut demand and strength load together.

## 2. Explosiveness

Use E1–E6 in order. Routes/directions are known before starting; this is directional practice, not reactive anticipation. E2 receives the main practice allocation. All takeoffs, pushes and exits have fast intent within demonstrated control; no maximum entry speed, maximal-distance landings or conditioning races. A continuous route or flight-to-exit is one drill, not a circuit of additional exercises.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Lateral Line Pogo** | **1 × 6 low continuous hops**. Both feet take off and land together, alternating across a line by only a few centimetres. Rest **45 s between attempts**; **60 s before the next exercise**. | Brief ankle-driven force reversals before larger lateral plants; keep the support dose very small. **Regress:** Reduce excursion and cadence if contacts become loud or posture folds. |
| E2 | **Shuffle-to-Sprint Transition** | **2 × 1 each initial shuffle direction**. Shuffle 1 m, brake before the corner, open the hips and accelerate 2 m forward; stop over the next 3 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Main lateral-to-forward force transfer; change locomotion and direction without jumping upright. **Regress:** Slow the shuffle if feet cross early or the turn becomes a locked-foot pivot. |
| E3 | **Lateral Open-Step to Sprint** | **1 × 1 each exit direction**. From a side-facing athletic stance, the near leg opens toward the exit first; accelerate 2 m, stop over 3 m. No crossover first step. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Isolate a direct hip-opening exit when crossing the legs would delay the push. **Regress:** Use a smaller opening step and slower first attempt if the planted knee twists. |
| E4 | **Lateral Hop to Stick — bilateral** | **1 × 1 each direction**. Two-foot lateral takeoff and landing, comfortable travel no more than 0.5 m; hold 2 s. Rest **45 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Accept lateral force through a shared base while keeping pelvis and trunk organized. **Regress:** Shorten travel if either foot lands markedly earlier or the knees collapse inward. |
| E5 | **Skater Bound Continuous — one out-and-back pair** | **1 × 1 pair each initial direction**. Bound laterally onto the opposite leg, reverse immediately for one return bound, then hold 2 s. Each flight stays within 0.5 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Practice absorption turning into another lateral push rather than ending every landing in stillness. **Regress:** Reduce both distances; stop if the first landing cannot support the return without a recovery step. |
| E6 | **Grounded Lateral Scoop Slam — proposed addition** | **1 × 2 releases each direction**. Use a light slam ball; shift from the outside hip and drive it low and diagonally down into the floor 1 m to the side. Feet stay grounded. Rest **45 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Express lateral leg-to-trunk force against an external load without adding landing contacts. **Regress:** Use a lighter ball and shorter release if arm effort replaces the lateral hip drive. |

**Exact setup and counting:**

- **E1:** Use the middle of a cleared lane; no obstacle height. Finish the sixth landing and reset. Exposure per attempt: 6 low-amplitude bilateral landings; final landing included.
- **E2:** Set a 1 m lateral approach perpendicular to a 2 m exit plus 3 m stop. Mirror one side at a time within the stated bay. Exposure per attempt: 1 direction change, 1 turn-braking phase and 1 terminal stop; no prescribed jump.
- **E3:** Start near metre 2 and drive toward 4, stopping by 7; mirror from 8 toward 6, stopping by 3. Exposure per attempt: One stationary initiation and terminal stop; no incoming-momentum cut.
- **E4:** Centre of lane, facing across it so lateral travel follows its length; clear 2 m beyond either landing. Exposure per attempt: 1 bilateral landing per attempt.
- **E5:** One cleared lane, centre near 5 m; 2 flights only, no continuing skater circuit. Exposure per attempt: 2 unilateral landings, one on each leg, per pair; flight reversal is not logged as a running cut.
- **E6:** Orient the throw along the lane, release into an empty 1 m target area and clear all bounce/retrieval space. Use an appropriate low-bounce ball. If clearance/floor use is unsuitable, use instead Stationary Lateral Push-Start Shuffle: 1 × 2 each direction, push from stillness into 2 m shuffle and stop over 2 m; rest 60 s between attempts. This proposed replacement starts square with no crossover or hip-opening run. Exposure per attempt: 1 ball release, no intended athlete flight. Replacement removes 4 throws and adds 4 shuffle attempts, 8 m travel plus up to 8 m stopping, and 4 terminal braking episodes; allow up to 2 extra minutes.

**Space and flow:** E2 requires a measured 8 m × 4 m clear bay: 1 m lateral entry, 2 m forward exit and 3 m final stop, with side/escape margins. Its transverse width is a requirement, not confirmed by the lane inventory. Other travel and the ball projection can be oriented along one 10 m lane. Lengths include final stopping; do not connect separate lanes into a longer runway. Verify lane width and side clearance on site. One athlete uses the entire active route at a time; close intersecting/neighboring paths as needed. Coach stands outside the plant and exit corridors. Returns occur only after the active attempt finishes, with the next athlete held until the route is clear. For throws, clear the entire flight/impact/retrieval area, throw away from people, let the ball settle and retrieve on the coach's release; do not catch the rebound. If that clearance is unavailable, use the stated replacement within the same slot. Stationary tasks require a clear landing/fall area; smaller travel never proves an unknown width is adequate.

**Quality decisions:** First scheduled attempts calibrate entry intent and count in the dose. After a fault, recover and reduce speed, flight distance or range in the **same** drill on the next scheduled attempt; no make-up attempts. Do not rename a previous drill as a regression to satisfy the unique-drill rule. Stop the task for repeated overrun, loss of knee/foot/pelvis control despite regression, or immediately for pain, dizziness, slipping or an uncontrolled plant. If a required skill/space cannot be established, defer that drill and record incomplete actual work; do not invent successful completion or force the written counts.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

Light/bodyweight strengthening, **at least 5 clean reps in reserve** or an equally easy hold. No shaking or burning; these sets must leave the athlete ready to lift. Rest 30 s between sides, 45 s between these exercises, then 60 s before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Mini-Band Lateral Walk** | **1 × 4 steps each way**. Very light loop above knees; small quiet steps, toes forward and knees aligned. Both directions are one set round. | Control the hip and knee during a lateral plant before a running exit. Use a lighter band/smaller step if pelvis sways or the task burns. |
| S2 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — self-anchored (proposed)** | **1 × 6 per foot**. Sit securely, use the other foot to hold a light loop; turn the working forefoot slightly outward against tension, 2 s return, keep knee still. | Keep the outside foot edge controlled when the lateral approach is braked. Use less tension/range for toe gripping or whole-leg rotation. |

## 4. Strength (Primary)

Use challenging, technically controlled working loads with **2–3 good repetitions in reserve**. Isometrics finish with about **5 s of clean holding capacity remaining**, without testing a maximum. Choose load from demonstrated ability and today's preparation sets, not age or a prior written load. Repeated lifts retain their technique/effort standard; recurring work is intentional strength progression, not a repeated explosive drill.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 6 per lunging leg**. step out, lower **2 s**, push back to the original stance; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs**. | Main frontal-plane acceptance and push-back strength for the shuffle plant. Keep the lunging foot grounded and knee aligned; sit into that hip without rotating away. Reduce width, depth or load for loss of control. |
| P2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6**, bilateral; **2 s down**, purposeful hip extension; 2–3 RIR. Rest **120 s between working sets**. | Retain hip-hinge force for driving the first running exit. Maintain a softly bent, nearly constant knee angle, weights close and a controlled back position; stop lowering at the owned range. |
| P3 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells, both feet on floor** | **1 × 6 per lead leg**. **3 s down**, smooth forceful rise; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs, 120 s between rounds**. | A small lead-leg knee/hip dose supports the new forward step after lateral braking. Keep full front-foot pressure and knee travel aligned with the foot. Reduce load/range for heel lift, knee drift or trunk collapse. |
| P4 | **Glute Bridge Iso Hold — long-lever hamstring variant** | **1 × 20 s**, bilateral. Heels farther from the hips than a standard bridge, knees still bent; bodyweight or a securely hand-held sandbag over the pelvis to reach the holding-effort target. | Heel-dig knee-flexor support complements the moving hinge without another heavy knee-dominant set. Try to pull the heels toward the body without sliding them; keep ribs down. Bring heels closer or remove load for cramp/lost height; stop for symptoms. |
| P5 | **Copenhagen Side Plank — short lever** | **1 × 15 s per supported top leg**. top knee/lower thigh on a stable bench, lower leg clear. Rest **60 s between sides**. Use bodyweight; if already controlled and too easy, secure a small dumbbell at the upper hip with the upper hand to reach the holding-effort target. | Adductor support limits unwanted sideways pelvic drop in the lateral plant. Keep shoulder comfortable and pelvis level. Use light lower-foot assistance if needed; stop for groin/shoulder pain or loss of position. |
| P6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **2 × 8**, bilateral; knees near 90°, forefeet on floor, **1 s at top, 2 s lowering, quiet bottom reset**; 2–3 RIR. Rest **90 s between sets**. | Bent-knee ankle force supports lateral compression and the first push. Secure the weights above the knees, maintain knee angle and repeatable heel rise. Reduce load if heel height falls or bouncing replaces muscle force. |

**Preparation sets, separate from work:** P1: 1 × 3 per side light; P2: 1 × 5 light; P3: 2 × 3 per leg, light then intermediate; P4: 1 × 8 s easy; P5: 1 × 5 s per side assisted; P6: 1 × 5 light. These use the same listed exercises; they are not new slots. Allow 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s between preparation rounds, 90 s before the first compound working bout and 60 s for other lifts. Rest at least 90 s between primary exercises. Additional ramps must be logged and extend time; never rush the lift to fit the clock.

**Equipment-fit replacements:** Use instead of the band, gentle hand resistance at the outer forefoot with the same easy reps if a loop cannot be secured. If no suitable stable Copenhagen support fits, use instead floor side-lying hip adduction, upper foot on the floor in front, 1 × 8 per lower leg at 2–3 RIR with 60 s between sides; add a small hand-secured dumbbell above the inner knee only if needed and comfortable. Its preparation is 1 × 4 easy reps per leg instead of the hold ramps. This proposed replacement adds 16 dynamic working reps and removes 30 s of working holds; preparation adds 8 reps and removes 10 s of holds. Bouts are unchanged; allow up to 2 extra minutes. For seated calf work, use a securely held sandbag instead of dumbbells when its shape and load fit better. All replacements **use instead of** the original slot, retain its working/preparation dose unless stated otherwise, and must meet the same technical effort target. Record changed implements/support before comparing a strength anchor. No unlisted rack, cable stack, sled, machine or throwing wall is assumed.

**Workload:** 6 locomotor attempts; 16 m declared movement paths and up to 18 m additional stopping (some approach paths already contain braking). 4 changes of travel direction; 10 locomotor braking episodes, which are **not foot-contact counts**. 8 bilateral and 4 unilateral flight landings; 4 throws. Keep intensity/flight types separate as described above. Light work: 2 rounds/4 side bouts, 20 reps plus 0 s holds. Primary: **8 rounds/11 bouts**, 52 reps plus 50 s holds. Preparation adds 11 bouts, 28 reps plus 18 s holds. Bilateral bouts count once; per-side work counts both sides. Access & Prepare 1, walking resets and outside training remain unquantified, not zero.

**Timing:** **71–83 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, before extra group queues: E1–E6 approximately 3, 8, 4, 4, 5, 6 minutes; light block 6; primary block 35. This includes attempts, reset/rest, demonstrations, ordinary setup and the stated preparation sets; the upper allowance covers load changes/extended recovery. Full duration remains unknown. Alternate athletes only when actual intervals supply their prescribed rest and the route clears. A coach must model the real class size, equipment quantities and booking; do not compress rests, delete slots or call a partial class complete to fit an assumed window.

**Scheduling:** This loads the legs through plants, landings and lifting. Roughly 48–72 hours before comparably demanding lower-body work is a starting planning interval, adjusted for actual recovery and the full sport/gym/tumbling schedule. A new drill does not mean recovered tissues. No observed response is yet available.

**Progression note:** New lateral transitions replace the earlier reversals; they do not imply a faster entry was earned. Running attempts drop while modest bilateral elastic/landing work is introduced; these loads are tracked separately. Repeated strength anchors: Retain the same RIR and technique on lateral lunge, RDL, split squat, bridge, Copenhagen and soleus. Reduce split squat to one round per side because the new rebound work adds lateral acceptance. No load increase is prescribed without actual feedback. Advance only one justified demand after reported control on both sides, intended effort and acceptable recovery; hold for unknown response, regress for loss of control or overload. Unique drills permit comparison of coaching criteria, not claims of improved test scores between different routes.

Grounded ball work replaces another jump exposure; no throwing wall is assumed. Verified 6 explosive/2 light/6 primary entries, meaningful doses, unique explosive movement identities, separate side/flight/braking accounting and preserved recovery.
