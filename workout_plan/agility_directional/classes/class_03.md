# Agility: Directional — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Redirect into a planned 45-degree exit while keeping the plant and first step aligned.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and demonstrated readiness for the selected movements. This is a conditional prescription; no athlete completion or improvement has been reported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** E2 is the diagonal running-cut task. A paused split-stance jump supplies directed push, a cut-to-stick isolates the terminal position, a crossover bound exposes crossing force acceptance, a split-step links a low landing to an exit, and a same-leg diagonal hop isolates single-leg absorption.

**Performance marker within E2:** Count E2 attempts where braking begins before the cone, the plant knee follows the foot and the first two steps remain on the 45-degree exit line; note left/right plant separately. Record attempts actually performed and valid attempts by side, entry intent and faults. Use scheduled attempts only. This drill is unique in the curriculum; its results are not a like-for-like time/distance comparison with a different class.

**Before delivery:** Confirm diagonal single-leg landing control and a clean low split-step before adding speed. Without reported response from Class 2, use modest approach intent and short flight distances. Review recent sport, gym, other Vortex classes and separately programmed tumbling. Missing feedback never proves tolerance. Use conservative entry speed and familiar controllable loading; do not raise approach speed, cut demand and strength load together.

Complete the **existing predetermined Access & Prepare 1**. Its content and duration are not supplied; neither is created or modified here. Include its actual exposures in the coach's full-session review.

## 2. Explosiveness

Use E1–E6 in order. Routes/directions are known before starting; this is directional practice, not reactive anticipation. E2 receives the main practice allocation. All takeoffs, pushes and exits have fast intent within demonstrated control; no maximum entry speed, maximal-distance landings or conditioning races. A continuous route or flight-to-exit is one drill, not a circuit of additional exercises.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Paused Split-Stance Diagonal Jump — proposed addition** | **1 × 1 per lead leg**. Hold a shallow split stance for 2 s, push diagonally forward within 0.5 m and land on both feet for a 2 s hold. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Produce a directed first push from stillness without relying on an incoming run. **Regress:** Shorten flight and stance if the rear leg catches up awkwardly. |
| E2 | **45-Degree Cut and Reaccelerate** | **2 × 1 per plant leg**. Run in 2 m, brake before the cone, cut 45 degrees and accelerate 2 m; stop within another 2.5 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Main early-brake-to-diagonal-drive practice. **Regress:** Lower approach speed if the athlete plants too far outside the body or drifts off the exit. |
| E3 | **45-Degree Cut-and-Stick** | **1 × 1 per plant leg**. Use a 1.5 m brisk entry, redirect through 45 degrees for 1 m and stop in a balanced staggered stance for 2 s; no sprint-out. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Own the diagonal receiving position separately from the main drill's running exit. **Regress:** Shorten the entry and lower its speed if the stop needs a recovery hop. |
| E4 | **Crossover Bound to Stick** | **1 × 1 each crossing direction**. From side-facing stance, cross the far leg to load it, then bound a short diagonal onto the other leg and hold 2 s. One flight only. Rest **75 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Accept the diagonal force produced after the hips cross the original base. **Regress:** Reduce the crossover width and bound distance if the pelvis rotates beyond control. |
| E5 | **Split-Step to Diagonal Sprint — proposed addition** | **1 × 1 each exit direction**. Make a very low two-foot split-step, land with feet under the hips and immediately push into a known 45-degree 2 m exit; stop over 2.5 m. Rest **90 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Turn a small preparatory landing into the first diagonal running step. **Regress:** Lower hop height and exit speed if the athlete sinks or adds a recovery jump. |
| E6 | **Single-Leg Diagonal Hop to Stick — proposed addition** | **1 × 1 per leg**. Hop diagonally forward no more than 0.4 m from one leg to that same leg; hold 2 s. Rest **60 s between attempts**; **90 s before the next exercise**. | Check and strengthen the rapid same-leg acceptance needed when the cut leg must support the body again. **Regress:** Shorten the hop for toe clawing, knee drift or free-foot touchdown. |

**Exact setup and counting:**

- **E1:** Clear a 3 m × 3 m landing area; mirror the lead leg and exit diagonal. No run-up. Exposure per attempt: 1 bilateral landing.
- **E2:** Use the stated bay; mark the incoming line, 45-degree exit, end of acceleration and end of stopping. Keep the straight-ahead abort corridor empty. Exposure per attempt: 1 cut, 1 turn-braking phase and 1 final stop.
- **E3:** Same bay but a separate short cone route. The 1 m outgoing leg includes its final stopping; keep a further 2 m clear beyond it. Exposure per attempt: 1 direction change, approach braking and terminal stopping; no flight prescribed.
- **E4:** One crossover ground step within 0.5 m; flight within 0.5 m in a 3 m × 3 m clear area. Record the ground step separately from flight distance. Exposure per attempt: 1 unilateral landing; one controlled ground entry step, not a sprint.
- **E5:** Place the stationary split-step at the cut point and use the clear diagonal exit corridor; no approach run or added hop. Exposure per attempt: 1 low bilateral landing, one running exit and final stop; no incoming-momentum cut.
- **E6:** 3 m × 3 m clear landing area; choose the diagonal that moves away from the free leg, then mirror. Exposure per attempt: 1 unilateral landing per leg.

**Space and flow:** The running cuts require an 8 m × 6 m cleared two-dimensional bay including run-off; this width is unconfirmed and must be measured. Mirror routes one at a time. The final 2.5 m stopping corridor continues along the exit heading, not back toward the approach. Lengths include final stopping; do not connect separate lanes into a longer runway. Verify lane width and side clearance on site. One athlete uses the entire active route at a time; close intersecting/neighboring paths as needed. Coach stands outside the plant and exit corridors. Returns occur only after the active attempt finishes, with the next athlete held until the route is clear. For throws, clear the entire flight/impact/retrieval area, throw away from people, let the ball settle and retrieve on the coach's release; do not catch the rebound. If that clearance is unavailable, use the stated replacement within the same slot. Stationary tasks require a clear landing/fall area; smaller travel never proves an unknown width is adequate.

**Quality decisions:** First scheduled attempts calibrate entry intent and count in the dose. After a fault, recover and reduce speed, flight distance or range in the **same** drill on the next scheduled attempt; no make-up attempts. Do not rename a previous drill as a regression to satisfy the unique-drill rule. Stop the task for repeated overrun, loss of knee/foot/pelvis control despite regression, or immediately for pain, dizziness, slipping or an uncontrolled plant. If a required skill/space cannot be established, defer that drill and record incomplete actual work; do not invent successful completion or force the written counts.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

Light/bodyweight strengthening, **at least 5 clean reps in reserve** or an equally easy hold. No shaking or burning; these sets must leave the athlete ready to lift. Rest 30 s between sides, 45 s between these exercises, then 60 s before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Hip Airplane — supported** | **1 × 4 per stance leg**. Bodyweight, hand support and slight hip hinge. Slowly open/close pelvis through a small owned arc without rolling the stance foot. | Control pelvis rotation over the planted leg before directing the diagonal exit. Reduce arc or use more hand support if knee/foot rotates uncontrollably. |
| S2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported standing hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot**. **5 s relaxed reset** between holds, **30 s between feet**, **60 s before P1**. Stand with most weight on the working foot and fingertip support. | Retain active foot pressure during an oblique plant. Strengthen active arch control while maintaining heel, big-toe base and little-toe base contact for planting. Keep toes long; do not roll onto the outside edge. Use bilateral weight support instead if arch control needs toe-clawing or ankle rolling. |

## 4. Strength (Primary)

Use challenging, technically controlled working loads with **2–3 good repetitions in reserve**. Isometrics finish with about **5 s of clean holding capacity remaining**, without testing a maximum. Choose load from demonstrated ability and today's preparation sets, not age or a prior written load. Repeated lifts retain their technique/effort standard; recurring work is intentional strength progression, not a repeated explosive drill.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|

Rest **60 s between legs, 120 s between rounds**. | Lead-leg knee/hip force for the 45-degree plant and first exit. Keep full front-foot pressure and knee travel aligned with the foot. Reduce load/range for heel lift, knee drift or trunk collapse. |
| P2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6**, bilateral; **2 s down**, purposeful hip extension; 2–3 RIR. Rest **120 s between working sets**. | Retain posterior hip force while the torso inclines into the new path. | P3 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 6 per lunging leg**. step out, lower **2 s**, push back to the original stance; 2–3 RIR. Rest **60 s between legs**. | Frontal acceptance supports the lateral component of the diagonal cut. Keep the lunging foot grounded and knee aligned; sit into that hip without rotating away. | P4 | **Glute Bridge Iso Hold — long-lever hamstring variant** | **1 × 20 s**, bilateral. | P6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **2 × 8**, bilateral; knees near 90°, forefeet on floor, **1 s at top, 2 s lowering, quiet bottom reset**; 2–3 RIR. **Preparation sets, separate from work:** P1: 2 × 3 per leg, light then intermediate; P2: 1 × 5 light; P3: 1 × 3 per side light; P4: 1 × 8 s easy; P5: 1 × 5 s per side assisted; P6: 1 × 5 light. These use the same listed exercises; they are not new slots. Allow 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s between preparation rounds, 90 s before the first compound working bout and 60 s for other lifts. Rest at least 90 s between primary exercises. Additional ramps must be logged and extend time; never rush the lift to fit the clock.

**Equipment-fit replacements:** If no suitable stable Copenhagen support fits, use instead floor side-lying hip adduction, upper foot on the floor in front, 1 × 8 per lower leg at 2–3 RIR with 60 s between sides; add a small hand-secured dumbbell above the inner knee only if needed and comfortable. Its preparation is 1 × 4 easy reps per leg instead of the hold ramps. This proposed replacement adds 16 dynamic working reps and removes 30 s of working holds; preparation adds 8 reps and removes 10 s of holds. Bouts are unchanged; allow up to 2 extra minutes. For seated calf work, use a securely held sandbag instead of dumbbells when its shape and load fit better. All replacements **use instead of** the original slot, retain its working/preparation dose unless stated otherwise, and must meet the same technical effort target. Record changed implements/support before comparing a strength anchor. No unlisted rack, cable stack, sled, machine or throwing wall is assumed.

**Workload:** 8 locomotor attempts; 25 m declared movement paths and up to 15 m additional stopping (some approach paths already contain braking). 6 changes of travel direction; 14 locomotor braking episodes, which are **not foot-contact counts**. 4 bilateral and 4 unilateral flight landings; 0 throws. Keep intensity/flight types separate as described above. Light work: 2 rounds/4 side bouts, 8 reps plus 30 s holds. Primary: **8 rounds/11 bouts**, 52 reps plus 50 s holds. Preparation adds 11 bouts, 28 reps plus 18 s holds. Bilateral bouts count once; per-side work counts both sides. Access & Prepare 1, walking resets and outside training remain unquantified, not zero.

**Timing:** **70–82 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, before extra group queues: E1–E6 approximately 4, 8, 4, 4, 5, 4 minutes; light block 6; primary block 35. This includes attempts, reset/rest, demonstrations, ordinary setup and the stated preparation sets; the upper allowance covers load changes/extended recovery. Full duration remains unknown. Alternate athletes only when actual intervals supply their prescribed rest and the route clears. A coach must model the real class size, equipment quantities and booking; do not compress rests, delete slots or call a partial class complete to fit an assumed window.

**Scheduling:** This loads the legs through plants, landings and lifting. Roughly 48–72 hours before comparably demanding lower-body work is a starting planning interval, adjusted for actual recovery and the full sport/gym/tumbling schedule. A new drill does not mean recovered tissues. No observed response is yet available.

**Progression note:** Introduce direction, not entry-speed overload. The 45-degree task carries forward early braking from Classes 1–2 without repeating their drills. Keep flight doses to two attempts in each supporting task. Repeated strength anchors: Retain the Class 2 one-round split-squat dose while introducing diagonal tasks; RDL and soleus retain two rounds and other lifts one. No load increase is prescribed without actual feedback. This avoids adding knee-dominant volume at the same time as a new cut direction. Advance only one justified demand after reported control on both sides, intended effort and acceptable recovery; hold for unknown response, regress for loss of control or overload. Unique drills permit comparison of coaching criteria, not claims of improved test scores between different routes.

**Audit summary:** Restricted the diagonal running exit and included its continued stopping path. Distinguished same-leg, opposite-leg and bilateral landings rather than treating all as equal. Cut-to-stick remains a small terminal-control task after the primary running cut. Final sequence review retained the preceding one-round split squat rather than restoring two rounds without athlete response. Verified 6 explosive/2 light/6 primary entries, meaningful doses, unique explosive movement identities, separate side/flight/braking accounting and preserved recovery. Next class consolidates braking with fewer directional cuts; review actual landing/plant control and strength effort before selecting demands.
