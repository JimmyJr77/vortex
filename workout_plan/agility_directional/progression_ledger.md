## Counting conventions

- A locomotor attempt includes its stated entry, turn if present, exit and final stopping. A failed/aborted attempt remains exposure; record the actual path. Counts of successful turns and attempts are separate.
- A direction change is a discrete redirection of travel (not only a 180-degree reversal), not a static body turn. E2 prescribes six running reversals; E5 four lateral reversals. E6 starts still and changes facing to run; it is an exit rehearsal, not an additional incoming-momentum cut.
- A braking episode is a phase of reducing travel speed. It is not one foot contact. Record individual contacts if observed; never infer them from episode counts. Braking included inside an approach/return distance is not added to that distance again.
- A bound rep is one flight and landing, including failed attempts. E3 uses one-leg takeoff to opposite-leg landing. Running contacts and shuffle steps are not separately counted as plyometric jumps.
- A strength **round** includes both sides where prescribed; a **bout** is each separately executed side/set. `2 × 6 per leg` means four bouts and 24 reps. Simultaneous bilateral work is one bout, not two. Isometric seconds are separate from dynamic reps; preparation is separate from working work.
- Access & Prepare 1, walks/returns and other training are excluded because actual content or travel is unknown. These are not complete-day totals or a claim of an inherently safe cap.
