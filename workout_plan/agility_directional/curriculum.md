## Exact seven-category framework

- Endurance
- sustained pace
- pacing control

### Sprinting
- Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)
- Maximum velocity

### Jumps
- Vertical
- Horizontal
- Lateral
- Force absorption
- Elastic rebound

### Agility
- Deceleration
- Change of direction/reacceleration
- Reactive agility and anticipation

### Object control & receiving
- Tracking
- Catching/receiving
- Controlling/manipulating objects

### Upper-body force generation
- Pushing
- Pulling
- Straight-line projection (such as chest passes)

### Rotational explosiveness
- Upper-body emphasis (rotational throws, punches, and swings)
- Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)

Strength, stability, explosiveness and resilience support the selected focus; they are not additional performance categories. Body control/tumbling is separate.
