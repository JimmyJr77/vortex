# Class 9 — design audit

**Previous exposure review:** Class 8 prescribed four discrete cuts, eight unilateral flight landings and ten primary bouts. Actual completion, symptoms and recovery remain unknown.

**Observed response:** None reported. The prior class and new class are prescriptions, not actual exposure.

**Selection and revision:** Removed long standard cone-test distances. Counted only intermediate triangle vertices as cuts, distinguished the rounded U from a planted shuttle and limited bounds to four landings. No best-time racing or exhaustive multi-cone intervals.

**Progression/workload decision:** Introduce linked corners by cutting route lengths and repetitions; the main Z receives two complete routes, four corner practices. Total discrete cuts rise to ten while unilateral landings and strength bouts fall; this is a conditional redistribution, not proof of readiness. Use one round per lift and a stable goblet squat instead of the supported single-leg hinge emphasis. RDL, lateral lunge, bridge, Copenhagen and soleus keep familiar execution/reserve. Calibrate the goblet load; do not raise other loads with the new route demand.
