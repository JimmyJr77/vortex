# Class 3 — design audit

**Previous exposure review:** Class 2 reduced locomotor exposure to six attempts while adding eight bilateral and four unilateral landings. No completed work or recovery was reported; new drill identities were checked against all twelve prior entries.

**Observed response:** None reported. The prior class and new class are prescriptions, not actual exposure.

**Selection and revision:** Restricted the diagonal running exit and included its continued stopping path. Distinguished same-leg, opposite-leg and bilateral landings rather than treating all as equal. Cut-to-stick remains a small terminal-control task after the primary running cut. Final sequence review retained the preceding one-round split squat rather than restoring two rounds without athlete response.

**Progression/workload decision:** Introduce direction, not entry-speed overload. The 45-degree task carries forward early braking from Classes 1–2 without repeating their drills. Keep flight doses to two attempts in each supporting task. Retain the Class 2 one-round split-squat dose while introducing diagonal tasks; RDL and soleus retain two rounds and other lifts one. No load increase is prescribed without actual feedback. This avoids adding knee-dominant volume at the same time as a new cut direction.
