# Class 6 — design audit

**Previous exposure review:** Class 5 prescribed ten locomotor attempts, eight mixed running/flight-to-run redirections and six flight landings; no actual completion is reported.

**Observed response:** None reported. The prior class and new class are prescriptions, not actual exposure.

**Selection and revision:** Separated backward reversal from same-heading open-turn mechanics and counted them differently. Omitted a longer backward course and reduced new lift working rounds to avoid combining novelty with volume escalation.

**Progression/workload decision:** Change gait demands while keeping entries at 2 m and all exits short. Only six true travel reversals are prescribed; body turns are not counted as extra cuts. New backward skill does not justify maximal speed. Keep lateral lunge, bridge and Copenhagen at their existing single-round doses. Introduce one round each of reverse lunge, hip thrust and supported standing calf work for backward-step loading and forward extension; calibrate loads with the listed ramps, not assumed prior ability.
