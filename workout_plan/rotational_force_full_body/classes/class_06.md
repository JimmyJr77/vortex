### E1 — Forward plant-and-open to kick stance

**Connection and coaching:** Support → output: Set the receiving foot before initiating the grounded cross. Accept forward arrival onto a leg before freeing the other thigh for a kick. Shorten the step if the athlete overstrides, stiffens the knee or drags a planted foot through the turn.

### E2 — Light-band rotational cross

**Connection and coaching:** Support → output: Set the receiving foot before initiating the grounded cross. Primary: transmit the turn into a fast forward punch. From a modest staggered stance, anchor behind the rear shoulder; turn the rear foot/hip then drive the hand forward at shoulder height. Stop short of locked elbow and return under control; never release the band. Reduce tension or reach if the wrist folds or arm leads the turn. Rest 90 s between side bouts/sets.

**Source:** [Upper body · Class 2 · E1](../../rotational_explosiveness_upper_body/classes/class_02.md). Source class labels describe the original lesson, not today’s ordering.

### E3 — Lead-leg low roundhouse without a gather step

Use a lower chamber/shorter extension instead if the athlete needs an extra gather or leans away; do not speed up a trapped rear-foot pivot.

### E4 — Rotational shadow hook

**Connection and coaching:** Accelerate → control: Compare lead-leg release and the short hand hook using the same support-first cue. Coordinate a short transverse hand arc with the trunk turn. With no implement or target, keep elbow bent near 90 degrees and fist between chest and shoulder height; pivot and swing the forearm as a compact unit, stopping near the midline. Reset fully. Shorten the arc if the fist outruns the trunk or shoulder rolls forward. Rest 60 s between side bouts/sets.

**Source:** [Upper body · Class 2 · E2](../../rotational_explosiveness_upper_body/classes/class_02.md). Source class labels describe the original lesson, not today’s ordering.

### E6 — Two-hand band backhand speed sweep

**Connection and coaching:** Recover → redirect: Retain hip control in the folded-leg whip while the band sweep tests a longer resisted hand path. Accelerate the transverse pulling/sweeping path while controlling its endpoint. Anchor at chest height on the starting side. Both hands begin near that side of the chest; pivot and sweep across toward the opposite side with softly bent elbows, stopping within a comfortable arc. Return slowly. Handoffs are controlled: secure the grip and wait for the feeder to release and step clear before turning. No new airborne-feed progression is added.
