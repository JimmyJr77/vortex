## Setup and delivery

For released balls, verify a light grippable implement, permitted low floor target about 2–3 m beyond release, and containment of the entire flight/rebound/roll; use a throwing wall only after verification. Pairing is a coaching connection, not simultaneous stations or a throw immediately after a jump. Secure band/rope anchors and the landmine base. Kicks stay below hip height with a soft terminal knee, active recoil and no contact or spinning kick. Handoffs are controlled: secure the grip and wait for the feeder to release and step clear before turning. No new airborne-feed progression is added.
