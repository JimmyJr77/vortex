### E1 — Split-stance explosive hip pivot — grounded, proposed exercise

**Connection and coaching:** Pivot → scoop: Let the support foot release with the hip turn in the grounded pivot, then retain that foot-led sequence when the hands project the ball. Isolate the support-foot release and hip-turn initiation before adding a swinging leg. Mirror the stance for the opposite direction. Keep knees soft and following toes; reduce turn size if a foot sticks, knee twists or trunk leads the turn. Stop if the surface will not permit controlled pivoting.

### E2 — Med Ball Rotational Scoop Toss — paused standing start; floor-target adaptation

**Connection and coaching:** Pivot → scoop: Let the support foot release with the hip turn in the grounded pivot, then retain that foot-led sequence when the hands project the ball. Primary anchor: generate rotational projection from a settled load. Stand side-on, feet at a comfortable athletic width; hold the ball near the rear hip and pause 2 seconds. Pivot the rear foot, turn the hips/trunk, and sweep both hands through the target. No preparatory bounce or step. Finish balanced for 2 seconds. Reduce ball mass or turn range if the arms lead or the athlete spins out.

**Source:** [Upper body · Class 1 · E1](../../rotational_explosiveness_upper_body/classes/class_01.md). Source class labels describe the original lesson, not today’s ordering.

### E3 — Standing low roundhouse shadow kick, proposed exercise

**Connection and coaching:** Kick → pushing release: Use the same freely turning support base to accelerate the thigh or the throwing hand; recover balance before resetting either task. Integrate stance-leg rotation with free-leg acceleration and recovery. Accelerate the hip/thigh briskly; keep a soft knee at extension and brake the leg actively. No target, impact, partner contact, head-height kick, jump or spinning kick. Use a shorter arc and less terminal extension instead if recoil or balance is lost. Never snap an unsupported knee into lockout.

**Source:** [Lower body · Class 1 · E2](../../rotational_explosiveness_lower_body/classes/class_01.md). Source class labels describe the original lesson, not today’s ordering.

### E4 — Med Ball Shot-Put Throw — rotational, floor-target adaptation

**Connection and coaching:** Kick → pushing release: Use the same freely turning support base to accelerate the thigh or the throwing hand; recover balance before resetting either task. Finish the turn with a unilateral pushing release. Stagger the feet with the nonthrowing foot forward. Support the light ball at the rear shoulder with the other hand, turn through the rear foot/hip, then drive the throwing hand forward; the support hand guides and releases. Do not wind the arm behind the shoulder or turn it into an overhead baseball throw. Use a smaller/lighter grippable ball if wrist position or speed is lost.

**Source:** [Upper body · Class 1 · E4](../../rotational_explosiveness_upper_body/classes/class_01.md). Source class labels describe the original lesson, not today’s ordering.

### E5 — Split-Stance Band-Resisted Knee Drive — light band

Give the free thigh a small resisted acceleration demand, isolating chamber initiation from E3's full kick. Keep the pelvis quiet and support foot secure. Reduce band tension for slow movement, toe snagging or trunk lean. If a suitable secure loop is unavailable, use instead an unresisted split-stance knee drive at the same dose; record the resistance change.

### E6 — Half-Kneeling Rotational Med Ball Throw — floor-target adaptation

**Connection and coaching:** Support the moving limb: Use the knee drive to own the stance leg, then reduce leg propulsion in the kneeling throw and keep the pelvis and trunk organized. Express trunk-to-hand acceleration with leg propulsion reduced. Target-side foot forward, opposite knee comfortably supported on the floor; ball begins beside the rear hip. Use a small comfortable thoracic turn and fast two-hand release; do not force the pelvis to remain rigid while twisting farther. Keep the front foot planted and pelvis quiet. Shorten the turn if the knee shifts or back arches; use a seated bench rotational throw instead of this slot if kneeling contact is intolerable.

## Setup and delivery

Use the existing separate 10 m lanes. Keep waiting athletes outside the swept-leg, landing and ball paths. For released balls, verify a light grippable implement, permitted low floor target about 2–3 m beyond release, and containment of the entire flight/rebound/roll; use a throwing wall only after verification. Use a non-rebounding slam ball for slams. Clear the area between the leg drill and hand drill of each pair. Pairing is a coaching connection, not simultaneous stations or a throw immediately after a jump. Secure band/rope anchors and the landmine base. Kicks stay below hip height with a soft terminal knee, active recoil and no contact or spinning kick. Handoffs are controlled: secure the grip and wait for the feeder to release and step clear before turning. No new airborne-feed progression is added.
