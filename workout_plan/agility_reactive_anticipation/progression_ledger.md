## Counting conventions

One jump/bound repetition is one takeoff-flight-landing event. Bilateral landings involve two feet; unilateral landings involve one. They are kept in separate intensity/type bins and never treated as equal stress. Failed attempts count in actual workload. Running/shuffle foot contacts are separate from programmed jumps and cannot be inferred exactly from metres.

A set **round** includes both sides where prescribed; a set **bout** is a separately performed side or bilateral set. Thus 2 × 5 per leg is two rounds, four bouts and 20 reps. A bilateral calf-raise rep is one simultaneous cycle loading two legs. Repetition totals are descriptive, not equivalent mechanical work. Walking resets, rests and terminal sticks are part of delivery, not extra slots. Coach demonstration adds no athlete exposure; athlete demonstrations/leading would add actual load and are not prescribed.
