# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 11 of 12

**Primary effort:** Keep the relevant opponent in view while support and attention change.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Integrate early body reading with a shift from passer to receiver in E1. The physical support tasks deliberately change how the athlete becomes available to move: a falling start, rising from half-kneeling, fixed-lead lateral gallop, a stable-pelvis throw and a single-leg landing-linked exit. Keep all routes short; this is not an exhausting reaction circuit.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: athlete selects B’s commitment rather than continuing to follow A’s arms, then reaches the correct line and stops. Record target-selection errors separately from direction/footwork errors. No timing-device estimate is required.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Passer-to-Receiver Attention Shift** | **1 × 4 trials, 2 receiver commitments each side**. After the coach’s soft release to the assistant, read the assistant’s commitment, move 1.5 m and stop within 1.5 m on the separate path. Rest 90 s between trials and 90 s before E2. Keep receiver movement clearly after release until reads are reliable. | Main target-selection task: transfer attention from the initiating passer to the opponent who now determines the movement response. |
| 2 | **Falling Start to Short Acceleration** | **1 × 2 starts × 3 m**. From tall standing, lean as one unit only until a natural forward step is needed, then accelerate 3 m and stop within 3 m. Do not fall so far that the foot cannot catch the body. Rest 60 s between starts, 60 s before E3. Reduce lean for stumbling. | Convert a controlled forward shift of the centre of mass into a prompt first support step. |
| 3 | **Half-Kneeling Rise-and-Run Start** | **1 × 1 per lead leg × 2 m**. Begin in a comfortable half-kneeling stance, hands free; drive through the front foot, rise and accelerate 2 m, stop within 2 m. No push off the floor with hands. Rest 60 s between starts, 60 s before E4. Slow the rise and shorten travel if balance or knee comfort is poor. | Create forward force while changing from knee/foot support to running support, without losing the line of travel. |
| 4 | **Fixed-Lead Lateral Gallop** | **1 × 2 gallop cycles each direction**. Two low gallop cycles travelling right with right leg leading, then two left with left leading. Each cycle has one low flight, lead-foot landing then trailing-foot contact. Rest 60 s between directions, 60 s before E5. Stay facing forward; stop for tangled feet. | Keep one leg leading through a short rhythmic lateral burst, a different contact order from alternating skips or non-flight shuffles. |
| 5 | **Half-Kneeling Rotational Scoop Toss** | **1 × 2 throws per kneeling orientation**. Half-kneel comfortably, load a light ball beside the rear hip, turn through an owned trunk/hip range and release with two hands into the clear lateral throw lane. Keep front foot/knee aligned; no stand-up, catch or forced spinal twist. Rest 30 s between throws, 60 s before E6. Reduce ball load/rotation for loss of pelvic control. | Produce a rapid trunk/hip turn over a fixed supported pelvis, helping keep a stable base when gaze and shoulders change direction. |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Hop-to-Cut Read** | **1 × 1 per takeoff leg = 2 trials**. Make one low forward hop on the same leg; coach commits to one of two shallow exit gates as a stable landing is achieved. Step-cut and accelerate 1.5 m, stop within 2 m. Use one exit each way across the two trials. Rest 75 s between trials and 90 s before strength. Cue after a deliberate settled landing if control is uncertain. | Select a direction after unilateral landing acceptance, linking a changing support base to a new exit. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mini-Band Lateral Walk — light** | **1 × 4 steps each direction**. Secure a light band loop above the knees, shallow athletic stance; take short steps with feet apart and pelvis level. Reduce tension for trunk sway. If no suitable loop, use standing manual-resistance hip abduction instead, 1 × 4/side. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light control of the pelvis as attention changes and one leg becomes the support. |
| 2 | **Half-Kneeling Pallof Press — light band** | **1 × 5/side, 2 s reach hold**. Coach supplies a steady sideways band pull at chest height while athlete half-kneels and presses forward without rotating. Use comfortable knee contact; use standing split stance instead if kneeling is unsuitable. Reduce tension if trunk turns. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Keep the trunk from being pulled off the chosen line when gaze/shoulders turn toward a new opponent. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Step-Up** | **1 × 6/side**. Use a stable box below the point where the pelvis tilts; whole lead foot supported. Drive up without a trailing-leg hop and step down under control. Lower box/load if the return drops. If no box fits, use floor-supported Split Squat instead at the same sets/reps per leg, 2–3 RIR, with no other split-squat slot in this class. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Maintain force during a support/height transition with a stable surface. |
| 2 | **Reverse Lunge — dumbbells** | **1 × 6/side**. Step one foot backward, lower under control, then use the front leg to return to standing. Keep the front heel down; shorten the step/load for wobble or push-off from the rear toes. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Single-leg force to recover from a lowered stance without a second high-volume unilateral block. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain hip-hinge strength while leaving capacity for the attention task. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Adductor/pelvis support for the landing-linked direction choice. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Ankle push from the support foot after the information target changes. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Bent-knee ankle force through lower stances and landing acceptance. |

**Anchor delivery:** This anchor needs a qualified coach plus a non-training cue assistant. On the separate ball path, coach A at 1 m softly rolls/passes to assistant B at 4 m. Athlete begins aligned with B on their own path. Once A releases, B makes one genuine body commitment left/right along the path; athlete shifts attention to B, moves 1.5 m along their own corresponding direction and stops within 1.5 m. Four trials, two each side. A and B never enter the athlete route. With unknown readiness, B waits clearly until after release before moving; cue overlap is not the default. Do not use another training athlete as an uncounted leader.
