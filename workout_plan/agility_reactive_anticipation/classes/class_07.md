# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 7 of 12

**Primary effort:** Read a likely passing lane before release and move through an oblique plant.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Extend commitment reading to anticipation of an intended lane. E1 briefly removes the final ball-flight information, so the athlete must use preparatory body cues. The rest of the class supplies alternating forward transfer, same-leg lateral acceptance, quarter-turn coordination, single-support vertical extension and curved approach force. These are general opponent cues, not sport-specific tactical mastery.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: four predicted lanes, scored correct/incorrect against the coach’s preselected release, plus plant/stop control. Record where the motion was frozen; scores from different freeze points are not directly comparable. Movement before any useful body information is guessing, not anticipation.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pre-Release Pass-Lane Prediction** | **1 × 4 predictions, 2 lanes each**. Coach freezes the preparation, athlete selects a 1.5 m diagonal gate, coach completes the preselected low release on the separate path; stop within 2 m. Rest 75 s between trials, 90 s before E2. Reveal more preparation if guesses dominate. | Main anticipation task: infer an intended pass lane from body preparation before ball flight makes the answer obvious. |
| 2 | **Alternating Forward Bounds** | **1 × 2 linked bounds = 2 landings**. Start from a short walking setup; bound left-to-right then right-to-left, one landing per leg, finish controlled. Do not seek maximal stride length. Rest 90 s before E3; shorten travel if pelvis drops or contact gets loud. | Transfer forward impulse from leg to leg without the same-leg landing used in earlier forward hops. |
| 3 | **Single-Leg Lateral Hop to Stick** | **1 × 1 per leg**. Hop a small distance outward on the right leg and land right; repeat on the left after 45 s. Hold 2 s; rest 60 s before E4. Shorten distance for extra hopping or knee drift. | Own a lateral landing back on the same leg, a different support demand from skater bounds. |
| 4 | **90-Degree Jump Turn to Stick** | **1 × 2 each turn direction**. Low two-foot jump with a quarter turn, both feet land and hold 2 s. Rotate through the whole body, not a fixed planted knee. Rest 30 s between jumps and 60 s before E5. Reduce turn angle if alignment is lost. | Reorient feet/hips together while airborne, then establish a usable two-foot base toward the chosen lane. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Vertical Hop to Stick** | **1 × 1 per leg**. Make one small vertical hop on the same leg, land under the hip and hold 2 s. Repeat the other leg after 60 s; rest 60 s before E6. Lower height for pelvic drop or an extra hop. | Express vertical extension from a single support without a crossing reach, keeping the body available to redirect from an anticipated lane. |
| 6 | **Inside-Arc Acceleration** | **1 × 2 arcs each direction × 4 m**. Run a marked gentle arc for 4 m at controlled acceleration effort, then stop within 2 m along its tangent. Lean as a whole body; no foot crossing. Rest 60 s between runs and 90 s before strength. Widen/slow the arc for unstable footing. | Apply continuous inward force over a gentle curved path rather than a single sharp plant. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Half-Kneeling Pallof Press — light band** | **1 × 5/side, 2 s reach hold**. Coach supplies a steady sideways band pull at chest height while athlete half-kneels and presses forward without rotating. Use comfortable knee contact; use standing split stance instead if kneeling is unsuitable. Reduce tension if trunk turns. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light resistance to trunk rotation while choosing an oblique line. |
| 2 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — light** | **1 × 6/ankle**. With heel supported and shin still, turn the forefoot slightly outward against a light band held securely by the coach, away from the face. Return slowly; no ankle rolling or knee turn. Reduce tension/range for loss of control. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Keep the lateral foot contact controlled under angled push-off. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **2 × 5/side**. Step sideways from standing, sit into the working hip with whole-foot pressure, then return to the starting stance. Reduce depth/load for groin discomfort, knee collapse or twisting. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain the step-out-and-return strength anchor for moving into an interception lane. |
| 2 | **Split Squat — dumbbells at sides** | **1 × 6/side**. Keep both feet on the floor in a stationary staggered stance; front heel down, controlled descent, drive through the lead leg. Reduce depth/load for pelvic rotation or rear-leg jumping. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Stable lead-leg extension to leave the anticipated route position. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Hip force and trunk control during the oblique plant without another long hinge block. |
| 4 | **Partner Manual-Resistance Hamstring Curl** | **1 × 8/side**. Lie prone on a comfortable floor surface; coach applies smooth resistance above the heel while the athlete bends and straightens the knee through an owned range. Keep hips down; no sudden pushes. Match resistance to 2–3 RIR, not a contest. If prone support/contact is unsuitable, use Seated Band Hamstring Curl instead, same sets/reps per side: sit on a stable bench, secure a light band at the heel with a coach holding steady forward tension, then pull the heel beneath the bench without moving the thigh. Qualify band/bench fit; no sudden release. This replaces only this slot and preserves totals. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Knee-flexor contribution during curved running, held at a controlled support dose. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Single-leg ankle drive as the interception path lengthens. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Bent-knee ankle force in the initial low intercept stance. |

**Anchor delivery:** Coach preselects a left/right low pass (two each). Athlete sees a natural step and trunk/arm preparation; coach briefly freezes before releasing. Athlete predicts the likely lane and moves 1.5 m diagonally toward their own projected gate on a separate path. Coach then completes the low roll/pass on the ball path, allowing prediction to be checked. Athlete stops within 2 m; never touches the ball. No spoken direction and no deliberately inconsistent decoy cues yet. With uncertain readiness, show a more complete, slower preparation before freezing rather than adding trials.

E6 uses a gentle 4 m arc within an up-to-10 m × 7 m area, including 2 m tangent stopping space; it is not a tight circle or full-speed curve. Bounds use short individualized distances inside the remaining clear strip. No additional runway is assumed. Full-route width is a requirement, not confirmed inventory: branch stopping extensions can exceed a 4 m bay.
