# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 4 of 12

**Primary effort:** Redirect toward an angled opening while preserving a controlled outside plant.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Consolidate the first three classes through a simple two-choice angled route. E1 is the fork-cut anchor. The supporting jobs are a discrete oblique plant, an elastic return, shared-foot acceptance after a lateral push, reverse-travel braking and ground-based lateral force release. No feints or late second cue are added today.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: four correct branch choices, foot/knee pointing consistently through the cut and controlled stopping. Record entry speed description, cue location and side; do not compare time with the linear or lateral drills.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opponent Fork-Cut Read** | **1 × 4 trials, 2 each branch**. Jog 1.5 m, take a 1.5 m 45-degree branch after the coach’s body commitment, stop within 2 m. Rest 60 s between trials and 90 s before E2. Cue earlier or slow the approach for a poor plant. | Main live choice of an angled path from a controlled approach. |
| 2 | **45-Degree Cut Bound to Stick** | **1 × 1 each plant side**. Use one controlled step into a planted foot, bound 45 degrees to the opposite-leg landing and hold 2 s. Short distance, no approach sprint. Rest 30 s between bounds, 60 s before E3. Reduce angle/distance if knee/foot rotation is uncontrolled. | Practice redirecting an oblique impulse across one outside plant, separately from a reactive running decision. |
| 3 | **Skater Rebound-Return** | **1 × 1 out-and-back pair = 2 bound landings**. Bound right-to-left and immediately left-to-right once; each leg receives one landing. Keep travel small and stop after the second landing. Rest 60 s before E4. Reduce distance for long uncontrolled ground contact. | Reverse force from one leg to the other without the terminal landing pause of Class 1’s skater bound. |
| 4 | **Lateral Single-Leg Takeoff to Two-Foot Landing** | **1 × 2 each takeoff leg**. Push sideways from one leg, land with both feet together in time and hold 2 s; two attempts per takeoff leg. Rest 30 s between attempts, 60 s before E5. Shorten distance for uneven landing. | Share landing acceptance across both feet while preserving a unilateral lateral push; consolidates force production at lower landing asymmetry. |
| 5 | **Backpedal-to-Stick** | **1 × 4 repetitions × 2 m**. Backpedal sharply but under control for 2 m; stop over the next ≤2 m, hold 2 s. Keep hips loaded and head facing forward; precheck the route. Rest 45 s between reps, 60 s before E6. Slow down if feet tangle or stopping drifts. | Own the brake when momentum is travelling backward, supporting the next retreat/reopen exposure. |
| 6 | **Stationary Lateral Medicine-Ball Scoop Heave** | **1 × 2 throws each direction**. Light ball held near outside hip, shallow hip load, drive through the floor and release sideways with two hands; pivot the feet with the hips, no planted-knee twist. No jump/catch. Rest 30 s between throws, 90 s before strength. Reduce load if the release slows. | Transfer leg/hip force sideways into a free release while both feet support the athlete. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Step-Down — light dumbbell** | **1 × 4/leg**. Lower for 3 s from a suitably low stable platform, free heel lightly taps; keep standing heel down. Use bodyweight or reduce range for pelvic/knee drift. If no platform fits, use a shallow supported single-leg squat instead, same dose. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light control of the outside knee/pelvis before loaded lateral patterns. |
| 2 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — light** | **1 × 6/ankle**. With heel supported and shin still, turn the forefoot slightly outward against a light band held securely by the coach, away from the face. Return slowly; no ankle rolling or knee turn. Reduce tension/range for loss of control. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Low-fatigue ankle control over an oblique plant; keep band work well below primary-strength effort. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cossack Squat — goblet dumbbell** | **2 × 5/side**. Start in a fixed wide stance; shift into one hip, bend that knee and keep the other leg long, then return to centre without stepping. Use a comfortable depth; reduce range for groin pain or heel roll. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Fixed-stance lateral hip force through a controlled range, calibrated as a new strength anchor. |
| 2 | **Dumbbell Step-Up** | **1 × 6/side**. Use a stable box below the point where the pelvis tilts; whole lead foot supported. Drive up without a trailing-leg hop and step down under control. Lower box/load if the return drops. If no box fits, use floor-supported Split Squat instead at the same sets/reps per leg, 2–3 RIR, with no other split-squat slot in this class. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Single-leg push and controlled descent for accepting weight over an angled plant. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain hip-hinge force with one set during this consolidation exposure. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Adductor support against lateral separation with no hold-time progression. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Maintain straight-knee ankle strength while limiting rebound overlap. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 8 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Maintain bent-knee ankle strength at a modest support dose. |
