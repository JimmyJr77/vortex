# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 1 of 12

**Primary effort:** Read a live opponent's first lateral commitment and reversal, then push, brake and redirect without losing a usable stance. **Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation.

**Athletes:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified supervision. This is the first prescription in this curriculum, not evidence of beginner status or of prior training completion. Actual readiness, recent sport/gym/tumbling workload and restrictions are unknown.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The limiting demand is coupling visible body movement to a decisive lateral response while controlling the plant. **Mirror Shuffle (E2)** is the performance anchor. Other jobs are quick ankle return, force from a stationary stance, opposite-leg acceptance, braking a moving shuffle, and opening into short pursuit. Strength loads the lateral plant/return, lead-leg extension, hip hinge, adductors and ankle drive. Observe correct direction, a controlled reversal and no advance guessing; do not reward speed that loses those qualities.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use bodyweight, rapid intent and full resets. E2 receives the largest decision-practice allocation. E1, E3, E4 and E5 train physical support tasks; they are not themselves anticipation tests. No athlete leads extra rounds: a coach supplies live cues, so the athlete's work is exactly the follower dose below.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Line Pogo** | **1 × 8 contacts total**, alternating across a floor seam; four each direction. Both feet together, low amplitude, quiet quick contacts. Finish the eighth landing without another rebound. Rest **60 s** before E2. | Brief ankle-driven lateral rebound exposure before the anchor. Keep torso stacked and travel only far enough to cross the seam; reduce amplitude if rhythm or foot control changes. |
| 2 | **Mirror Shuffle — primary anchor** | **3 × 2 bouts, up to 4 s each**; one initial left and one initial right per set, order hidden. Start facing the coach; each bout has one live reversal. Rest **45 s between bouts, 90 s between sets**, and 90 s before E3. | Read the coach's developing hip/trunk displacement, push in the same global direction, brake and redirect when it changes. Stay in shuffle footwork with feet apart. Six bouts contain up to six initial decisions and six reversal decisions; record both separately. Shorten the coach's excursion/speed if the athlete cannot stay controlled. |
| 3 | **Bilateral Lateral Jump to Stick — paused start** | **1 × 2 each direction = 4 jumps.** From a comfortable quarter squat, pause **2 s**, then drive sideways without another dip; use natural arms. Two-foot takeoff and landing; stick **2 s**. Reset/rest **20 s between jumps**, then **60 s** before E4. | Express lateral force from a held athletic stance with two-leg support, without relying on repeated rebound momentum. Choose a distance the athlete can own; shorten it for an extra recovery step or lost pause. |
| 4 | **Lateral Skater Bound to Stick** | **1 × 2 each direction = 4 bounds**: two right-leg takeoffs to left-leg landings and two left-to-right. Rapid push, submaximal controllable distance, **2 s single-leg stick**. Rest **25 s between bounds**, then **60 s** before E5. | Transfer force from one leg to the opposite plant and accept body mass over that foot. Keep pelvis level and knee following foot direction. Shorten distance for hopping out, inward knee collapse or trunk spill. |
| 5 | **Lateral Shuffle to Stick — rapid entry variant** | **1 × 2 each direction = 4 repetitions of 2 m.** Push sharply from stillness, shuffle 2 m, then arrest travel within the next **1 m** and hold **2 s**. No crossover. Rest **30 s between reps**, then **90 s** before E6. | Isolate rapid force production into a predictable lateral brake so the athlete has a physical stopping strategy for E2. Lower the hips through several controlled braking contacts; avoid a reaching, locked-knee final plant. Reduce entry speed if the stopping boundary is missed. |
| 6 | **Crossover Step to Sprint Cone — live pursuit variant** | **1 × 2 each direction = 4 starts × 3 m.** Face the coach across separate paths; react to the coach opening and departing left/right, open the hips and accelerate 3 m in that direction. Walk down within the remaining **2 m**. Rest **60 s between starts**, then **90 s** before Section 3. | Turn a read into a longer lateral pursuit using crossover-to-running footwork, complementing E2's short shuffle reversals. Keep eyes on the opponent during commitment, then on the clear route; no deliberate backward head turn while running. Reduce to **2 m acceleration + 3 m stopping** if necessary, use instead of the 3 m version. |

**Live-cue setup and scoring:** Orient lateral travel along the length of the lane. Athlete starts at the 5 m midpoint. For E2, mark a working interval from 4–6 m; coach moves 0.5–1 m in the initial direction, reverses once at an unannounced moment, and finishes within that interval. Cued movement is at most 3 m per bout, with up to 1 m additional travel for a controlled stop. Allow a 1 m stopping buffer at each end of the interval. Start after a variable 1–3 s ready period; no spoken direction, counting rhythm, deliberate feint or 180° spin. The coach's preparatory weight shift is useful information: read it, but do not move before any directional body information. Finish by 4 s even if the response was incomplete; do not add attempts to obtain a good score.

Reserve two parallel paths with **at least 2 m verified face-to-face separation** between coach and athlete, each clear of other users. This is a layout requirement, not a known lane width. For E6, start at 5 m, accelerate to 2 m or 8 m and stop by 0 m or 10 m; coach stays on the separate path. No chase into another athlete's path, touch, race finish or outside run-off. Mark bounds/jumps along the lane with enough landing space; no maximal-distance target. If separate live-cue paths cannot be established, delivery of this class remains unresolved—cones or verbal calls would change the perceptual task.

**E2 quality marker:** For each scheduled bout record initial direction correct/incorrect, response to reversal correct/incorrect, whether movement began before directional information, and whether the athlete kept the base and stopped inside the buffer. Also record cue provider, initial direction, excursion, footwear and surface. These are coaching observations, not milliseconds of reaction time. Timing-gate synchronization/video capability is unverified; do not report reaction time or infer anticipation gains from sprint time.

**Quality rule:** First scheduled repetitions establish today's execution at a controlled speed; they count toward the dose. Increase intent within the existing reps only while control is evident. For two consecutive technical misses, end that drill or use its stated reduced demand for remaining reps. Pain, slipping or unsafe stopping ends the affected task immediately. Record actual omissions; never force the written count or add make-up work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep these easy, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions or ample hold time available. They should leave the athlete ready to lift.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Step-Down — light dumbbell** | **1 × 4 per leg = 8 reps.** Use the lowest stable step/box that permits a light free-heel tap while the standing heel stays down; **3 s lower**, controlled return. Rest **30 s between sides**, 30 s afterward. Bodyweight may replace the dumbbell. | Rehearse pelvis/knee/foot control over the lateral plant without adding another demanding lunge dose. Shorten range or remove load for pelvic drop. If no suitably low platform fits, use instead a **shallow, hand-supported single-leg squat to a comfortable depth**, same dose/tempo; record the replacement. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill — standing, both feet supported** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per foot = 6 holds.** Work one target foot at a time while both feet stay on the floor; 5 s relaxation between holds, **15 s to change target foot**, then 30 s before Section 4. | Keep heel, first and fifth metatarsal contacts while lightly raising the arch, supporting the push/plant base without calf pre-fatigue. Keep toes long. Reduce effort or use the seated variant instead if toes curl or the ankle rolls. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

These are challenging strength sets, not a circuit. For dynamic work choose a load leaving **2–3 technically sound reps in reserve (RIR)** at the written dose. Reassess after each set; reduce load for grinding, range loss or compensation. No maximal tests. Use a controlled **2 s lowering**, smooth purposeful ascent, and normal breathing unless a row says otherwise. Perform both sides before the next round.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell, step out and return** | **2 × 5 per leg = 20 reps.** Step sideways, sit into that hip with whole-foot pressure, push back to the starting stance. Rest **90 s between legs; 120 s between rounds and after the final round**. | Main frontal-plane strength anchor: load force acceptance and return from the outside plant. Choose a repeatable step length and depth without groin discomfort or knee collapse. Reduce load/range if return requires twisting or dragging the foot. |
| 2 | **Split Squat — dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 6 per leg = 24 reps.** Stationary floor-supported stance, front foot flat, controlled descent and lead-leg drive. Rest **90 s between legs; 120 s between rounds and after the final round**. | Load lead-leg knee/hip extension for the first response step and pursuit acceleration in a stable staggered stance. This supplies fore–aft force capacity distinct from P1's lateral step-and-return. Shorten range or reduce load for rear-leg jumping or pelvic rotation. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** Soft knees; hips travel back, weights close; finish tall without leaning back. Rest **120 s between sets and after the second**. | Load hip-extensor force in the flexed-hip position used to brake and drive out, without another knee-dominant lunge. End descent where trunk/pelvic control stops; reduce load/range for rounding or lumbar extension. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s per side = 30 s total.** Forearm under shoulder; upper knee bent about 90°, with knee/lower thigh supported on a stable bench; lower leg lifted clear. Select leverage allowing approximately **5 s of clean hold reserve**, not a maximal hold. Rest **60 s between sides and 60 s afterward**. | Challenge the upper-leg adductors and pelvic support against lateral separation, complementing rather than repeating the loaded lunge. Keep hips level; stop for groin/knee/shoulder pain or sagging. If the setup/effort cannot be qualified, use instead **side-lying hip adduction, 1 × 8 per side at 2–3 RIR**, with the lower leg lifting under control and the upper foot planted in front; a coach-secured light dumbbell above the lower knee may supply load. Same side rest and 60 s exit recovery. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8 per leg = 16 reps.** Flat floor, nearly straight knee; one hand lightly on a fixed support, weight in the other. Controlled heel rise, brief top checkpoint, **2 s lower**. Rest **60 s between legs and 75 s afterward**. | Load the straighter-knee ankle push used as the pursuit step lengthens. Hand support prevents balance from limiting calf force. Use bodyweight instead if it already gives 2–3 RIR; reduce load/range for heel-height loss or ankle rolling. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **2 × 8 = 16 bilateral reps.** Stable bench, knees approximately right angles; secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees, feet flat at the bottom. **2 s lower**, brief bottom pause, rise without bouncing. Rest **90 s between sets**. | Load ankle drive with knees flexed, matching the lower shuffle/plant stance. P5 and P6 deliberately differ by knee position; only three total set rounds across them limit overlapping calf fatigue. Reduce load for shortening heel travel or bouncing. |

**Load preparation, separate from working sets:** Before P1 and P2, perform **1 × 3 per leg** at an easy load. Before P3, **1 × 5** easy. Before P4, **1 × 5 s per side** in the selected lever; before P5, **1 × 4 per leg** easy; before P6, **1 × 5** easy. Allow 30 s between preparation sides and **60 s after compound preparation, 45 s after P4–P6 preparation** before working sets. This is 30 dynamic preparation reps plus 10 s of holds, all in listed exercises. Add only necessary ramp sets for a large load gap and log their dose/time. If P4 is replaced, prepare with 1 × 4 adduction reps per side instead of its two 5 s holds.
