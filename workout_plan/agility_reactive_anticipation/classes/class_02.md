# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 2 of 12

**Primary effort:** Read forward commitment, accelerate briefly and own the stop.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Transfer Class 1’s body-read principle from side-to-side tracking to forward release. The main problem is initiating from real weight transfer without guessing. E1 is the live-release anchor; ankle rebound, forward impulse, staggered drive, a known brake and a forward ballistic release supply distinct physical jobs.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** For each of six E1 starts: movement after visible commitment, smooth first two steps, and stop before the 8 m line without a reaching locked-knee plant. Record correct read/control separately; no reaction-time claim.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Forward Release Chase** | **2 × 3 starts × 3 m**. Rapid first steps at a controllable speed; brake within 3 m. Rest 45 s between starts, 90 s between sets and 90 s before E2. Stop for guessing or boundary loss. | Main live read-to-forward-drive task; identify opponent departure rather than memorizing a sound cue. |
| 2 | **Forward–Back Line Hops** | **1 × 8 bilateral contacts**. Both feet cross a visible floor seam forward/back, four each way; low amplitude, stop after contact eight. Rest 60 s before E3; reduce amplitude for heel slap or ankle roll. | Quick fore–aft ankle return supports the first acceleration contacts and the later backward pressure of braking. |
| 3 | **Standing Broad Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 jumps**. Two-foot takeoff and landing, natural arms; choose a controllable distance and hold 2 s. Rest 30 s between jumps, 60 s before E4. Shorten distance for extra steps. | Whole-body forward impulse from a parallel stance, without a perceptual or unilateral landing demand. |
| 4 | **Sprint-to-Stick Deceleration** | **1 × 4 repetitions × 3 m**. Begin at 2 m; accelerate to 5 m and brake by 8 m using several controlled contacts. No sudden locked-leg stop. Rest 45 s between reps and 60 s before E5. Reduce entry speed for overshoot. | Isolate a known forward braking boundary so the live task is not limited by an absent stopping strategy. |
| 5 | **Split-Stance Broad Jump to Parallel Landing** | **1 × 2 per lead leg**. Pause in a comfortable staggered stance, jump forward and land with feet parallel; no stance-switch jumps. Two starts each lead. Rest 30 s between attempts, 60 s before E6; reduce distance for rotation. | Express forward drive from an asymmetric starting base, then share acceptance across both feet. |
| 6 | **Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — forward** | **1 × 4 throws**. Light ball, quarter squat, quick leg drive and low two-hand underhand release. No jump or return catch. Stay inside the marked throw zone. Rest 30 s between throws and 90 s before strength; reduce ball load for slow arm-dominated release. | Link hip/knee extension to a forward release while keeping the athlete grounded; supports whole-body forward projection. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — light** | **1 × 6/ankle**. With heel supported and shin still, turn the forefoot slightly outward against a light band held securely by the coach, away from the face. Return slowly; no ankle rolling or knee turn. Reduce tension/range for loss of control. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light ankle control against outward rolling during the first planted acceleration step. |
| 2 | **Heel-Dig Bridge Hold — light bilateral** | **2 × 10 s**. On a comfortable floor surface, heels down and knees bent; lift hips only to a stacked trunk and gently pull heels toward the hips without moving them. Keep easy and breathe; lower height for cramp or back extension. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Easy posterior-chain positional tension before the stronger hinge; do not turn it into hamstring fatigue. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goblet Squat** | **2 × 6 bilateral**. Hold one dumbbell at the chest; sit between the hips with whole-foot pressure and rise smoothly. Use an owned depth; reduce load/range for heel lift or trunk collapse. 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Bilateral knee/hip extension for projecting forward from the initial athletic stance. |
| 2 | **Reverse Lunge — dumbbells** | **1 × 6/side**. Step one foot backward, lower under control, then use the front leg to return to standing. Keep the front heel down; shorten the step/load for wobble or push-off from the rear toes. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Front-leg force through the return step without another full two-set unilateral block. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Retained hip-hinge anchor for braking with the hips back and returning to extension. |
| 4 | **Partner Manual-Resistance Hamstring Curl** | **1 × 8/side**. Lie prone on a comfortable floor surface; coach applies smooth resistance above the heel while the athlete bends and straightens the knee through an owned range. Keep hips down; no sudden pushes. Match resistance to 2–3 RIR, not a contest. If prone support/contact is unsuitable, use Seated Band Hamstring Curl instead, same sets/reps per side: sit on a stable bench, secure a light band at the heel with a coach holding steady forward tension, then pull the heel beneath the bench without moving the thigh. Qualify band/bench fit; no sudden release. This replaces only this slot and preserves totals. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Direct knee-flexor force supports control of the lower leg through the first running steps. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Retained straighter-knee ankle push as the pursuit stride opens. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Bent-knee ankle drive for the initial low stance; one set limits accumulated calf work. |

**Anchor delivery:** Coach and athlete face the same direction on parallel paths, coach half a step ahead. Coach visibly shifts weight and leaves after a variable 1–3 s interval; no verbal go. Athlete reads the departure, accelerates from 2 m to 5 m, and stops by 8 m. Six departures; reset by walking outside the active path. Record cue provider and start geometry. Hold a slower coach departure if initiation or stopping is not controlled.

**Space and delivery:** Use one 10 m athlete lane and a separate parallel coach path with at least 2 m verified separation. E1/E4 start at 2 m, travel 3 m, brake within 3 m, leaving 2 m at each end. Jump travel stays within 2–6 m, with a clear 2 m landing buffer. E6 releases a light medicine ball forward from 2 m into an empty 2–7 m catch-free landing zone; one thrower, retrieve only after the ball stops. Choose load/trajectory that remain inside it. If no ball is light enough for a fast low release, delivery of E6 requires an appropriate ball rather than extra loaded jumps.
