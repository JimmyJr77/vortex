# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 5 of 12

**Primary effort:** Retreat under control, reopen the hips and recover the opponent’s route.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Build from Class 4’s backward brake into orientation change. E1 is a live backpedal-to-turn task. Backward ankle travel, a small backward landing, a planned drop-step, split-stance force and a gap-preservation task support different parts of retreat and recovery. Avoid blind full-speed running backward.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: correct exit, no crossing/tangling of the retreat feet, head/hips reorient together and controlled stop. Record cue timing and retreat speed; a faster departure with a poor turn is not an advance.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Backpedal Turn-and-Go Read** | **1 × 4 trials, 2 exits each**. Backpedal 1.5 m, step-turn toward the body cue, accelerate 2 m, stop in 2 m. Rest 75 s between trials and 90 s before E2. Start slower/cue earlier if feet cross or the turn twists a fixed knee. | Main reorientation from retreat momentum to a live opponent exit. |
| 2 | **Backward Travelling Pogo Hops** | **1 × 6 bilateral contacts**. Both feet together, six low contacts over no more than 1.5 m, eyes forward and route prechecked. Stop after six. Rest 60 s before E3; reduce travel for heel slap or leaning backward. | Produce repeated small rearward ankle impulses while preserving an upright torso; unlike reciprocal line hops, every landing travels backward. |
| 3 | **Backward Broad Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 jumps**. From parallel feet, make a short low backward jump into a clear landing area and hold 2 s. No maximum distance. Rest 45 s between jumps and 60 s before E4; shorten distance for stepping/falling backward. | Accept a small backward displacement over both feet before asking the athlete to redirect from retreat. |
| 4 | **Drop-Step Diagonal Launch** | **1 × 2 each exit × 2 m**. From facing stance, step the exit-side foot back/open, turn through the feet, accelerate 2 m diagonally and stop in 2 m. No shuffle or crossover chase. Rest 45 s between attempts and 60 s before E5. Reduce exit angle for knee twisting. | Practice opening the hip and aligning the first drive step before adding a live retreat cue. |
| 5 | **Split-Squat Jump to Same-Stance Stick** | **1 × 2 per lead leg**. Jump upward from a shallow split stance and land in the same stance; both feet contact, front leg accepts the bias. Hold 2 s. Rest 30 s between jumps, 60 s before E6. Lower height for pelvic rotation or unstable front-foot contact. | Rapid lead-leg extension with an asymmetric landing base, supporting the push out of an opened hip position. |
| 6 | **Gap-Preservation Retreat** | **1 × 3 bouts, ≤4 s and ≤2 m each**. Coach advances once then eases/stops on the separate path; athlete retreats only far enough to keep the marked starting longitudinal gap, then brakes. Cap retreat at 2 m plus 1 m stopping. No turn or chase. Rest 60 s between bouts and 90 s before strength. Slow coach approach if the athlete runs out of space. | Read closing rate to preserve a useful defensive gap instead of following only a direction. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mini-Band Lateral Walk — light** | **1 × 4 steps each direction**. Secure a light band loop above the knees, shallow athletic stance; take short steps with feet apart and pelvis level. Reduce tension for trunk sway. If no suitable loop, use standing manual-resistance hip abduction instead, 1 × 4/side. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light lateral hip control while the stance opens and closes. |
| 2 | **Heel-Dig Bridge Hold — light bilateral** | **2 × 10 s**. On a comfortable floor surface, heels down and knees bent; lift hips only to a stacked trunk and gently pull heels toward the hips without moving them. Keep easy and breathe; lower height for cramp or back extension. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Easy heel-driven posterior-chain position before retreat-supporting strength; keep effort low. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reverse Lunge — dumbbells** | **2 × 6/side**. Step one foot backward, lower under control, then use the front leg to return to standing. Keep the front heel down; shorten the step/load for wobble or push-off from the rear toes. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Retreat-step acceptance and front-leg drive to recover forward position. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain hip-hinge control for braking backward momentum without excessive lumbar motion. |
| 3 | **Hip Thrust — sandbag** | **1 × 8 bilateral**. Support upper back on a stable bench, secure the sandbag across the hip crease, drive through both feet and finish with ribs stacked. No lumbar arch; reduce load for pelvic loss. If bench fit fails, use a floor glute bridge instead, same dose, recording the shorter range. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Hip-extension force to finish the first recovery step, limited to one set. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Support pelvic/adductor control as the feet open from a facing stance. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Ankle push after the body reorients into pursuit. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Bent-knee ankle force during low retreat and plant positions. |

**Anchor delivery:** Coach stands in view on a separate parallel route. Athlete starts at 6 m facing toward the 0 m end and backpedals 1.5 m toward 7.5 m while coach advances slowly on the other route; coach then visibly opens the hips toward one of two forward-diagonal exits. Athlete plants, turns using steps rather than twisting a planted knee, and runs 2 m toward the selected exit, then stops within 2 m. Four trials, two exits each. Coach commits before the retreat ends; when readiness is unknown, begin with the exit body cue already visible and a walking retreat.

**Space and delivery:** Use a verified up-to-10 m long × 7 m wide combined bay within the 10 m length; retreat from 6 to 7.5 m and exits return diagonally toward the 0 m end so the turn never needs extra runway beyond 10 m. Mark two 2 m exits with 2 m stopping extensions. Keep coach ≥2 m from the athlete’s path. Backward jumps and pogos use a prechecked empty 2–6 m strip; nobody stands behind the athlete. E6 uses parallel paths and a coach position offset longitudinally, with no physical chase/tag. Full-route width is a requirement, not confirmed inventory: branch stopping extensions can exceed a 4 m bay. Compact same-drill option: keep the 1.5 m retreat but reduce E1 to a 1 m diagonal exit plus 1 m stop; E4 likewise uses a 1 m diagonal launch plus 1 m stop, at controlled speed. Keep trial counts and turn directions. Those paired diagonal branches fit a verified 4 m-wide bay with body margins. E1 movement becomes 10 m plus up to 4 m stopping; E4 movement becomes 4 m plus up to 4 m stopping. At setup, mark complete body/landing envelopes, both possible routes, run-off and the coach position; a floor-line diagram alone does not establish clearance. If even the compact footprint is unavailable, resolve the layout before delivery.

**Readiness and stopping:** First scheduled attempts, at controlled demand, count toward the listed dose and establish today’s execution. Hold the simpler stated version when recent execution/recovery is unknown. Stop the affected exercise for pain, slipping or unsafe landing/stopping; after two consecutive technical misses, reduce the stated distance, speed or cue complexity for the remaining attempts, or end it. Do not add make-up reps or force the paper count. Record actual omissions and modified rules. Landings are controlled; no maximal jump-distance chase.
