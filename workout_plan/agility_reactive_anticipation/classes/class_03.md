# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Ignore an uncommitted feint, then leave decisively when the opponent truly goes.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Class 2 separated a genuine forward departure from the physical start. This class adds response inhibition without adding longer runs. E1 is the commit-or-hold anchor; a timed split-step, unilateral forward acceptance, force from sitting, lateral elastic return and reading an actual release address different reasons an athlete may be late or fooled.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** Inside E1 record six cue classifications: correct go direction or correct hold, any premature first step, and controlled stop. Four go trials and two hold trials are prescribed; record false starts as actual extra locomotor exposure, not successes.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Commit-or-Hold Gate Burst** | **2 × 3 trials: 4 go, 2 hold in total**. On go, burst 2 m and stop within 1 m; on hold, stay ready for 3 s. Rest 45 s between trials, 90 s between sets and 90 s before E2. Make the true hip shift clearer for decision errors. | Main inhibition task: distinguish a shoulder feint from hip commitment, then produce the correct lateral first step. |
| 2 | **Opponent-Timed Split-Step Pounce** | **1 × 4 responses × 2 m**. Coach visibly loads and steps forward; athlete makes one low split-step and on landing drives forward 2 m, stops within 3 m. No repeated bouncing. Rest 45 s between reps and 60 s before E3; reduce hop height and cue speed for late/unstable landings. | Time a small two-foot unweighting so the landing is available for a forward push when the opponent departs. |
| 3 | **Single-Leg Forward Hop to Stick** | **1 × 2/leg**. Hop forward from and land on the same leg, hold 2 s; use a short owned distance. Rest 30 s between hops, 60 s before E4. Shorten for extra hopping, knee collapse or trunk spill. | Accept forward momentum over one plant without the shared landing used in Class 2. |
| 4 | **Seated Broad Jump** | **1 × 3 jumps**. Sit feet-flat on a stable bench of suitable height; pause fully, stand/jump forward in one forceful action, land on two feet and hold 2 s. Do not rock repeatedly. Rest 45 s between jumps and 60 s before E5. Use a shorter jump if landing control is lost. | Produce forward impulse after external seat support removes the standing countermovement, making initiation deliberate. |
| 5 | **Low-Hurdle Lateral Rebound Return** | **1 × 2 out-and-back pairs = 4 landings**. Both feet together over a low hurdle and immediately back once; stop after the return. One pair begins on each side. Keep height modest, feet clear. Rest 60 s between pairs and 60 s before E6; lower hurdle for clearance or stiffness loss. | Reverse lateral force quickly after a small obstacle crossing, instead of the deliberate sticks used earlier. |
| 6 | **Hand-Release Line Intercept** | **1 × 4 releases, 2 each direction**. Coach may move hands but gently rolls the ball only once; athlete bursts ≤2 m laterally on the separate path after release, braking within 1 m. No ball contact/catching. Rest 45 s between trials and 90 s before strength; simplify hand movement if guessing appears. | Wait for the actual ball-release line when hand positioning is ambiguous, then move the body into alignment on a separate path. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill — both feet supported** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s/foot**. Keep both feet supported and work one target foot at a time: maintain heel and metatarsal contact while gently raising the arch, with long toes. Relax 5 s between holds; use seated support instead if toes curl. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Maintain a planted foot base for a split-step landing without more calf fatigue. |
| 2 | **Half-Kneeling Pallof Press — light band** | **1 × 5/side, 2 s reach hold**. Coach supplies a steady sideways band pull at chest height while athlete half-kneels and presses forward without rotating. Use comfortable knee contact; use standing split stance instead if kneeling is unsuitable. Reduce tension if trunk turns. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Resist a sideways trunk pull while the limbs act, supporting a stable stance against a misleading shoulder cue. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goblet Squat** | **2 × 6 bilateral**. Hold one dumbbell at the chest; sit between the hips with whole-foot pressure and rise smoothly. Use an owned depth; reduce load/range for heel lift or trunk collapse. 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain bilateral force from a held ready position; no increase is inferred from the new decision task. |
| 2 | **Split Squat — dumbbells at sides** | **1 × 6/side**. Keep both feet on the floor in a stationary staggered stance; front heel down, controlled descent, drive through the lead leg. Reduce depth/load for pelvic rotation or rear-leg jumping. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Stable lead-leg drive after the split-step landing, with one set/leg to manage unilateral landing overlap. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain hinge strength so holding a feint does not require a collapsed trunk. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Target adductor/pelvis support as lateral go/hold decisions load the plant. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Controlled single-leg ankle force for leaving promptly after commitment. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Bent-knee ankle force from the low ready position with a modest calf dose. |

**Anchor delivery:** Coach faces the athlete on a parallel path, athlete at the 5 m midpoint. In each set present one left commitment, one right commitment and one shoulder-only feint with feet/hips remaining centred; shuffle the order without telling the athlete. A true hip displacement means burst 2 m along the athlete lane in the same global direction and stop within another 1 m. On the feint, hold the stance for the 3 s trial. If this is too difficult, the coach makes the difference more obvious within the same six trials; do not substitute a memorized colour cue.

**Space and delivery:** Use the 10 m lane from 2–8 m for lateral travel: start 5 m, accelerate toward 3/7 m, stop by 2/8 m. A separate coach/ball path must have ≥2 m verified separation. E2 travels forward from 2–4 m then stops by 7 m. E6 uses a gently rolled light medicine ball on the coach path; athlete runs only on the separate path to align with its left/right release line, never into the ball. A stable bench fitting feet-flat seated jumping and one sufficiently low hurdle are required for E4/E5; lower the obstacle/distance rather than increasing jump height.

**Readiness and stopping:** First scheduled attempts, at controlled demand, count toward the listed dose and establish today’s execution. Hold the simpler stated version when recent execution/recovery is unknown. Stop the affected exercise for pain, slipping or unsafe landing/stopping; after two consecutive technical misses, reduce the stated distance, speed or cue complexity for the remaining attempts, or end it. Do not add make-up reps or force the paper count. Record actual omissions and modified rules. Landings are controlled; no maximal jump-distance chase.
