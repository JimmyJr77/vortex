# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 8 of 12

**Primary effort:** Resolve a second direction decision after the first movement has begun.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Class 7 used early information to choose one lane. E1 now presents a second real body commitment after the athlete is moving, at low entry speed and short distance. Reciprocal split-stance force, bilateral forward rebound, lateral skipping impulse, rotational release and a grounded U-turn supply distinct reacceleration tools. Complexity, not distance or fatigue, is the intended change.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: log first and second decisions separately, including a correct first choice followed by an incorrect second choice. Also record whether the athlete could brake/reorient with the knee tracking the foot. No faster-entry progression if a second cue produces an uncontrolled reach.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-Junction Pursuit Read** | **1 × 4 trials with two decisions each**. Travel 1.5 m + 1 m + 1 m, then stop within 2 m; receive exactly two body cues. Rest 90 s between trials and 90 s before E2. Use a clear pause between cues for uncertain control. | Main re-planning task: respond to a new opponent commitment after initiating the first route. |
| 2 | **Split-Switch Jump to Stick** | **1 × 2 per starting lead = 4 jumps**. Low jump from a shallow split stance, switch legs in flight, land split and hold 2 s. Full reset between jumps; no continuous scissor fatigue set. Rest 30 s between attempts, 60 s before E3. Reduce height/switch length for pelvic rotation. | Exchange lead/trail leg roles rapidly so either leg can receive the next force demand. |
| 3 | **Forward Two-Foot Rebound Pair** | **1 × 2 pairs = 4 landings**. Two small consecutive forward jumps from two feet; rebound after the first, stick the second. Reset outside the active strip. Rest 75 s between pairs, 60 s before E4. Shorten travel for collapsed knees or loud prolonged contacts. | Re-use a forward landing for another forward push without pausing, supporting a route that continues after the second read. |
| 4 | **Lateral Power Skip** | **1 × 2 skip hops each direction = 4 hop landings**. Step-hop sideways, landing each hop on its takeoff foot; alternate the stepping support, two hops each way over ≤2 m/direction. Ordinary connecting steps are additional locomotor contacts. Rest 60 s between directions and 60 s before E5. Keep height modest and stop for foot crossing/tangling. | Coordinate a lateral step into an upward/sideways single-leg impulse while keeping the trunk facing the play. |
| 5 | **Rotational Medicine-Ball Shot-Put** | **1 × 2 throws each arm side**. Hold a light ball at the rear shoulder, load the rear hip, pivot feet/hips and push-release forward with one arm plus guiding hand. No elbow-only throw, jump or catch. Rest 30 s between throws, 60 s before E6. Reduce load if hips/feet do not turn together. | Express a pivoting leg/hip drive through a unilateral release, complementing two-handed scoop throws rather than duplicating them. |
| 6 | **Short U-Turn Reacceleration** | **1 × 1 turn each way = 2 trials**. Accelerate 2 m, brake through short steps in the turn pocket, turn 180 degrees using several foot contacts, accelerate 1 m back and stop in ≤2 m. Rest 75 s between trials and 90 s before strength. Slow entry if turning requires a fixed-knee twist. | Practise a grounded full reversal as one possible recovery when the second route is closed. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mini-Band Lateral Walk — light** | **1 × 4 steps each direction**. Secure a light band loop above the knees, shallow athletic stance; take short steps with feet apart and pelvis level. Reduce tension for trunk sway. If no suitable loop, use standing manual-resistance hip abduction instead, 1 × 4/side. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light frontal hip control while successive decisions change support legs. |
| 2 | **Half-Kneeling Pallof Press — light band** | **1 × 5/side, 2 s reach hold**. Coach supplies a steady sideways band pull at chest height while athlete half-kneels and presses forward without rotating. Use comfortable knee contact; use standing split stance instead if kneeling is unsuitable. Reduce tension if trunk turns. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Resist trunk rotation so the next foot placement is not pulled off line by the upper body. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 6/side**. Keep both feet on the floor in a stationary staggered stance; front heel down, controlled descent, drive through the lead leg. Reduce depth/load for pelvic rotation or rear-leg jumping. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Primary force anchor for transferring work between lead legs after a second decision. |
| 2 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 5/side**. Step sideways from standing, sit into the working hip with whole-foot pressure, then return to the starting stance. Reduce depth/load for groin discomfort, knee collapse or twisting. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain the lateral plant-return pattern with reduced sets alongside reciprocal jumps. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Maintain hinge force while keeping fatigue available for two-decision learning. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Adductor/pelvic force under a reversal without longer hold or lever progression. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Ankle push from either reselected lead leg. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Low-stance ankle force after the second plant. |

**Anchor delivery:** Use a marked two-junction path in a verified up-to-10 m × 7 m bay, coach on a protected parallel path. Athlete travels 1.5 m toward junction one; coach commits to a 1 m left/right diagonal branch. After the athlete has taken its first step, coach either continues that route or opens toward a second 1 m branch. Athlete follows the body information once more, then stops within 2 m. Four trials: two initial choices each side; balance continue/change on the second cue. No unannounced third cue. Start with a clear pause between cues if actual single-cue control is unknown; the pause remains inside the same drill and trial count.

**Space and delivery:** Mark approach, both junctions and branch stopping buffers before starting. The entire route is ≤3.5 m movement plus 2 m stopping, within the 10 m longitudinal limit; minimum combined-bay width is a delivery requirement to verify. Close crossing traffic. E5 uses an empty throw lane from 2 m into a low 2–7 m landing zone; do not catch the return. E6 uses a 1 m-wide turning pocket, starting 2 m, moving to 4 m and returning toward 3 m before stopping; no pivot on a locked foot. Full-route width is a requirement, not confirmed inventory: branch stopping extensions can exceed a 4 m bay. Compact same-drill option: E1 approach 1 m, first branch 0.5 m, second branch 0.5 m, then 1 m stopping, with cues clearly separated. Keep four two-decision trials and no extra cuts. Mark both complete branch possibilities inside a verified 4 m-wide bay; total E1 movement 8 m plus up to 4 m stopping. This reduces travel, not the number of decisions. At setup, mark complete body/landing envelopes, both possible routes, run-off and the coach position; a floor-line diagram alone does not establish clearance. If even the compact footprint is unavailable, resolve the layout before delivery.

**Readiness and stopping:** First scheduled attempts, at controlled demand, count toward the listed dose and establish today’s execution. Hold the simpler stated version when recent execution/recovery is unknown. Stop the affected exercise for pain, slipping or unsafe landing/stopping; after two consecutive technical misses, reduce the stated distance, speed or cue complexity for the remaining attempts, or end it. Do not add make-up reps or force the paper count. Record actual omissions and modified rules. Landings are controlled; no maximal jump-distance chase.
