# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 10 of 12

**Primary effort:** Choose the available route and reach an interception line with fewer wasted steps.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** After timing a window in Class 9, select where to go when one route is covered. E1 is an offensive two-gate choice, not a follow-the-coach task. Unilateral quarter-turn landing, a raised two-foot target, a sharper grounded cut, diagonal trunk force and an inside-route interception give distinct mechanical or perceptual support.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: correct open-gate choice, no extra movement toward the covered gate, and control through the plant/stop. Record both route choices and the coach’s coverage position. Different task rules mean earlier choice percentages are context, not direct test scores.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-Gate Advantage Choice** | **1 × 4 trials, 2 open routes each**. Coach clearly occupies one corresponding gate on the separate path; athlete bursts 1.5 m toward the other, stops within 2 m. Rest 75 s between trials and 90 s before E2. Increase cue clarity if the athlete moves toward coverage. | Main affordance task: select the route an opponent cannot currently cover instead of mirroring their direction. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Quarter-Turn Hop to Stick** | **1 × 1 per leg per turn direction = 4 hops**. Low hop on the same leg with a quarter turn, then hold 2 s; each leg turns once each way. No loaded twist on the floor. Rest 45 s between hops and 60 s before E3. Use a smaller turn within this drill for uncertain control. | Reorient onto a single-leg base, distinct from Class 7’s shared two-foot quarter-turn landing. |
| 3 | **Low Box Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 jumps**. Two-foot jump onto a low stable box, land fully on it and hold 2 s; step down, never jump off. Rest 45 s between jumps and 60 s before E4. Lower box if feet clip or landing requires a deep tuck/squat. | Express force toward a raised target while reducing the landing fall distance, preserving quality for route choices. |
| 4 | **90-Degree Run-Through Cut** | **1 × 2 cuts each side**. Run 2 m, brake through short contacts, redirect 90 degrees for 1 m and stop within 2 m. Do not pivot on a locked knee. Rest 60 s between trials, 60 s before E5. Slow entry and use a wider stepping turn if needed. | Produce a sharper grounded redirection than the earlier oblique cut, using a short controlled approach. |
| 5 | **Slam Ball Rotational Slam — diagonal** | **1 × 2 each diagonal = 4 slams**. Light low-rebound slam ball: lift comfortably, drive it down just outside one foot while allowing both feet/hips to pivot; finish balanced. No catch/rebound jump. Rest 30 s between slams, 60 s before E6. Reduce load/range for lumbar arch or a fixed-knee twist. | Rapidly direct trunk/hip force diagonally while feet pivot and the body remains stacked; supports decisive reorientation. |
| 6 | **Inside-Route Interception** | **1 × 4 routes, 2 each side**. Coach takes a slow 3 m curved route to one of two gates on a separate path. Athlete reads the exit and travels the corresponding direct 1.5 m diagonal to their own parallel gate; stop within 2 m. No contact or shared finish. Rest 75 s between trials, 90 s before strength. Slow/show more of the coach arc if the target is unclear. | Use opponent path information to select a shorter intercept line rather than following the entire curved route. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Half-Kneeling Pallof Press — light band** | **1 × 5/side, 2 s reach hold**. Coach supplies a steady sideways band pull at chest height while athlete half-kneels and presses forward without rotating. Use comfortable knee contact; use standing split stance instead if kneeling is unsuitable. Reduce tension if trunk turns. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Light resistance to unintended trunk rotation while the athlete chooses a route. |
| 2 | **Lateral Step-Down — light dumbbell** | **1 × 4/leg**. Lower for 3 s from a suitably low stable platform, free heel lightly taps; keep standing heel down. Use bodyweight or reduce range for pelvic/knee drift. If no platform fits, use a shallow supported single-leg squat instead, same dose. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Control the knee and pelvis over one foot before the more demanding unilateral strength work. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Lunge — goblet dumbbell** | **1 × 5/side**. Step sideways from standing, sit into the working hip with whole-foot pressure, then return to the starting stance. Reduce depth/load for groin discomfort, knee collapse or twisting. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Maintain outside-plant and push-back strength for rejecting the covered route. |
| 2 | **Dumbbell Step-Up** | **1 × 6/side**. Use a stable box below the point where the pelvis tilts; whole lead foot supported. Drive up without a trailing-leg hop and step down under control. Lower box/load if the return drops. If no box fits, use floor-supported Split Squat instead at the same sets/reps per leg, 2–3 RIR, with no other split-squat slot in this class. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Lead-leg force onto a stable support, matching the raised-target and single-support work. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Retain hip-hinge strength for controlled sharp-cut braking. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Support adductor and pelvic force when the exit direction changes. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Maintain ankle push from the newly selected route leg. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Maintain bent-knee ankle force for a low sharper plant. |

**Anchor delivery:** Mark two athlete exit gates 1.5 m diagonally left/right from the start, each with 2 m stopping space. Coach on a protected parallel representation of the gates visibly covers one before the athlete moves. Athlete chooses the other, open gate, then stops. Four trials, two each open route, order hidden. The opponent’s gate coverage is the information, not a spoken opposite-direction command. If the choice is uncertain, use a clearly wider coach commitment without making the athlete run faster.

**Space and delivery:** Verify an up-to-10 m × 7 m combined bay containing both exit routes/buffers and the separate coach path. E3 uses a stable low box with full-foot landing area; height must allow a quiet tall landing without a high knee tuck, and a safe step-down route. E4 has 2 m approach, 1 m 90-degree exit and 2 m buffer. E5 uses a verified low-rebound slam ball and isolated floor zone, no overhead obstruction or person near the bounce path. E6 maps separate corresponding gates for coach and athlete; paths never intersect. Full-route width is a requirement, not confirmed inventory: branch stopping extensions can exceed a 4 m bay. Compact same-drill option: E1 and E6 use 1 m diagonal travel plus 1 m stopping (four trials each: 4 m travel and up to 4 m stopping per drill). Rotate E4 so a 1 m approach crosses the width and its 1 m 90-degree exit plus 2 m stopping lies along lane length (four trials: 8 m travel and up to 8 m stopping). Keep the same decision rules/counts and controlled speed. Verify a 4 m-wide bay with margins and a protected coach position. At setup, mark complete body/landing envelopes, both possible routes, run-off and the coach position; a floor-line diagram alone does not establish clearance. If even the compact footprint is unavailable, resolve the layout before delivery.
