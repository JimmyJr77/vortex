# Reactive Agility and Anticipation — Class 6 of 12

**Primary effort:** Close space, brake in time and deny the opponent’s first exit.

**Framework:** Agility > Reactive agility and anticipation. **Baseline:** ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and current readiness. Prior classes are prescribed, not known to have been performed.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Use the retreat/recovery context to train the opposite problem: approaching without overrunning. E1 is a controlled closeout with a live exit. Vertical extension/landing, forward obstacle clearance, a fore–aft single-leg rebound, moving lateral force release and time-to-arrival reading support the brake and next push.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** E1: select the right exit while keeping the stopping route available, without a reaching final plant or running past the junction. Log approach length, early-cue regression and correct/control results separately.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the primary performance task and comes first while fresh. Coaches provide cues; athletes do not add leader rounds or extra tests. Physical support drills are not themselves evidence of anticipation gains.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Closeout-to-Exit Denial** | **1 × 4 trials, 2 exits each**. Approach 3 m, take the indicated 1 m exit, stop in 2 m. Coach commits before the final metre of approach. Rest 75 s between trials and 90 s before E2. Cue earlier/shorten approach for overshoot. | Main task: absorb an approach while reading where the opponent can leave, then direct the next step. |
| 2 | **Countermovement Jump to Stick** | **1 × 4 jumps**. Natural arm swing, jump vertically and land on both feet; hold 2 s. Do not chase maximal height. Rest 30 s between jumps, 60 s before E3. Lower height for noisy/asymmetric or unstable landings. | Rapid extension followed by bilateral force acceptance, supporting a tall-to-low closeout transition. |
| 3 | **Low-Hurdle Forward Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 jumps**. Two-foot takeoff over one low hurdle, two-foot landing and 2 s hold; walk around to reset, not back-jump. Rest 45 s between attempts, 60 s before E4. Lower hurdle or shorten travel if clearance/landing fails. | Coordinate forward clearance with deliberate shared-foot braking beyond an external obstacle. |
| 4 | **Single-Leg Forward–Back Rebound** | **1 × 1 pair/leg = 4 landings**. On one leg hop a short distance forward then immediately back once, stick the return; repeat on the other leg after 75 s. Rest 60 s before E5. Reduce distance for a long or unstable reversal contact. | Reverse a small forward impulse back through the same plant, supporting a closeout that must give ground. |
| 5 | **Step-In Lateral Medicine-Ball Scoop Toss** | **1 × 2 throws each direction**. Take one deliberate side step, load that hip, pivot both feet as needed and release a light ball sideways into the cleared lane. No jump/catch. Rest 30 s between throws and 60 s before E6. Shorten step/load if the knee twists or the release is slow. | Convert one incoming step into lateral leg/hip impulse; differs from stationary lateral release by accepting approach momentum. |
| 6 | **Rolling-Ball Arrival Intercept** | **1 × 4 trials × 2 m**. Coach rolls a light ball 3 m along the separate path at a moderate consistent speed. Athlete may choose the departure moment, travel 2 m to their own parallel line and stop within 2 m. No ball contact. Rest 60 s between trials and 90 s before strength. Slow the roll if the only solution is an unsafe rush. | Judge time to arrival, choosing when to leave so the body reaches a line under control before a slow moving object does. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening only; finish ready for primary strength, not fatigued.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Step-Down — light dumbbell** | **1 × 4/leg**. Lower for 3 s from a suitably low stable platform, free heel lightly taps; keep standing heel down. Use bodyweight or reduce range for pelvic/knee drift. If no platform fits, use a shallow supported single-leg squat instead, same dose. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Easy knee/pelvis alignment in controlled lowering before the closeout-related strength work. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill — both feet supported** | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s/foot**. Keep both feet supported and work one target foot at a time: maintain heel and metatarsal contact while gently raising the arch, with long toes. Relax 5 s between holds; use seated support instead if toes curl. Rest 30 s between sides/holds and 30 s before the next exercise. Stay at RPE 3–4/10, at least five clean reps or ample hold time in reserve. | Preserve the planted tripod during the transition from braking to pushing. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working doses below. Dynamic sets: **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; isometric primary holds: about **5 s clean hold reserve**. Use controlled lowering (about 2 s unless specified), purposeful ascent and normal breathing. No failure or maximal tests; choose loads through demonstrated technique, not age. These are serial strength sets, not a circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goblet Squat** | **2 × 6 bilateral**. Hold one dumbbell at the chest; sit between the hips with whole-foot pressure and rise smoothly. Use an owned depth; reduce load/range for heel lift or trunk collapse. 120 s between rounds and 120 s before the next exercise. | Load whole-leg force acceptance and return from the lowered closeout stance. |
| 2 | **Dumbbell Step-Up** | **1 × 6/side**. Use a stable box below the point where the pelvis tilts; whole lead foot supported. Drive up without a trailing-leg hop and step down under control. Lower box/load if the return drops. If no box fits, use floor-supported Split Squat instead at the same sets/reps per leg, 2–3 RIR, with no other split-squat slot in this class. Rest 90 s between sides; 120 s before the next exercise. | Lead-leg extension and a controlled descent without extra lateral lunge volume. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 bilateral**. Hinge from standing with soft knees; keep weights close and stop descent where the pelvis/spine remain controlled. Finish tall without leaning back. Reduce range/load for rounding. Rest 120 s before the next exercise. | Hip hinge contributes to decelerating the approach without collapsing the trunk. |
| 4 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 s/side**. Upper knee bent about 90 degrees on a stable bench, forearm under shoulder; lift the lower leg and keep pelvis level. Select a short lever with about 5 seconds clean hold reserve. Stop for groin, shoulder or knee pain. If bench/support is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead, 1 × 8/side at 2–3 RIR; record changed hold/repetition totals. For that replacement, prepare with 1 × 4 easy reps/side instead of the two 5 s preparation holds; retain side rest. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Adductor/pelvis support during the immediate lateral denial step. |
| 5 | **Single-Leg Calf Raise — supported standing, dumbbell** | **1 × 8/side**. Stand on flat floor with a nearly straight knee and light hand support; raise the heel, pause briefly and lower for 2 seconds. No bounce or ankle roll. Reduce load when heel height declines. Rest 60 s between sides; 60 s before the next exercise. | Straight-knee push during the approach-to-exit transition. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise — bilateral, bent-knee** | **1 × 10 bilateral**. Use a stable bench, knees near right angles, secure dumbbells on distal thighs above knees. Lower heels for 2 seconds to the floor, pause, then rise. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened range. Rest 60 s before the next exercise. | Bent-knee ankle force in the closeout brake and low first exit step. |

**Anchor delivery:** In a verified up-to-10 m × 7 m bay, athlete begins at 1 m and approaches a junction at 4 m under control. Coach outside the route and visible beyond it commits left/right before the athlete reaches 3 m. Athlete lowers through the last approach steps, redirects 1 m toward that exit and stops within the next 2 m. Four trials, two each direction. No hand block, collision, extra sprint or airborne attempt to deny the coach. If the plant is uncertain, cue at the start and shorten the approach to 2 m.

**Space and delivery:** The junction, two short exits and all buffers must fit within the measured combined bay; coach occupies a protected position outside them, ≥2 m away. E3 uses a low hurdle with clearance verified for the athlete; E4 needs only small forward/back distances. E5 releases a light ball into an empty 2–7 m length of one lane, no return catch. E6 coach rolls a ball on a separate parallel lane while athlete travels on their own marked 2 m route with 2 m stopping space; no attempt to touch/stop the ball and no shared finish corridor. Full-route width is a requirement, not confirmed inventory: branch stopping extensions can exceed a 4 m bay. Compact same-drill option: rotate the E1 T so the approach crosses the width for only 1 m and its 1 m left/right exits plus 2 m stops run along the 10 m lane length. Place the coach ahead of the junction on a separate protected position. Keep four trials, early cue and controlled speed; E1 movement becomes 8 m plus up to 8 m stopping. Verify the approach, body margins and coach space within a 4 m-wide bay. This remains closeout-and-denial, not a new exercise. At setup, mark complete body/landing envelopes, both possible routes, run-off and the coach position; a floor-line diagram alone does not establish clearance. If even the compact footprint is unavailable, resolve the layout before delivery.

**Readiness and stopping:** First scheduled attempts, at controlled demand, count toward the listed dose and establish today’s execution. Hold the simpler stated version when recent execution/recovery is unknown. Stop the affected exercise for pain, slipping or unsafe landing/stopping; after two consecutive technical misses, reduce the stated distance, speed or cue complexity for the remaining attempts, or end it. Do not add make-up reps or force the paper count. Record actual omissions and modified rules. Landings are controlled; no maximal jump-distance chase.
