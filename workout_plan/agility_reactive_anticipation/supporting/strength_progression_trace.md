## Light-strength continuity

The two stabilization/resilience slots deliberately reuse short-foot, controlled step-down, light ankle eversion, easy heel-dig holds, light hip walks or half-kneeling anti-rotation work when those jobs recur. These remain **light strengthening**, with per-side dose and reserve stated in each class; they are not extra repeated agility drills. Do not turn them into pre-fatigue challenges. Keep the easiest effective load; progression here is better control or an owned range, not burnout.

The no-repeat request prevents a repeated agility-test protocol across these workouts. Compare strength only when variant, load, range, tempo, set context and recovery are sufficiently matched; the fact that the exercise name recurs is not enough.
