# Drill identity and local-source register — all 12 classes

The 72 scheduled agility/explosive prescriptions have distinct names and reviewed execution identities. Movement families recur; these are not claimed to be 72 unrelated movement patterns. Uniqueness rests on support/contact sequence, contraction, trajectory, incoming momentum or decision problem—not spelling, load or cone colour. Identity strings in the JSON are local audit descriptions, **not exercise-library identifiers**. Strength and stabilization may recur intentionally.
