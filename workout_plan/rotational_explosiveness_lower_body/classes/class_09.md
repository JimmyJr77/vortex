# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 9 of 12

**Primary effort:** Create a small amount of separation, produce a low rotational counter-kick and recover without uncontrolled spin or drift. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 8, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The new task is a change in bodyweight direction. Supporting jobs cover backward loading before a turn, lateral return force, hip unwinding with a raised free leg, a short hooked return sweep and a stable backward catch. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per kicking leg after one controlled retreat step, with no second retreat, trapped knee or extra recovery hop. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Drop-step loaded rotational jump to stick** | **1 × 2 per loading side = 4 attempts.** Take one short drop step backward to load the named hip, gather onto two feet and drive into a low turn toward the original forward line. Finish the turn in the air and land on two feet, holding 2 s. Keep the drop/gather/flight within 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Reverse a small backward load into forward-facing rotational leg force. Use a shorter drop and smaller turn instead if the gather twists a planted knee or balance is lost. |
| 2 | **Retreat-step low roundhouse counter** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** Make one short retreat step onto the support leg, arrest the backward drift, turn that support foot with the pelvis and execute a low opposite-leg roundhouse. Recoil into a stable stance within the 2 m segment. No opponent or pad. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Directly change backward bodyweight travel into a controlled rotational counter action. Use a smaller/slower retreat or a brief settled plant instead if the athlete continues drifting; do not reach with the kick to compensate. |
| 3 | **Two-bound lateral return to starting leg** | **1 × 1 per initial takeoff leg = 2 attempts.** One attempt is a short bound to the opposite leg and one return bound to the original leg, then a 2 s stick. No turn. Mirror the initial leg; two attempts give four unilateral landings. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Qualify opposite-leg acceptance and pushback needed to recover from lateral displacement. Use instead two separately reset short bounds if immediate return is not proven; same four landings, with extra reset time. Reduce distance for a hard receiving knee. |
| 4 | **Pre-chambered hip-unwind step-down** | **1 × 2 per raised leg = 4 attempts.** Start with a low folded-knee chamber and the pelvis comfortably turned, using light fingertip support if needed. Briskly unwind the pelvis with a freely turning support foot while bringing the raised leg back to a rear stance position; finish still. No kick extension. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Recover the rotational base when the free leg begins suspended instead of available on the floor. Use a smaller starting turn/chamber instead for foot sticking or a slapping return; do not torque the standing knee backward. |
| 5 | **Low bent-knee hook shadow sweep** | **1 × 2 per sweeping leg = 4 attempts.** From a comfortable open kick stance, lift the free thigh low, extend only slightly and sweep inward while folding the knee, then place the foot under control. Keep the arc small, below hip height, and coordinate the support-foot turn; no spin or contact. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Practice the coupled inward thigh sweep and knee folding used to recover a rotational leg action. Use an entirely folded-knee inward hook instead if the longer lever cannot be controlled; stop for groin or knee discomfort. |
| 6 | **Backward push-off to parallel two-foot catch** | **1 × 2 per lead push leg = 4 attempts.** From a shallow split stance, push backward a short distance from the named lead leg and receive the low flight on two parallel feet. Stay facing forward, no turn, and hold 2 s. Travel at most 0.5 m on a coach-cleared path. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Accept a small backward displacement in a stable base without adding a single-leg rotating landing. Use less displacement instead if the heels slap or trunk tips; clear the route before every attempt and do not jump into an unseen queue. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** The retreat path must be clear and the low hook action coached at a short lever. Immediate unilateral return bounds are conditional; without demonstrated control, use the separated bounds in the same slot. No new maximum range, spinning or opponent pressure is added. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** Supported hip control limits pelvic spill during unwinding; easy foot doming keeps pressure organized when direction changes. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain planted push capacity after the retreat with one working round. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **2 × 6 per leg/side = 24 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, **120 s between rounds**, then **120 s**. | Main strength investment: posterior-chain control and hip extension when arresting backward travel and restoring stance. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain lateral/adductor return force without a second lateral-squat round after the hooked sweeps and return bounds. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Keep direct hip-turn torque reproducible while practicing return-direction control. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active chamber force during the counter action and suspended recovery. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Support the soft-knee ankle base during withdrawal and the backward catch. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Strength progression decision:** Move the extra compound round from Class 8 split squat to the hinge: [1,2,1,1,1,1], still 88 working reps. The hinge now serves backward-momentum control; maintain current qualified load and avoid simultaneous range/load progression. A small load increase on a recurring lift is optional only with reported completed clean work at the target reserve, acceptable recovery and current readiness. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain. Do not increase load, range and sets together.

The two-bound drill has two flights per attempt, already counted. Separated bounds preserve four landings but change reactive demand.
