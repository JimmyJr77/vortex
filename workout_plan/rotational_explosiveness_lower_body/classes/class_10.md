# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 10 of 12

**Primary effort:** Select the requested rotational action from an early coach cue, then execute it with the same control as planned work. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 9, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The added demand is committing the correct support leg/direction before force expression. Familiar movement families reduce unnecessary new mechanics. These are explicitly cue-based variants, not six newly invented movement patterns; each has a different support or launch job. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: correct-cue AND technically valid E2 attempts out of four per leg. Record wrong-leg choices, anticipations and kick-quality faults separately; no reaction-time or impact-force score is claimed. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Point-cued open-step hip turn** | **1 × 2 per called turn direction = 4 attempts.** Begin square and still. After the coach points left or right, take one short opening step and turn the feet/pelvis toward that line, then hold a kick stance 2 s. Cue is given before any movement; no kick or jump. Rest **60 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Select the support/turn direction before the leg-driven release. Use an announced direction instead if cue errors dominate; preserve the same physical drill and record a planned version. |
| 2 | **Coach-called-leg low roundhouse** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per called kicking leg = 8 attempts.** Start from a parallel athletic stance. The coach calls the kicking leg before movement; shift to the other leg, pivot with the pelvis and execute one low roundhouse with active recoil. Each round has two calls for each leg in a privately varied order. Rest **60 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Directly link support-leg selection to the hip-led kick without an extra entry task. Use a preannounced leg instead if the choice causes a rushed plant; cue accuracy never overrides safe mechanics. |
| 3 | **Early-point rotational jump to stick** | **1 × 2 per called turn direction = 4 attempts.** From a still parallel stance, receive the coach direction cue, then use a natural dip and a low two-foot turn up to 90 degrees. Land aligned and hold 2 s. The cue arrives before the dip, never in flight. Rest **60 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Commit a turn direction before takeoff and preserve landing organization despite a decision demand. Use an announced smaller turn instead if the athlete starts early or cannot land facing the intended line. |
| 4 | **Cone-color call to forward plant and chamber** | **1 × 2 per called chamber leg = 4 attempts.** Two cone colors are assigned to left/right chamber legs and remain outside the travel path. On the coach color call, take the corresponding short support-foot entry, turn that foot/pelvis and lift the called low knee chamber. No kick extension; hold 2 s. Rest **60 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Map a cue to the correct receiving foot before accelerating the free thigh. Use a direct leg-name cue instead if color mapping interferes; shorten entry for any loss of foot/knee alignment. |
| 5 | **Auditory-go pre-chambered thigh whip** | **1 × 2 per moving leg = 4 attempts.** Set a low bent-knee chamber on the named leg. Wait still for the coach go word, then make one short brisk roundhouse thigh sweep with the knee folded and support foot free to turn; reset fully. Vary the go timing modestly, without long fatiguing holds. Rest **60 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Release proximal rotation from a prepared leg position without anticipating the start. Use a predictable countdown instead if waiting posture fails; keep the pre-cue hold at or below about 3 s. |
| 6 | **Direction-call lateral bound to split catch** | **1 × 2 per called travel direction = 4 attempts.** After an early left/right call, push laterally from the opposite leg and land on two feet in a shallow split stance. Hold 2 s. No airborne turn, midair cue or rebound; flight stays within 1 m. Rest **60 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Select a lateral push while arriving in a stable base for the next rotational effort. Use an announced direction or shorter bound instead if choice disrupts the two-foot catch. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap. For Class 10's varied cue order, use 60 s between attempts within each round and 90 s between rounds.

**Readiness and stop rules:** All cues arrive before launch. Coach privately balances direction/leg exposure in each round and may vary attempt order; use 60 s between attempts regardless of side and 90 s between rounds. A direction error counts as an attempt and is not repeated for free. If choices degrade movement, use the announced replacements rather than add more trials. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** The same easy hip/foot work emphasizes quiet positions after decision drills; it is not another reaction block. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain the decisive planted push after the correct leg is selected. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Preserve hip-hinge strength for receiving bodyweight without adding a second hinge round. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Keep lateral plant force for either selected direction. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **2 × 8 per leg/side = 32 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, **90 s between rounds**, then **60 s**. | Main strength investment: direct controlled hip-turn torque, kept independent of the cue-speed task. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active chamber lift after support-leg selection. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Keep the flexed-knee ankle base reproducible during quick commitment. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Lighter load-preparation sets, additional to working sets:** P1: 1 × 4/side; P2: 1 × 3/side; P3: 1 × 3/side; P4: 1 × 4/side; P5: 1 × 3/side; P6: 1 × 5 bilateral (**39 reps total**). Use the same listed movement with bodyweight or lighter resistance. P1–P3: 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s before working work; P4–P5: 20 s between preparation sides, 45 s before working work; P6: 60 s before working work. Extra small ramps are allowed only to qualify that exercise's load and must be logged with their time. This is not a replacement preparation sequence. If manual resistance is used, preserve range/tempo and a credible reserve; do not claim a precise load progression from it.

**Strength progression decision:** Reallocate Class 9 hinge emphasis [1,2,1,1,1,1] to [1,1,1,2,1,1], 92 working reps. Relative loading stays 2–3 RIR; the extra hip-rotation round replaces the hinge round and does not prescribe heavier resistance. A small load increase on a recurring lift is optional only with reported completed clean work at the target reserve, acceptable recovery and current readiness. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain. Do not increase load, range and sets together.

**Workload:** 8 planned flight/landings (**8 bilateral, 0 unilateral**); **20 grounded explosive efforts**; **8 kick actions** (kick actions are a task count, not extra attempts to add). Eight bilateral landings, from the cued turn jumps and split catches. Twenty non-flight efforts include eight kicks. There are twenty-eight planned cue opportunities; correctness is logged separately from physical attempts and no additional no-go trials are hidden in the dose. Primary strength: **7 rounds / 13 separate working bouts / 92 reps**, plus 39 lighter preparation reps. Stabilization: 8 slow cycles plus six 5 s foot holds. Failed attempts still add exposure. Unintended hops, landing corrections and actual entry/return steps are logged separately; no equivalent-contact safety score is used.

**Delivery and timing:** Approximately **85–92 minutes outside Access & Prepare 1**, with prompt equipment access. The audit models actual prescribed resets, rests, both sides, preparation sets and 13 minutes for instruction/setup/recording. Group queues, extra coaching, ramps and longer recovery are additional. Full session duration is unresolved; do not assume a 60- or 90-minute total booking fits. Cones are visual identifiers outside the landing/sweep area, not obstacles. Entries/bounds stay within 1–2 m plus 2 m clear recovery inside a 10 m lane. One active cued athlete at a time unless staffing and separation are actually verified. Confirm lane width, swept-leg clearance, waiting/return areas, bench fit, secure band loops and staffing. Run each athlete's four sections in order; equipment rotation is not permission to do strength before explosiveness. No throwing wall, pad, cable machine, external band anchor, rack or device capability is presumed.

**Recovery and placement:** Review actual recent/upcoming kicking, sport, gym, other focus classes and separately programmed tumbling. Around 48–72 hours before another comparably demanding lower-body exposure is an initial consideration, adjusted for real workload and recovery. These are numbered classes, not twelve consecutive training days. A different focus name does not remove overlapping adductor, calf or hip loading.
