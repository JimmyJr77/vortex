# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 6 of 12

**Primary effort:** Produce a short clean rotational kick from a simple base while reducing landing and strength volume. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 5, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** This is a workload consolidation, not a claim that unfamiliar drills are already mastered. Supporting tasks simplify balance or remove large travel: ankle-led release, leg-driven turning without an arm swing, a low outward sweep, a retreat plant and supported return-direction hip torque. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per leg from the same parallel stance, with one weight shift, no extra step and a controlled low finish. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Supported calf-pop to low knee chamber** | **1 × 2 per support leg = 4 attempts.** With fingertip bench support, keep the support knee softly bent and make a brisk heel rise while bringing the opposite bent knee to a low chamber. The supporting forefoot stays down; no hop or large turn. Lower and reset. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Coordinate the ankle-led heel release with unloading the free leg, without another landing. Use a smaller heel rise/chamber instead if the ankle rolls or the forefoot leaves the floor. |
| 2 | **Parallel-stance low roundhouse** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** Start with both feet parallel in a comfortable athletic stance. Shift onto the support leg and turn its foot/pelvis into one low roundhouse with the other leg; recoil and return to the original parallel base. No preselected front/rear fighting stance or entry step. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Organize support selection and the hip turn from a neutral two-foot base. Widen the starting base slightly or reduce the kick arc instead if the athlete needs a rescue step; feet must be free to turn. |
| 3 | **Hands-on-hips rotational jump to stick** | **1 × 2 per turn direction = 4 attempts.** Keep hands resting on the hips. Use a small natural dip and turn in a low two-foot jump through a controllable arc up to 90 degrees; land aligned and hold 2 s. No arm wind-up or repeated bounce. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Observe leg/hip-driven rotational takeoff with arm-swing assistance removed. Use a smaller turn instead if removing the arms disrupts landing; do not chase the angle achieved with free arms. |
| 4 | **Supported low outward crescent sweep** | **1 × 2 per sweeping leg = 4 attempts.** With light fingertip support, start the low free foot near the midline and sweep it outward in a small arc with a soft knee and comfortable hip turn, then fold the knee and set down. No high lift or forced crossing start. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Express the outward hip sweep and its arrest, complementing the inward return action examined in Class 4. Use a bent-knee outward sweep instead if the longer lever causes groin pull or pelvic lean; do not rotate against a fixed support foot. |
| 5 | **Retreat-step hip-open plant** | **1 × 2 per receiving leg = 4 attempts.** Take one short step backward onto the named leg and open feet/pelvis together into a shallow kick stance. Stop the backward drift within the same 2 m segment and hold 2 s. No kick, run or fast blind travel. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Retain a usable rotational base while receiving backward bodyweight instead of advancing or moving laterally. Use a shorter, slower retreat instead if the athlete cannot see/control the clear route or heels collide. |
| 6 | **Seated self-resisted hip external-rotation acceleration** | **1 × 2 per hip = 4 attempts.** Sit with thighs supported and knee near 90 degrees. The working foot moves a short distance inward as the hip externally rotates; the same-side hand gives very light opposing pressure at the inner lower shin. Accelerate smoothly, then return slowly with knee/pelvis still. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Give the return-direction hip rotators a small rapid torque task with standing balance removed. Reduce pressure/range instead for any knee/ankle motion or pinching; never jerk into end range. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** Use supported versions and small arcs; the new neutral-start kick still needs qualification. The external-rotation task must move at the hip with a quiet knee. If unfamiliar coordination needs slow instruction, log technique-only attempts rather than insist on full speed. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** The same easy hip/foot strengthening preserves stable execution conditions while the overall demanding work is reduced. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain a challenging planted-leg push with one working round rather than adding fatigue during consolidation. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Preserve posterior-chain strength and controlled stance support at reduced total volume. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain lateral hip/adductor force with one round after the preceding lateral emphasis. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Keep a reproducible direct internal-rotation strength anchor while the explosive task also samples the return direction. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active chamber-lifting strength without a second round. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Maintain bent-knee ankle force with one working set and full control. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Lighter load-preparation sets, additional to working sets:** P1: 1 × 4/side; P2: 1 × 3/side; P3: 1 × 3/side; P4: 1 × 4/side; P5: 1 × 3/side; P6: 1 × 5 bilateral (**39 reps total**). Use the same listed movement with bodyweight or lighter resistance. P1–P3: 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s before working work; P4–P5: 20 s between preparation sides, 45 s before working work; P6: 60 s before working work. Extra small ramps are allowed only to qualify that exercise's load and must be logged with their time. This is not a replacement preparation sequence. If manual resistance is used, preserve range/tempo and a credible reserve; do not claim a precise load progression from it.

**Strength progression decision:** Reduce Class 5 [1,1,2,1,1,2] to one round per lift [1,1,1,1,1,1]: 76 working reps and 11 working bouts. Relative effort remains challenging at 2–3 RIR; volume is reduced, not replaced by light balance work. A small load increase on a recurring lift is optional only with reported completed clean work at the target reserve, acceptable recovery and current readiness. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain. Do not increase load, range and sets together.

**Workload:** 4 planned flight/landings (**4 bilateral, 0 unilateral**); **24 grounded explosive efforts**; **12 kick actions** (kick actions are a task count, not extra attempts to add). Only four bilateral landings are planned, all from the arms-constrained rotational jump. Twenty-four non-flight efforts include twelve kick/sweep actions. Supported and seated rapid tasks are non-flight efforts, not landing contacts. Primary strength: **6 rounds / 11 separate working bouts / 76 reps**, plus 39 lighter preparation reps. Stabilization: 8 slow cycles plus six 5 s foot holds. Failed attempts still add exposure. Unintended hops, landing corrections and actual entry/return steps are logged separately; no equivalent-contact safety score is used.
