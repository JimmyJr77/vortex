# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 8 of 12

**Primary effort:** Turn a short forward entry into a low hip-led roundhouse while controlling forward drift. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 7, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The limiting demand is receiving forward momentum without a reaching, locked support knee. Supporting tasks connect a short approach to rotation, unilateral forward acceptance, the plant-to-chamber link, rear-leg propulsion and a controlled forward finish. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per leg with one support-foot entry step, no extra brake step, free foot/pelvic rotation and a finish inside the marked segment. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-step approach quarter-turn jump to stick** | **1 × 2 per turn direction = 4 attempts.** Take exactly two short controlled approach steps, gather onto two feet and jump through a turn of no more than 45 degrees. Land aligned on two feet and hold 2 s. The approach plus flight fits within 2 m, with 2 m recovery clear. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Convert small forward arrival into leg-driven rotation before the direct kick task. Use shorter approach steps and less turn instead if the gather stalls or landing drifts beyond the boundary. |
| 2 | **Forward step-in low roundhouse** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** With the kicking leg initially rear, step the support foot a short distance forward, accept weight with a soft knee, let that foot turn and launch the low rear-leg roundhouse. Recoil and recover within the same 2 m work segment. No run-up or pad. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Directly translate controlled forward arrival into pelvic rotation and free-leg acceleration. Use a shorter/slower step instead for overstriding or extra braking steps; preserve controlled brisk kick intent after the qualified plant. |
| 3 | **Forward bound to opposite-leg stick** | **1 × 2 per takeoff leg = 4 attempts.** From a short stationary split stance, push forward from one leg into one modest bound and land on the opposite leg. Stay facing forward and hold 2 s; no turn or second bound. Flight fits within 1.5 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Prepare the receiving leg for forward bodyweight without simultaneously demanding a rotating landing. Use a shorter bound or light free-toe assist at the finish instead if the receiving knee stiffens or another hop is needed. |
| 4 | **Forward plant to low rotational chamber** | **1 × 2 per chambering leg = 4 attempts.** Make one short forward receiving step onto the support foot, turn that foot/pelvis together and lift the free folded knee into a low rotational chamber. Hold 2 s, then set the foot down. Do not extend the lower leg. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Isolate acceptance-to-free-thigh timing inside the forward-entry kick without another full extension dose. Shorten the step/chamber instead if the athlete rises onto a rigid knee or has to hop. |
| 5 | **Rear-leg power step to opposite-leg plant** | **1 × 2 per push leg = 4 attempts.** Start in a compact split stance with the named leg rear. Drive from that rear hip/forefoot into one brisk forward step onto the other leg, keeping at least one foot grounded; finish with a soft receiving knee and pelvis facing the original line. No turn or kick; distance at most 1 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Supply forward propulsion before the support leg redirects it into rotation, separating the incoming push from the kick mechanics. Use a shorter step instead if the athlete unintentionally bounds; log any actual flight rather than treating it as grounded. |
| 6 | **Low roundhouse with forward step-down finish** | **1 × 2 per kicking leg = 4 attempts.** From a stationary rear-leg stance, execute a low controlled roundhouse and fold the knee, then place that foot into a short forward split stance instead of returning it to the original rear position. Arrest the pelvis with both feet aligned; no spinning through or second kick. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Control residual forward/rotational momentum when the kicking leg becomes the next lead foot. Use less kick arc and a shorter step-down instead if the athlete over-rotates or lands across the support foot. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** Forward entry stays short and planned. The athlete must show a soft receiving knee and enough room to stop; no ability is inferred from the preceding linked-contact prescription. Use shorter paths rather than faster approaches. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** The supported airplane reinforces pelvic control over the receiving hip; short-foot work supports the forward plant without more impact. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **2 × 6 per leg/side = 24 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, **120 s between rounds**, then **120 s**. | Main strength investment is the forward receiving leg and its drive into the turn. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Control the support hip as forward momentum is arrested. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Preserve lateral/adductor strength to keep the plant from spilling sideways during rotation. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain direct hip torque after a controlled sagittal arrival. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Supply active chamber lift once the forward support leg has accepted weight. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Maintain the bent-knee ankle contribution to the forward plant and heel release. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |
