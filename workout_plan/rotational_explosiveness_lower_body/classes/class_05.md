# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 5 of 12

**Primary effort:** Receive a short lateral entry on a usable support leg and turn that arrival into a low roundhouse. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 4, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The key demand is accepting lateral bodyweight without overstriding or trapping the receiving knee. Support drills distinguish a step-behind push, split-base catch, crossover entry, rapid adduction and an out-and-return plant. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per kicking leg with one controlled shuffle entry, no extra stutter step, coordinated foot/hip turn and a quiet recovered stance. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Step-Behind Lateral Bound to Stick** | **1 × 2 per travel direction = 4 attempts.** Use one small step behind to load the outside hip, then push into one short lateral bound onto the opposite leg. Face forward without an airborne turn and hold the landing 2 s. The entry and flight together fit within 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Produce lateral force after a compact cross-behind load, before adding a kick task. Keep the crossing step shallow and feet clear; use a shorter step-behind instead for tangling or a heavy landing. |
| 2 | **Single-shuffle low roundhouse** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** From a kick stance, make one short lateral shuffle toward the support side, settle that foot without an extra stutter and launch the opposite low roundhouse. Recoil and recover the stance. Entry and finish stay within 2 m; no target or continuous shuffling. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Directly convert arriving lateral mass into support-foot/pelvic rotation. Use a shorter/slower single shuffle instead if the plant overstrides or the knee cannot turn freely; maintain a brisk controlled hip action. |
| 3 | **Lateral bound to split-stance catch** | **1 × 2 per push leg = 4 attempts.** Push laterally from one leg and land on two feet arranged in a shallow split stance, the intended support foot slightly ahead. Hold 2 s; no rotation during flight or rebound. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Accept lateral impulse in a two-foot base already arranged to transfer into a kick stance. Keep the split small and both knees soft; use a shorter bound instead if the rear foot slaps down late. |
| 4 | **Crossover entry to rotational knee chamber** | **1 × 2 per chambering leg = 4 attempts.** Take one short crossover travel step and then uncross onto the support foot; rotate with that foot while lifting the free bent knee into a low chamber. Put it down into stance after a 2 s finish. No kick extension or flight; the full entry fits inside 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Organize the hip turn after a crossover approach without adding another full kick dose. Use less crossing distance and a lower chamber instead if feet tangle; do not turn while the legs remain crossed. |
| 5 | **Side-lying rapid short-lever adduction lift** | **1 × 2 per lower leg = 4 attempts.** Lie on the side with the upper foot planted comfortably in front. Keep the lower knee slightly bent; lift the lower thigh/leg a short distance briskly toward the upper side, then lower slowly. The pelvis stays stacked, no external load or jerking. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Give the adductors a small fast concentric task that supports drawing the pelvis/leg back over the lateral plant. Use less travel instead if the pelvis rolls; stop for groin discomfort. This is short-range active acceleration, not a fast stretch. |
| 6 | **Lateral jab-step return to kick stance** | **1 × 2 per outside plant leg = 4 attempts.** Make one short lateral jab step onto the outside leg, immediately push back toward the start and settle into a shallow kick stance with feet/pelvis aligned. No kick, jump or second reversal; out-and-return path fits within 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Use the lateral receiving leg to redirect bodyweight and restore a rotational launch base. Reduce excursion/speed instead for a stiff or reaching plant; the knee follows the moving foot throughout. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** Confirm short shuffle/crossover control before adding the hip turn; initial attempts use the smallest functional entry. The side-lying drill needs a comfortable clean floor area, not an assumed mat or extra equipment. Keep bound distance submaximal. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** The airplane supplies low-load control over the receiving hip; foot holds keep the outside plant from rolling off its base. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain the final planted push used after the shuffle with one challenging round. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Steady the support hip and trunk during incoming lateral momentum. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **2 × 6 per leg/side = 24 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, **120 s between rounds**, then **120 s**. | Main compound investment: load lateral hip/adductor acceptance and return from the outside plant. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain direct hip-turn torque once lateral arrival is controlled. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Keep sufficient active chamber strength without adding a second hip-flexion round after the full kicks. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **2 × 8 bilateral = 16 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Rest **90 s between sets**. | Load the bent-knee ankle base that receives and redirects the short lateral entry. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Lighter load-preparation sets, additional to working sets:** P1: 1 × 4/side; P2: 1 × 3/side; P3: 1 × 3/side; P4: 1 × 4/side; P5: 1 × 3/side; P6: 1 × 5 bilateral (**39 reps total**). Use the same listed movement with bodyweight or lighter resistance. P1–P3: 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s before working work; P4–P5: 20 s between preparation sides, 45 s before working work; P6: 60 s before working work. Extra small ramps are allowed only to qualify that exercise's load and must be logged with their time. This is not a replacement preparation sequence. If manual resistance is used, preserve range/tempo and a credible reserve; do not claim a precise load progression from it.

**Strength progression decision:** From Class 4 [1,1,1,1,2,1], move emphasis to [1,1,2,1,1,2]: 96 working reps, returning to the earlier Class 1–2 prescription total while shifting the extra compound round to the lateral squat. This is a conditional task allocation, not evidence of recovered capacity or automatic heavier loading. A small load increase on a recurring lift is optional only with reported completed clean work at the target reserve, acceptable recovery and current readiness. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain. Do not increase load, range and sets together.

**Workload:** 8 planned flight/landings (**4 bilateral, 4 unilateral**); **20 grounded explosive efforts**; **8 kick actions** (kick actions are a task count, not extra attempts to add). Eight flight/landings: four opposite-leg step-behind bounds and four split-stance bilateral catches. Twenty non-flight efforts include eight kicks. Four jab-step efforts contain one reversal each; do not equate reversals or movement metres with jump contacts. Primary strength: **8 rounds / 14 separate working bouts / 96 reps**, plus 39 lighter preparation reps. Stabilization: 8 slow cycles plus six 5 s foot holds. Failed attempts still add exposure. Unintended hops, landing corrections and actual entry/return steps are logged separately; no equivalent-contact safety score is used.

**Delivery and timing:** Approximately **78–85 minutes outside Access & Prepare 1**, with prompt equipment access. The audit models actual prescribed resets, rests, both sides, preparation sets and 13 minutes for instruction/setup/recording. Group queues, extra coaching, ramps and longer recovery are additional. Full session duration is unresolved; do not assume a 60- or 90-minute total booking fits. Entries, bounds and jab-step out-and-return each fit inside a 2 m work segment plus 2 m recovery area in one 10 m lane. Verify enough width for lateral movement and locate queues outside all swept/landing paths. Confirm lane width, swept-leg clearance, waiting/return areas, bench fit, secure band loops and staffing. Run each athlete's four sections in order; equipment rotation is not permission to do strength before explosiveness. No throwing wall, pad, cable machine, external band anchor, rack or device capability is presumed.

**Recovery and placement:** Review actual recent/upcoming kicking, sport, gym, other focus classes and separately programmed tumbling. Around 48–72 hours before another comparably demanding lower-body exposure is an initial consideration, adjusted for real workload and recovery. These are numbered classes, not twelve consecutive training days. A different focus name does not remove overlapping adductor, calf or hip loading.

**Progression note:** Class 4 isolated free-leg control; Class 5 adds short planned lateral arrival to the hip turn. Keep entry distance and speed low until actual plants are controlled; if that condition is not met, shorten the same entry rather than assume a harder approach has been earned. New drills supply the stated learning job, not evidence of improved capacity. Later classes remain conditional on their own prerequisites.
