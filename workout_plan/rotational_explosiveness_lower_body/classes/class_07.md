# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 7 of 12

**Primary effort:** Use a small controlled load-and-release action to initiate a hip turn without losing the support-foot base. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 6, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The new variable is the time between loading and force expression, not kick height, long travel or maximum jump height. The supporting jobs cover split-stance ankle coupling, grounded hip loading, free-thigh reversal, stance exchange and a small lateral rebound. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of two per leg with a quiet preparatory landing, a coordinated grounded pivot and no extra bounce before the low kick. Log linked and paused versions separately. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-contact split-stance rebound hop** | **1 × 1 per lead leg = 2 attempts.** One attempt is exactly two low hops in the same shallow split stance: land once and rebound once, then hold the second landing 2 s. Feet do not exchange or turn. Mirror the lead leg; two attempts total give four bilateral landings. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Qualify short load-release timing through a kick-like split support base. Use instead two separately reset low split-stance hops if rebound position is unproven; retain four landings and allow extra reset time. Stop for heel slap or knee drift. |
| 2 | **Single-bounce start low roundhouse** — primary anchor | **2 × 1 per kicking leg = 4 attempts.** Make one low two-foot bounce in the rear-leg kick stance, land quietly, then launch the low rear-leg roundhouse while the support foot turns freely. Recoil to stance. One attempt contains one preparatory landing and one grounded kick, not an airborne kick. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Transfer a small elastic preload into the direct rotational task. Use instead bounce, two-second landing hold, then the low kick if immediate transfer is unproven; same landing/kick counts, longer reset. Do not add a second bounce. |
| 3 | **Lateral hip rock-to-pivot release** | **1 × 2 per loaded hip = 4 attempts.** With both feet initially grounded in a comfortable stance, shift a short distance into the named hip and immediately push out of that load into a small hip turn. Let the feet turn with the pelvis and finish in kick stance; no step-in or jump. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Express force after a small continuous lateral preload without another landing. Use a smaller rock or a brief pause instead if the turn becomes a lumbar twist or foot pressure is lost. |
| 4 | **Bent-knee counter-sweep to chamber** | **1 × 2 per moving leg = 4 attempts.** Hold the free knee folded below hip height, move the thigh a small distance outward/back under control, then reverse briskly into a low forward roundhouse chamber as the support foot releases. No knee extension or ballistic end-range stretch. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Coordinate a short free-thigh loading reversal with hip-turn initiation. Use a smaller counter-sweep or a still start instead if the pelvis tips or the knee is flung beyond control. |
| 5 | **Grounded switch-step rotational knee drive** | **1 × 2 per driving leg = 4 attempts.** Exchange front/rear foot positions with two short grounded steps, let the new support foot settle and turn, and drive the free folded knee across into a low rotational chamber. No hop, knee extension or contact; finish within 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Link a fast support-role exchange to proximal rotational output without a full additional kick. Use slower, shorter exchange steps instead until the legs uncross cleanly; never pivot while the feet are tangled. |
| 6 | **Two-contact bilateral lateral rebound hop** | **1 × 1 per initial travel direction = 2 attempts.** One attempt is exactly two low lateral hops: cross a floor line on two feet and rebound back once, then hold 2 s. No turn or hurdle. Mirror the first direction; two attempts give four bilateral landings. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Qualify lateral load reversal at the ankle/hip without a single-leg rotational landing. Use instead two separately reset lateral hops with the same four landings if immediate return is unproven; shorten travel before considering faster contact. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** Linked contacts are conditional on controlled isolated landings and current recovery; no prior pass is inferred. Without evidence, start with the explicit separated-contact replacements inside the planned counts, and record that the reactive version was not completed. A kick remains grounded after its bounce. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** Easy hip cycles and foot holds restore precise positions after the timing drills without becoming a fatigue block. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **2 × 6 per leg/side = 24 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, **120 s between rounds**, then **120 s**. | Main strength investment supports the planted push after a short load-release event. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain posterior-chain control without an additional hinge round after the new contact timing. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Keep lateral hip/adductor force for rocking and lateral rebound at one round. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Preserve a controlled hip-turn torque anchor without adding speed to loaded rotation. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active chamber strength after the bounce and stance exchange tasks. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Retain bent-knee ankle force with one working set after the added contact timing. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |
