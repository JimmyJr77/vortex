# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 11 of 12

**Primary effort:** Express a crisp low rotational kick along a preselected empty line and finish in a repeatable support position. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 10, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The demand is controlling direction and stopping the leg without reducing every attempt to slow rehearsal. Supporting jobs cover a marked bilateral finish, a qualified single-leg turn, drop-step loading, a precise chamber stop and a crossover-to-base recovery. These are planned tasks, not additional reactive trials. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per leg that pass through the coach-defined empty directional window with a soft knee, active recoil and a two-second finish in the chosen stance. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rotational jump to an offset stance gate** | **1 × 2 per turn direction = 4 attempts.** Cones outside the landing area define a comfortable shallow split-stance finish, not an obstacle. From parallel feet, use one low rotational jump up to 45 degrees and land with the chosen support foot slightly ahead inside that gate. Hold 2 s. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Control the final two-foot arrangement after rotational force rather than simply completing an angle. Use a larger gate or smaller turn instead if targeting disrupts landing; never land on or jump toward a cone. |
| 2 | **Low roundhouse through an empty direction gate** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** The coach identifies an empty low kick line with cones placed outside the sweep and a visual height reference from outside reach. From a stationary rear-leg stance, kick through that line below hip height, keep the knee soft, recoil and recover. Nothing is struck. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Directly preserve hip-led acceleration while controlling the kick direction and recovery endpoint. Use a wider visual window or shorter arc instead if accuracy causes knee lockout or leaning; target accuracy alone is not a valid rep. |
| 3 | **Small single-leg turn hop to same-leg stick** | **1 × 2 per support leg = 4 attempts.** From the named leg, make one low hop with a small in-air turn, no more than 30 degrees, and land on that same foot already aligned with the pelvis. Hold 2 s. No travel goal or rebound. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Qualify control of rotational force on the leg that must remain the kick support. Only use the single-leg version after a controlled small unturned hop is known. Otherwise use instead a single-leg takeoff to a two-foot turning catch, same four flights; record four bilateral instead of unilateral landings. |
| 4 | **Short drop-step lead-leg roundhouse** | **1 × 2 per kicking leg = 4 attempts.** The kicking leg begins in front. Take one small rear-foot drop step to load the support hip, turn that foot freely and deliver a low lead-leg roundhouse without another gather. Recoil into a comfortable split stance; stay within 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Use a compact rear-hip load to free the lead leg while retaining the planned kick line. Use a shallower drop step instead if the lead leg drags or the support knee twists; do not add speed and step length together. |
| 5 | **Supported fast chamber lift to a fixed stop** | **1 × 2 per lifting leg = 4 attempts.** With fingertip support and the foot initially beneath the hip, accelerate the folded knee into a coach-selected low chamber height and stop it there for 2 s without overshooting. Lower slowly; no kick arc or band. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Practice cutting off free-thigh acceleration at the selected chamber, supporting a consistent low kick trajectory. Use a lower stop point instead if the pelvis tips or the leg overshoots; do not force end-range holds. |
| 6 | **Crossover recovery to a marked kick base** | **1 × 2 per initial crossing leg = 4 attempts.** From a slightly open stance, take one short crossover recovery step, then uncross the feet as the pelvis turns into a premarked comfortable kick base. Stop for 2 s with both feet supporting. No knee lift, kick or jump; path stays within 2 m. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Recover an exact useful two-foot base after reorientation, instead of leaving the feet crossed or too narrow. Use less crossover distance and a wider base instead if foot placement is inconsistent; do not force a fast turn while crossed. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** Precision is subordinate to a free support-foot turn and controlled knee. Do not assume single-leg rotation is earned by earlier written hops; use the two-foot catch unless that control is actually known. Cones and coach references stay outside all leg/landing paths. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** Easy hip/foot strengthening reinforces repeatable alignment after the precision tasks; no extra balance challenge is added. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain the main planted push while reserving attention for accurate explosive finishes. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Keep posterior-chain control without a second hinge round. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain lateral/adductor support for the kick line and crossover recovery. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Preserve direct hip-turn strength with one controlled round after the precision drills. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active chamber strength at a reproducible height without adding a second round. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Keep ankle force and full heel travel with one challenging set. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Lighter load-preparation sets, additional to working sets:** P1: 1 × 4/side; P2: 1 × 3/side; P3: 1 × 3/side; P4: 1 × 4/side; P5: 1 × 3/side; P6: 1 × 5 bilateral (**39 reps total**). Use the same listed movement with bodyweight or lighter resistance. P1–P3: 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s before working work; P4–P5: 20 s between preparation sides, 45 s before working work; P6: 60 s before working work. Extra small ramps are allowed only to qualify that exercise's load and must be logged with their time. This is not a replacement preparation sequence. If manual resistance is used, preserve range/tempo and a credible reserve; do not claim a precise load progression from it.

**Strength progression decision:** Reduce Class 10 [1,1,1,2,1,1] to [1,1,1,1,1,1]: 76 working reps in 11 bouts. Keep the same challenging relative effort rather than chase fatigue before the final comparison class. A small load increase on a recurring lift is optional only with reported completed clean work at the target reserve, acceptable recovery and current readiness. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain. Do not increase load, range and sets together.

**Workload:** 8 planned flight/landings (**4 bilateral, 4 unilateral**); **20 grounded explosive efforts**; **12 kick actions** (kick actions are a task count, not extra attempts to add). Eight landings by default: four bilateral gated catches plus four qualified same-leg turn landings. If E3 uses its two-foot catch, all eight landings are bilateral. Twenty grounded efforts include twelve kicks. Spatial misses remain attempts; there are no bonus target attempts. Primary strength: **6 rounds / 11 separate working bouts / 76 reps**, plus 39 lighter preparation reps. Stabilization: 8 slow cycles plus six 5 s foot holds. Failed attempts still add exposure. Unintended hops, landing corrections and actual entry/return steps are logged separately; no equivalent-contact safety score is used.

**Delivery and timing:** Approximately **71–78 minutes outside Access & Prepare 1**, with prompt equipment access. The audit models actual prescribed resets, rests, both sides, preparation sets and 13 minutes for instruction/setup/recording. Group queues, extra coaching, ramps and longer recovery are additional. Full session duration is unresolved; do not assume a 60- or 90-minute total booking fits. Each stationary drill has full swept-leg clearance; moving footwork stays within 2 m plus 2 m recovery in one 10 m lane. Cones mark safe external boundaries and must not occupy landing spots. **Recovery and placement:** Review actual recent/upcoming kicking, sport, gym, other focus classes and separately programmed tumbling. Around 48–72 hours before another comparably demanding lower-body exposure is an initial consideration, adjusted for real workload and recovery. These are numbered classes, not twelve consecutive training days. A different focus name does not remove overlapping adductor, calf or hip loading.
