# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 4 of 12

**Primary effort:** Sweep the free leg quickly through a low rotational kick and arrest it in a controlled re-chamber before putting it down. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 3, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The demand shifts from starting the body to accelerating and recovering the moving thigh. The supporting jobs isolate lateral chamber lift, single-leg upward push, inward sweep, knee-flexor recoil and stance control while the free leg stays raised. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per leg with an immediate soft-knee recoil and a two-second re-chamber hold without a rescue touchdown. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Supported fast lateral chamber lift** | **1 × 2 per lifting leg = 4 attempts.** Use light fingertip bench support. Lift the bent free knee briskly outward to a low roundhouse chamber, keeping the knee folded and the pelvis tall; lower under control. This is a short lift, not a leg swing through end range. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Accelerate the thigh into the lateral chamber before the lower leg contributes. Reduce lift height instead if the pelvis hikes or the trunk leans; support must remain light. |
| 2 | **Low roundhouse to suspended re-chamber** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** From a stationary rear-leg kick stance, perform one low roundhouse. Immediately fold the knee and recover the thigh to a low chamber; hold the foot off the floor for 2 s before placing it back in stance. The athlete does not jump. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Directly train the rapid return and controlled arrest of the kicking leg, rather than allowing it to fall after extension. Use a shorter extension/low chamber instead if the foot drops or the support knee twists; hold quality matters more than range. |
| 3 | **Single-leg knee-drive jump to two-foot stick** | **1 × 2 per takeoff leg = 4 attempts.** Push vertically from the named leg while driving the other bent knee upward; land softly on two feet and hold 2 s. Keep height controlled and do not turn in flight. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Coordinate stance-leg extension with free-thigh acceleration while simplifying landing support. Use lower height or a small rear-toe assist at takeoff instead if force production becomes a balance struggle; record assistance. |
| 4 | **Low inward crescent shadow sweep** | **1 × 2 per sweeping leg = 4 attempts.** With a soft knee, sweep the low free foot from slightly outside toward the midline in a small inward arc, coordinated with a comfortable support-foot/pelvic turn. Finish by folding the knee and placing the foot down. No crossing beyond a controllable midline range. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Express an inward thigh/adductor sweep that helps recover the leg after rotational extension. Use a bent-knee inward sweep instead at the same dose if the longer lever pulls at the groin; never force a high or locked-knee crescent. |
| 5 | **Supported rapid knee-flexion recoil** | **1 × 2 per moving leg = 4 attempts.** Stand with fingertip support and the moving thigh nearly vertical. Briskly fold that knee to bring the heel toward the back of the thigh through a comfortable range, then lower slowly. Keep the hip quiet and avoid a ballistic heel strike. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Isolate knee-flexor acceleration for the folding phase of re-chamber without another whole kick. Use a smaller fold instead for hamstring cramp, back arching or hip swing; symptoms end the task. |
| 6 | **Forward support-leg hop with chamber held** | **1 × 2 per support leg = 4 attempts.** Hold the free knee in a low bent chamber and make one very short forward hop on the support leg, landing on that same leg with the chamber maintained. Hold 2 s; travel no more than 0.5 m, no turn. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Keep the supporting leg organized while the other leg remains unavailable to catch bodyweight during recoil. Use a smaller hop or an assisted free-toe touchdown instead if the chamber drops or another hop is needed; record the assistance. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** The coach must qualify low suspended-chamber balance and a pain-free inward arc. No high crescent, locked-knee kick, repeated snap or fast stretch is prescribed. If recoil cannot be controlled, reduce the lever/height inside the same slot. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** Airplane work reinforces pelvic control during a raised-leg task; short-foot holds support the forefoot base without adding calf fatigue. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain stance-leg push without spending extra sets before the chamber-strength emphasis. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Load posterior-chain control that steadies the support hip during free-leg recoil. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Supply adductor/lateral hip capacity for the inward sweep and return to stance. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain a controlled hip-turn strength contribution while reducing its set share from Class 3. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **2 × 8 per leg/side = 32 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, **90 s between rounds**, then **60 s**. | Main strength investment: active chamber lifting and controlled lowering, separate from rapid recoil skill. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Support the flexed-knee ankle base while the free leg is suspended. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Lighter load-preparation sets, additional to working sets:** P1: 1 × 4/side; P2: 1 × 3/side; P3: 1 × 3/side; P4: 1 × 4/side; P5: 1 × 3/side; P6: 1 × 5 bilateral (**39 reps total**). Use the same listed movement with bodyweight or lighter resistance. Extra small ramps are allowed only to qualify that exercise's load and must be logged with their time. This is not a replacement preparation sequence. If manual resistance is used, preserve range/tempo and a credible reserve; do not claim a precise load progression from it.

**Strength progression decision:** Class 3 allocated two rounds to hip internal rotation. Shift that second round to P5 hip flexion: [1,1,1,1,2,1], still 92 working reps. This is a change in strength job emphasis, not an automatic band-load increase. A small load increase on a recurring lift is optional only with reported completed clean work at the target reserve, acceptable recovery and current readiness. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain. Do not increase load, range and sets together.

**Workload:** 8 planned flight/landings (**4 bilateral, 4 unilateral**); **20 grounded explosive efforts**; **12 kick actions** (kick actions are a task count, not extra attempts to add). Eight flight/landings: four unilateral takeoffs to bilateral landings plus four same-leg hop landings. Twenty grounded efforts include twelve kick/sweep actions; the re-chamber hold is part of each kick, not an extra rep. Primary strength: **7 rounds / 13 separate working bouts / 92 reps**, plus 39 lighter preparation reps. Stabilization: 8 slow cycles plus six 5 s foot holds. Failed attempts still add exposure. Unintended hops, landing corrections and actual entry/return steps are logged separately; no equivalent-contact safety score is used.
