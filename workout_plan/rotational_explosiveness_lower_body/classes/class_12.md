## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split-stance explosive hip pivot** | **1 × 2 per turn direction = 4 attempts.** Repeat the Class 1 short split stance and comfortable approximately 45-degree grounded hip/foot turn. Both forefeet stay down as heels may release; finish still 2 s. Mirror the stance and keep 20 s between all four attempts. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **20 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Match the modest pre-anchor rotational initiation dose used before the original kick assessment. Use the original qualified arc if recorded; reduce it for foot sticking or knee twist and record the changed condition. |
| 2 | **Standing low roundhouse shadow kick** — primary anchor | **3 × 2 per kicking leg = 12 attempts.** Repeat the Class 1 rear-leg short fighting stance, low kick line and controlled partial extension. Turn the support foot with the pelvis, accelerate the free thigh, actively recoil and return to the same stance with a 2 s hold. Foot remains below hip height. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Provide the matched direct-task comparison within the prescribed workout. Use only the original qualified height/arc if available. A necessary regression becomes a new condition, not a directly comparable score; never force the old version for a test. |
| 3 | **90-Degree Hop to Stick** | **2 × 2 per turn direction = 8 attempts.** Repeat the Class 1 two-foot countermovement takeoff, freely moving arms, turn angle and two-foot 2 s stick. Use the original recorded angle if it was smaller than 90 degrees; no approach or rebound. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Provide a second matched lower-body rotational-output observation with a simple bilateral base. Use a smaller turn instead if control requires it, but record it as a changed condition. Do not add height, distance or retries to chase a score. |
| 4 | **Step-behind gathered low roundhouse** | **1 × 1 per kicking leg = 2 attempts.** Use one short step-behind gather to load the kicking-side hip, then uncross onto the support foot before delivering one low roundhouse and recovering. The gather plus kick stays inside 2 m; no turn while the legs remain crossed. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Sample how a compact step-behind load can feed the known hip-led kick. Use a very small non-crossing gather instead if foot clearance is doubtful; log that replacement. One attempt per leg is a probe, not a mastery claim. |
| 5 | **Angle-out step to the original low kick line** | **1 × 1 per kicking leg = 2 attempts.** Make one short outward-diagonal support-foot step off the original centerline, then direct the low rear-leg roundhouse back toward the original empty line. Recoil with aligned feet; total travel stays within 2 m, no target contact. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Keep the rotational kick line organized when the body has moved slightly off that line. Use a smaller off-line step instead if aiming back traps the support knee; the foot must pivot freely. |
| 6 | **Low roundhouse with lateral recovery placement** | **1 × 1 per kicking leg = 2 attempts.** From a stationary rear-leg stance, deliver one low roundhouse, fold the knee and place the returning foot into a short lateral recovery stance instead of its original rear footprint. Finish with the pelvis/feet aligned and no follow-on shuffle or kick. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Control the return leg when restoring a base slightly off the original line after rotational output. Use a smaller kick arc and recovery width instead if the foot crosses behind the support knee or the athlete keeps spinning. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** Prior attendance and response are unknown. Repeat a baseline only when its original condition is currently appropriate; otherwise record the changed version. Use all prescribed kicks below hip height with active recoil. The three application probes have only one attempt per leg and remain optional in execution if control or recovery is inadequate; record omitted work rather than fabricate completion. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** The same easy hip and foot strengthening preserves continuity before primary strength; it does not add another performance assessment. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain a challenging planted push after the matched output observations, without increasing the final-class dose. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Keep hip-hinge support strength traceable against earlier prescriptions. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain lateral/adductor force for the off-line and recovery placements. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Retain the direct hip-torque strength anchor at its established technical range. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active chamber lifting and lowering after the brief application kicks. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Keep a reproducible bent-knee calf strength condition rather than adding a final burnout. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |
