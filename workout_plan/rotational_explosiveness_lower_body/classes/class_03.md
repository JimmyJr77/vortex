# Lower-Body Rotational Explosiveness — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Initiate a hip-led low kick without a bounce, preparatory dip or extra gathering step. Exact framework: **Rotational explosiveness > Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)**.

**Foundation and reviewed evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting and landing technique, supervised by a coach qualified to teach the listed kick/plant actions. Reviewed Class 2, the saved progression/workload and drill records, and athlete_feedback.md. Prior work is **prescribed, not observed**; attendance, loads, response and all other training remain unknown. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned higher level or a consecutive calendar day.

**Daily stimulus brief:** A brief still start removes an immediate countermovement. The supporting jobs distinguish bilateral turn impulse, single-leg lateral drive, isolated hip-turn acceleration, free-thigh acceleration without toe push and release from a loaded stance. **E2 is today's anchor.** Marker: valid E2 attempts out of four per leg after a visibly still two-second start, with no redip and a controlled recoil. Observe only within its scheduled attempts; no extra test block. Record actual variant, stance, side, range and attempted/valid reps. A different drill is not a directly comparable force/speed test.

## 2. Explosiveness

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All are single drills, including any natural entry/recoil in a kick; integrated flights and kick actions are separately counted in the audit. Bodyweight unless specified. Brisk/explosive intent means rapid controlled force expression, not rushed recovery or maximal range. Mirror the stated working side. For every kick: foot below hip height, knee never snapped into lockout, active recoil, no pad/contact, jumping/spinning kick or unplanned extension. For every turn, feet and knees follow the pelvis; no twisting a fixed knee. Finish each attempt still for 2 s unless the row specifies linked contacts.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paused quarter-turn squat jump** | **1 × 2 per turn direction = 4 attempts.** Hold a comfortable quarter squat for 2 s, then jump and turn up to 90 degrees without another dip. Use a two-foot landing already facing the new line and hold 2 s. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **60 s** before the next entry/section. | Produce rotational takeoff impulse without immediate dip assistance. Use a smaller turn instead for late orientation; a redip invalidates the held-start marker but counts as an attempt. |
| 2 | **Low roundhouse from a held split-squat start** — primary anchor | **2 × 2 per kicking leg = 8 attempts.** Kicking leg starts rear. Hold a shallow split squat 2 s, then drive through the front support leg into a low roundhouse, actively recoil and return to the original split stance. No initial bounce or deeper dip. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, **90 s between rounds**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Link stance-leg force from stillness to the free thigh and pelvic turn. Make the starting squat shallower instead if rising produces a lurch; do not force a deep hold or fast knee lockout. |
| 3 | **Split-stance lateral jump to parallel landing** | **1 × 2 per lead push leg = 4 attempts.** From a shallow split stance with most weight on the named lead leg, push laterally away from that leg into a short flight; bring feet parallel for a two-foot stick. Face the same direction throughout; no scissor exchange. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Load asymmetrical lateral push while simplifying the receiving base to two feet. Shorten distance or reduce the rear-foot contribution; do not cross the legs in flight. |
| 4 | **Seated light-band hip-rotation acceleration** | **1 × 2 per hip = 4 attempts.** Sit with thighs supported, hips/knees near 90 degrees and a very light loop at the lower shins. Start still, then move one foot a short distance outward briskly by rotating at the hip; knee/pelvis stay quiet. Return slowly; no bouncing or forced end range. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Give the hip internal rotators a small rapid force-expression task without the balance or whole-kick demand. Use self-resistance from the opposite hand instead if no suitable loop fits, or reduce range/tension for knee movement; speed is controlled, never a ballistic joint wrench. |
| 5 | **Rear-leg hover-start knee drive** | **1 × 2 per driving leg = 4 attempts.** Stand on one leg with fingertip bench support. Hold the other toes just clear of the floor slightly behind the body for 2 s, then drive that bent knee forward/up briskly. Set it down under control before resetting; do not push off its toes. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Accelerate the free thigh from stillness without assistance from the swinging foot on the floor. Keep the hover close to neutral and chamber low; use a smaller path instead if the back arches or the support leg hops. |
| 6 | **Lateral release step from held split stance** | **1 × 2 per push leg = 4 attempts.** Hold a shallow split stance for 2 s with the named leg in front. Push from that leg into one short lateral step, allow the feet/pelvis to open together up to 45 degrees, and finish in a stable kick stance. No jump or follow-on kick. Rest **20 s between attempts**, **60 s between sides/directions**, then **90 s** before the next entry/section. | Release planted leg force into a grounded turn when there is no incoming momentum. Shorten the step and turn for foot sticking or overstriding; keep motion within 2 m. |

**Rest counting:** The between-attempt interval applies within one side-bout; the stated side-switch or round interval replaces it at that boundary. Do not add both intervals to the same gap. A one-rep side-bout has no within-side gap.

**Readiness and stop rules:** The coach must see a still start and freely turning feet. E4 uses only a short comfortable hip arc and very light resistance; do not convert it into forceful knee/ankle rotation. First scheduled attempts may use the stated smaller-range replacement; they still count. After a miss, extend recovery and apply the row's correction to remaining attempts. Repeated loss of control means stop that slot and log omitted work; pain, joint pinching, dizziness or an uncontrolled landing stops the task immediately. If only slow rehearsal is possible, record technique-only work rather than explosive completion. Never force fourteen completed slots for an unready athlete.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience)

**Exactly 2 exercises.** Light bodyweight strengthening, approximately **RPE 3–4/10**, with at least five clean repetitions' reserve or comparable ease. These should not tire the legs before primary strength.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Hip Airplane Kick Prep** — hand-supported, small-range strengthening execution | **1 × 4 per stance leg = 8 reps.** Light fingertip support on a stable bench; shallow single-leg hinge. Slowly open the pelvis a small amount over the stance hip and return to square: **3 s out, 3 s return** is one rep. Foot stays planted and knee tracks toes. Rest **30 s between sides**, then **30 s**. | Apply low-load eccentric/concentric control around the support hip, limiting pelvic spill during the kick. Hand support removes balance as the limiting task. Shrink the arc or use light trailing-toe support instead if knee alignment or foot pressure changes; no forced end-range rotation. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill** — standing, both feet supported | **1 × 3 holds × 5 s per target foot = 6 holds / 30 foot-seconds.** Both feet remain supported; gently draw the target forefoot toward the heel without moving either contact or curling toes. Relax **5 s between holds**, allow **15 s to switch feet**, then **60 s** before Section 4. | Strengthen low-level arch control for a stable forefoot base as the support heel releases during the pivot. This is active foot-muscle work, not a calf raise. Reduce effort or **use the seated version instead**, same holds, if toes claw or the ankle rolls. |

**Today's light-strength jobs:** Small supported airplane cycles prepare the hip to control a held base; foot holds reinforce quiet pressure before heel release. Retaining these two low-load strength tasks is intentional: preserve controllable hip/foot capacity while the explosive tasks change. They must remain easy.

## 4. Strength (Primary)

**Exactly 6 exercises.** All working sets should be challenging with **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Approximately 2 s lower, 1 s controlled lift and 1 s reset per rep; P4 uses 2 s outward foot motion/2 s return. No maximal tests, technical failure, timed circuits or unstable loaded pivots. Retain a reproducible stance/range for each recurring lift; select actual load from that athlete's current execution, not a written earlier number.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Split Squat** — stationary, both feet on floor | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Dumbbells at sides; front foot flat, rear forefoot supported. Descend through a repeatable comfortable range and drive up through the lead leg. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Maintain planted knee/hip extension with a smaller single working round while hip torque gets the main investment. Reduce load/depth if pelvis rotates, front heel lifts or rear-leg push dominates. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — dumbbell, fingertip support | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** One hand on a stable bench; hold the dumbbell in the other, weight near the stance leg. Soft knee, hips back, pelvis square; finish tall. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Preserve hip-extensor control of the stationary support leg. Stop descent before the pelvis opens or back rounds; reduce load/range. If hand support is unsuitable, use instead a kickstand RDL at the same side dose. |
| 3 | **Loaded Cossack Squat** — goblet dumbbell, qualified depth | **1 × 6 per leg/side = 12 working reps.** Wide stance; sit toward the working hip while the opposite leg lengthens, then push back to center. Complete one side before switching. Rest **90 s between sides**, then **120 s**. | Load the lateral hip range used in the held-stance release without a large adductor dose. Keep the loaded foot supported and knee aligned; reduce depth/load for groin pulling or pelvic collapse. Deep range is not mandatory. |
| 4 | **Seated band hip internal rotation** — proposed strength exercise | **2 × 8 per leg/side = 32 working reps.** Sit on a stable bench, hips/knees near 90°, thighs supported. A loop around the lower shins resists the working foot moving outward, lightly clear of the floor, while that knee stays in place; the other foot is firmly planted. **2 s outward rotation, 2 s controlled return.** Rest **60 s between sides**, **90 s between rounds**, then **60 s**. | Primary strength investment: controlled hip internal-rotation torque from a still, supported setup. Use modest rotation, enough resistance for 2–3 clean RIR, and a quiet knee/pelvis. If a secure loop cannot be fitted, use instead self-resisted seated hip internal rotation: opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 5 | **Standing Cable or Band Hip Flexion** — band-only strength execution | **1 × 8 per leg/side = 16 working reps.** Stable upright stance; loop at the forefeet secured by the support foot, optional fingertip bench support. Raise the bent knee to a comfortable chamber and lower without swinging. Rest **60 s between sides**, then **60 s**. | Maintain active free-thigh lifting force without toe push or torso swing. Reduce tension/height if the pelvis tips or foot slips. If the loop cannot be secured, use instead self-resisted seated bent-knee hip flexion, hands resisting the distal thigh, same reps/tempo. Log manual resistance as less reproducible. |
| 6 | **Seated Dumbbell Calf Raise** — bilateral, bent knees | **1 × 8 bilateral = 8 working reps.** Stable bench, feet on floor and knees near 90°. Secure dumbbells on the distal thighs above the knees; raise heels without rolling outward and lower fully with a brief floor pause. Finish after this working set; no finisher. | Preserve bent-knee ankle drive for rising from the stationary support stance. Reduce load for bouncing or shortened heel travel. If bench fit is unsuitable, use instead supported standing bent-knee calf raises with a secured dumbbell, same bilateral dose; log the changed position. |

**Lighter load-preparation sets, additional to working sets:** P1: 1 × 4/side; P2: 1 × 3/side; P3: 1 × 3/side; P4: 1 × 4/side; P5: 1 × 3/side; P6: 1 × 5 bilateral (**39 reps total**). Use the same listed movement with bodyweight or lighter resistance. P1–P3: 30 s between preparation sides, 60 s before working work; P4–P5: 20 s between preparation sides, 45 s before working work; P6: 60 s before working work. Extra small ramps are allowed only to qualify that exercise's load and must be logged with their time. This is not a replacement preparation sequence. If manual resistance is used, preserve range/tempo and a credible reserve; do not claim a precise load progression from it.

This reallocates sets toward hip torque while reducing split-squat/calf work; it does not prescribe heavier bands or prove increased capacity. Missing feedback means hold the loading intent and use the current qualified load; no automatic heavier weights. Reduce load/range for technique loss; stop for pain.
