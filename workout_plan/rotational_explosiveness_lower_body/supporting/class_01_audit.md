## Space and delivery model

- Operate one athlete at a time in the active kick/bound area until a coach verifies widths, swept-leg clearance, adjacent lanes, floor grip, return paths and waiting positions. Three lanes do not establish three simultaneous safe kicking stations.
- Grounded pivots/kicks and rotational jumps need a clear footprint around the whole athlete, including a possible recovery step. For bounds, reserve at most 2 m of planned flight/travel and a separate clear 2 m recovery zone within one 10 m lane. Do not position waiting athletes or weights in that zone. Athlete flight distance remains submaximal and individualized.
- Without enough width for the small turn, E4 may **use instead a shorter lateral bound to stick without the airborne turn**, same 1 × 2/direction, preserving the unilateral lateral-plant job. Record it as a different condition. If even that cannot fit with clearance, do not deliver E4 in the constrained area; revise the layout/prescription rather than inventing space.
- For strength, establish a stable bench/support station and enough clear floor for the split/Cossack stances. Fit the loop without compression/slippage or damaging it; no fixed band anchor is presumed. The standing loop does not resist the athlete through a pivot. P4's working foot is lightly clear of the floor while moving outward, with the knee/pelvis still; the other foot secures its position.
- With several athletes, the coach can batch the same exercise in order; waiting may supply recovery only when the actual gap meets the written rest. Do not run a station circuit that puts strength before an athlete's explosive work. Maintain supervision of the kicking/landing area and challenging lifts; no unverified coach-to-athlete ratio is stated.
- Band/manual or stance replacements retain slots/dose, not identical measurement conditions. The fallback pathways have similar active-rep budgets but may need more instruction. If no challenging controlled strength version can be qualified, the individual's exposure is incomplete, not a passed six-exercise strength session.

## Combined workload and recovery review

The full prescription has 12 flight/landings, including four unilateral rotational plants, plus 26 grounded explosive efforts. E5 contributes four short lateral entry-to-turn paths (up to 8 m in total); E4 adds up to 8 m of bound displacement. These are not sprint distances. Forty-eight compound working reps overlap with stance-leg/hip loading; another 48 reps load rotation, chamber and ankle actions. S1/S2 remain easy, but are not zero-load placeholders. The 39 load-preparation reps are additional and have their own clock. These figures are descriptive; they are not a safety threshold or interchangeable stress units.

No athlete recovery or actual exposure to the sibling plans is known. Review their potentially overlapping squat/hinge/calf/adductor and jumping/cutting work only if the athlete actually did it. Include real sport/kicking and separate tumbling. Use the class's contextual recovery guidance; no automatic weekly calendar, maximal load assignment or assumed adaptation is justified.
