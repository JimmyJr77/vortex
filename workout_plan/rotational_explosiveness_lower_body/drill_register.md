# Explosive drill history and uniqueness rule

**User preference added during Class 1 design, 2026-09-13:** Try to force unique drills; strength lifts may recur as needed for progression.

Default to **six fresh, purposeful explosive drills per new class**. Check names and actual execution against this register before selection. A different label, band color, angle or repetition count alone does not make a new drill. Seek a meaningful difference in initiation, force direction, support, entry, flight/grounded behavior or task constraint that serves the daily effort. Do not introduce unnecessary complexity or unrelated work simply to fill six novel slots.

Strength and light stabilization exercises may recur when their specific jobs remain useful; preserve their load/range/effort history in the progression ledger. A repeated explosive assessment is an exception requiring a clear comparison purpose, counted within the six slots, not an extra testing block or default recurrence. Different drills do not yield directly comparable performance scores.

## Class 1 — prescribed, actual execution unknown

| Slot | Exercise | Execution signature and distinct job | Prescription |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Split-stance explosive hip pivot | In-place grounded initiation from split stance; both forefeet remain down; foot release and pelvis turn, no free-leg swing. | 1 × 2/direction |
| E2 | Standing low roundhouse shadow kick | Stationary low noncontact whole-kick task: support pivot, free-thigh rotation, controlled partial extension/recoil and stance finish. | 3 × 2/kicking leg |
| E3 | 90-Degree Hop to Stick | Bilateral countermovement rotational flight with bilateral landing; up to 90°; no approach or rebound. | 2 × 2/direction |
| E4 | Lateral Bound to Rotational Stick | Unilateral lateral takeoff to opposite-leg landing; small airborne turn; lateral plant acceptance. | 1 × 2/travel direction |
| E5 | Lateral step-in to rotational plant | Grounded short lateral entry and receiving-foot turn into kick stance; arriving bodyweight, no flight/kick. | 1 × 2/receiving leg |
| E6 | Split-Stance Band-Resisted Knee Drive | Light resisted free-thigh acceleration; no transverse turn, kick extension or hop; controlled reset. | 1 × 3/driving leg |

**Revision during this run:** The initial paused rotational squat-jump candidate was replaced by E5 to avoid two closely similar rotational jump slots. It was not delivered, is not a finalized Class 1 drill, and is not an observed exposure. At the original Class 1 stopping point, no later class had been drafted. Classes 2–12 were subsequently developed sequentially under the expanded user authorization.

**Current handoff:** All twelve classes are written. Use this record when revising a class from actual feedback; retain the stated distinctions and comparison exceptions rather than automatically creating more drills.
