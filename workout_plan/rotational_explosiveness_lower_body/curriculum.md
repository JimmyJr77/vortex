The style reference is Overtime Athletes' public emphasis on leg-driven rotational output supported by strength and deliberate movement sequencing. This curriculum adapts that emphasis to the lower-body output requested here. It does not reproduce an OTA program or its promotional performance claims. No contact target or force measurement is available in the prescription. Shadow practice addresses hip-turn/leg control; it cannot establish impact force, target accuracy or sport-specific contact competence.

**Class 1 design revisions:** Preferred grounded pivot and chamber tasks over arm-dominant throws/presses; replaced the planned paused-jump variant with a grounded entry-to-turn drill after the user requested stronger drill uniqueness; limited the unilateral hinge and lateral squat to one challenging set per side after the main split squat; used seated rotation/ankle strength so balance does not limit all six primary slots. These choices keep force application concentrated on the selected kick while respecting the six-slot strength requirement.
