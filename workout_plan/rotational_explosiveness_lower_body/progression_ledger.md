## Counting conventions

One flight/landing is one jump or bound repetition; bilateral and unilateral takeoffs/landings remain distinct. Grounded hip pivots, kicks and knee drives are recorded separately from flights. A kick is one complete launch/recoil/reset, not separate exercise entries for each phase. Failed or abbreviated attempts still count in actual exposure; valid attempts are a separate quality count. No extra attempts are prescribed to achieve a score.

A **round** includes both sides where stated; a **bout** is each separately performed side/set. Thus 2 × 6/leg is two rounds, four bouts and 24 reps. Simultaneous bilateral repetitions are not doubled. Counts across unlike exercises describe planned work, not equal mechanical stress. Short-foot seconds are aggregate foot-seconds, distinct from wall-clock time and repetitions.
