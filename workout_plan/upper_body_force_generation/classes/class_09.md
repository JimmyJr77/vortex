# Upper-body force generation — Class 9 of 12

**Primary effort:** Time a controlled plant with forceful upper-body projection and pulling.

**Baseline and current evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting technique and qualified supervision. Reviewed Class 8, saved workload/progression, drill use and available feedback before this prescription. No athlete completion, symptoms, load tolerance or improvement has been reported. This is a conditional planned class, not the next calendar day. New tasks require demonstrated control in the first prescribed attempts; no separate readiness test is added.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The main task is one-step two-arm projection. A contralateral step-to-one-arm release and backward traction step extend the force-transfer problem in clearly different ways. Familiar short pulls and guided pressing limit extra learning demand; a modest wall push-off gives fixed-contact force expression without another ball release.

**Quality marker, within E1:** In E1 record valid attempts out of three per lead foot: one controlled step, release timed to the plant, rear foot grounded, no trunk twist and a balanced finish inside the marked movement area. This moving release is not directly comparable with stationary pass distance. Record valid attempts and actual work, including misses; an invalid attempt consumes a rep. No bonus test attempts. Do not compare measurements from different drills as the same test.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Light resistance, rapid purposeful effort and controlled resets. Use the least assistance/load needed to preserve speed and position; no generic age-based weight. No failure sets or conditioning circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Medicine Ball Step-In Chest Pass — one forward plant** | **1 × 3 per lead foot** | Primary timing of a controlled forward plant with a straight-ahead two-arm release. Start behind the marked release line; take one small forward step and release as that foot accepts weight. Rear foot stays grounded; finish balanced without jumping or running through. Alternate lead-foot blocks. Complete one lead foot side, rest 60 s, then the other; 6 total reps. Reset 20 s between reps. Use a stationary shallow-countermovement chest pass if plant/release timing is not demonstrated or the whole step cannot fit the inspected bay; log zero travel steps **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. Log changed laterality/exposure; stop if control cannot be restored. |
| 2 | **Medicine Ball Step-In Shot Put — forward-facing single arm** | **1 × 2 per arm** | Connect the opposite-foot plant to an independent arm release without transverse rotation. Small step with the foot opposite the throwing arm; push a manageable ball forward as that foot plants. No side-on preload or follow-through step. Complete one arm side, rest 60 s, then the other; 4 total reps. Reset 20 s between reps. Use the fixed-stance forward-facing shot-put throw if step timing or ball control fails; use a self-anchored band press if containment is absent **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. Log changed laterality/exposure; stop if control cannot be restored. |
| 3 | **Battle-Rope Traction Pull — one backward step per draw** | **1 × 4** | Transfer a controlled backward plant into a hand-led traction effort. With a manageable free rope laid in the inspected corridor, take one small backward step while drawing the rope toward the hip, then stop and reset. Alternate two draws per arm; no rope around the body or fixed load attached. Reset 8 s between reps. Use the seated hand-over-hand free-rope pull for four total draws if traction/step control or clearance is unsuitable; log zero steps **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. | 4 | **Band Lat Pulldown — single-arm rapid elbow drive** | **1 × 2 per arm** | Retain a brief independent arm draw after the traveling traction task. | 5 | **Two-Hand Landmine Push Press — shallow dip, feet stay planted** | **1 × 3** | Reinforce leg-to-arm timing against a guided load with no travel. Use a strict light landmine press if timing is unsuitable or a self-anchored band press if bar mass is excessive **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. Log changed laterality/exposure; stop if control cannot be restored. |
| 6 | **Standing Wall Power Push-Off — return to upright** | **1 × 3** | Express a brief horizontal push while the body moves away from a fixed hand contact. At an inspected structural wall, feet fixed and close enough for a modest lean. Hands at chest height; push quickly into brief hand separation and return only to balanced upright. Slowly reset the lean; no rebound hand catch or backward step. Reset 10 s between reps. Use a non-flight fast incline push-up if wall structure or balance is unsuitable **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. Log changed laterality/exposure; stop if control cannot be restored. |

**Recovery and quality:** Rest **90 seconds between exercises**, including after E6 before Section 3; extend to 2–3 minutes if control, attention or speed has not returned. Returns are controlled, not explosive, unless the row explicitly describes a linked action. Stop the set for pain, jerking, unwanted rotation, hard/asymmetric hand reception or clear loss of speed; reduce load/complexity for remaining work only if control returns. Otherwise stop and record the shortfall. Do not make up missed reps or count a non-flight press as a hand landing.

**Delivery:** Allocate and inspect a movement zone of up to 2 m within one 10 m lane for the start, single step and balanced stop; this is a programming requirement, not confirmed free space. Ball flight/bounce/roll needs additional contained clearance within the actual site. If both cannot fit, use stationary replacements. Keep backward rope stepping away from ball paths. The wall push-off needs a structural wall; no wall is inferred merely from medicine-ball ownership. Ball releases require an inspected throw-rated wall with a protected rebound area, or a clear open corridor containing flight, bounce and roll. No rebound catches unless explicitly prescribed in a row. Retrieve stopped balls on the coach's signal; nobody waits downrange. Three separate 10 m lanes do not confirm throwing containment or extra run-off. Equipment quantities, ball mass/size, anchor ratings, bar minimum load and support dimensions remain unconfirmed. Every replacement occupies its original slot only; a resisted press replacing a throw removes that release exposure and cannot supply the same assessment.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light, controlled strengthening; keep at least **5 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. These sets should not tire the athlete before primary lifting.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Light-Band Pallof Press — fixed split stance** | **1 × 5 per anchor side** | Control unwanted trunk rotation while force passes through a planted lead foot. Rated side attachment at chest height, light tension. Press hands forward 1 s, hold 2 s, return 2 s without rotating; change anchor-facing side after rest. Complete one anchor side side, rest 30 s, then the other; 10 total reps. Use a light single-arm supine dumbbell press hold for 1 × 5 repetitions with a 2 s top hold per arm if no side attachment is available; record changed support **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. Log changed laterality/exposure; stop if control cannot be restored. |
| 2 | **Bilateral Band External Rotation — elbows at sides** | **1 × 8** | Light shoulder-rotator strengthening to control upper-arm position during the day's press and pull. Unanchored band between hands, elbows at ribs; rotate outward 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s without shrugging. Use a lower-tension band and shorter comfortable range **instead of this slot** if the stated execution is unsuitable; follow any explicitly stated replacement dose; otherwise retain the remaining dose. Log changed laterality/exposure; stop if control cannot be restored. |

Rest **45 seconds between exercises** and **60 seconds after S2** before strength preparation. Use lighter resistance or shorter comfortable range if control fades. Stop for symptoms rather than completing a nominal count.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

These are **working sets**, each finishing with approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Select challenging loads/assistance individually; a small set count does not mean a light effort. No forced reps, maximum testing, unstable supports or timed rotation. Work in order.

**Load-preparation sets of these same listed lifts:** P1: 1 × 6 then 1 × 3; P2: 1 × 5 per arm; P3: 1 × 3; P4: 1 × 5; P5: 1 × 5; P6: 1 × 5. Use very light resistance; where two preparation sets are listed, the second is intermediate. Keep at least 5 technical reps in reserve. Rest 60 seconds between preparation rounds, 30 seconds between arms within a preparation round, then 90 seconds before P1/P2 working sets or 60 seconds before P3–P6 work. Preparation totals **37 repetitions, 7 rounds / 8 bouts**. Extra load-finding reps add actual workload and time; never add an exercise.

**Strength transitions:** Rest **2 minutes after P1, P2 and P3**, and **90 seconds after P4 and P5**, before preparing the next lift. Increase load only when an available prescribed set can remain at the stated range, tempo and reserve; if the next increment is too large, hold the load. All symptom, speed and technique stops take precedence over the planned count.

**Workload and timing:** 6 throw bilateral; 4 throw unilateral; 4 alternating rope pull; 4 rapid pull unilateral; 6 rapid press bilateral. Primary work totals **68 task repetitions in 8 rounds / 10 bouts**; resilience totals **18 task repetitions**. Bilateral cycles count once; unilateral doses count both arms. These exposure types are not equivalent stress units. No lower-body jumping, sprinting or rotational throw work is added. Estimate **62–71 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, including listed work, resets, recovery, preparation and instruction; additional group queues or extra coaching add time. Complete session length is unresolved. Match the actual booking rather than compressing recovery.

**Scheduling:** Review recent/upcoming throwing, contact sport, lifting and hand-supported tumbling. Plan the next demanding upper-body exposure on a nonconsecutive occasion, commonly 48–72 hours later initially, then adjust to actual recovery and schedule. Elapsed time is not proof of readiness. Body control remains separate.

**Progression note:** Class 8 used the pulling-emphasis strength allocation. This class returns to the Class 1/2 balanced eight-round strength prescription, 68 task reps, at familiar demonstrated effort rather than higher weights. The new variable is plant-to-arm coordination; no sprint or jump is added. There are 14 prescribed movement steps: six bilateral-pass steps, four single-arm-throw steps and four rope-draw steps. They are not sprint metres or jump contacts. Without demonstrated plant control, stationary same-slot versions are the default. Future progression needs actual balanced finishes, controlled release line, technical strength reserve and recovery.

**Audit summary:** Only one step per moving repetition; removed running approaches, run-through finishes and overhead receiving. Ten throws, no catches and no hand landings are prescribed. Both lead feet and throwing arms are explicitly counted. Two new tasks, two related variants and two justified repeats are identified; no attempt to label implement-only changes as new. Whole-session and group fit remain conditional on inspected space and actual preparation time. Count, per-side workload and timing checks are saved with the class.
