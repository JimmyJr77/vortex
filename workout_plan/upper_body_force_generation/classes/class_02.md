# Upper-body force generation — Class 2 of 12

**Primary effort:** Accelerate the arms in pushing and pulling while the trunk supplies a stable base; retain a straight-ahead release.

**Baseline and current evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting technique and qualified supervision. Reviewed Class 1, saved workload/progression, drill use and available feedback before this prescription. No athlete completion, symptoms, load tolerance or improvement has been reported. This is a conditional planned class, not the next calendar day. New tasks require demonstrated control in the first prescribed attempts; no separate readiness test is added.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Remove leg contribution from the main release, then practice distinct arm-force tasks: guided unilateral pressing, sequential grip-to-pull transfer, assisted vertical pulling, elastic press acceleration and shoulder-only downward pulling. The last two pulling tasks support the requested Pulling subcategory; they are not presented as horizontal chest-pass tests.

**Quality marker, within E1:** In E1, record controlled forward releases out of 6 attempts: both knees remain down, hips remain tall, ribs do not flare and the ball exits symmetrically. Record ball and target. This kneeling task is not directly comparable with Class 1 standing throws. Record valid attempts and actual work, including misses; an invalid attempt consumes a rep. No bonus test attempts. Do not compare measurements from different drills as the same test.

**Recovery and quality:** Rest **90 seconds between exercises**, including after E6 before Section 3; extend to 2–3 minutes if control, attention or speed has not returned. Returns are controlled, not explosive, unless the row explicitly describes a linked action. Stop the set for pain, jerking, unwanted rotation, hard/asymmetric hand reception or clear loss of speed; reduce load/complexity for remaining work only if control returns. Otherwise stop and record the shortfall. Do not make up missed reps or count a non-flight press as a hand landing.

**Delivery:** Use a single supervised throwing bay first, then move to a separate landmine/floor area and an inspected bar/band attachment. Bar and band stations must not lie in the throwing or rope-retrieval path. Rope length/diameter and box/bar fit are not assumed; use the listed same-slot replacement when unsuitable. Ball releases require an inspected throw-rated wall with a protected rebound area, or a clear open corridor containing flight, bounce and roll. No rebound catches unless explicitly prescribed in a row. Retrieve stopped balls on the coach's signal; nobody waits downrange. Three separate 10 m lanes do not confirm throwing containment or extra run-off. Equipment quantities, ball mass/size, anchor ratings, bar minimum load and support dimensions remain unconfirmed. Every replacement occupies its original slot only; a resisted press replacing a throw removes that release exposure and cannot supply the same assessment.
