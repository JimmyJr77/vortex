# Upper-body force generation — Class 11 of 12

**Primary effort:** Integrate receiving, a controlled plant and organized arm-force onset without inventing an athlete-specific weakness.

**Baseline and current evidence:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting technique and qualified supervision. Reviewed Class 10, saved workload/progression, drill use and available feedback before this prescription. No athlete completion, symptoms, load tolerance or improvement has been reported. This is a conditional planned class, not the next calendar day. New tasks require demonstrated control in the first prescribed attempts; no separate readiness test is added.

**Daily stimulus brief:** No deficit has been reported, so the chosen emphasis is integration. E1 links a gentle received load to one plant and forward release. Cued one-arm pressing and bilateral pulling emphasize readiness-to-force onset; three familiar short tasks preserve body-assisted pulling, higher forward projection and hand-contact force expression.

**Quality marker, within E1:** In E1 record valid cycles out of two per lead foot: clean receipt, exactly one controlled step, forward release on plant and a balanced stop. Record any simplified version and actual catches/steps. Do not label one lead foot weak without observations. Record valid attempts and actual work, including misses; an invalid attempt consumes a rep. No bonus test attempts. Do not compare measurements from different drills as the same test.

**Recovery and quality:** Rest **90 seconds between exercises**, including after E6 before Section 3; extend to 2–3 minutes if control, attention or speed has not returned. Returns are controlled, not explosive, unless the row explicitly describes a linked action. Stop the set for pain, jerking, unwanted rotation, hard/asymmetric hand reception or clear loss of speed; reduce load/complexity for remaining work only if control returns. Otherwise stop and record the shortfall. Do not make up missed reps or count a non-flight press as a hand landing.

**Delivery:** Coach supervises the entire receive-step cycle in one protected lane. An inspected movement zone up to 2 m plus contained ball flight/bounce must fit; use stationary replacements otherwise. Keep the overhead throw and hand-flight support separate from that lane. Cue drills use voice only and require no timing device or added reaction station. Ball releases require an inspected throw-rated wall with a protected rebound area, or a clear open corridor containing flight, bounce and roll. No rebound catches unless explicitly prescribed in a row. Retrieve stopped balls on the coach's signal; nobody waits downrange. Three separate 10 m lanes do not confirm throwing containment or extra run-off. Equipment quantities, ball mass/size, anchor ratings, bar minimum load and support dimensions remain unconfirmed. Every replacement occupies its original slot only; a resisted press replacing a throw removes that release exposure and cannot supply the same assessment.

**Load-preparation sets of these same listed lifts:** P1: 1 × 6 then 1 × 3; P2: 1 × 5 per arm; P3: 1 × 3; P4: 1 × 5; P5: 1 × 5; P6: 1 × 5. Use very light resistance; where two preparation sets are listed, the second is intermediate. Keep at least 5 technical reps in reserve. Rest 60 seconds between preparation rounds, 30 seconds between arms within a preparation round, then 90 seconds before P1/P2 working sets or 60 seconds before P3–P6 work. Preparation totals **37 repetitions, 7 rounds / 8 bouts**. Extra load-finding reps add actual workload and time; never add an exercise.

**Strength transitions:** Rest **2 minutes after P1, P2 and P3**, and **90 seconds after P4 and P5**, before preparing the next lift. Increase load only when an available prescribed set can remain at the stated range, tempo and reserve; if the next increment is too large, hold the load. All symptom, speed and technique stops take precedence over the planned count.

Estimate **61–70 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, including listed work, resets, recovery, preparation and instruction; additional group queues or extra coaching add time.
