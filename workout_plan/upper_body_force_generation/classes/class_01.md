# Upper-body force generation — Class 1 of 12

**Primary effort:** Produce forceful horizontal pushes and pulls with a steady trunk; express the pushing action in a fast, straight-ahead chest-pass release.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting technique, under qualified supervision. This is a first curriculum exposure, not automatically a first lifting session. Select resistance from demonstrated control and speed. Attendance, recent loading, individual restrictions and response are unknown.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Train a decisive forward release and a strong backward draw without substituting torso rotation or lumbar extension. The stationary Medicine Ball Chest Pass is the performance anchor. Supporting jobs are rapid pulling from a pause, brief hand push-off and force acceptance, a small preload into projection, pulling through rising resistance, and independent arm projection. Light scapular and shoulder control precede challenging press, row and elbow-strength work.

**Quality marker, inside E1:** Record valid releases out of the nine prescribed attempts. A valid attempt starts with a one-second still chest hold, uses simultaneous hand release, sends the ball through the declared forward corridor and finishes with ribs over pelvis and both feet planted. Record ball mass/type, stance, target arrangement and misses; a miss still consumes an attempt. This is a technique record, not a validated power test. No extra test throws and no fabricated speed or distance results.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance that allows a visibly quick effort with control; ball mass is athlete-specific, not a universal age-based weight. Start with E1 while fresh. These are discrete efforts, not a timed circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Medicine Ball Chest Pass — stationary, paused** | **3 × 3** | **Main straight-line release practice.** Face forward, feet parallel, soft knees, ball at chest. Hold still for 1 second, then punch both hands forward together; no dip, step or twist. Reset **20 seconds** between attempts, including retrieval; rest **90 seconds** between sets. Record the quality marker above. |
| 2 | **Two-Hand Landmine Bent-Over Row — fast pull from a pause** | **2 × 3** | Produce rapid bilateral pulling force from a stationary arm position against implement inertia. Straddle the bar, hinge and grip the sleeve securely with both hands; keep the trunk still. Pull quickly toward lower ribs without hip drive; lower for 2 seconds, then hold the extended-arm start for 3 seconds before the next pull. Keep the load in hand. Rest **90 seconds** between sets. Use a light bar/load that actually moves quickly. |
| 3 | **Plyo Push-Up — hands elevated** | **1 × 3** | Express a brief, fast closed-chain push and accept the return through both arms. Use an inspected, immovable bench or box at a height allowing a small simultaneous hand release. Lower under control, reverse promptly, push off, then receive softly with elbows yielding. Reset **10 seconds** between reps; no clap or repeated bouncing. |
| 4 | **Medicine Ball Chest Pass — shallow countermovement** | **1 × 3** | Connect a small preload to the same forward release. Unlike E1's still start, use one shallow knee/hip dip with a small ball draw toward the chest, then an immediate forward drive. Keep feet planted and shoulders facing forward; no jump, run-up or deep squat. Reset **20 seconds** between attempts. |
| 5 | **Band Row — seated, fast pull** | **1 × 4** | Keep accelerating the horizontal draw as elastic resistance increases toward the finish. Sit tall, knees slightly bent; use an intact band securely around the mid-soles of both shod feet, only in a manufacturer-permitted setup. Pull quickly to lower ribs; return for 2 seconds and reset for 2 seconds without rocking. Hands stay below chest height; inspect for slip before loading. |
| 6 | **Medicine Ball Shot Put Throw — forward-facing, no rotation** | **1 × 2 per arm** | Give each arm independent forward-projecting work while the trunk resists turning. Use a light, controllable ball at the throwing-side shoulder; the other hand supports setup then releases before the throw. Fixed split stance, opposite foot forward; no step or deliberate rotational preload. Reset **20 seconds** between throws and rest **60 seconds** before changing arm and lead foot. **4 throws total.** |

**Between exercises:** Rest **90 seconds** after the last effort before the next exercise; setup time may supply this rest only if the athlete is actually resting. Extend explosive set/transition recovery to **2–3 minutes** when speed or attention has not returned. Rest 90 seconds after E6 before Section 3. Never shorten recovery to finish a clock.

**Throwing and quality:** Use an inspected throw-rated wall with a clear rebound zone, or an open release corridor that contains the flight, bounce and roll. Select and record the target before E1; use the same target arrangement for repeat comparisons. Lane length alone does not establish containment. Athletes do **not** catch returning balls. Retrieve only after the ball stops and the coach clears entry. No athlete waits downrange. Finish a set when speed plainly falls, hands release unevenly, the torso substitutes for the arms, or E3 hand landing becomes hard/asymmetric. Rest and use an easier replacement for remaining prescribed work only if quality returns; otherwise stop that exercise and record the shortfall. Do not make up failed attempts.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep these light, smooth and well short of fatigue, with at least **5 clean repetitions in reserve**. They should leave the athlete ready to lift.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scapular Push-Up — quadruped, dynamic** | **1 × 6** | Strengthen controlled shoulder-blade travel against light bodyweight so the chest-pass finish and push-up support do not depend on elbow bending or trunk sag. Hands and knees remain down; keep elbows straight, let the chest settle slightly, then push the floor away. Use 2 seconds inward, 2 seconds outward and a 2-second finish hold. Stop for shrugging, elbow bend or lumbar movement. |
| 2 | **Bilateral Band External Rotation — elbows at sides** | **1 × 8** | Apply light shoulder-rotator loading while controlling the upper-arm position used in pressing and rowing. Hold an unanchored light band between hands; elbows near 90 degrees at the ribs. Rotate forearms outward for 2 seconds, hold 1 second, return for 2 seconds. Stop for wrist substitution, elbow drift or shoulder discomfort. |

Rest **45 seconds** between these exercises and **60 seconds** after S2 before strength preparation. Reduce band tension or quadruped loading if the reserve disappears; do not turn this section into a burn or mobility sequence.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

The table lists **working sets**. Every working set should finish with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**. Use controlled lowering and a decisive lifting intent; no grinding, forced reps or maximal tests. This is strength work, not a rotation against the clock. Work in the listed order.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Floor Press — two dumbbells, neutral grip** | **2 × 6** | Main loaded horizontal push: develop force through a reproducible floor-limited pressing range with a reproducible lower boundary. Lie supine, knees bent, feet planted; coach supervises pickup and set-down. Lower for 2 seconds until upper arms touch gently, pause 1 second without relaxing, then press decisively. No bridge, wrist collapse or bouncing. Rest **2½ minutes** between working sets. |
| 2 | **One-Arm Row — dumbbell, hand and knee on bench** | **2 × 6 per arm** | Main loaded horizontal pull: give each arm challenging shoulder-extension and elbow-flexion work while support limits trunk rotation. Back stays level; draw elbow toward hip without pulling it far behind the trunk. Lift decisively, hold 1 second, lower for 2 seconds. Complete left, rest **60 seconds**, then right; rest **2 minutes** before the second round. **24 repetitions total.** |
| 3 | **Push-Up — controlled full repetition** | **1 × 8** | Strengthen the press with shoulder blades free to move and the trunk held as one unit, complementing the floor-supported press. Use 2 seconds down, a decisive ascent and 1 second at the top; finish by pushing the floor away without adding a separate scapular cycle. Choose hand elevation or band resistance so 8 reps leave 2–3 in reserve. No hand flight. |
| 4 | **Dumbbell Rear-Delt Row — bilateral, hinged** | **1 × 8** | Load the backward draw at a wider upper-arm angle, supporting control of the arm-open position preceding a chest pass. This complements P2's tucked-elbow pull. Hold a steady hip hinge, elbows about 60 degrees from the torso; draw toward upper ribs, pause 1 second, lower for 2 seconds. End before the shoulder rolls forward or the back starts lifting the weight. |
| 5 | **Dumbbell Skull Crusher — floor-supported** | **1 × 8** | Give elbow extension a small, direct strength dose for the final pressing action without another full pressing compound. Lie supine, one dumbbell in each hand, neutral grip, upper arms near vertical. Bend elbows to lower the dumbbells beside the ears for 2 seconds, then extend under control; do not move the shoulders to finish. Coach controls setup and clearance. |
| 6 | **Dumbbell Hammer Curl — simultaneous arms** | **1 × 8** | Give elbow flexors direct loading for completing the draw in rows without another shoulder-extension set. Stand still, neutral grips, upper arms at sides; lift in 1 second, hold 1 second and lower for 2 seconds. No hip swing or wrist collapse. Both arms move together; this is **8 bilateral cycles**, not 8 alternating reps. |

**Preparation sets, separate from working sets:** P1: **1 × 6 very light, then 1 × 3 intermediate**, resting 60 seconds between them and 90 seconds before working sets. P2: **1 × 5 per arm light**, 30 seconds between arms and 90 seconds before work. P3: **1 × 3 at an easier leverage/resistance**. P4, P5 and P6: **1 × 5 light each**. Rest 60 seconds after each of the P3–P6 preparation sets. All preparation stays at least 5 reps from technical failure. These total **37 repetitions, 7 set rounds / 8 side-specific bouts**, all of listed exercises. Any extra load-finding reps must be recorded and add time; do not improvise extra exercises.

**Between strength exercises:** Rest **2 minutes** after P1, P2 and P3; **90 seconds** after P4 and P5, before the next exercise's preparation. If a working load would leave fewer than 2 clean reps, reduce it. If it leaves clearly more than 3, adjust the next available prescribed set or future exposure; do not add bonus sets. Stop for pain, loss of control or persistent compensation, even if work remains. P3 and P4 are distinct strengthening tasks, not hidden explosive or stabilization sets.

**Replacement rules:** Replacements occupy the same slot and remaining dose; never add them to the session. Raise E3's hand support if needed; if even a small hand release is unsuitable, use a fast incline push-up with hands staying down **instead of E3**, recording zero hand-landing events. If the minimum landmine load is too slow or its sleeve cannot be gripped securely, use a light two-hand kettlebell row from the same paused hinge **instead of E2**, 2 × 3 with identical recovery. If E5's seated band setup slips, use a bent-over band row with the band securely under both feet **instead of E5**, 1 × 4 at the same tempo; record the changed resistance direction. Use thigh-supported One-Arm Row **instead of P2** if no suitable bench support fits, preserving side dose and trunk control. Use lighter implements/resistance and suitable range for other rows, not extra repetitions.

**Unconfirmed release area or one-arm ball fit:** Use the specific replacement-only band presses in [the delivery audit](../class_01_audit.md) if a throwing slot cannot be delivered. They preserve rapid pushing practice but remove that slot's free-release practice; record the direct-practice gap and changed throw totals. If no suitable replacement can preserve controlled, meaningful work, do not represent an interrupted class as fully delivered.

**Timing and scheduling:** Allow approximately **65–75 minutes after Access & Prepare 1**, including listed resets, rest, load-preparation sets, demonstrations and equipment changes for an athlete with equipment access. Group queues and extra coaching can add time.
