## Counting convention

A bilateral dynamic repetition is one cycle, although both arms work. One unilateral repetition is one arm's cycle. A set **round** is the table's set count; a unilateral round has two **bouts**, one per arm. Therefore `2 × 6 per arm` is 2 rounds, 4 bouts, 12 left and 12 right repetitions, 24 total. Do not compare the raw 24 with a bilateral 12 without stating the convention.

A throw is one release/attempt, including a miss or invalid attempt; there are no bonus retries. A bilateral hand-landing event in a plyometric push-up is one event with two individual hand contacts. These events are recorded separately from lower-body jump contacts. A non-flight replacement is rapid pushing, not a hand landing. Lower-body contacts, throws and rapid rows are not interchangeable workload units. Preparation sets, light strengthening and primary working sets remain separate.
