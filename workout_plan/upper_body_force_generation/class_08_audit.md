**Review:** Read classes/class_07.md, curriculum.md, progression_ledger.md, drill_usage.md, athlete_feedback.md. No observed athlete work supplied. User authorizes sequential completion through Class 12; this class is saved before the next is designed.

**Stimulus and consequential choices:** The main continuous power row connects a small hip impulse to arm force without a separate lower-body exercise. Alternating draws, elbow reversal and unilateral downward release provide differentiated pulling jobs. A small kneeling forward pass and shoulder-only pull preserve complementary force directions without new high-volume work.

**Audit findings and adjustments:** Main power rows remain six controlled pulls; no weight release, separate hinge exercise or jumping. Seven total throws, no catches or hand landings. One new force-transfer task, three related variants and two justified repeats are disclosed. No invented low-bar/rack station or sled is required.

That is eight rounds, ten bouts and 66 task reps. This returns to a known prescription, not an observed successful dose.
