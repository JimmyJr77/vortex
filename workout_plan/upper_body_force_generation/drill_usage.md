# Drill use and uniqueness review

**Owner preference:** Try to force unique drills; strength lifts may recur as needed for progressions. **Status:** Classes 1–12 prescribed; actual delivery unknown. This register tracks prescribed use, not completed training.

Read this register and all saved classes before every new class. Choose previously unused, directly relevant explosive drill executions by default. Check within the proposed class as well as across classes. Changes only to name, load, dose, grip, stance, support height, tempo or implement do not by themselves establish a genuinely new task. Record the distinct mechanical/coordination job of a related variant, and prefer a different suitable task over stacking near-duplicates.

## Strength recurrence

S1 quadruped Scapular Push-Up, S2 Bilateral Band External Rotation, and P1–P6 strength lifts can recur for a documented continuing role or progression. Floor Press and One-Arm Row are useful candidates for comparable strength records. Each recurrence still needs previous dose → current dose → reason → next criterion; no automatic increase. A lift prescribed as an explosive drill follows the drill rule in that section.

## Required next-class review

For each proposed explosive slot record:

1. The required daily job and why the selected drill directly addresses it.
2. Prior exact use and related-family matches from this register.
3. The substantive new force or coordination task, beyond cosmetic variation.
4. Dose, recovery, equipment, technical prerequisites and combined workload appropriate to available feedback.
5. If repeating: the specific learning, reassessment or equipment reason, and why an equally direct feasible new option was not selected.

Update this table only after finalizing a class. Keep the twelve-class map provisional. Do not equate unlike drills as repeated performance tests, assume previous prescriptions were performed, add extra test slots or use novelty to justify more load or complexity.
