A changed exercise is a new reference, not an earned increase. The class-level note explains allocation changes; each row identifies the continuing mechanical job.

**Primary criterion:** Advance only after actual completion at the prescribed range/tempo and 2–3 technical RIR, with satisfactory recovery and no limiting symptoms. Consider one smallest practical load change on the priority lift; hold if increments are too large or feedback is absent. Reduce load/dose or use the listed replacement when control or recovery does not support the plan.

**Light-strength criterion:** Retain light resistance and at least 5 technical RIR. Increase only if needed to preserve a modest strengthening stimulus with controlled joint/torso position; do not use these slots for fatigue or maximal holds.
