## Counting conventions

A release is one thrown or slammed ball; floor contact by the ball is not a human landing. Count invalid attempts and any actually performed preparation/extra attempts in delivered exposure even when the quality score is invalid. No replacement attempts are prescribed. “Per side” means both directions/arms, not dominant-side-only training.

For unilateral exercises one round includes a separately performed set on each side; these are two physical set bouts. P1 uses alternating traverses: one one-way traverse is one rep, the reverse traverse is the next rep. Its six-rep set has three reps in each direction, not six each. Strength eccentric returns are included in the repetition unless explicitly counted as the next traverse. Light holds below occur within the listed press repetitions, not extra reps.
