# Combined strength recurrence

Prescribed work only. Each row uses its combined-class job, with earlier source exercises named explicitly when they are absent from that class. Actual completion, loads and recovery remain unknown.

## Class 1

|---|---|---|
| S1 — Scapular Push-Up — quadruped, dynamic | First combined strength execution → 1 × 6 | Keep this light support at ≥5 technical RIR. Strengthen controlled shoulder-blade travel against light bodyweight so the chest-pass finish and push-up support do not depend on elbow bending or trunk sag. Hands and knees remain down; keep elbows straight, let the chest settle slightly, then push the floor away. Use 2 seconds inward, 2 seconds outward and a 2-second finish hold. Stop for shrugging, elbow bend or lumbar movement. Use instead: a smaller controlled shoulder-blade excursion at the same easy dose; do not turn it into an elbow-bending push-up. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| S2 — Tibialis Raise — wall-supported | First combined strength execution → 1 × 6 | Keep this light support at ≥5 technical RIR. Light ankle dorsiflexor loading to control foot position before a stable push/landing. Back against an inspected wall, heels on floor; lift forefeet 2 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Adjust foot distance to stay easy. Use instead: a seated unweighted toe raise through comfortable range. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| P1 — Goblet Squat — dumbbell | First combined strength execution → 2 × 6 | Keep the working load at 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Bilateral knee/hip extension through a repeatable full-foot push. Lower 3 s, stand decisively in about 1 s; hold a manageable goblet load and retain comfortable depth without heels lifting. Use instead: a lighter goblet squat through controlled comfortable range; a two-dumbbell front hold may replace a limiting goblet grip. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| P2 — Floor Press — two dumbbells, neutral grip | First combined strength execution → 2 × 6 | Keep the working load at 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Main loaded horizontal push: develop force through a reproducible floor-limited pressing range with a reproducible lower boundary. Lie supine, knees bent, feet planted; coach supervises pickup and set-down. Lower for 2 seconds until upper arms touch gently, pause 1 second without relaxing, then press decisively. No bridge, wrist collapse or bouncing. Rest 2½ minutes between working sets. Use instead: lighter dumbbells and the same comfortable floor-limited range while preserving the stated reserve. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| P3 — Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — straight knees | First combined strength execution → 1 × 8 | Keep the working load at 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Plantar-flexion force with the knees extended for the final upward/forward push. Use stable hand support if needed; rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s to the floor. Keep knees softly straight and heels tracking over the forefoot. Use instead: a lighter supported standing calf raise; use individual load or leverage that retains the required technical reserve. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| P4 — Dumbbell Rear-Delt Row — bilateral, hinged | First combined strength execution → 1 × 8 | Keep the working load at 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Load the backward draw at a wider upper-arm angle, supporting control of the arm-open position preceding a chest pass. This complements the source exercise “One-Arm Row — dumbbell, hand and knee on bench”'s tucked-elbow pull. Hold a steady hip hinge, elbows about 60 degrees from the torso; draw toward upper ribs, pause 1 second, lower for 2 seconds. End before the shoulder rolls forward or the back starts lifting the weight. Use instead: lighter dumbbells and a smaller controlled rear-delt row range; keep the hinge still. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| P5 — Seated band hip internal rotation | First combined strength execution → 1 × 5 per side/direction | Keep the working load at 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Load controlled internal-rotation torque directly at the hip, complementing the larger leg push used to organise the selected grounded lower turn. Sit on a stable bench with thighs supported and hips and knees near 90 degrees. Fit a secure loop around the lower shins. Keep the other foot planted while the working foot moves a short distance outward, lightly clear of the floor, through hip internal rotation. Keep that knee and the pelvis still. Take 2 seconds outward and 2 seconds returning; do not force end range or twist the knee or ankle. Use instead: Reduce range or band tension for knee, ankle or pelvic movement. If the loop cannot be fitted securely, use self-resisted seated hip internal rotation with the opposite hand resisting the outside of the lower shin instead; manual resistance is a changed and less reproducible load. Apply this change only to the same remaining combined dose and recovery; it replaces the original execution and adds no extra task or make-up attempts. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
| P6 — Landmine Rotation | First combined strength execution → 1 × 6 alternating traverses total | Keep the working load at 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Load the turning arc and controlled endpoints that transmit lower-body force into the selected upper rotational action. Secure the landmine and qualify the bar, grip and minimum load. Hold the sleeve or suitable grip in both hands with elbows softly bent. Turn the feet and hips with a modest side-to-side loaded arc while keeping ribs over pelvis. Each one-way traverse is one rep; the return is the next counted rep. Take about 2 seconds per traverse and settle briefly at each endpoint. Avoid trunk lean, a trapped knee or an uncontrolled finish. Use instead: Reduce the load or arc for loss of alignment. If a secured, manageable landmine setup cannot be qualified, use a standing two-hand band rotation with a verified side anchor and the same one-way traverse counting instead; log the changed setup. Apply this change only to the same remaining combined dose and recovery; it replaces the original execution and adds no extra task or make-up attempts. This replacement uses only the remaining combined dose/rest unless explicitly stated; log changed support, event type, load and added time. Actual controlled completion at the stated range/tempo/reserve and satisfactory recovery; change one relevant load/lever variable, otherwise hold or reduce. |
