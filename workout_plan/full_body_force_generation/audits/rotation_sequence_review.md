## Substantive task selection

Every class includes an actual lower rotational action: a coordinated grounded foot/pelvis turn, a hip-led low sweep/kick, or direct seated hip-rotation acceleration. Pure sagittal knee lifts, generic lateral steps and nonrotational jumps were not mislabeled as lower rotation. The seated internal/external-rotation tasks directly express hip torque; they are not claimed to be whole-body kicks or equivalent power measurements.

The upper selections retain actual trunk/foot turning or a deliberately constrained small thoracic turn, followed by the defined hand path. They cover paused and countermovement projections, short pushing/sweeping arcs, retained resistance, planned entries and controlled handoffs. The mechanical connections are coaching links between separate exercises. They do not authorize simultaneous jump/kick/throw combinations or supersets.

The four force entries in each explosive section already supply the sagittal projection and receiving work. The rotational selections add **no human jump landings, sprint attempts or sprint metres**. Lower source Class 3 E4 is deliberately reused in combined Class 12, and lower source Class 1 E5 in combined Class 15, instead of inventing a rotating identity for a nonrotational source drill. Cue and entry variants remain related tasks; they are not advertised as unrelated exercise families.

Primary rotation support has a concrete job: landmine rotation loads a controlled turning arc; landmine rotational press adds compound pushing linked to the turn; band chop/lift loads the declared diagonal; Cossack squat supplies lateral knee/hip force and stance acceptance; seated internal rotation supplies direct hip torque; band hip flexion loads active chamber control. The Cossack selections provide a substantive compound where the other lower-force pair is bridge/calf dominant. The added press in the two pull-dominant upper-force pairings supplies missing compound pushing. It is accurately described as a complementary pressing path rather than a reproduction of every throw trajectory. Rapid seated hip rotation and its challenging controlled strength version are separately dosed speed and strength exposures, not two different movement identities or one set counted twice.

## Delivery limits and handoff

Kicking competence is individually qualified within the scheduled first attempts; lifting experience alone does not establish it. Kicks stay low and noncontact with active recoil, a soft terminal knee and freely coordinated support-foot/pelvis motion. A trapped knee, pain or loss of control stops or modifies remaining work. A slow-only rehearsal is not assumed explosive completion. Proposed executions retain their stated identity limits; no new canonical database identity is inferred.

Ball tasks require a suitable light/grippable implement, permitted impact surface and verified containment of flight, rebound and roll, with people outside the entire path. The plan does not assume a throwing wall, low rebound, suitable minimum landmine load, safe anchor, lane width, feeder competence or equipment quantity. Controlled handoffs require secure grip and full feeder release/clearance before motion; the rotational additions do not introduce airborne feeds. A substituted task replaces only remaining work at the combined dose/recovery, and multiple substitutions must be checked for duplication. Unsafe or unqualified work is recorded as incomplete rather than forced to satisfy the row count.

Class numbers indicate ordered exposures, not consecutive days or an automatically fixed calendar. Actual sport, gym, throwing, kicking, jumping, running and separate tumbling must inform scheduling and recovery. The standalone source workouts are not additional work to perform beside these combined classes. Feedback, actual workloads, attendance, loads, recovery and observed improvement remain unknown.
