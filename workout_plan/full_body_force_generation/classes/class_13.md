# Full Body Force Generation — Class 13 of 36

**Stage 5, lesson A: Receive, redirect and stick**

**Primary effort:** Convert a small received load or contact into the next force action, then regain control.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Broad Jump — two linked projections, final stick as the controlled lower-body force anchor. Convert a small received load or contact into the next force action, then regain control.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Broad Jump — two linked projections, final stick. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Receive and redirect force:** Broad Jump — two linked projections, final stick and Medicine Ball Receive-and-Return Chest Pass — single coach feed: Use the first controlled reception to organize the next projection, then regain control; keep the ball feed and the broad-jump contact small enough to redirect cleanly.
- **E3 + E4 — Controlled return into the finish:** Bent-Knee Calf Raise — single-leg rapid supported rise and Band Lat Pulldown — controlled upward reach into fast draw: Use a controlled lowering or loaded reach before the rapid ankle or arm finish, with the support base stable and no trunk swing.
- **E5 + E6 — Hip initiation into hand force:** Single-shuffle low roundhouse and Med Ball Shuffle-to-Rotation Throw — short floor-target version: Both endpoints express rotation after one small lateral arrival, with separate stations and full resets.
