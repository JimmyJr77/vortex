# Full Body Force Generation — Class 33 of 36

**Stage 11, lesson C: Brief contact to forward projection**

**Primary effort:** Place crossover recovery into a controlled rotational base and couple a separate step-in hip turn to an upper-body pushing release. Small linear leg, ankle and arm-force tasks support placement and the finish.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Crossover recovery to a marked kick base as the grounded rotational force anchor. Recover an exact useful two-foot base after reorientation, instead of leaving the feet crossed or too narrow.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Crossover recovery to a marked kick base. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Crossover recovery to a marked kick base and Step-in rotational medicine-ball shot-put: A familiar upper step-in press pairs with grounded crossover recovery to a marked kick base; no added jump contacts.
- **E3 + E4 — Directed forward release:** Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — fixed split stance, forward release and Medicine Ball Overhead Forward Throw — fixed stance, arm-driven: Keep the release line and body finish organized; use the scoop's leg drive and the overhead throw's arm action as distinct force routes.
- **E5 + E6 — Brief contact and controlled reception:** Ankle Pogo — two pairs with a full reset and Plyo Push-Up — hands elevated: Use the prescribed small foot or hand contact to organize the next force action, then complete the full reset without extending the sequence.
