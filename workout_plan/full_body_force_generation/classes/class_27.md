# Full Body Force Generation — Class 27 of 36

**Stage 9, lesson C: Backward traction and forward leg drive**

**Primary effort:** Generate compact folded-leg and hand sweeps through controlled hip and trunk turns. Grounded lunge return, leg-driven forward projection and linear arm traction supply plant and finish support.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Low bent-knee hook shadow sweep as the grounded rotational force anchor. Practice the coupled inward thigh sweep and knee folding used to recover a rotational leg action.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Low bent-knee hook shadow sweep. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Low bent-knee hook shadow sweep and Short crossover-to-band backhand speed sweep: Upper crossover-to-retained sweep and low folding hook sweep retain transverse outputs without another airborne task.
- **E3 + E4 — Plant to direct force:** Forward Lunge — rapid return to the starting foot line and Battle-Rope Traction Pull — one backward step per draw: Use the declared forward or backward plant to organize the connected leg or arm effort, then stop and reset within the controlled movement corridor.
- **E5 + E6 — Leg impulse into implement force:** Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — walk-step forward release and Two-Hand Landmine Push Press — shallow dip, feet stay planted: Sequence the declared leg drive into the guided press or scoop release, keeping the finish balanced and free of an extra jump.
