# Full Body Force Generation — Class 14 of 36

**Stage 5, lesson B: Preload through supported limbs**

**Primary effort:** Use a small preload to drive through a supported arm or leg without unnecessary travel.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Two-Hand Landmine Push Press — shallow dip, feet stay planted as the controlled upper-body force anchor. Use a small preload to drive through a supported arm or leg without unnecessary travel.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Two-Hand Landmine Push Press — shallow dip, feet stay planted. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Coordinate a permitted preload:** Two-Hand Landmine Push Press — shallow dip, feet stay planted and Countermovement Jump — hands fixed at hips: Use the declared preload to sequence force into the jump or guided press, finishing without an extra dip or turn.
- **E3 + E4 — Independent force with stable support:** Medicine Ball Shot Put Throw — controlled arm draw into forward release and Single-Leg Hip Hinge — rapid return with hand support: Keep the supporting limb and trunk organized while the arm reverses to release or the hip returns from its hinge; use only the specified arm draw or hip motion.
- **E5 + E6 — Supported rotational force:** Crossover entry to rotational knee chamber and Drop-step rotational medicine-ball scoop throw: Rear upper loading step and lower crossover-to-chamber differentiate how weight arrives before rotation.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
