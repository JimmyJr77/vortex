# Full Body Force Generation — Class 3 of 36

**Stage 1, lesson C: Lead-leg projection and independent release**

**Primary effort:** Use a controlled lateral plant to initiate grounded hip rotation and a diagonal upper-body finish. Retain forward leg and independent arm projection as supporting force work.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Lateral step-in to rotational plant as the grounded rotational force anchor. Convert arriving lateral bodyweight into a grounded foot-and-hip turn with a controlled split-stance finish.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Lateral step-in to rotational plant. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Lateral step-in to rotational plant and Med Ball Overhead to Side Slam — non-rebounding slam-ball execution: Downward arm finish plus lateral bodyweight accepted into a grounded hip turn.
- **E3 + E4 — Preload into forward projection:** Standing Broad Jump — dip, project and stick and Medicine Ball Chest Pass — shallow countermovement: Use the declared small preload to direct force forward, finishing balanced without an extra approach or torso turn.
- **E5 + E6 — Lead-side support and finish:** Step-Up — fast grounded drive, rear foot joins and Medicine Ball Shot Put Throw — forward-facing, no rotation: Maintain pressure through the declared support stance and finish balanced as the lead leg rises or the independent arm releases.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
