# Full Body Force Generation — Class 30 of 36

**Stage 10, lesson C: Split-base push and catch-set release**

**Primary effort:** Initiate a compact hip-turning thigh action from the early cue and express a separate unloaded upper-body backhand arc. Split-base leg force and catch-set forward release support an organized start and finish.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Auditory-go pre-chambered thigh whip as the grounded rotational force anchor. Release proximal rotation from a prepared leg position without anticipating the start.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Auditory-go pre-chambered thigh whip. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Auditory-go pre-chambered thigh whip and Unloaded two-hand rotational backhand sweep: Unloaded upper backhand and early auditory-go folded-thigh sweep produce compact rotational arcs with no release or jump.
- **E3 + E4 — Receive control before another impulse:** Split Squat Jump — fixed stance, same-stance reception and Medicine Ball Catch-Set Chest Pass — gentle coach feed: Control the prescribed foot reception or gentle ball feed before repeating, preserving the split-base landing and the set-before-release requirement.
- **E5 + E6 — Retained-load acceleration with a steady finish:** Kettlebell Swing — continuous two-hand chest-height float and Band Biceps Curl — rapid neutral-grip pull: Keep the body organized as the hips or elbows accelerate their retained load, then control the return without an extra trunk swing.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot.
