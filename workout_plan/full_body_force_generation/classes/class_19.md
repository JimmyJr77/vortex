**Stage 7, lesson A: Independent takeoff and received return**

**Primary effort:** Express force through one takeoff leg and one returning arm while keeping reception controlled.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Single-Leg Vertical Takeoff — two-foot floor reception as the controlled lower-body force anchor. Express force through one takeoff leg and one returning arm while keeping reception controlled.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Single-Leg Vertical Takeoff — two-foot floor reception. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Independent impulse and reception:** Single-Leg Vertical Takeoff — two-foot floor reception and Medicine Ball Two-Hand Receive to Single-Arm Forward Return: Organize force through one takeoff leg or one returning arm, then control the two-foot landing or the body's release finish.
- **E3 + E4 — Stable support for an independent finish:** Bent-Knee Calf Raise — single-leg rapid supported rise and One-Arm Row — supported dumbbell speed pull: Keep the supported trunk and force-producing side organized while the calf or arm completes its draw, without adding a pelvic roll.
- **E5 + E6 — Hip initiation into hand force:** Lateral hip rock-to-pivot release and Externally supplied rotational scoop return: Controlled coach handoff adds supplied-ball organization; lower rock-to-pivot adds a small controlled reversal without a bounce.
