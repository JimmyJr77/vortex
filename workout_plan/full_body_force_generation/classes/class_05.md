# Full Body Force Generation — Class 5 of 36

**Stage 2, lesson B: Guided push and supported ascent**

**Primary effort:** Accelerate a guided arm push and a supported upward effort.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Landmine Press — one-arm strict speed press as the controlled upper-body force anchor. Accelerate a guided arm push and a supported upward effort.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Landmine Press — one-arm strict speed press. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Drive from an organized base:** Landmine Press — one-arm strict speed press and Kettlebell Swing — continuous two-hand chest-height float: Keep the support pressure organized as force begins; the strict press uses no leg dip, while the retained swing expresses its deliberate hip impulse.
- **E3 + E4 — Supported upward force:** Foot-Supported Pull-Up — fast ascent, controlled return and Seated Vertical Jump — low bench start to floor landing: Drive upward from the declared support without rocking for momentum, then control the return or two-foot reception.
- **E5 + E6 — Supported rotational force:** Pre-chambered bent-knee roundhouse whip and Two-hand band backhand speed sweep: Transverse hand sweep and short-lever thigh sweep express rotation without more release or terminal kick volume.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
