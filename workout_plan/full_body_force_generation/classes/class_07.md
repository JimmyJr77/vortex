**Stage 3, lesson A: Split-base projection and downward draw**

**Primary effort:** Project from a fixed split base while expressing clear downward and forward arm force.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Split-Stance Broad Jump — fixed lead, two-foot reception as the controlled lower-body force anchor. Project from a fixed split base while expressing clear downward and forward arm force.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Split-Stance Broad Jump — fixed lead, two-foot reception. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Directed force from a fixed base:** Split-Stance Broad Jump — fixed lead, two-foot reception and Medicine Ball Overhead Slam — tall kneeling, arm-driven: Start from the declared support position and direct force into the forward jump or downward arm release; maintain a controlled finish in the chosen line.
- **E3 + E4 — Distal force finish:** Calf Raise — single-leg fast supported finish, no flight and Medicine Ball Chest Pass — stationary, paused: Keep the body organized while force passes through the ankle rise or forward hand release; do not add a leg bounce to the stationary pass.
- **E5 + E6 — Hip initiation into hand force:** Low roundhouse from a held split-squat start and Med Ball Countermovement Rotational Throw — floor target: Upper reversal projection and lower still-start rotational force are distinct tasks; no assumption that one proves mastery of the other.
