# Full Body Force Generation — Class 29 of 36

**Stage 10, lesson B: Supported ascent and shoulder force**

**Primary effort:** Express a supported lower-body rise and a brief shoulder-driven upper action.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Dumbbell Reverse Fly — quick lift, controlled return as the controlled upper-body force anchor. Express a supported lower-body rise and a brief shoulder-driven upper action.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Dumbbell Reverse Fly — quick lift, controlled return. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Supported ascent and shoulder opening:** Dumbbell Reverse Fly — quick lift, controlled return and Step-Up — fast grounded drive, rear foot joins: Stabilize the body through the support leg or held hinge so the intended knee drive or shoulder opening supplies the motion.
- **E3 + E4 — Controlled hip and hand push:** Standing Wall Power Push-Off — return to upright and Hip Thrust — bench-supported rapid rise: Use stable support to produce the hip rise or hand push-off, finishing tall without lumbar extension or an added rebound.
- **E5 + E6 — Supported rotational force:** Coach-called-leg low roundhouse and Med Ball Rotational Scoop Toss — paused floor-target start: Familiar settled upper scoop lowers novelty while a called lower leg changes preparation responsibility.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
