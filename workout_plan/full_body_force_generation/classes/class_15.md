# Full Body Force Generation — Class 15 of 36

**Stage 5, lesson C: Retained hip return and linked hand push**

**Primary effort:** Accept a lateral arrival into a grounded hip turn and arrest a retained upper-body rotational arc. Linked hand pushes and leg-driven projection support a controlled impulse and finish.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Lateral step-in to rotational plant as the grounded rotational force anchor. Convert arriving lateral bodyweight into a grounded foot-and-hip turn with a controlled split-stance finish.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Lateral step-in to rotational plant. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Lateral step-in to rotational plant and Close-held medicine-ball rotational snap-to-stop: Retained upper endpoint control paired with familiar lateral step-to-turn. Lower Class 5 jab-step has no explicit turn, so source Class 1 E5 is reused honestly.
- **E3 + E4 — Brief return-to-force link:** Kettlebell Swing — staggered-stance continuous pair and Plyo Push-Up — two linked pairs on elevated hands: Use the controlled return of the retained hip load or hand contact to organize the next impulse, then complete the planned reset.
- **E5 + E6 — Directed implement projection:** Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — upward-forward leg drive and Medicine Ball Overhead Forward Throw — fixed stance, arm-driven: Direct the ball along the declared forward path and keep the body's finish organized; preserve leg drive for the scoop and the arm-led overhead release.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed.
