# Full Body Force Generation — Class 26 of 36

**Stage 9, lesson B: Forward plant into controlled projection**

**Primary effort:** Project from a controlled forward entry and regain support before repeating.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Medicine Ball Step-In Shot Put — forward-facing single arm as the controlled upper-body force anchor. Project from a controlled forward entry and regain support before repeating.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Medicine Ball Step-In Shot Put — forward-facing single arm. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Forward entry into a directed impulse:** Medicine Ball Step-In Shot Put — forward-facing single arm and Walk-In Broad Jump — step-close forward takeoff: Time the controlled forward entry to the leg projection or independent arm release, then regain support without running through.
- **E3 + E4 — Push from stable support and finish controlled:** Standing Wall Power Push-Off — return to upright and Step-Down to Step-Up — quick grounded reversal: Use a secure support contact to produce the next push, finishing upright or on the step; neither task adds a drop or a rebound hand catch.
- **E5 + E6 — Supported rotational force:** Pre-chambered hip-unwind step-down and Drop-step rotational medicine-ball shot-put: Upper drop-step pushing release and lower supported hip unwind express force out of a loaded orientation.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
