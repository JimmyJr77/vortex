# Full Body Force Generation — Class 24 of 36

**Stage 8, lesson C: Ankle advance and forceful draw**

**Primary effort:** Practice separate hip-led low kicking and a descending upper-body rotational release, then regain controlled support. Small ankle advances, grounded leg return and linear arm draws support onset and finish.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Low roundhouse with forward step-down finish as the grounded rotational force anchor. Control residual forward/rotational momentum when the kicking leg becomes the next lead foot.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Low roundhouse with forward step-down finish. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Low roundhouse with forward step-down finish and Two-hand descending backhand medicine-ball throw: Descending upper hand arc and lower kick with a controlled forward recovery finish require distinct endpoint control.
- **E3 + E4 — Compact forward and elbow impulse:** Ankle Pogo — three small forward advances and Band Biceps Curl — loaded return into quick reversal: Keep the action compact as the ankles advance the body or elbows reverse the loaded curl, retaining control before the next effort.
- **E5 + E6 — Grounded return and shoulder draw:** Reverse Lunge to Knee Drive — fast grounded return and Band Straight-Arm Pulldown — rapid shoulder extension: Control the support position through the leg return or shoulder-only pull, finishing without a trunk crunch or unnecessary travel.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
