# Full Body Force Generation — Class 6 of 36

**Stage 2, lesson C: Ankle rebound and overhead draw**

**Primary effort:** Organize a forward plant into a grounded hip turn and compact rotational hand arc. Ankle rebound, a supported split-leg rise and linear arm pressing/pulling support that base and finish.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Forward plant-and-open to kick stance as the grounded rotational force anchor. Accept forward arrival onto a leg before freeing the other thigh for a kick.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Forward plant-and-open to kick stance. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Forward plant-and-open to kick stance and Rotational shadow hook: Compact unloaded upper turn paired with forward arrival into an open lower stance.
- **E3 + E4 — Compact ankle and arm finish:** Ankle Pogo in Place — short bilateral set and Band Chest Press — simultaneous fast extension, no release: Keep the action compact and the support organized, with a clear ankle or arm finish; retain the pogo's brief contacts without speeding the band's return.
- **E5 + E6 — Stable-base upward force:** Split Squat — fast supported rise, no flight and Band Straight-Arm Pulldown — rapid shoulder extension: Hold the support stance steady while the leg rises or shoulders draw the arms down, with no trunk crunch or unwanted turn.
