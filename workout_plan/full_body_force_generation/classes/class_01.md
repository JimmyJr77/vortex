# Full Body Force Generation — Class 1 of 36

**Stage 1, lesson A: Still-start upward and forward force**

**Primary effort:** Start a decisive upward leg push before a still-base forward release.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Squat Jump — settled quarter-squat start as the controlled lower-body force anchor. Start a decisive upward leg push before a still-base forward release.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Squat Jump — settled quarter-squat start. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Settled force onset:** Squat Jump — settled quarter-squat start and Medicine Ball Chest Pass — stationary, paused: Settle the body and load before accelerating; use the same clear start discipline for upward leg projection and forward hand release.
- **E3 + E4 — Force through a stable finish:** Calf Raise — rapid supported rise, no flight and Band Row — seated, fast pull: Keep the support base organized while force builds toward the ankle-rise or arm-draw finish; reset without rocking the torso.
- **E5 + E6 — Hip initiation into hand force:** Split-stance explosive hip pivot and Med Ball Rotational Scoop Toss — paused standing start; floor-target adaptation: Settled rotational projection beside an isolated grounded hip/foot initiation.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
