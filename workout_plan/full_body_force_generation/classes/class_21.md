# Full Body Force Generation — Class 21 of 36

**Stage 7, lesson C: Single-leg control with guided projection**

**Primary effort:** Reorganize a grounded stance into rotational knee drive and a controlled upper-body hand sweep. Single-leg support and guided or free forward pressing reinforce force transfer.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Grounded switch-step rotational knee drive as the grounded rotational force anchor. Link a fast support-role exchange to proximal rotational output without a full additional kick.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Grounded switch-step rotational knee drive. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Turn and control the finish:** Grounded switch-step rotational knee drive and Two-hand band backhand speed sweep: Predictable retained upper sweep and grounded lower stance exchange preserve rotation while avoiding linked-jump volume.
- **E3 + E4 — Supported knee drive and guided projection:** Single-Leg Squat Rise — supported rapid quarter-range and Landmine Push Press — single arm, shallow dip: Keep pressure through the supporting leg as force begins; retain the squat rise's support and the press's deliberate shallow dip-to-arm sequence.
- **E5 + E6 — Small projection from organized support:** Single-Leg Forward Takeoff — two-foot floor reception and Medicine Ball Chest Pass — stationary, paused: Direct the single-leg forward impulse or stationary hand release from a controlled base, then finish balanced without adding a run-up.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
