# Full Body Force Generation — Class 25 of 36

**Stage 9, lesson A: Step-close vertical and forward release**

**Primary effort:** Time a controlled foot plant with upward leg projection or forward arm release.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established lifting/landing technique, individually qualified ballistic/kicking skill and supervision. All athlete execution, load and recovery remain unreported.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Open with Walk-In Vertical Jump — step-close takeoff as the controlled lower-body force anchor. Time a controlled foot plant with upward leg projection or forward arm release.

**Quality marker:** Main observation: Walk-In Vertical Jump — step-close takeoff. Within its listed attempts, record the declared starting position, side, movement/receiving control and a stable finish. Also record the partner task in the first connection under its own criteria; do not treat unlike drills as equivalent force tests. A miss consumes the attempt; no additional testing slots or retries are added.

**Connections:**

- **E1 + E2 — Plant before projection:** Walk-In Vertical Jump — step-close takeoff and Medicine Ball Step-In Chest Pass — one forward plant: Organize the controlled foot plant before the upward jump or forward release, retaining the declared step pattern and a balanced finish.
- **E3 + E4 — Controlled reversal with a stable trunk:** Glute Bridge — controlled lower to rapid reversal and Band Lat Pulldown — single-arm rapid elbow drive: Keep the torso supported and organized while the hip reverses upward or the independent arm draws, without adding rotation to either source task.
- **E5 + E6 — Hip initiation into hand force:** Retreat-step low roundhouse counter and Lateral-step two-hand medicine-ball backhand throw: Lateral upper entry and retreating lower entry demand controlled direction-specific plants before rotation.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Six separate explosive tasks: two upper force, two lower force, one upper rotation and one lower rotation. The opener is today’s main quality observation; every other task has a specific supporting job. Use bodyweight/light resistance and rapid intent with controlled execution, preserving the written reset and side/set recovery. Extend rest to 2–3 minutes when needed. A miss consumes the attempt; no bonus test or make-up reps.

Stop for pain, unsafe implement path, trapped support knee, uncontrolled reception, lost alignment or clear slowing. Regress only remaining work when control returns; otherwise log the stopped slot. Source alternative doses are capped by the remaining combined dose. No new flight is added by the rotational selections.
