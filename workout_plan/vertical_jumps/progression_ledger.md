## Counting conventions

One jump repetition is one takeoff/flight/landing event, including a failed attempt. A bilateral landing is one event involving two feet, not one low-intensity-equivalent unit. A unilateral takeoff to a two-foot landing is recorded separately. Pogo reps include the final landing. A throw is one release; a ball impact is not an athlete landing. Approaches are recorded separately from flights. No jump/throw equivalence score is used.

For strength, a **set round** is the written set number including both sides where prescribed; a **performed set bout** counts each separately executed side. For example, 1 × 6 per leg is one round, two bouts, twelve repetitions. Repetition totals across exercises are descriptive and do not represent equal tissue demand. Preparation sets are separate. Rest, walking retrieval and holds used only to finish a jump do not create exercise entries.
