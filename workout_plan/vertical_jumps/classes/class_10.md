# Vertical Jumps — Class 10 of 12

**Primary effort:** Organize a short walking entry into a stable two-foot base, then express a clean vertical takeoff. **Gather-and-Hold Vertical Jump** is the primary task: two foot placements, a two-second tall settle, then one countermovement jump. The settle intentionally removes immediate approach momentum; this is an entry-to-standing integration task, not a fast approach-jump test.

**Foundation and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified supervision. Reviewed Classes 1–9, relevant prior standing/approach/split/press/step work, drill history including replacements, workload, identities, audits and available feedback. No actual attendance, Class 9 loading decision, performance, restrictions or recovery is supplied. Today's emphasis is a conditional teaching opportunity, not a diagnosed deficit or earned progression. Six formats change meaningful task constraints within familiar movement families; actual familiarity remains unknown.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Record valid attempts out of the six scheduled, by starting lead, plus entry length, stance, arm use, settle and landing control. Valid means exactly two walking placements into a comfortable parallel base, two seconds still and tall, one coordinated dip/arm swing, upward flight and a two-second bilateral landing hold without extra hop or foot adjustment. The first scheduled attempt from each lead checks current control; no extra test attempts. Different entry/start/arm protocols are not matched height tests. Use any force-plate measurement only if its actual setup and protocol are verified.

**Style:** Focused jump practice, small complementary power doses and challenging lower-body strength follow the broad approach illustrated by [OTA's vertical-power examples](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/my-top-3-exercises-for-increasing-vertical-power/). Today's exact starts, doses and non-flight adaptations are programming decisions, not an OTA-validated youth protocol.

| 1 | **Gather-and-Hold Vertical Jump — two walking placements, tall settle** — proposed execution | **3 × 2 = 6 jumps**, one left-first and one right-first entry each set. **2 s tall settle before the dip; 2 s landing hold. 20 s after walking return/reset between reps; 120 s between sets and before E2.** | Main task: establish a square, balanced takeoff base after movement, then direct force upward. | 3 | **Low Alternating Split-Stance Pogo — two switches then stop** — proposed variant | **2 × 2 = 4 low jumps**. Use a very small stagger, small knee motion and low clearance; switch front/back without crossing the feet across the body's midline. Both feet leave and meet the floor together. Use parallel low pogos if contacts become heavy, uneven or tangled. |
| 4 | **Paused Vertical Scoop Toss — no flight** — proposed variant | **2 × 2 = 4 throws**. Extend the legs and swing the ball upward/slightly forward in one continuous action; no preparatory re-dip, backswing or arm-only heave. Forefeet remain on the floor; heels may rise. Use less depth/a lighter ball for stalled drive or trunk loss. Let the ball land and settle; retrieve only on the coach's clear signal. Never catch its return. |
| 5 | **Split-Stance Dumbbell Push Press — fixed feet, no flight** — proposed variant | **1 × 3 per lead leg = 6 presses**, light dumbbells. About **1 s overhead hold, 2 s controlled lowering**. **5 s after lowering/reset between reps; 90 s between leads and before E6.** | Transfer a rapid leg impulse through the trunk into a retained vertical load from a staggered base. Keep feet hip-width apart laterally with a short fore/aft stagger; shallow dip then brisk leg drive and press. Both legs contribute. Feet stay planted; no step, jump, receiving dip or split jerk. Choose a load allowing obvious leg-driven speed, stable rack and comfortable overhead finish. Reduce load/stagger for twisting, slow pressing, back arch or lost foot contact. |
| 6 | **Lateral Step-Up — preplanted explosive knee drive, no flight** — proposed variant | **1 × 2 per leg = 4 rises**. Brief knee-up hold; about **3 s controlled free-foot lowering**. **20 s after return/reset between same-leg reps; 60 s between legs; 90 s before Section 3.** | Generate upward lead-leg force while controlling side-to-side pelvis motion, supporting a square plant rather than lateral drift. Stand side-on beside a low stable box, facing forward, with the nearer whole foot preplanted. Drive up without pivoting, raise the free knee and lower its foot back beside the box. Keep the lead foot on the box for both reps, then step it off. Use secure light hand support/lower height for pelvic shift; avoid pronounced free-leg push, lateral leap or a balance grind. |
