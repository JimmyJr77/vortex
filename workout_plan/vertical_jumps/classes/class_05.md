# Vertical Jumps — Class 5 of 12

**Primary effort:** Produce decisive upward force through each takeoff leg while keeping the foot, knee and pelvis organized. **Single-Leg Takeoff to Two-Foot Box Landing** is the primary drill. Supporting work uses a staggered push, low ankle rebound, a deeper supported start, rapid hip extension and a leg-driven ball release. The supporting staggered tasks use both feet; they are not falsely counted as isolated single-leg force.

**Foundation and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified supervision. Classes 1–4, their workload/decision records, drill history and current feedback were reviewed. No actual athlete attendance, execution, working load or recovery has been supplied. Class 4's prescribed skips do not establish same-leg landing competence or tolerance. Today's defaults use two-foot jump landings; the single-leg takeoff itself remains a demand to assess through the scheduled work.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Record valid attempts out of three per takeoff leg. A valid attempt begins on that foot without a preparatory hop or push from the free foot, uses a controlled shallow dip and arm swing, projects upward to a low box with little knee tuck, and finishes with both whole feet supported and a 2 s landing hold. Record box height/gap, actual takeoff side, any extra foot contact, drift, knee/pelvis fault and intent. Box height is not jump height; this is not a matched comparison with Class 4's two-foot takeoff or floor skips. Do not add attempts to complete a score or equalize sides.

**Style reference:** OTA's [basketball vertical-jump example](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/vertical-jump-training-for-basketball/) includes one-foot box takeoffs with two-foot landings. This class adapts that task to six total attempts and the stated cohort; it does not copy the source's dose, box progression or outcome claims. The distinct defaults preserve related force-development roles; repeated regressions are disclosed when needed.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use this order. E1 receives six primary attempts; E2 is a small supporting jump dose, and E3 is only low ankle rebound. E4–E6 express force rapidly without intended athlete flight. All bodyweight work stays unloaded; ball/bell loads must allow crisp, controlled motion. For side-based work, begin left then right unless a known actual prior order justifies reversing it; record the chosen order.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Single-Leg Takeoff to Two-Foot Box Landing** | **1 × 3 per takeoff leg = 6 jumps**. **30 s after step-down/return between same-leg attempts; 90 s between legs; 120 s before E2.** | Main task: direct one leg's dip-to-drive force upward. Start stationary on one foot, free foot clear; no approach or repeated hop. Swing the arms, extend the stance leg and land on a low stable box with both whole feet. Hold 2 s, stand and step down. Choose a small gap and easily controlled target, not a height challenge. Lower box/intent or use the floor replacement for knee collapse, pelvic rotation, deep tuck or edge contact. |
| 2 | **Staggered-Stance Vertical Jump to Parallel Landing** — proposed exact execution | **1 × 2 per lead leg = 4 jumps**. **20 s between same-lead attempts; 60 s between leads; 90 s before E3.** | Rehearse a lead-leg-biased upward push with help from the rear foot, then organize a square two-foot landing. Start hip-width with one foot slightly behind, rear heel light; use one comfortable dip and arm swing. Both feet contribute to takeoff. Bring the feet to a parallel landing and hold 2 s without rotating. Use modest flight; reduce the stagger/intent for twist, excessive rear-foot drive or an unstable landing. No force-sharing percentage is assumed. |
| 3 | **Split-Stance Pogo — low, same lead throughout** | **1 × 4 per lead leg = 8 low jumps**, continuous within each four-jump bout. **60 s between leads; 90 s before E4.** | Practice quick ankle return in an asymmetric takeoff base without a full single-leg rebound landing. Both feet leave/return together in a short comfortable stagger, same lead throughout; stay low, tall and centered with small knee motion. Count every landing, including the final one. Narrow the stagger or stop for scissoring, drifting, louder contacts or loss of rhythm. Do not convert this into high split jumps or single-leg pogos. |
| 4 | **Half-Kneeling Get-Up to Stand — rapid lead-leg drive** — proposed power execution | **1 × 3 per lead leg = 6 non-flight rises**. Rise briskly, hold the free knee up briefly, then **lower over about 3 s** to the same half-kneeling start. **20 s between same-lead efforts; 60 s between leads; 90 s before E5.** | Express upward force from greater front-leg flexion with a supported starting position. Start with the lead whole foot flat and rear knee on a comfortable floor, torso organized over the lead foot. Drive through the lead leg to stand; let the rear leg follow into a knee lift, without jumping or pushing hands on the thigh. Rear toes may assist the initial transition; do not claim a pure one-leg force measure. Use secure light hand support if balance limits drive. If it remains slow/grinding or knee contact is uncomfortable, use the shallower standing replacement below. |
| 5 | **Staggered-Stance Kettlebell Swing — two-handed, no flight** — proposed stance variant | **1 × 3 per lead leg = 6 swings**, continuous within each three-swing bout. **60 s between leads; 90 s before E6.** | Express rapid hip extension with a lead-leg bias while retaining rear-foot support. Front foot stays flat; rear forefoot is slightly behind, heel raised, with enough width and bell clearance. Hinge with square hips, drive tall and let the bell float no higher than chest height; arms guide it. Park before changing stance. Use a light bell; no twisting, hopping or arm lift. First scheduled swings establish control; reduce load/stagger or use the bilateral replacement if stance control limits the hinge. Two start/park sequences are additional handling work, not extra swings. |
| 6 | **Split-Stance Vertical Scoop Toss — no flight** — proposed exact execution | **1 × 2 per lead leg = 4 throws**. **20 s after retrieval/reset between same-lead throws; 60 s between leads; 90 s before Section 3.** | Transfer a lead-leg-biased dip/extension into an upward/slightly forward ball release while the rear foot supports the base. Hold a light ball in both hands, use a comfortable short split stance, dip without twisting and drive tall through the legs before the arms release. Both feet remain in contact with the floor; heels may rise. Keep hips/shoulders square and finish balanced. Reduce load/stance or use a parallel base if rearward rocking, arm-only throwing or uncontrolled range dominates. No distance target or catching the return. |

**Readiness and quality:** The first scheduled E1/E2 attempts use controlled intent to check their exact stance/takeoff/landing; stronger later intent depends on demonstrated control. E3 begins low with both feet contributing. E4 must permit a brisk rise and controlled descent; it is not slow balance practice used to fill an explosive slot. E5 requires a controlled hinge, grip, stance and start/park, rather than presumed mastery from an earlier written swing. E6 requires a verified release/landing-and-roll area. These execution checks consume scheduled repetitions; no additional athlete demonstrations or trial sets are prescribed.

Stop immediately for pain, giving way, dizziness, disabling fear or unsafe equipment/space. After a clear quality decline, extend recovery 30–60 s. If the next scheduled attempt still deteriorates, reduce intent/load/complexity or end the task. Record failed/omitted attempts, incidental contacts and actual variants. Remaining attempts may use a replacement, but already attempted work counts against the slot's original budget; never restart a full dose after a switch.

**Equipment and layout:** Only the listed equipment categories and three separate 10 m lanes are confirmed. Match low-box/bench/step dimensions and stable hand supports to the athlete; allow a clear jump/fall zone, controlled step-down and return route. The kneeling surface must be comfortable and non-slip; no padding or extra equipment is presumed available. Keep the swing arc, parked bell and waiting athletes apart. Use one active athlete per supervised zone. For E6 verify ball mass/type, overhead clearance and a closed upward/forward landing-and-roll sector within actual available space; do not test an uncertain boundary with a throw. Wait until the ball settles and the coach signals clear before retrieval, and keep all others outside the sector.

**Replacements occupy the original slot:** If an appropriate E1 box cannot be arranged, **use a single-leg vertical takeoff to two-foot floor landing instead of E1**, same six-attempt budget, rests and 2 s landing holds. This repeats an earlier drill, removes six elevated landings/six step-downs and keeps six unilateral takeoffs; it is not a claim that floor landing has lower impact. If single-leg takeoff itself is unsuitable, **use a natural-arm floor Countermovement Jump instead of E1**, same total six attempts in two three-attempt blocks, same rests and holds; record the loss of the unilateral job and use no extra compensating work.

If E2's changed landing stance cannot be controlled, **use same-lead Split-Squat Jump to Stick instead of E2**, 1 × 2 per lead within the original budget, same rests and low intent, landing back in the takeoff split stance for 2 s. If E3's stagger disrupts low contacts, **use eight low stationary bilateral ankle pogos instead of E3**, sharing the original eight-attempt budget, in two four-contact bouts with the same 60 s / 90 s rests. These repeat earlier formats for execution, not novelty.

If floor contact, range or balance prevents E4's rapid drive, **use a supported Split-Stance Power Rise instead of E4**, 1 × 3 per lead from a shallow standing split stance, rise rapidly without flight and lower over 3 s, same rests and budget. This is the earlier offered regression with a smaller range. If E5's stance is unsuitable, **use a bilateral two-handed Kettlebell Swing instead of E5**, two three-swing bouts sharing the six-swing budget, same rests; park between bouts. If E6's split base interferes with the release, **use the earlier parallel-stance non-flight vertical scoop toss instead of E6**, two two-throw bouts, same rests and four-throw budget. If neither appropriate equipment/space nor a productive replacement is available, resolve that slot before delivery or log it unperformed; do not invent a seventh drill.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light and controlled: leave **at least 5 clean repetitions in reserve**, or comparable ease on holds. Retain the small established dose so these exercises do not undermine the main strength work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Step-Down — low lateral heel-tap variant** | **1 × 5 per leg = 10 reps**, bodyweight or light dumbbell. **3 s down, 1 s return. 30 s between legs; 45 s before S2.** | Lightly load the stance leg while holding knee, pelvis and foot alignment through flexion. This supports a consistent plant/dip position. Use a suitably low stable box; touch the free heel without pushing from it. Reduce range/load for hip drop, knee collapse or loss of stance-foot contact. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported single-leg hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot = 6 holds**. Bodyweight; **5 s relaxed between holds, 30 s between sides, 60 s before primary preparation.** | Strengthen active arch control while keeping heel and both forefoot bases in contact. Use hand support, a soft knee and long toes so foot control, rather than balance, is trained. Ease the contraction if toes claw or the ankle rolls; stop for cramping that does not resolve. |

If no suitably low stable step is available, **use a hand-supported single-leg shallow squat instead of S1**: 1 × 5 per leg with the same tempo/rest, free foot clear, light effort; this remains a proposed replacement without a claimed canonical ID. **Use bilateral standing Short-Foot Drill instead of S2** if single-leg arch control is inconsistent: 1 × 3 five-second holds with both feet together, 5 s relaxed between holds, then 60 s before primary preparation. These replace slots; they are not added exercises.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises
