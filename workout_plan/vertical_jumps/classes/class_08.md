# Vertical Jumps — Class 8 of 12

**Primary effort:** Consolidate leg-led upward projection with a repeatable dip and organized landing. **No-Arm-Swing Box Jump** is the primary task. Brief support work changes the split-stance landing, ankle impulse, seated start and loaded hip start while preserving upward force as the job. A simple vertical scoop toss recurs to retain leg-to-arm release practice without adding another new throw constraint.

**Foundation and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified supervision. Reviewed saved Classes 1–7, relevant standing/approach and rebound work, audits, progression/workload records, drill history and available feedback. Actual athlete completion, working loads, technical response and recovery remain unknown. Class 7's written rebounds do not establish earned height, faster contacts or a heavier load. Consolidation here describes the programming aim, not demonstrated athlete mastery.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Record valid attempts out of six. A valid attempt uses hands at the hips during one controlled dip/takeoff, projects upward without a preparatory step/hop, and lands with both whole feet on a suitably low box for a 2 s hold. Record box height/gap, dip depth, arm policy, intent, knee/pelvis control, forward drift and tuck. Release the arms to recover balance if necessary and record the change; never preserve a score at the expense of landing control. Box height is not jump height. This is not a matched retest of the earlier hands-on-hips floor CMJ.

**Style reference:** OTA's [pogo example](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/vertical-jump-warm-up-series/) includes alternating lower/higher pogo efforts. This class uses six controlled contacts with only a modest amplitude difference, not the source's maximal alternating jumps or full sequence. Existing Access & Prepare 1 remains unchanged.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use this order. E1 has six primary jumps, E2 only two isolated switches, and E3 six ankle contacts. E4–E6 have no intended athlete flight. Bodyweight work is unloaded; bell and ball loads must allow rapid controlled action. New task checks use scheduled repetitions, not extra trials.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No-Arm-Swing Box Jump** | **3 × 2 = 6 jumps**. **20 s after step-down/return between reps; 120 s between sets and before E2.** | Main task: direct leg-driven extension upward with arm assistance removed at takeoff. Start stationary, feet parallel, hands at hips; make one comfortable dip and jump to a low stable box with a small gap. Land with both whole feet, hold 2 s, stand, step down and reset. Keep the target easily controlled, without deep knee tuck or a forward dive. Lower target/intent or use the floor replacement for edge contact, drift, knee loss or arm-assisted takeoff. |
| 2 | **Lunge Switch Jump — isolated, opposite-lead landing** | **1 × 1 per starting lead leg = 2 jumps**. Start left lead for the first, right lead for the second. **60 s between starting leads; 90 s before E3.** | Preserve upward projection while the feet exchange lead in flight. Start in a short, hip-width split stance with both feet contributing, use a shallow dip/natural arms, jump modestly upward and exchange front/back feet once. Land in the opposite short split stance and hold 2 s; reset completely. Front foot lands flat, rear forefoot supported, knees/pelvis organized. No long scissor reach, rotation or immediate next jump. Use the same-lead replacement if switching compromises control; this is not a single-leg takeoff or landing. |
| 3 | **Pulsing Pogo Jump — alternating low/modestly taller** — proposed exact execution | **1 × 6 jumps**, continuous: low, modestly taller, low, modestly taller, low, modestly taller. **90 s before E4.** | Adjust ankle impulse while keeping the body tall and the landing under the hips. Both feet leave/land together; keep knee motion small and the taller contacts clearly submaximal. Count all six landings, including the final stop. The sequence has three low and three modestly taller ankle flights; do not turn the latter into deep squat jumps. Reduce the amplitude difference or use uniform low pogos for loud contacts, collapse, drift or rhythm loss. |
| 4 | **Explosive Sit-to-Stand — no flight** — proposed power execution | **2 × 3 = 6 rapid stands**. Lower back to the seat over **about 3 s**, settle fully for **2 s**, then begin the next effort after the stated rest. **20 s after seated reset between reps; 90 s between sets and before E5.** | Produce upward knee/hip force from a supported still start, without a countermovement or new jump landing. Sit on a stable fitted bench/box with both feet flat, arms crossed comfortably; lean the trunk forward enough to stand and drive up briskly without rocking, bouncing off the seat or jumping. Finish tall and return under control. Use a higher suitable seat or arms for counterbalance if needed; record the changed condition. A slow/grinding stand is not the intended power task. |
| 5 | **Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing — two hands, no flight** — proposed source variant | **2 × 3 = 6 swings**, each a separate start and park. **20 s after parking/reset between reps; 90 s between sets and before E6.** | Generate rapid hip force anew on each attempt rather than using a continuous returning-bell cycle. Set a light bell securely just in front, hinge to grip with both hands, hike it close, extend the hips so it floats no higher than chest height, then allow the return and park under control. Both feet stay planted; arms guide rather than lift. Settle the bell completely before the next start. Reduce load or use the continuous two-hand replacement if repeated hikes/parks compromise grip, hinge, trajectory or safe placement. Six starts and six parks are handling work in addition to the six counted swings. |
| 6 | **Vertical Scoop Toss — parallel stance, no flight** — deliberately recurring exact execution | **2 × 2 = 4 throws**. **20 s after retrieval/reset between throws; 90 s between sets and before Section 3.** | Retain a simple leg-to-arm upward release after the other starting conditions. Hold a light ball in both hands, feet parallel; dip comfortably, extend the legs and let the arms carry the ball upward/slightly forward into the inspected sector. Forefeet remain in floor contact; heels may rise. Finish balanced with ribs over pelvis, no back arch, arm-only heave or return catch. Reduce ball load/range for poor leg-led release. This repeats earlier scoop work for its direct job, not as a novel drill or matched performance test. |

**Readiness and quality:** First scheduled E1/E2 attempts use controlled intent to assess the exact takeoff/landing. E2 remains a two-jump support dose; do not add practice switches. E5 requires a secure two-hand grip and a controlled start/park on every rep; previous written swing work does not prove this. E6's simpler identity does not establish familiarity or clearance. Already attempted work counts against the original slot budget; never restart a full replacement dose or make up missed work elsewhere.

**Equipment and layout:** Three separate 10 m lanes and listed equipment types are confirmed; quantities, dimensions, loads and widths are not. Verify a low stable jump target, appropriate seat/step/bench, secure hand supports, clear fall/step-down/return routes, a non-slip floor and adequate short split-stance space. No approach or platform drop is prescribed. Keep the full bell arc, start/park area and waiting athletes separate. For E6 verify a manageable ball, overhead clearance and a closed upward/forward landing-and-roll sector. Do not test an uncertain boundary with a throw. **Replacements occupy the original slot:** If no suitable E1 box is available, **use Class 7's hands-on-hips isolated floor Countermovement Jump instead of E1**, sharing the six-flight budget in 3 × 2 with the same rests and 2 s landing holds. This removes six elevated landings/six dismounts and adds six floor landings; lower impact is not presumed. These repeats preserve useful jobs without adding contacts.

For E4, **use the same Sit-to-Stand with a higher stable seat or free arm counterbalance instead of the arms-crossed version** when that restores brisk, controlled standing; retain the six-rep budget, seated reset and rests. Do not pull on an unverified support or create a different exercise circuit. If no suitable seat/execution exists, resolve or log that slot omitted.

For E5, **use the earlier continuous two-handed Kettlebell Swing instead of the dead-stop version**, two three-swing bouts sharing six total swings, **90 s between bouts and before E6**, with one start/park per bout. A full substitution changes six start/parks to two and removes the four between-rep rests; its model is 96 s shorter. Any partially attempted starts/swings/parks still count in actuals. E6 may use a lighter suitable ball; if a safe release/sector is unavailable, resolve or record omission. No extra exercise is added.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light and controlled: leave **at least 5 clean repetitions in reserve**, or comparable ease on holds. Retain the small established dose so these exercises do not undermine the main strength work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Step-Down — low lateral heel-tap variant** | **1 × 5 per leg = 10 reps**, bodyweight or light dumbbell. **3 s down, 1 s return. 30 s between legs; 45 s before S2.** | Lightly load the stance leg while holding knee, pelvis and foot alignment through flexion. This supports a consistent plant/dip position. Use a suitably low stable box; touch the free heel without pushing from it. Reduce range/load for hip drop, knee collapse or loss of stance-foot contact. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported single-leg hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot = 6 holds**. Bodyweight; **5 s relaxed between holds, 30 s between sides, 60 s before primary preparation.** | Strengthen active arch control while keeping heel and both forefoot bases in contact. Use hand support, a soft knee and long toes so foot control, rather than balance, is trained. Ease the contraction if toes claw or the ankle rolls; stop for cramping that does not resolve. |

If no suitably low stable step is available, **use a hand-supported single-leg shallow squat instead of S1**: 1 × 5 per leg with the same tempo/rest, free foot clear, light effort; this remains a proposed replacement without a claimed canonical ID. **Use bilateral standing Short-Foot Drill instead of S2** if single-leg arch control is inconsistent: 1 × 3 five-second holds with both feet together, 5 s relaxed between holds, then 60 s before primary preparation. These replace slots; they are not added exercises.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |

| 2 | **Split Squat — stationary, dumbbells** | **2 × 4 per leg = 16 working reps**.
