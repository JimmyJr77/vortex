# Vertical Jumps — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Produce a powerful, leg-driven vertical takeoff from organized starting positions. **Seated Box Jump is the primary performance drill**; the other drills address constrained arm action, split-stance force, upright flight, rapid single-leg extension and rapid hip extension.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified supervision. Classes 1–2 were prescribed; attendance, execution and recovery remain unknown. Today's new drill formats are not assumed mastered. Use the first scheduled attempts to confirm execution at controlled intent; increase intent only within the prescribed dose when that execution is demonstrated. No extra tryouts or make-up reps.

**Drill selection:** All six default explosive drills are different from those in Classes 1–2. This applies the user's new preference for unique drills while retaining useful strength lifts. Seated power is directly inspired by [Overtime Athletes' Seated Box Jump example](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/top-10-vertical-drills-7-seated-box-jump/); the exact doses and youth delivery are this curriculum's decisions.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Record valid attempts out of six. A valid attempt starts seated and still for 2 s, with feet flat and the torso already set for takeoff; no backward rocking, extra dip or repeated seat bounce. Drive upward with coordinated arms, land with both whole feet supported on the low box, hold a controlled landing 2 s, stand, then step down. Record seat height, target-box height, gap and any rocking, excessive knee tuck or unstable landing. Box height is not jump height. This new drill is not a matched retest of the earlier standing CMJ or paused Squat Jump.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Follow this order. The seated task receives the largest jump dose and comes first. All jumps are bodyweight; keep supporting flight modest. E5 is a rapid force-expression task without intended flight; E6 is ballistic hip-extension work. Neither is mislabeled as a jump contact.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Box Jump** | **3 × 2**, still seated **2 s** before each takeoff; **20 s after returning to the start between attempts; 120 s between sets and before E2.** | Main task: produce upward power from a supported stationary start. Use an individually suitable seat with feet flat; lean to the takeoff posture before the hold, then drive upward without rocking or pushing hands on the seat. Land on a stable low box and hold 2 s. Choose a target that can be cleared through upward projection with little knee tuck. Lower the box/intent for a deep tucked landing or edge contact; stop for instability. |
| 2 | **Volleyball Block Jump — stationary, hands initially high** | **2 × 2**. Start with hands near forehead, elbows bent; **20 s between attempts; 90 s between sets and before E3.** | Express vertical leg drive when a large backward arm swing is unavailable. Use a short comfortable dip, drive upward and reach overhead without drawing the hands behind the body. Land bilaterally and hold 2 s. No net, ball or approach. Reduce height/intent for trunk arch, drifting or uncontrolled landing. |
| 3 | **Split-Squat Jump to Stick — same lead leg** | **1 × 2 per lead leg = 4 jumps**, low controlled flight. **20 s between same-lead attempts; 60 s between leads; 90 s before E4.** | Practice upward force from an asymmetric two-foot base. Start in a comfortable split stance, dip slightly and jump upward; retain the same lead leg in flight and land back in the same split stance for a 2 s hold. Both feet contribute; this is not counted as a single-leg takeoff. No scissor switch. Shorten stance/reduce height for pelvic rotation or unstable landing. |
| 4 | **Low Hurdle Hop to Stick — forward, two feet** — explicit proposed execution | **1 × 3** individual hops. **20 s between attempts; 90 s before E5.** | Make an upright upward projection clear one small external obstacle, then own the landing without a rebound. Use only enough forward travel to clear it, both feet taking off/landing together, then hold 2 s. Keep the hurdle low enough that the athlete need not tuck deeply or leap for distance. Lower/remove the obstacle for clipping or excessive travel; never chase hurdle height. |
| 5 | **Step-Up — explosive bodyweight knee-drive variant** — proposed execution | **1 × 3 per leg = 6 rapid rises**. **20 s between same-leg efforts; 60 s between legs; 90 s before E6.** | Express force quickly through one loaded lead leg with no planned jump landing. Keep its whole foot on a low stable box; drive rapidly to stand while the free knee rises, hold briefly, then lower the free foot to the floor over about 3 s. Keep the lead foot on the box for that side's three efforts. Minimize push from the trailing foot; no hop off the box. Lower box/use light hand support if balance limits speed or alignment. |
| 6 | **Kettlebell Swing — two-handed, to chest height** | **2 × 4**, crisp continuous swings within each set; **90 s between sets and before Section 3.** | Express rapid hip extension, complementing the jump drills' larger knee-extension demand. Use a light manageable bell, a braced hinge and a sharp hip drive that floats the bell; arms guide rather than lift. Finish tall without leaning back. The first four prescribed swings use a conservative load and count toward the eight. Reduce load for arm pulling, squat-like motion or lost back position; park the bell and stop if control is not restored. |

**Quality and readiness:** E1–E4's first scheduled attempts are controlled execution checks and still count. Use stronger takeoff intent on later scheduled attempts only when technique and landing control warrant it; record intent. E5 requires controlled step-up/lowering and a clear lead-leg drive. E6 requires demonstrated hinge and secure bell start/park; basic lifting experience alone does not prove swing competence. Keep these new formats modest and stop an unsuitable task rather than force fourteen completed slots.

After a clear decline, extend recovery 30–60 s. If the next scheduled effort still deteriorates, reduce intent/load/complexity; end the exercise when quality remains poor. Stop immediately for pain, giving way, dizziness, disabling fear or unsafe equipment/space. Count invalid attempts and incidental contacts; no extra attempts to improve a score.

**Equipment and layout:** Seat, target box, step-up box and hurdle heights/quantities are unconfirmed. Use individually suitable stable equipment with whole-foot support and manageable steps down. The seated target must be close enough for upward projection without a long forward leap, with a clear gap/fall zone and safe exit; no height or gap is presumed confirmed. Reserve a jump/landing zone plus its return path within the available 10 m lane length and verified width. Only one athlete moves in a zone at a time. Box changes occur during the planned transitions, not while another athlete is jumping. Set the swing station away from all jump traffic with front/rear/side bell clearance.

**Replacements occupy the original slot:** If a suitable target box is unavailable but a safe seat and floor-landing area exist, **use a Seated Vertical Jump to floor landing instead of E1**: same 3 × 2, 2 s seated hold, 2 s landing hold and rests; position the feet/seat so the flight and landing clear the seat. This proposed execution changes six elevated landings into six floor landings and removes six box step-downs. If neither arrangement works, resolve E1 before delivery. If a hurdle's fit or construction is unsuitable, **use a two-foot forward hop over a virtual low line between two cones instead of E4**, keeping 1 × 3 and the same resets/landing holds; cones sit beside the flight path, not under it. If no suitable low box exists for E5, **use a supported Split-Stance Power Rise instead of E5**: 1 × 3 per lead leg, from a shallow split stance drive rapidly to tall through the lead leg without leaving the floor, then lower over 3 s; same rests, proposed replacement. It remains a low-impact force-expression support task, not another jump or primary lift.

If a manageable kettlebell is unavailable but an appropriately weighted dumbbell is securely held by both hands, **use a two-handed dumbbell swing instead of E6**, same 2 × 4 and rests; verify grip and clearance. If swing execution cannot be coached within these scheduled reps, stop that task and record it, rather than improvise another exercise. Regressions may trade uniqueness for appropriate execution; they do not authorize additional drills.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light and controlled: leave **at least 5 clean repetitions in reserve**, or comparable ease on holds. Retain the small established dose so these exercises do not undermine the main strength work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Step-Down — low lateral heel-tap variant** | **1 × 5 per leg = 10 reps**, bodyweight or light dumbbell. **3 s down, 1 s return. 30 s between legs; 45 s before S2.** | Lightly load the stance leg while holding knee, pelvis and foot alignment through flexion. This supports a consistent plant/dip position. Use a suitably low stable box; touch the free heel without pushing from it. Reduce range/load for hip drop, knee collapse or loss of stance-foot contact. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported single-leg hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot = 6 holds**. Bodyweight; **5 s relaxed between holds, 30 s between sides, 60 s before primary preparation.** | Strengthen active arch control while keeping heel and both forefoot bases in contact. Use hand support, a soft knee and long toes so foot control, rather than balance, is trained. Ease the contraction if toes claw or the ankle rolls; stop for cramping that does not resolve. |

If no suitably low stable step is available, **use a hand-supported single-leg shallow squat instead of S1**: 1 × 5 per leg with the same tempo/rest, free foot clear, light effort; this remains a proposed replacement without a claimed canonical ID. **Use bilateral standing Short-Foot Drill instead of S2** if single-leg arch control is inconsistent: 1 × 3 five-second holds with both feet together, 5 s relaxed between holds, then 60 s before primary preparation. These replace slots; they are not added exercises.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

**Load selection:** Target approximately **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve** on working sets, supported by coach observation and athlete report. No fixed increase over the earlier written classes is prescribed. If a compatible load was actually recorded and the athlete is ready, it can inform today's selection. Without that record, establish the load through the preparation sets below; do not invent a previous weight. Finish each repetition with purposeful upward force and controlled lowering, without grinding, forced reps or maximal testing.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Double Front-Rack Squat — dumbbells** | **3 × 6 working reps**. About **3 s down**, stand decisively. **150 s between working sets and before P2 preparation.** | Maintain the main bilateral knee/hip-extension force base for the vertical drive. Keep the rack and trunk organized over the whole foot through a repeatable, controlled depth. Reduce load/range for heel lift, knee collapse or trunk/rack loss. Keep the same useful range where actual prior records exist. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — dumbbells** | **2 × 6 working reps**. **3 s down, about 1 s up. 120 s between working sets and before P3 preparation.** | Challenge hip extension from a flexed-hip position, complementing the squat's larger knee excursion. Keep dumbbells close, knees softly bent and hips traveling back. End the descent before back rounding/pelvic tuck; reduce load or range if hinge control is lost. |
| 3 | **Split Squat — stationary, dumbbells** | **1 × 6 per leg = 12 working reps**. **3 s down, about 1 s up. 75 s between legs; 120 s before P4 preparation.** | Maintain a challenging lead-leg force contribution for each side of takeoff without a second large squat volume. Keep the rear forefoot grounded, heel lifted, and pelvis controlled. Reduce load or use hand support if balance limits leg force. One set per leg remains intentional. |
| 4 | **Hip Thrust — sandbag across pelvis, upper back on bench** | **1 × 8 working reps**. **1 s up, 1 s hold, 1 s down. 90 s before P5 preparation.** | Load the terminal part of hip extension with supported trunk geometry, complementing the RDL's flexed-hip emphasis. Stop at aligned ribs/pelvis rather than arching the back. Reduce load for lumbar extension or loss of stable bench contact. |
| 5 | **Standing Calf Raise — supported single-leg, dumbbell** | **1 × 8 per leg = 16 working reps**. **1 s up, 1 s hold, 1 s down. 60 s between legs; 75 s before P6 preparation.** | Challenge each ankle's nearly straight-knee plantarflexion for the late upward push. Work from the floor with secure hand support; keep heel travel vertical and knee angle steady. Use bodyweight if it meets the effort target, otherwise add manageable load. Reduce load for shrinking range or knee bending. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **2 × 10 working reps**, both legs together. **1 s up, 1 s hold, 1 s down**; quiet heel return. **90 s between working sets.** | Maintain ankle-force work with bent knees, complementing P5 and the ankle's contribution through the dip-to-drive action. Keep knees approximately over the feet, hold loads securely and keep the knee angle stable. Reduce load for bounce, knee extension or loss of heel range. |

**Separate load-preparation sets:** P1 **2 × 5**, light then moderate, **60 s between preparation sets and 120 s before working**. P2 **1 × 5 light**, then **90 s**. P3 **1 × 3 light per leg**, **30 s between legs**, then **90 s**. P4 **1 × 5 light**, then **60 s**. P5 **1 × 4 light per leg**, **30 s between legs**, then **45 s**. P6 **1 × 5 light**, then **60 s**. Keep these non-fatiguing; use the working exercise's tempo. Additional ramp sets needed for a particular load must be logged and added to the clock. They are not new exercises or a recreated Access & Prepare sequence.

Stop a working set at the technical limit even if the target reps are not reached, then reduce the next load. Extend recovery when needed. **Use two kettlebells instead of dumbbells in P1** if they provide a manageable rack and appropriate load. **Use a sandbag floor Glute Bridge instead of P4** if bench fit/stability is unsuitable: retain 1 × 5 light preparation, 60 s to work, 1 × 8 working at the same tempo and 90 s before P5 preparation; record the different support/range. All loads must be manageable to pick up, position and put down. Secure hand supports against movement.
