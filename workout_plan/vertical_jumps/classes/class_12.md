# Vertical Jumps — Class 12 of 12

**Primary effort:** Express a high-quality standing vertical jump and record an honest final curriculum observation. **Countermovement Jump** deliberately retains Class 11's exercise, six-attempt structure and rests. No actual Class 11 result or compatible baseline has been supplied, so the default is an initial observation, not an improvement claim.

**Foundation and review:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, under qualified supervision. Reviewed Classes 1–11, the exact framework/map, assessment protocol, workload/progression, drill history including replacements, identities, audits and available feedback. Athlete attendance, load, competence, restrictions, measurements and recovery remain unknown. E1 repeats for a stated assessment purpose; five supporting formats change relevant start, stance or reset conditions after that observation. Written completion is not earned progression.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Retain Class 11's two rehearsal/check slots and four conditional candidate slots. Record every attempt as valid, modified, invalid or omitted with reason, actual intent/cue/conditions and rest. Valid execution is a stationary parallel start, one self-selected dip with natural arm swing, upward two-foot takeoff and a controlled bilateral **2 s landing hold** without an extra hop/step. Pair 1 is excluded from candidate summaries. Use the [assessment protocol](../assessment_protocol.md); no extra attempts replace failures, and no verified measurement means execution-only observation.

**Style:** [OTA's vertical-jump programming article](https://blog.overtimeathletes.com/vertical-jump-training-how-to-program-explosive-bounce-for-serious-athletes/) informs the combination of direct jumping, small elastic/power doses and challenging lower-body strength. The exact variants, doses and assessment rules are coaching decisions for this specification, without guaranteed gains or whole-session validation.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use this order. E1's observation precedes all supporting work. Six E1, two E2 and four E3 jumps total **twelve bodyweight bilateral floor landings**. E4–E6 have no intended athlete flight. First scheduled work checks each task's execution; no additional practice, tests or compensating work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Countermovement Jump — natural arm swing, stick landing** — deliberately recurring | **3 × 2 = 6 jumps**. Set 1: two controlled execution checks; sets 2–3: up to four candidate jumps with maximum-height intent **only while control/readiness permit**. **30 s after landing/reset between reps; 120 s between sets and before E2.** | Main performance task: coordinate a natural dip and arm swing into direct upward propulsion. Start stationary with the same comfortable stance, use one self-selected dip, jump upward and hold the bilateral landing **2 s**. No approach, pre-hop, pause in the squat, loaded implement or rebound. Keep the declared cue/arm/stance protocol stable in sets 2–3. Reduce intent or stop for uncontrolled drift/landing; record modified work without treating it as comparable maximal output. |
| 2 | **Paused Staggered-Stance Vertical Jump to Parallel Landing** — proposed variant | **1 × 1 per lead leg = 2 jumps**, modest flight. Hold a comfortable shallow staggered squat **2 s**, then jump without a new dip; hold the parallel two-foot landing **2 s**. **60 s between leads; 90 s before E3.** | Produce upward force from a still asymmetric base and organize it into a square landing. Both feet contribute: lead foot flat, rear forefoot slightly behind, adequate lateral width. Set arms still behind before the pause, then swing forward/up with leg extension. Bring feet parallel without twisting or chasing height. The pause differs from Class 5's active-dip staggered jump; the parallel finish differs from Class 10's same-lead paused split jump. Reduce stagger/intent or replace for a re-dip, twist or unstable finish. |
| 3 | **Low In-Out Ankle Pogo — parallel feet, small width change** — proposed variant | **2 × 2 = 4 low jumps**. Each bout begins at comfortable hip width, lands slightly wider, then returns to the original width and holds **2 s**. **90 s between bouts and before E4.** | Maintain quick ankle return while accurately placing both feet beneath a centered trunk, supporting a repeatable takeoff base. Keep the width change small, toes oriented forward, knees soft with little bend, and both feet leaving/landing together. No feet-together squeeze, deep wide squat, arm jack, crossing or distance target. One linked transition per bout. Use ordinary in-place pogos if width changes cause rolling, collapse, heavy contacts or rhythm loss. |
| 4 | **Paused Split-Stance Vertical Scoop Toss — no flight** — proposed variant | **1 × 2 per lead leg = 4 throws**, light ball held low between thighs from a shallow split squat/hinge. Hold still **2 s** before release. **20 s after retrieval/reset between same-lead throws; 60 s between leads; 90 s before E5.** | Transfer a settled lead-leg-biased force impulse into upward/slightly forward underhand release while keeping the trunk square. Both feet support the base; forefeet remain on the floor and heels may rise. Extend legs and carry the ball through continuously, without re-dip, twist, arm-only heave or backward lean. This changes the moving split scoop and the earlier parallel pause. Reduce load/stagger/depth if rapid leg-led release is lost. Wait for ball settlement and the coach's clear retrieval signal; no return catch. |
| 5 | **Staggered-Stance Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing — two hands, no flight** — proposed variant | **1 × 2 per lead leg = 4 swings**, each with its own hike, single swing and controlled park. **20 s after parking/reset between same-lead reps; 90 s between leads and before E6.** | Express a brief hip impulse from a biased but supported base with a fresh start each time. Use both hands, front foot flat and rear forefoot slightly behind, enough width for a clear bell path, and square hips. Hike a light bell, drive tall to float it no higher than chest height, then return/park before resetting. No twist, hop, arm lift or extra swing. This differs from the earlier continuous staggered swing and parallel dead-stop versions. Reduce load/stagger or use a parallel base if stance compromises hinge, grip or park control. |
| 6 | **Staggered-Stance Explosive Sit-to-Stand — no flight** — proposed variant | **1 × 2 per leg = 4 rises**: the named working foot is the foot nearer the seat; the assisting foot is slightly farther forward. About **1 s rise, 1 s tall hold, 3 s lowering, 2 s seated settle**. **20 s after seated reset between same-leg reps; 60 s between foot arrangements; 90 s before Section 3.** | Drive upward from a supported still start with greater demand intended through the nearer leg, while both feet assist. Use a fitted stable seat and a small fore/aft offset with both feet flat, arms crossed comfortably. Arrange torso over the nearer foot before the seated settle, stand briskly without rocking or pushing hands on the seat, then return under control. Keep pelvis level; no jump or claimed force-sharing percentage. Reduce offset/use a higher fitted seat or free arms for counterbalance if the rise grinds or shifts sideways. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light and controlled: leave **at least 5 clean repetitions in reserve**, or comparable ease on holds. Retain the small established dose so these exercises do not undermine the main strength work.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Step-Down — low lateral heel-tap variant** | **1 × 5 per leg = 10 reps**, bodyweight or light dumbbell. **3 s down, 1 s return. 30 s between legs; 45 s before S2.** | Lightly load the stance leg while holding knee, pelvis and foot alignment through flexion. This supports aligned knee/pelvis control through the standing jump dip and landing. Use a suitably low stable box; touch the free heel without pushing from it. Reduce range/load for hip drop, knee collapse or loss of stance-foot contact. |
| 2 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported single-leg hold** | **1 × 3 holds of 5 s per foot = 6 holds**. Bodyweight; **5 s relaxed between holds, 30 s between sides, 60 s before primary preparation.** | Strengthen active arch control while keeping heel and both forefoot bases in contact. Use hand support, a soft knee and long toes so foot control, rather than balance, is trained. Ease the contraction if toes claw or the ankle rolls; stop for cramping that does not resolve. |

If no suitably low stable step is available, **use a hand-supported single-leg shallow squat instead of S1**: 1 × 5 per leg with the same tempo/rest, free foot clear, light effort; this remains a proposed replacement without a claimed canonical ID. **Use bilateral standing Short-Foot Drill instead of S2** if single-leg arch control is inconsistent: 1 × 3 five-second holds with both feet together, 5 s relaxed between holds, then 60 s before primary preparation. These replace slots; they are not added exercises.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Double Front-Rack Squat — dumbbells** | **2 × 6 working reps**. About **3 s down**, stand decisively. **150 s between working sets and before P2 preparation.** | Primary strength investment: challenge bilateral knee/hip extension and trunk organization for the standing vertical takeoff. | 2 | **Split Squat — stationary, dumbbells** | **1 × 4 per leg = 8 working reps**: left then right. **3 s down, about 1 s up. 90 s between sides; 150 s after the final side before P3 preparation.** | Challenge each lead leg's force and alignment in a stable split base, supporting each leg's contribution to a balanced two-foot takeoff. | 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — dumbbells** | **2 × 6 working reps**. **3 s down, about 1 s up. 120 s between working sets and before P4 preparation.** | Challenge hip extension from flexion, supporting hip drive out of the dip and complementing the knee-dominant squat patterns. | 4 | **Hip Thrust — sandbag across pelvis, upper back on bench** | **1 × 8 working reps**. **1 s up, 1 s hold, 1 s down. 90 s before P5 preparation.** | Maintain a small terminal hip-extension contribution with supported trunk, distinct from RDL range. | 5 | **Standing Calf Raise — supported single-leg, dumbbell** | **1 × 8 per leg = 16 working reps**. **1 s up, 1 s hold, 1 s down. 60 s between legs; 75 s before P6 preparation.** | Challenge each ankle's nearly straight-knee force for late upward push-off. Use the floor range and secure hand support, keeping heel travel vertical and knee angle steady. Bodyweight is acceptable if it reaches the effort target; otherwise use a manageable load. Reduce load for shortening range or knee bending. |
| 6 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **2 × 10 working reps**, both legs together. **1 s up, 1 s hold, 1 s down**; quiet heel return. **90 s between working sets.** | Retain bent-knee ankle-force capacity for the dip/drive phase. Keep knees approximately over feet, loads secure and knee angle stable. Reduce load for bouncing, knee extension or loss of heel range; the preceding pogo dose does not prove readiness for heavier calf work. |
