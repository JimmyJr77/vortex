# Protracted Running — Class 10 of 12

**Primary effort:** Repeat accurate foot return and stance force with full resets, using a smaller primary-strength allocation for consolidation.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Previous classes are prescriptions; actual completion, loads, response and recovery remain unknown. This is a conditional next exposure, not earned overload.

**Scope and style:** Protracted running → Endurance; sustained pace; pacing control. Overtime Athletes-inspired elastic, unilateral and force training, adapted for gym support. No actual running, sprints, skipping, conditioning or tumbling. Gym work does not directly supply or test endurance, sustained running pace or pacing decisions. [Style/evidence and limits](../supporting/source_and_exercise_register.md).

**Daily stimulus brief:** The target is consistency of the initial push and receiving position, not fatigue tolerance. E1 provides repeated isolated ankle-hop observations on each leg. Small split-stance travel, shared diagonal receiving and a low raised same-leg finish vary placement/support. Dead-stop hip drive removes swing carryover; single-leg bridge drive supplies rapid terminal hip force without a landing. Familiar foot control and a small pelvic-control task precede six meaningful single-round strength roles.

**Quality marker inside E1:** For each leg, record clean centred landings in the two sets of two isolated ankle hops. Start each attempt tall and still, keep knee motion small and land over the foot for 2 s. Compare only matching same-leg full-reset attempts; do not compare them with continuous pogo contact rhythm. Use scheduled attempts only; record failures/stops, not make-up reps. Qualitative control is not a measurement of force, tendon stiffness, running economy or ground-contact time.

**Prior-work review:** Class 9 prescribed four loaded landings plus thirty nonflight force efforts and seven primary rounds. Actual response remains unknown. Class 10 uses unloaded isolated contacts, twelve single-leg events in total, and six primary rounds. The step-up returns at one set per leg rather than the earlier two; this is deliberate consolidation, not a response to assumed fatigue. The [preceding class](class_09.md), map, ledger, feedback and unresolved delivery issues were reviewed before this prescription. No actual athlete data was added.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is the main full-reset observation, first while fresh. E2/E3 keep travel small; E4 uses only two attempts per leg if a suitable raised surface exists. E5/E6 have no flight. Do not compress the rests to make this a repeatability-under-fatigue test. Bodyweight unless a light implement/band is specified. Ballistic loads must permit crisp acceleration without grinding.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Single-Leg Ankle Hop to Stick — full reset** | **2 × 2 hops per leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Main repeatability task: initiate a small ankle-led push from stillness and return over the same foot. Hands on hips, tall stance and soft knee. One low hop without a preparatory bounce, hold same-leg landing 2 s, fully reset. Keep knee/hip dip small; lower height for wobble rather than adding support complexity. |
| 2 | **Split-Stance Forward Pogo** | **1 × 4 hops per lead-leg stance**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Maintain split-base ankle support during a tiny forward displacement, without switching the lead leg. Both feet leave and return together in the same short split stance; four low hops travel no more than one own foot length total. Pelvis faces forward. Shorten travel/stance for uneven contact or drift; walk to reset. |
| 3 | **Two-Foot Diagonal Jump to Stick** | **1 × 2 jumps per diagonal direction**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Place the feet under the body after a small combined forward/frontal push while both legs share receiving. From parallel feet jump forward and slightly outward over a short declared distance, land parallel and hold 2 s. Toes/pelvis stay forward, no twisting turn. Reduce distance for lateral collapse or reaching. |
| 4 | **Single-Leg Vertical Jump to Low Box — same-leg landing** | **1 × 2 jumps per leg**; 120 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Use hip/knee extension from one leg and receive on that leg at a low raised surface, distinct from E1's tiny ankle-only push. Start near a very low stable box, small whole-leg dip, jump up onto the same foot, hold 2 s, then step down. No knee tuck or target-height challenge. This needs current competent single-leg receiving and a genuinely suitable low surface. |
| 5 | **Dead-Stop Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing** | **2 × 3 single swings**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 10 s between repetitions | Repeat a fast hip drive from a parked implement, removing the assistance of a preceding swing cycle. Set a light bell slightly in front, hinge and hike once, drive one swing below chest height, then park. Completely reset grip/stance before the next single. Feet stay planted. Reduce load for back extension, arm lifting or uncertain parking. |
| 6 | **Explosive Single-Leg Floor Bridge** | **1 × 4 hip drives per side**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Express fast stance-side hip extension near the finish without adding foot contacts. Working foot flat, other thigh held still; drive pelvis up quickly and lower over 2 s, keeping hips level and ribs down. No floor launch. Use a light kickstand foot instead if rotation prevents a clean single-leg drive; log changed support. |

**Quality and recovery:** Every attempted flight counts, even an invalid landing. Never add demonstration/test/make-up repetitions. A sequence contains only the stated linked contacts; walk between setups. Keep the same left/right order and surface within a comparison. Stop the set for visible speed/control loss; lower the stated amplitude/resistance or extend rest 30–60 s before any remaining scheduled attempt. Stop affected work for pain, limping, dizziness or persisting loss of control.

**Exercise-specific equipment/readiness route:** E4 requires a very low stable target appropriate to same-leg receiving; use a same-leg vertical jump to a floor stick instead when it is absent, 1 × 2 per leg and the same rest. That replacement still uses a modest hip/knee dip and natural arms, unlike E1's ankle-only still-start task. If single-leg receiving is not suitable, use two-foot receiving instead for those four attempts; add four individual foot contacts in the log. E5 without competent single-swing handling uses a familiar two-hand continuous swing instead at 2 × 3/rest, six swings total, recorded as a coupling change. All platforms/supports must fit the athlete without reaching or leaning to preserve balance. All alternatives are replacements, never additions. If no suitable version of a required task is available, record an incomplete/modified delivery; the written fourteen entries do not oblige an athlete to finish unsafe work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep at least five clean reps available, or equivalent ease for holds; no burning/shaking or pre-fatigue goal.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill — seated target-foot holds** | **1 × 3 five-second holds per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Retain low-fatigue active arch control before the foot handles the primary loaded work. Sit with both feet supported; gently shorten one target arch without toe curl or heel lift. Release fully between holds. Ease pressure for cramping. This is a familiar strength/control task, not a new drill family. |
| 2 | **Supported Hip Hike — low step** | **1 × 5 reps per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Lightly strengthen pelvic elevation/control over a supported stance hip. Hand support, stance foot on a low stable step, free foot clear. With knee softly straight, lower free-side pelvis slightly for 2 s and return to level for 2 s; do not shrug, bend sideways or push off the free foot. Reduce range for loss of control. If no suitable edge exists, use a supported shallow single-leg squat instead at the same count/tempo to retain stance-pelvis control. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging resistance/leverage with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; holds finish with about five clean seconds available. Coach-observed control supports the athlete’s effort report. No fixed weight or percentage increase, maximal test, forced rep or failure set. The table lists working sets.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Step-Up** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Keep a challenging stance-leg force role with one productive set per leg. Use a stable low box placing the working thigh below horizontal; whole foot on top. Rise 1 s using the upper leg, lower 3 s; no push/jump from the trailing foot. Reduce load/height for pelvic hike. If no suitable low box exists, use floor Split Squat instead at the same working/preparation counts and tempo; record the different task. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 reps**; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain a familiar loaded hip-hinge force role at one challenging set. Soft knees, hips back, DBs close; lower 3 s and rise in 1 s. Stop the descent before back rounding; reduce load/range if the hinge becomes spinal movement. |
| 3 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells** | **1 × 8 reps bilateral**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain knee-flexed ankle-force work without more calf-volume escalation. Both legs together, knees near 90°, heels start on floor, hands secure DBs on lower thighs above knees. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load for bounce/lost heel height. If bench fit is unsuitable, use supported standing bent-knee calf raises instead, same reps/rest, and log changed knee angle. |
| 4 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg** | **1 × 8 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Keep one straight-knee calf set per leg after the twelve unilateral receiving events. Work from floor, near-straight soft knee, one DB and stable hand support. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load or use bodyweight for lost range; use 1 × 8 bilateral instead of per-leg work if even unilateral bodyweight is too demanding, with 1 × 4 bilateral preparation. |
| 5 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 4 cycles**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain knee-flexor force through the existing small cycle dose. Keep a floor bridge while four small alternating heel steps go out and four return; eight steps at about 1 s each = one full cycle. Choose reach so four cycles are challenging with 2–3 whole cycles available. Shorten reach for pelvic sag or cramping; do not add steps or cycles. |
| 6 | **Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain resisted leg recovery while the pelvis is supported. Secure loop band around both forefeet; nonworking leg straight with heel grounded. Flex working hip/knee toward thigh-vertical in 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s without arching or moving the anchor heel. Reduce tension/range for pelvic tilt. If a secure loop is unavailable, use seated DB hip flexion instead with hands securing the DB above the knee: same dose/rest, small controlled thigh lift; preparation unloaded. |

**Separate load-preparation sets in these same exercises:** Dumbbell Step-Up: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 60 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells: 1 × 5 reps, then 60 s. Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells: 1 × 6 reps, then 45 s. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 45 s; 30 s to change sides on each preparation round. Glute Bridge Walkout: 1 × 1 cycles, then 45 s. Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 45 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Use the listed working tempo with lighter resistance/shorter lever; for time holds use the stated shorter duration. Log further preparation and add its time. These are not new exercise slots or challenging working sets.

**Prescribed workload:** 24 landing events (12 two-foot, 12 single-leg), 36 individual foot contacts; primary 6 rounds / 9 bouts, 54 reps + 4 cycles; preparation 6 rounds / 9 bouts, 33 reps + 1 cycles. Other explosive work: 6 single swings of Dead-Stop Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing; 8 hip drives of Explosive Single-Leg Floor Bridge. E1 eight + E2 eight + E3 four + E4 four = 24 landing events: twelve two-foot and twelve single-leg, 36 foot contacts. E4 adds four grounded box descents. E5 has six single swings with six setup hikes/parks that do not add swing repetitions; E6 has eight nonflight hip drives. Strength has six working rounds, with both sides still fully prescribed. Isolated-hop resets are recovery, not more hops. These totals exclude unknown preparation-sequence and outside workload. A round includes both sides; separately performed sides are separate bouts. Unlike landings/throws/lifts are not equivalent units of stress. [Full per-exercise ledger](../supporting/class_10_record.json).

Use separate cleared bays within the three separate 10 m lanes; check width, queues and equipment quantities onsite. Rest after the last set may include the next setup once, not twice. **Scheduling:** Review actual recent/upcoming running, races, sport, gym and tumbling outside this class. Roughly 48–72 h from another comparably demanding lower-body exposure is an initial planning reference, not automatic clearance or a weekly calendar. These short sets can still load calf/Achilles, knee extensors and hamstrings substantially. Move or reduce a session when actual recovery requires it.
