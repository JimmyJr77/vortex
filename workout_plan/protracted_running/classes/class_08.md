# Protracted Running — Class 8 of 12

**Primary effort:** Produce a short forward push and receive it under a controlled stance position without an external drop or run-up.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Previous classes are prescriptions; actual completion, loads, response and recovery remain unknown. This is a conditional next exposure, not earned overload.

**Scope and style:** Protracted running → Endurance; sustained pace; pacing control. Overtime Athletes-inspired elastic, unilateral and force training, adapted for gym support. No actual running, sprints, skipping, conditioning or tumbling. Gym work does not directly supply or test endurance, sustained running pace or pacing decisions. [Style/evidence and limits](../supporting/source_and_exercise_register.md).

**Daily stimulus brief:** After the prescribed linked-contact emphasis, this class restores isolated receiving. E1 anchors the coordinated countermovement forward push. Raised receiving, nominated single-leg receiving, low obstacle clearance, a stationary split-stance exchange and a grounded preload-to-rebound task expose different foot-placement demands. Primary step-up strength supports raising body mass through the stance leg; the remainder retains ankle, hinge and leg-recovery roles.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Count controlled finishes within each two-attempt set: one dip and coordinated arms, two-foot landing beneath the body held for 2 s without an extra step. Keep the modest chosen distance repeatable; farther is not automatically better for this support task. Use scheduled attempts only; record failures/stops, not make-up reps. Qualitative control is not a measurement of force, tendon stiffness, running economy or ground-contact time.

**Prior-work review:** Class 7 prescribed 29 landings, twelve single-leg receiving events and linked alternatives, but no actual completion is known. Class 8 restores full resets and uses only four single-leg receiving events. The new main step-up uses the same 2 × 6 per-leg working count as the earlier split-squat anchor; the task change is conditional, not an automatic load progression. The [preceding class](class_07.md), map, ledger, feedback and unresolved delivery issues were reviewed before this prescription. No actual athlete data was added.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 is first with two sets; the more demanding nominated-leg receiving follows a shared raised landing task while fresh. The final three tasks have small doses and full resets; no long horizontal or switching-lunge circuit. Bodyweight unless a light implement/band is specified. Ballistic loads must permit crisp acceleration without grinding.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Broad Jump to Stick — countermovement with arms** | **2 × 2 jumps**; 120 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Main integrated forward push with coordinated hip/knee extension and arm action, followed by owned receiving. One smooth dip, swing arms forward and jump a modest distance; bring both feet under the body and hold 2 s. No reaching heels far ahead. Shorten distance for a backward fall or extra step; no maximum-distance test. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Forward Jump to Low Box — two-foot landing** | **1 × 2 jumps per takeoff leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Express each leg's forward/upward push while using a low raised two-foot finish to reduce fall distance. Start close to a very low stable box, one-foot push, land with both feet flat on top without tucking and hold 2 s. Step down. Lower target/shorten gap for knee tuck or a cramped landing; do not jump off the box. |
| 3 | **Two-Foot Forward Jump to Single-Leg Stick** | **1 × 2 jumps per receiving leg**; 120 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Concentrate a small forward receiving demand on one stance leg while sharing the takeoff effort. Very low two-foot forward push, no more than one own foot length; land on the nominated foot underneath the pelvis and hold 2 s. Reduce travel/flight for collapse or extra steps. This needs demonstrated current single-leg receiving control. |
| 4 | **Single Low Hurdle Jump to Stick** | **1 × 2 jumps**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Clear a small obstacle through a purposeful push and place both feet accurately without a rebound demand. One suitably low hurdle, stationary two-foot takeoff, land just beyond and hold 2 s. No preceding bounce or linked second jump. Lower/remove the hurdle rather than tucking knees or stretching the landing. |
| 5 | **Split Jump Switch to Stick** | **1 × 2 jumps per starting lead leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Change which leg receives the lead support role during a short, controlled explosive stance exchange. Small split stance, shallow dip, low jump and exchange lead legs once; land in a short split stance and hold 2 s. Reset before another jump. No traveling, repeated jumping-lunge rhythm or maximal depth; reduce stance length for twisting. |
| 6 | **Grounded Snap-Down to Low Rebound Jump** | **1 × 3 rebound jumps**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Organize a rapid lowering into a responsive push without adding the energy of a drop from a box. Start tall with heels lightly raised but forefeet grounded; quickly settle heels and bend hips/knees slightly, then make one small vertical rebound jump and stick 2 s. The initial snap-down has no flight. Reduce speed/depth if alignment is lost; no extra pogo afterward. |

**Quality and recovery:** Every attempted flight counts, even an invalid landing. Never add demonstration/test/make-up repetitions. A sequence contains only the stated linked contacts; walk between setups. Keep the same left/right order and surface within a comparison. Stop the set for visible speed/control loss; lower the stated amplitude/resistance or extend rest 30–60 s before any remaining scheduled attempt. Stop affected work for pain, limping, dizziness or persisting loss of control.

**Exercise-specific equipment/readiness route:** All boxes/hurdles must fit the athlete and be stable; no minimum jump height is required. E2 without a suitable low box uses same-leg forward push to a two-foot floor landing instead, same 1 × 2 per takeoff leg/rest, four two-foot events. E3 uses a two-foot receiving landing instead when one-leg control is insufficient, 1 × 4 total with 20 s resets and 120 s afterward; four events remain but add four individual foot contacts. Remove E4's hurdle instead of adding height. Use a no-switch Split-Squat Jump to Stick instead of E5 if stance exchange is not controlled, same counts/rest. E6 uses a slower grounded lowering before the single jump instead if rapid preload fails; no extra flight. All alternatives are replacements, never additions. If no suitable version of a required task is available, record an incomplete/modified delivery; the written fourteen entries do not oblige an athlete to finish unsafe work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep at least five clean reps available, or equivalent ease for holds; no burning/shaking or pre-fatigue goal.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lateral Step-Down — low heel tap** | **1 × 4 reps per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Lightly strengthen knee/pelvis alignment during lowering into the stance leg. Use a low stable step, free heel reaches beside the box; lower 3 s and rise 1 s without pushing from the free heel. Reduce range for hip drop. Use supported shallow single-leg squat instead if no suitable low step exists, same dose and tempo. |
| 2 | **Short-Lever Hamstring Bridge Hold** | **1 × 10 seconds, both heels**; 45 s after each set/side | Light hamstring/pelvic support before the stronger hinge and walkout tasks. Supine with heels close and knees well bent; gently lift hips to a low comfortable bridge while pressing heels down. Hold level with easy breathing, not maximal effort. Lower height/bring heels closer for cramp or back arch; ten seconds is a hold, not ten reps. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging resistance/leverage with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; holds finish with about five clean seconds available. Coach-observed control supports the athlete’s effort report. No fixed weight or percentage increase, maximal test, forced rep or failure set. The table lists working sets.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dumbbell Step-Up** | **2 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Primary stance-leg force: raise body mass through a supported forward foot placement. Use a stable low box placing the working thigh below horizontal; whole foot on top. Rise 1 s using the upper leg, lower 3 s; no push/jump from the trailing foot. Reduce load/height for pelvic hike. If no suitable low box exists, use floor Split Squat instead at the same working/preparation counts and tempo; record the different task. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift — hand supported, dumbbell** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain a supported one-leg hip hinge so receiving stance is backed by loadable hip force. One DB opposite working leg, free hand on stable support. Soft stance knee, free leg travels back, pelvis faces floor; lower 3 s and rise 1 s. Support removes balance as the limiter. Reduce load/range for hip opening or trunk rotation. |
| 3 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells** | **1 × 8 reps bilateral**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Keep controlled knee-flexed ankle force after the isolated receiving work. Both legs together, knees near 90°, heels start on floor, hands secure DBs on lower thighs above knees. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load for bounce/lost heel height. If bench fit is unsuitable, use supported standing bent-knee calf raises instead, same reps/rest, and log changed knee angle. |
| 4 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg** | **1 × 8 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain a limited straight-knee calf force role without additional impacts. Work from floor, near-straight soft knee, one DB and stable hand support. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load or use bodyweight for lost range; use 1 × 8 bilateral instead of per-leg work if even unilateral bodyweight is too demanding, with 1 × 4 bilateral preparation. |
| 5 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 4 cycles**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Challenge knee-flexor control through changing lever length after the brief easy hold. Keep a floor bridge while four small alternating heel steps go out and four return; eight steps at about 1 s each = one full cycle. Choose reach so four cycles are challenging with 2–3 whole cycles available. Shorten reach for pelvic sag or cramping; do not add steps or cycles. |
| 6 | **Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain resisted leg recovery with the pelvis stabilized. Secure loop band around both forefeet; nonworking leg straight with heel grounded. Flex working hip/knee toward thigh-vertical in 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s without arching or moving the anchor heel. Reduce tension/range for pelvic tilt. If a secure loop is unavailable, use seated DB hip flexion instead with hands securing the DB above the knee: same dose/rest, small controlled thigh lift; preparation unloaded. |

**Separate load-preparation sets in these same exercises:** Dumbbell Step-Up: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 60 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift — hand supported, dumbbell: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 60 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells: 1 × 6 reps, then 45 s. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 45 s; 30 s to change sides on each preparation round. Glute Bridge Walkout: 1 × 1 cycles, then 45 s. Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 45 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Use the listed working tempo with lighter resistance/shorter lever; for time holds use the stated shorter duration. Log further preparation and add its time. These are not new exercise slots or challenging working sets.

**Prescribed workload:** 21 landing events (17 two-foot, 4 single-leg), 38 individual foot contacts; primary 7 rounds / 12 bouts, 72 reps + 4 cycles; preparation 6 rounds / 10 bouts, 34 reps + 1 cycles. Other explosive work: none. Default landings: E1 four + E2 four + E3 four + E4 two + E5 four + E6 three = 21 events: 17 two-foot, four single-leg, 38 individual foot contacts. E2 adds four grounded box descents. E6 has three nonflight snap-downs plus three airborne-jump landings; heel settling is not an extra jump. E5 always lands on two feet despite switching lead leg. These receiving types are not equal mechanical loads. These totals exclude unknown preparation-sequence and outside workload. A round includes both sides; separately performed sides are separate bouts. Unlike landings/throws/lifts are not equivalent units of stress.
