# Protracted Running — Class 6 of 12

**Primary effort:** Keep the pelvis supported over the foot during small frontal and diagonal force demands.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Previous classes are prescriptions; actual completion, loads, response and recovery remain unknown. This is a conditional next exposure, not earned overload.

**Scope and style:** Protracted running → Endurance; sustained pace; pacing control. Overtime Athletes-inspired elastic, unilateral and force training, adapted for gym support. No actual running, sprints, skipping, conditioning or tumbling. Gym work does not directly supply or test endurance, sustained running pace or pacing decisions. [Style/evidence and limits](../supporting/source_and_exercise_register.md).

**Daily stimulus brief:** This consolidation exposure addresses unwanted side-to-side pelvic motion in stance, not sport cutting. E1 supplies a small lateral rhythm marker; E2/E3 use limited single-leg acceptance in diagonal and lateral vectors. Raised two-foot receiving, split-stance forward release and a grounded lateral step-up express force with different support conditions. Light arch and trunk work set up targeted hip/adductor strength.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Count centred contacts in each six-hop lateral set while pelvis and feet face forward, without crossing feet or increasing travel. Keep the same small gap. Lateral-hop rhythm cannot be compared numerically with fore-aft or stationary pogo performance. Use scheduled attempts only; record failures/stops, not make-up reps. Qualitative control is not a measurement of force, tendon stiffness, running economy or ground-contact time.

**Prior-work review:** Class 5 prescribed twelve bilateral landings and eight primary rounds emphasizing hips/hamstrings. No actual response is known. Class 6 reintroduces ten single-leg receiving events, matching the written Class 3 count rather than increasing that exposure; primary volume falls to seven rounds while frontal strength roles replace some posterior-chain work. The [preceding class](class_05.md), map, ledger, feedback and unresolved delivery issues were reviewed before this prescription. No actual athlete data was added.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The bilateral lateral ankle task comes first, then the short single-leg tasks before fatigue. Raised bilateral receiving and two planted/supporting-force tasks complete the explosive block without a long bound circuit. Bodyweight unless a light implement/band is specified. Ballistic loads must permit crisp acceleration without grinding.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Line Pogo Side-to-Side** | **2 × 6 hops**; 60 s after each set/side, including the last | Main frontal foot-return task with a small repeatable displacement. Both feet together, hop sideways across a seam or imaginary line marked by cones outside the area; less than one foot length of travel. Keep toes/pelvis forward and knees soft. Shorten travel for rolling ankles or hip sway. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Diagonal Hop to Stick** | **1 × 2 hops per receiving leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Accept a small combined forward/lateral push over one leg, controlling the pelvis across two directions. Hop forward and slightly outward on the same leg over no more than one own foot length; hold 2 s. Walk back. Keep the diagonal consistent and modest; reduce distance for knee drift or hip rotation. |
| 3 | **Skater Bound to Stick** | **1 × 3 bounds per receiving leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Transfer body mass from one leg to the other through a short lateral push and stable receiving stance. Start on the opposite foot, push a short distance sideways, land on the named leg and hold 2 s. Reset by walking before another attempt to that side. No long-distance reach, crossover sweep or repeated alternating sequence; shorten distance for extra steps. |
| 4 | **Lateral Box Jump — two-foot landing** | **1 × 2 jumps per direction**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Express a frontal push with shared receiving at a low raised surface, reducing dependence on single-leg landing skill. Stand beside a suitably low stable box; small two-foot jump sideways onto it, face forward, hold 2 s and step down. No turn or knee tuck. Reduce height/sideways gap for twisting or cramped landing space. |
| 5 | **Split-Stance Medicine Ball Forward Scoop Toss** | **1 × 3 throws per lead-leg stance**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Direct hip/leg force forward while the split base resists unwanted pelvic rotation. Small stationary split stance, light ball low, shallow dip and fast forward scoop. Both feet remain planted, pelvis faces forward, no twist or catch. Reduce ball mass/stance offset if the rear foot pivots or the back arches. |
| 6 | **Explosive Lateral Step-Up — no flight** | **1 × 4 step-ups per side**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 5 s between repetitions | Accelerate the body over a laterally positioned stance foot while keeping the pelvis level, without another jump landing. Working foot flat on a low box beside the body. Drive up quickly using that leg; foot remains on the box and free knee lifts slightly, then lower over 2 s. No push or jump from the floor foot; lower box height for hip hike. |

**Quality and recovery:** Every attempted flight counts, even an invalid landing. Never add demonstration/test/make-up repetitions. A sequence contains only the stated linked contacts; walk between setups. Keep the same left/right order and surface within a comparison. Stop the set for visible speed/control loss; lower the stated amplitude/resistance or extend rest 30–60 s before any remaining scheduled attempt. Stop affected work for pain, limping, dizziness or persisting loss of control.

**Exercise-specific equipment/readiness route:** Boxes for E4/E6 must be low, stable and wide enough; if E4 is unsuitable, use a two-foot lateral jump to floor stick instead, 1 × 2 per direction, same rest, four two-foot landings. For E6 without a suitable low box, use a fast shallow lateral-lunge return to a stable two-foot stand instead, 1 × 4 per direction at the same rest, no flight. E5 needs contained throwing clearance; use a split-stance held-ball hip drive without release instead, same 1 × 3 per lead side and resets/rest, replacing six throws with six held-ball accelerations. P6 requires an appropriate stable knee support or its side-lying adduction replacement. All alternatives are replacements, never additions. If no suitable version of a required task is available, record an incomplete/modified delivery; the written fourteen entries do not oblige an athlete to finish unsafe work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep at least five clean reps available, or equivalent ease for holds; no burning/shaking or pre-fatigue goal.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short-Foot Drill — supported single-leg stance** | **1 × 3 five-second holds per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Light arch strengthening under one-leg support to maintain a stable contact platform during frontal drift. Use hand support, soft knee and long toes; hold the target arch gently without clawing. Release fully between holds. Use two feet supported instead at the same target-foot holds if single-leg loading causes gripping or cramping. |
| 2 | **Side Plank — knees bent** | **1 × 12 seconds per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Light lateral-trunk strengthening to resist pelvic drop while the lower limb changes support. Forearm under shoulder, knees bent, lift into a shoulder–hip–knee line and breathe easily. Keep the upper hip stacked; shorten hold or lower hips slightly for shaking. This remains easy before the challenging adductor task. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging resistance/leverage with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; holds finish with about five clean seconds available. Coach-observed control supports the athlete’s effort report. No fixed weight or percentage increase, maximal test, forced rep or failure set. The table lists working sets.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain the main stance-leg knee/hip force anchor with a stable base. Floor split stance, front foot planted, pelvis square; lower 2 s and rise firmly for 1 s. Reduce load/range for collapse or balance loss. Use one DB with stable hand support instead of two unsupported DBs if needed; counts stay the same. |
| 2 | **Goblet Lateral Lunge** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Add direct frontal hip/knee force for accepting small lateral displacement. Hold one DB securely at chest. Step sideways, sit into the stepping hip with full foot planted, return to centre without jumping; 3 s out/down, 1 s return. Keep knee with toes and pelvis square. Shorten step/range or reduce DB for inward knee collapse. |
| 3 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift — hand supported, dumbbell** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain supported single-leg hip force while keeping the pelvis square. One DB opposite working leg, free hand on stable support. Soft stance knee, free leg travels back, pelvis faces floor; lower 3 s and rise 1 s. Support removes balance as the limiter. Reduce load/range for hip opening or trunk rotation. |
| 4 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells** | **1 × 8 reps bilateral**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain a small knee-flexed ankle-force set while frontal control tasks change. Both legs together, knees near 90°, heels start on floor, hands secure DBs on lower thighs above knees. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load for bounce/lost heel height. If bench fit is unsuitable, use supported standing bent-knee calf raises instead, same reps/rest, and log changed knee angle. |
| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg** | **1 × 8 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Return to the established single straight-knee calf set per leg for ankle-force coverage. Work from floor, near-straight soft knee, one DB and stable hand support. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load or use bodyweight for lost range; use 1 × 8 bilateral instead of per-leg work if even unilateral bodyweight is too demanding, with 1 × 4 bilateral preparation. |
| 6 | **Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever** | **1 × 15 seconds per side**; 45 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Challenge adductor force for pelvic support over the stance leg. Top knee on a suitable stable bench, forearm under shoulder, bottom leg clear; hold a straight shoulder–pelvis–knee line while breathing. Select support so 15 s is challenging with about 5 s clean hold available. If support/lever is unsuitable, use side-lying hip adduction instead: 1 × 8 per side, 2 s up/1 s hold/2 s down, light 1 × 3 preparation, same side/round rests; add a hand-secured DB above the lower knee only if needed for the strength effort. |

**Separate load-preparation sets in these same exercises:** Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides: 1 × 6 reps per side, then 30 s; 1 × 3 reps per side, then 90 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Goblet Lateral Lunge: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 60 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift — hand supported, dumbbell: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 60 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells: 1 × 6 reps, then 45 s. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 45 s; 30 s to change sides on each preparation round. Copenhagen Plank — Short Lever: 1 × 5 seconds per side, then 45 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Use the listed working tempo with lighter resistance/shorter lever; for time holds use the stated shorter duration. Log further preparation and add its time. These are not new exercise slots or challenging working sets.

**Prescribed workload:** 26 landing events (16 two-foot, 10 single-leg), 42 individual foot contacts; primary 7 rounds / 13 bouts, 72 reps + 30 seconds; preparation 7 rounds / 13 bouts, 44 reps + 10 seconds. Other explosive work: 6 throws of Split-Stance Medicine Ball Forward Scoop Toss; 8 step-ups of Explosive Lateral Step-Up — no flight. Twelve lateral pogo contacts + four diagonal single-leg hops + six opposite-leg bounds + four lateral box landings = 26 events: 16 two-foot and ten single-leg, 42 foot contacts. E4 includes four grounded step-down descents. E5 adds six throws; E6 eight grounded drives. Primary holds total 30 s across both sides, not 30 reps. P6 dynamic replacement changes that 30 s hold exposure to 16 working adduction reps plus six light preparation reps; record the route instead of combining both. These totals exclude unknown preparation-sequence and outside workload. A round includes both sides; separately performed sides are separate bouts. Unlike landings/throws/lifts are not equivalent units of stress. [Full per-exercise ledger](../supporting/class_06_record.json).
