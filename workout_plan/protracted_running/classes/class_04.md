# Protracted Running — Class 4 of 12

**Primary effort:** Express ankle force with a flexed knee while retaining a stable trunk and controlled foot platform.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Previous classes are prescriptions; actual completion, loads, response and recovery remain unknown. This is a conditional next exposure, not earned overload.

**Scope and style:** Protracted running → Endurance; sustained pace; pacing control. Overtime Athletes-inspired elastic, unilateral and force training, adapted for gym support. No actual running, sprints, skipping, conditioning or tumbling. Gym work does not directly supply or test endurance, sustained running pace or pacing decisions. [Style/evidence and limits](../supporting/source_and_exercise_register.md).

**Daily stimulus brief:** The day's demand is rapid plantarflexor action when the knee is already flexed, a stance-support condition. E1 is the main shallow-knee spring task. Small forward travel and a short obstacle pair add contact placement; seated takeoff supplies knee/hip force from a supported start; two no-flight tasks supply rapid bent-knee ankle and hip extension. Light lateral-ankle control and pelvis control avoid a calf pre-fatigue block.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Count five low bounces per set that preserve the declared shallow knee bend without progressively sinking or straightening. Keep height small and repeatable. This is a joint-position/control marker, not proof of soleus isolation or a contact-time target. Use scheduled attempts only; record failures/stops, not make-up reps. Qualitative control is not a measurement of force, tendon stiffness, running economy or ground-contact time.

**Prior-work review:** Class 3 prescribed 16 landings including 10 single-leg events, eight primary rounds and a supported single-leg hinge. No response is known. Class 4 removes single-leg jump receiving and restores the familiar bilateral RDL with the same twelve working hinge reps; the bent-knee calf lift moves first in primary strength without a preset load increase. The [preceding class](class_03.md), map, ledger, feedback and unresolved delivery issues were reviewed before this prescription. No actual athlete data was added.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

E1 receives two short sets while fresh; E2/E3 are very small complementary ankle exposures. E4 has full resets; E5/E6 are fast concentric actions with feet supported, not fatigue circuits. Bodyweight unless a light implement/band is specified. Ballistic loads must permit crisp acceleration without grinding.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Low Squat Pogo — shallow knee bend** | **2 × 5 hops**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Main task: express ankle spring while preserving a modest knee-flexed support position. Set a small athletic knee bend, hands on hips, bounce only a little off the floor with both feet. Keep the knee bend approximately steady, not rigidly locked; reduce height or stop if each contact becomes a deeper squat. |
| 2 | **Forward-Traveling Low Pogo** | **1 × 6 hops**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Keep ankle-driven support underneath the body during small forward displacement. Both feet together, low contacts, at most 1 m total travel. Use tiny increments and finish still after hop six. Shorten travel for reaching feet ahead or excessive knee dip; walk back after the set. |
| 3 | **Low Hurdle Hop Pair — two linked jumps** | **1 × 2 two-jump sequences**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 25 s between repetitions | Organize two quick support contacts around a low clearance demand without building a long jumping circuit. Two very low forgiving hurdles, spacing close enough for controlled upright hops within 2 m total. Jump over each once, hold the second landing 2 s. No preparatory bounce, no third jump; reduce height/spacing for tucking or reaching. |
| 4 | **Seated Squat Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 jumps**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Produce knee/hip extension quickly from a supported start while the ankle transfers that force into takeoff. Sit upright on a stable box/bench with feet flat and thighs around or above parallel; pause 2 s without rocking, hands at hips. Jump modestly up from the seat, land on both feet and hold 2 s. Lower takeoff intent if feet slide, rocking appears or the landing is uncontrolled. |
| 5 | **Seated Bent-Knee Calf Raise — fast concentric** | **1 × 6 bilateral heel rises**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Accelerate plantarflexion with the knees flexed under light external resistance, complementing slower challenging P1 strength. Both feet supported on floor, knees near 90°, light DBs held securely on thighs. Lift heels quickly without the forefeet leaving, lower over 2 s. No bounce or grinding; use less load if heel height/speed declines. |
| 6 | **Explosive Floor Glute Bridge** | **1 × 5 hip drives**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Supply fast hip-extension support so the day does not depend entirely on repeated ankle work. Supine, knees bent, feet planted. Drive the pelvis up quickly, stop at ribs/pelvis alignment and lower over 2 s. Bodyweight only, no launching shoulders or feet off the floor. Reduce range for back arching. |

**Quality and recovery:** Every attempted flight counts, even an invalid landing. Never add demonstration/test/make-up repetitions. A sequence contains only the stated linked contacts; walk between setups. Keep the same left/right order and surface within a comparison. Stop the set for visible speed/control loss; lower the stated amplitude/resistance or extend rest 30–60 s before any remaining scheduled attempt. Stop affected work for pain, limping, dizziness or persisting loss of control.

**Exercise-specific equipment/readiness route:** The hurdle pair requires suitably low obstacles and room for two controlled landings. If obstacle height/spacing is unsuitable, use two marked floor positions instead of hurdles with the same two-jump sequences, removing clearance demand and preserving four two-foot landings. E4 requires a stable seat at a suitable height; use a paused floor Squat Jump instead if seating fails, same 1 × 3 and rest. E5 uses only a light securely held DB; use bodyweight instead if no suitable load fits. Bench/box quantities and dimensions remain unconfirmed. All alternatives are replacements, never additions. If no suitable version of a required task is available, record an incomplete/modified delivery; the written fourteen entries do not oblige an athlete to finish unsafe work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep at least five clean reps available, or equivalent ease for holds; no burning/shaking or pre-fatigue goal.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Lightly strengthen lateral ankle control to keep the forefoot platform organized during spring contacts. Seated, loop light band around both forefeet with heels grounded; hold the nonworking foot straight while the target forefoot turns gently outward without rotating the knee. 2 s out, 2 s return. Use less band tension for whole-leg movement; without a suitable loop, use gentle hand-resisted eversion instead at the same dose. |
| 2 | **Dead Bug Heel Tap** | **1 × 4 reps per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Light trunk strengthening to keep ribs/pelvis quiet while a thigh changes position, supporting controlled stance-to-swing organization. Supine, hips/knees bent, arms pointing up. Lower one heel to a light floor tap over 2 s and return 2 s while the other leg stays still. Breathe, no back arching. Shorten the heel reach instead if pelvis moves; this must remain easy. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging resistance/leverage with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; holds finish with about five clean seconds available. Coach-observed control supports the athlete’s effort report. No fixed weight or percentage increase, maximal test, forced rep or failure set. The table lists working sets.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells** | **2 × 8 reps bilateral**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Primary force anchor today: challenge knee-flexed plantarflexion after the low-dose rapid work. Both legs together, knees near 90°, heels start on floor, hands secure DBs on lower thighs above knees. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load for bounce/lost heel height. If bench fit is unsuitable, use supported standing bent-knee calf raises instead, same reps/rest, and log changed knee angle. |
| 2 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain stance-leg knee/hip force while keeping the foot platform planted. Floor split stance, front foot planted, pelvis square; lower 2 s and rise firmly for 1 s. Reduce load/range for collapse or balance loss. Use one DB with stable hand support instead of two unsupported DBs if needed; counts stay the same. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 reps**; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Return to the familiar bilateral hinge to preserve hip force without another unilateral balance demand. Soft knees, hips back, DBs close; lower 3 s and rise in 1 s. Stop the descent before back rounding; reduce load/range if the hinge becomes spinal movement. |
| 4 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg** | **1 × 8 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Keep one straight-knee calf set per leg as a limited complement to the day's knee-flexed focus. Work from floor, near-straight soft knee, one DB and stable hand support. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load or use bodyweight for lost range; use 1 × 8 bilateral instead of per-leg work if even unilateral bodyweight is too demanding, with 1 × 4 bilateral preparation. |
| 5 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 4 cycles**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Preserve knee-flexor loading while the hip is supported, avoiding further standing ankle demand. Keep a floor bridge while four small alternating heel steps go out and four return; eight steps at about 1 s each = one full cycle. Choose reach so four cycles are challenging with 2–3 whole cycles available. Shorten reach for pelvic sag or cramping; do not add steps or cycles. |
| 6 | **Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain resisted thigh recovery under a quiet pelvis with no added contacts. Secure loop band around both forefeet; nonworking leg straight with heel grounded. Flex working hip/knee toward thigh-vertical in 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s without arching or moving the anchor heel. Reduce tension/range for pelvic tilt. If a secure loop is unavailable, use seated DB hip flexion instead with hands securing the DB above the knee: same dose/rest, small controlled thigh lift; preparation unloaded. |

**Separate load-preparation sets in these same exercises:** Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells: 1 × 6 reps, then 45 s. Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides: 1 × 6 reps per side, then 30 s; 1 × 3 reps per side, then 90 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells: 1 × 5 reps, then 60 s. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 45 s; 30 s to change sides on each preparation round. Glute Bridge Walkout: 1 × 1 cycles, then 45 s. Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 45 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Use the listed working tempo with lighter resistance/shorter lever; for time holds use the stated shorter duration. Log further preparation and add its time. These are not new exercise slots or challenging working sets.

**Prescribed workload:** 23 landing events (23 two-foot, 0 single-leg), 46 individual foot contacts; primary 9 rounds / 13 bouts, 80 reps + 4 cycles; preparation 7 rounds / 11 bouts, 43 reps + 1 cycles. Other explosive work: 6 bilateral heel rises of Seated Bent-Knee Calf Raise — fast concentric; 5 hip drives of Explosive Floor Glute Bridge. E1 10 + E2 6 + E3 four linked-jump landings + E4 3 = 23 two-foot landing events, 46 foot contacts. E3 has two sequences of exactly two jumps, not two reps of an open-ended series. E5 contributes six nonflight heel rises and E6 five hip drives. No single-leg landing or drop from a height is prescribed. Light eversion has twelve reps; dead-bug work has eight heel taps. E5 fast light work and P1 challenging controlled work are separate performances and separate loads. These totals exclude unknown preparation-sequence and outside workload. A round includes both sides; separately performed sides are separate bouts. Unlike landings/throws/lifts are not equivalent units of stress.
