# Protracted Running — Class 9 of 12

**Primary effort:** Express leg force rapidly against manageable external resistance while keeping the foot and pelvis organized.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Previous classes are prescriptions; actual completion, loads, response and recovery remain unknown. This is a conditional next exposure, not earned overload.

**Scope and style:** Protracted running → Endurance; sustained pace; pacing control. Overtime Athletes-inspired elastic, unilateral and force training, adapted for gym support. No actual running, sprints, skipping, conditioning or tumbling. Gym work does not directly supply or test endurance, sustained running pace or pacing decisions. [Style/evidence and limits](../supporting/source_and_exercise_register.md).

**Daily stimulus brief:** The limiting demand is maintaining fast force expression when an implement adds resistance. E1 gives a very small externally loaded takeoff exposure; landmine squat and split-hinge drives offer guided planted-foot force, supported hip thrust addresses hip finish, seated thigh lift addresses leg recovery, and straight-knee heel rise addresses ankle force. The latter five have no flight. Light ankle and offset-trunk work precede familiar challenging strength.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Across two sets of two jumps, record secure DB handling, a crisp takeoff and a controlled two-foot landing under the body. Choose the smallest useful load that does not visibly disrupt speed or position; no universal kilograms, velocity target or maximum-load test. If loaded execution is unsuitable, the unloaded route is the correct recorded result. Use scheduled attempts only; record failures/stops, not make-up reps. Qualitative control is not a measurement of force, tendon stiffness, running economy or ground-contact time.

**Prior-work review:** Class 8 prescribed 21 landings and seven primary rounds with a new step-up. No actual response is known. Class 9 reduces jump landings to four, replaces most impact with short resisted rapid-force tasks and retains seven primary rounds. External resistance is a conditional task constraint, not proof of strength gained over eight written classes. The [preceding class](class_08.md), map, ledger, feedback and unresolved delivery issues were reviewed before this prescription. No actual athlete data was added.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Only E1 includes flight and it receives two tiny sets. Every subsequent row uses a light implement for fast concentric force with controlled return; stop well before fatigue reduces speed. Bodyweight unless a light implement/band is specified. Ballistic loads must permit crisp acceleration without grinding.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Loaded Squat Jump — light dumbbells** | **2 × 2 jumps**; 120 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Main task: retain coordinated takeoff and receiving while a small external mass adds force demand. Very light DBs at sides, one shallow dip, modest vertical jump, land both feet under the hips and hold 2 s with arms quiet. Never throw or drop the DBs. Use unloaded jumps instead if grip, landing or speed is compromised. |
| 2 | **Landmine Squat-to-Forefoot Drive — no flight** | **1 × 4 drives**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 5 s between repetitions | Express coordinated hip/knee/ankle extension against a guided forward-up load without another landing. Secure landmine and suitable light bar/load; both hands hold the end at chest. From a shallow squat drive tall onto forefeet in one continuous rise, keep forefeet grounded, then lower over 2 s. No overhead press or arm push; reduce load if the bar controls posture. |
| 3 | **Split-Stance Landmine Hinge Drive** | **1 × 3 drives per lead-leg stance**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 5 s between repetitions | Direct fast hip-extension force through an asymmetric base while the guided implement limits unnecessary swing travel. Light landmine end held in both hands low in front, short split stance. Hinge with softly bent knees, drive hips tall quickly and return over 2 s. Both feet stay planted, no arm curl or pelvis twist; shorten hinge/rack distance for trunk loss. |
| 4 | **Explosive Hip Thrust — light dumbbell, bench supported** | **1 × 4 hip drives**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Accelerate terminal hip extension through a supported range against a small external load. Upper back on a stable suitable bench, feet grounded, hands secure a light pelvic DB. Drive up quickly to ribs/pelvis alignment, lower over 2 s; do not launch the implement or arch the back. Use less load/range if speed or control fades. |
| 5 | **Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion — fast thigh lift** | **1 × 4 thigh lifts per side**; 60 s after each set/side, including the last | Accelerate the thigh against light resistance while the supported pelvis removes a balance limit. Sit tall with foot grounded, hands securing a light DB above the knee. Lift the thigh a small amount quickly, lower over 2 s without leaning back. No loose weight on a foot; reduce load/range for pelvic movement. |
| 6 | **Standing Calf Raise — fast concentric, supported single leg** | **1 × 4 heel rises per side**; 60 s after each set/side, including the last | Express fast plantarflexion through the straight-knee stance configuration without leaving the floor. One light DB, stable hand support, near-straight soft knee. Rise quickly through the forefoot, return over 2 s; forefoot never leaves. Use bodyweight instead if external load changes speed or ankle alignment. |

**Quality and recovery:** Every attempted flight counts, even an invalid landing. Never add demonstration/test/make-up repetitions. A sequence contains only the stated linked contacts; walk between setups. Keep the same left/right order and surface within a comparison. Stop the set for visible speed/control loss; lower the stated amplitude/resistance or extend rest 30–60 s before any remaining scheduled attempt. Stop affected work for pain, limping, dizziness or persisting loss of control.

**Exercise-specific equipment/readiness route:** E1 requires secure light DB handling and prior current unloaded jump control; use unloaded countermovement jumps instead at the same 2 × 2/rest if needed. E2/E3 require a secured landmine with a manageable total bar/handle mass; availability does not prove a light enough setup. Use a light goblet squat-to-forefoot drive instead of E2, same 1 × 4/no flight, or a light two-hand DB split-stance hinge drive instead of E3, same 1 × 3 per lead side/no flight. E4 uses a light loaded floor bridge instead if bench fit fails, same 1 × 4/rest, changed range recorded. E5 can be unloaded instead if no safe small DB fits; it remains a rapid thigh-lift task. If even unloaded controlled flight or implement handling is unsuitable, record modified/incomplete work rather than adding contacts. All alternatives are replacements, never additions. If no suitable version of a required task is available, record an incomplete/modified delivery; the written fourteen entries do not oblige an athlete to finish unsafe work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep at least five clean reps available, or equivalent ease for holds; no burning/shaking or pre-fatigue goal.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Band Ankle Inversion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Light medial-ankle/foot control to preserve the arch platform under resisted leg drive. Seated, target heel grounded; loop a light band around forefoot and hold ends slightly to the outside of that foot. Turn forefoot gently inward against tension for 2 s and return 2 s without rotating the knee. Reduce tension for whole-leg movement; gentle hand resistance may replace the band at the same dose. |
| 2 | **Split-Stance Suitcase Hold — light dumbbell** | **1 × 12 seconds per loaded arm**; 30 s after each set/side | Light anti-side-bend trunk/pelvic strengthening for transferring force through an asymmetrically loaded stance. One light DB beside the body, opposite foot slightly forward; keep both feet grounded and ribs over pelvis. Hold tall without leaning or shrugging, breathe easily. Switch arm and lead foot for the other side; use less weight for sway. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging resistance/leverage with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; holds finish with about five clean seconds available. Coach-observed control supports the athlete’s effort report. No fixed weight or percentage increase, maximal test, forced rep or failure set. The table lists working sets.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain the familiar main stance-force anchor while external-speed tasks change. Floor split stance, front foot planted, pelvis square; lower 2 s and rise firmly for 1 s. Reduce load/range for collapse or balance loss. Use one DB with stable hand support instead of two unsupported DBs if needed; counts stay the same. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 reps**; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Supply one challenging hip-hinge set after the limited fast hinge/thrust work. Soft knees, hips back, DBs close; lower 3 s and rise in 1 s. Stop the descent before back rounding; reduce load/range if the hinge becomes spinal movement. |
| 3 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells** | **1 × 8 reps bilateral**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain controlled knee-flexed ankle force alongside the fast straight-knee drill. Both legs together, knees near 90°, heels start on floor, hands secure DBs on lower thighs above knees. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load for bounce/lost heel height. If bench fit is unsuitable, use supported standing bent-knee calf raises instead, same reps/rest, and log changed knee angle. |
| 4 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg** | **1 × 8 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Keep the established controlled straight-knee strength role; this is a separate slower, heavier performance from E6. Work from floor, near-straight soft knee, one DB and stable hand support. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load or use bodyweight for lost range; use 1 × 8 bilateral instead of per-leg work if even unilateral bodyweight is too demanding, with 1 × 4 bilateral preparation. |
| 5 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 4 cycles**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain knee-flexor strength through changing heel lever without another loaded standing hinge. Keep a floor bridge while four small alternating heel steps go out and four return; eight steps at about 1 s each = one full cycle. Choose reach so four cycles are challenging with 2–3 whole cycles available. Shorten reach for pelvic sag or cramping; do not add steps or cycles. |
| 6 | **Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain supported challenging hip-flexor strength, distinct from E5's small fast light-load dose. Secure loop band around both forefeet; nonworking leg straight with heel grounded. Flex working hip/knee toward thigh-vertical in 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s without arching or moving the anchor heel. Reduce tension/range for pelvic tilt. If a secure loop is unavailable, use seated DB hip flexion instead with hands securing the DB above the knee: same dose/rest, small controlled thigh lift; preparation unloaded. |

**Separate load-preparation sets in these same exercises:** Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides: 1 × 6 reps per side, then 30 s; 1 × 3 reps per side, then 90 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells: 1 × 5 reps, then 60 s. Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells: 1 × 6 reps, then 45 s. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 45 s; 30 s to change sides on each preparation round. Glute Bridge Walkout: 1 × 1 cycles, then 45 s. Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 45 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Use the listed working tempo with lighter resistance/shorter lever; for time holds use the stated shorter duration. Log further preparation and add its time. These are not new exercise slots or challenging working sets.

**Prescribed workload:** 4 landing events (4 two-foot, 0 single-leg), 8 individual foot contacts; primary 7 rounds / 11 bouts, 66 reps + 4 cycles; preparation 7 rounds / 11 bouts, 43 reps + 1 cycles. Other explosive work: 4 drives of Landmine Squat-to-Forefoot Drive — no flight; 6 drives of Split-Stance Landmine Hinge Drive; 4 hip drives of Explosive Hip Thrust — light dumbbell, bench supported; 8 thigh lifts of Seated Dumbbell Hip Flexion — fast thigh lift; 8 heel rises of Standing Calf Raise — fast concentric, supported single leg. Only E1 has flight: four two-foot landings, eight individual foot contacts. Other rapid work totals four landmine squat drives, six split-hinge drives, four supported hip drives, eight thigh lifts and eight heel rises; these are 30 nonflight force efforts, not 30 jump contacts. Speed-task loads differ from primary-strength loads, and no rep is counted in both blocks. All stated replacements preserve the default landing count but change resistance/support. These totals exclude unknown preparation-sequence and outside workload. A round includes both sides; separately performed sides are separate bouts. Unlike landings/throws/lifts are not equivalent units of stress. [Full per-exercise ledger](../supporting/class_09_record.json).

**Delivery and timing:** Allow about **67–77 min beyond Access & Prepare 1** for one coached athlete or an efficiently equipped small wave, including stated recovery, lift preparation, handling and notes. The full class duration is unresolved. Maintain this exercise order; no timed circuit. Use separate cleared bays within the three separate 10 m lanes; check width, queues and equipment quantities onsite. Rest after the last set may include the next setup once, not twice. Bigger groups, scarce implements or extra instruction extend the clock. Check any 60/90-minute booking against actual preparation and group flow rather than borrowing another program’s timetable. [Timing and audit](../supporting/class_09_audit.md).

**Scheduling:** Review actual recent/upcoming running, races, sport, gym and tumbling outside this class. Roughly 48–72 h from another comparably demanding lower-body exposure is an initial planning reference, not automatic clearance or a weekly calendar. These short sets can still load calf/Achilles, knee extensors and hamstrings substantially. Move or reduce a session when actual recovery requires it.

**Progression note:** A heavier implement is not the goal. Select load for fast controlled execution today, and hold it until actual speed/control and recovery are reported. Do not advance explosive and strength loads together automatically. Class 10 should consolidate repeatable low-cost force tasks, using familiar anchors where comparison is more useful than novelty.
