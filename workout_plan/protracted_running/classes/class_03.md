# Protracted Running — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Produce and receive force over one stance leg while keeping the pelvis controlled.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Previous classes are prescriptions; actual completion, loads, response and recovery remain unknown. This is a conditional next exposure, not earned overload.

**Scope and style:** Protracted running → Endurance; sustained pace; pacing control. Overtime Athletes-inspired elastic, unilateral and force training, adapted for gym support. No actual running, sprints, skipping, conditioning or tumbling. Gym work does not directly supply or test endurance, sustained running pace or pacing decisions. [Style/evidence and limits](../supporting/source_and_exercise_register.md).

**Daily stimulus brief:** The limiting demand is stance-leg force with the pelvis stacked over the foot. E1 provides small lateral single-leg push/acceptance practice; E2 separates bilateral takeoff from unilateral receiving, E3 gives a lower-balance frontal push, E4 adds rapid supported elevation, E5 makes hip drive asymmetric, and E6 accelerates the free thigh without trunk lean. Light hip rotation/adduction work supports the same stance objective.

**Quality marker inside E1:** Within the three scheduled lateral hops per leg, count landings held for 2 s with the knee aligned to the foot and pelvis controlled, without an extra step. Keep the small distance the same on each attempt; this is not a test of maximum lateral distance. Use scheduled attempts only; record failures/stops, not make-up reps. Qualitative control is not a measurement of force, tendon stiffness, running economy or ground-contact time.

**Prior-work review:** Class 2 prescribed 31 two-foot landing events and no single-leg landings, with 9 primary rounds. Class 1 included 14 single-leg events. Neither was reported performed. Class 3 limits its unilateral receiving dose to 10 events and retains all strength roles while changing the bilateral RDL to a supported single-leg hinge with the same 12 total working reps. The [preceding class](class_02.md), map, ledger, feedback and unresolved delivery issues were reviewed before this prescription. No actual athlete data was added.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Place E1/E2 first while balance and landing quality are fresh; the two-foot E3 is a small complementary dose. E4–E6 have no flight, keeping impact down while adding rapid unilateral force tasks. Bodyweight unless a light implement/band is specified. Ballistic loads must permit crisp acceleration without grinding.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Single-Leg Lateral Hop to Stick** | **1 × 3 hops per landing leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Primary single-leg stance task: push outward and accept a small sideways displacement without pelvic collapse. From one foot hop outward no more than one own foot length, land on that same foot and hold 2 s. Walk back to reset. Reduce distance for knee drift; stop for repeated balance steps. |
| 2 | **Two-Foot Vertical Jump to Single-Leg Stick** | **1 × 2 jumps per receiving leg**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 20 s between repetitions | Focus on one-leg receiving while the takeoff force is shared, separating acceptance from unilateral propulsion. Use a very low two-foot jump, land on the nominated foot directly below the body and hold 2 s. Do not tuck the free knee high or chase height. Reduce flight first; if unilateral receiving is unsuitable, use a two-foot landing instead at 1 × 4 total, same rests between attempts. |
| 3 | **Two-Foot Lateral Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 jumps per direction**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 15 s between repetitions | Express a small frontal push using both legs so hip-force practice does not depend entirely on single-leg balance. Jump a short distance sideways, both feet take off and land together, hold 2 s, walk back. Keep toes facing forward and knees tracking; shorten distance for rotation. No crossover, cutting or approach. |
| 4 | **Explosive Low Step-Up — no flight** | **1 × 4 step-ups per side**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last; 5 s between repetitions | Accelerate the body upward through the supported stance leg while reducing the need for a jump landing. One whole foot on a low stable box, working thigh below horizontal. Drive up quickly with that foot staying on the box; free knee lifts modestly, then lower over 2 s. No push-off from the floor foot and no top-foot flight. Reduce box height for pelvic hike. |
| 5 | **Kickstand Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing** | **1 × 4 swings per lead-leg stance**; 90 s after each set/side, including the last | Bias fast hip extension toward the lead leg while the rear toes provide a stable support base. Short staggered stance, rear toes grounded, light bell in both hands. Hinge and snap tall; bell floats below chest height without rotation, stepping or jumping. Park after four. Use less bell load or less stance offset if pelvis turns. |
| 6 | **Supported Standing Band Hip Flexion — fast knee drive** | **1 × 4 knee drives per side**; 60 s after each set/side, including the last | Accelerate the swing thigh while the other leg supplies quiet support; complements the loaded supine strength task later. Secure loop band around forefeet; stable hand support, stance foot stays planted. Lift only the working thigh quickly, lower over 2 s and reset that foot before the next rep. No alternating jogging, foot switches or trunk lean. Reduce band tension for pelvic tilt. |

**Quality and recovery:** Every attempted flight counts, even an invalid landing. Never add demonstration/test/make-up repetitions. A sequence contains only the stated linked contacts; walk between setups. Keep the same left/right order and surface within a comparison. Stop the set for visible speed/control loss; lower the stated amplitude/resistance or extend rest 30–60 s before any remaining scheduled attempt. Stop affected work for pain, limping, dizziness or persisting loss of control.

**Exercise-specific equipment/readiness route:** E1–E3 use short marked positions with no obstacles. E4 requires a suitably low stable box; if it is unavailable, use a fast floor split-squat rise to a brief heel lift instead, 1 × 4 per side, back toes and front forefoot remain grounded, 2 s return and the same rest; no flight. E5 requires a competent swing: use bilateral swings instead at 1 × 8 total if asymmetric control fails, retaining no-impact accounting and 90 s afterward. E6 without a secure loop uses an unresisted quick supported knee lift instead, same dose/rest; this remains speed practice. Stable hand-support heights must permit the specified trunk position. All alternatives are replacements, never additions. If no suitable version of a required task is available, record an incomplete/modified delivery; the written fourteen entries do not oblige an athlete to finish unsafe work.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Keep at least five clean reps available, or equivalent ease for holds; no burning/shaking or pre-fatigue goal.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hip Airplane — Supported** | **1 × 3 open-close cycles per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Lightly load the stance hip while controlling pelvic rotation around a stable femur. Hand support, small single-leg hinge, slowly open the pelvis slightly and return square in 6 s. Knee follows toes; free foot can use a light kickstand instead if rotation/balance is uncontrolled. Stay far from fatigue. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Adduction** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 30 s after each set/side | Light adductor strengthening to support the pelvis over the stance leg during frontal drift. Top leg bent with foot on floor in front; bottom leg straight. Lift the bottom thigh 2 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s without rolling. Use a smaller lift if groin effort becomes strenuous; bodyweight only. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use challenging resistance/leverage with about **2–3 technically sound repetitions in reserve**; holds finish with about five clean seconds available. Coach-observed control supports the athlete’s effort report. No fixed weight or percentage increase, maximal test, forced rep or failure set. The table lists working sets.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides** | **2 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Keep the main unilateral knee/hip force anchor while the explosive receiving conditions change. Floor split stance, front foot planted, pelvis square; lower 2 s and rise firmly for 1 s. Reduce load/range for collapse or balance loss. Use one DB with stable hand support instead of two unsupported DBs if needed; counts stay the same. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift — hand supported, dumbbell** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 120 s after each complete round, including the last | Move the hinge force task onto one supported stance leg without allowing balance to limit resistance. One DB opposite working leg, free hand on stable support. Soft stance knee, free leg travels back, pelvis faces floor; lower 3 s and rise 1 s. Support removes balance as the limiter. Reduce load/range for hip opening or trunk rotation. |
| 3 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells** | **2 × 8 reps bilateral**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Preserve bent-knee ankle force for the stance leg; no automatic calf-load increase. Both legs together, knees near 90°, heels start on floor, hands secure DBs on lower thighs above knees. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load for bounce/lost heel height. If bench fit is unsuitable, use supported standing bent-knee calf raises instead, same reps/rest, and log changed knee angle. |
| 4 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg** | **1 × 8 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Maintain the smaller straight-knee plantarflexor exposure after the limited single-leg landings. Work from floor, near-straight soft knee, one DB and stable hand support. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Reduce load or use bodyweight for lost range; use 1 × 8 bilateral instead of per-leg work if even unilateral bodyweight is too demanding, with 1 × 4 bilateral preparation. |
| 5 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 4 cycles**; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Retain hamstring knee-angle control without another unsupported standing task. Keep a floor bridge while four small alternating heel steps go out and four return; eight steps at about 1 s each = one full cycle. Choose reach so four cycles are challenging with 2–3 whole cycles available. Shorten reach for pelvic sag or cramping; do not add steps or cycles. |
| 6 | **Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion** | **1 × 6 reps per side**; 60 s between sides; 90 s after each complete round, including the last | Load the hip flexors under supported trunk conditions, distinct from E6's brief standing speed task. Secure loop band around both forefeet; nonworking leg straight with heel grounded. Flex working hip/knee toward thigh-vertical in 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s without arching or moving the anchor heel. Reduce tension/range for pelvic tilt. If a secure loop is unavailable, use seated DB hip flexion instead with hands securing the DB above the knee: same dose/rest, small controlled thigh lift; preparation unloaded. |

**Separate load-preparation sets in these same exercises:** Split Squat — two dumbbells at sides: 1 × 6 reps per side, then 30 s; 1 × 3 reps per side, then 90 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift — hand supported, dumbbell: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 60 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated dumbbells: 1 × 6 reps, then 45 s. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise — supported single leg: 1 × 4 reps per side, then 45 s; 30 s to change sides on each preparation round. Glute Bridge Walkout: 1 × 1 cycles, then 45 s. Supine Band-resisted Hip Flexion: 1 × 3 reps per side, then 45 s; 20 s to change sides on each preparation round. Use the listed working tempo with lighter resistance/shorter lever; for time holds use the stated shorter duration. Log further preparation and add its time. These are not new exercise slots or challenging working sets.

**Prescribed workload:** 16 landing events (6 two-foot, 10 single-leg), 22 individual foot contacts; primary 8 rounds / 13 bouts, 80 reps + 4 cycles; preparation 7 rounds / 12 bouts, 44 reps + 1 cycles. Other explosive work: 8 step-ups of Explosive Low Step-Up — no flight; 8 swings of Kickstand Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing; 8 knee drives of Supported Standing Band Hip Flexion — fast knee drive. Default: six same-leg lateral landings plus four bilateral-takeoff/single-leg landings = 10 single-leg events; E3 supplies six two-foot events. E4–E6 have no planned flight: eight step-up drives, eight swings and eight thigh drives. E2 bilateral replacement adds four individual foot contacts but no landing events. Grounded step-up descents are not jump landings. No hip-airplane cycle is counted as two exercises. These totals exclude unknown preparation-sequence and outside workload. A round includes both sides; separately performed sides are separate bouts. Unlike landings/throws/lifts are not equivalent units of stress.
