## Counting conventions

A landing event is one return to the floor after a hop/jump, including the final settling landing; a two-foot event has two individual foot contacts, a single-leg event has one. Initial stance, walking resets and grounded heel steps are not jump landings. Attempted failed jumps still contribute to actual exposure. Do not replace stopped attempts with extra reps.

One **set round** includes both sides where prescribed. Each separately performed side is a **bout**. Thus 2 × 6 per leg = 2 rounds, 4 bouts, 24 reps. Simultaneous bilateral strength reps are one movement each. Walkouts are complete out-and-back cycles, not individual steps or eight hidden exercises. Counts describe exposure and are not a common measure of tissue load.
