## Per-exercise relevance audit

E1 receives the main investment for repeated ankle spring. E2 adds one-leg support without forward projection. E3 adds a small horizontal push and same-leg acceptance; its quick takeoff earns the explosive slot, and the stick controls the subsequent demand. E4 adds hip/knee rapid force from stillness; E5 adds asymmetric rapid leg drive with controlled split landing. E6 trains ballistic hip extension with no further impacts. These are different force/stance constraints, not renamed load variants. E4/E5 intentionally receive limited volume because their mechanical transfer to distance running is less direct than the spring/stance anchors.

S1 loads the active arch and S2 the hip abductors at low fatigue. P1 prioritizes unilateral knee/hip force, P2 loaded hip extension, P3/P4 different knee positions during plantarflexion, P5 changing-knee-angle hamstring work, and P6 resisted hip flexion. P2/P5 share hamstrings with different joint actions; E6/P2 share a hinge with different speed/load demands. Those overlaps are intentional and dose-limited. No exercise is claimed to reproduce running exactly or independently fix running mechanics.

## Workload check

Default planned landings: **16 + 8 + 6 + 3 + 6 = 39 events**, comprising **25 two-foot and 14 single-leg events**, **64 individual foot contacts**. E5 includes both feet, with three efforts per lead leg; it is not six unilateral landings. Other ballistic exposure: **10 swings**, no throws in the default route. E6 replacement changes this to **6 throws and no swings**, with unchanged landing counts. Running/sprinting: **0 m prescribed**. These totals exclude the unknown preparation and external activities.

Light work: **2 rounds / 4 bouts**, **8 five-second arch holds + 16 hip raises**. Primary working work: **9 rounds / 13 bouts**, **80 ordinary reps + 4 full walkout cycles**. Preparation: **7 rounds / 11 bouts**, **43 ordinary reps + 1 shortened walkout cycle**. Four heel steps out and four back define each cycle. The [ledger](../progression_ledger.md) records all substitutions, per-side dosing and different load types. Counts alone do not establish suitability or recovery.

## Timing model and delivery test

Model: one athlete or a small supervised group that can preserve the same order with sufficient matched equipment. This is not a confirmed cohort size or staffing ratio. Work/rest clocks start separately for each athlete. Numbers include demonstrations, normal load selection/handling and specified rest, including final recovery before the next task. Equipment setup during that rest is not added twice. Extra instruction, more preparation or queues extend the model.

| Portion | Modeled minutes | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Explosiveness E1–E6 | 22–25 | 11 separately performed set bouts; low-hop bouts about 5–10 s, isolated-jump bouts about 35–45 s including resets, swings about 10–15 s. About 15.5 min of prescribed post-bout rest, plus demonstrations and positioning. |
| Stabilization S1–S2 | 4–5 | About 2.3 min of hold/movement/release work; 4 × 30 s recovery with side changes, plus brief instruction. |
| P1 Split Squat | 11–13 | 18 lighter reps then 24 working reps across 8 bouts; stated change-leg/round recovery, load selection and handling. |
| P2 DB Romanian Deadlift | 6–7 | 5 lighter + 12 working reps, controlled lowering, 60 s preparation recovery and 2 × 2 min working recovery. |
| P3 Bent-Knee Soleus Raise | 5–6 | 6 lighter + 16 working reps at about 4 s each, 45 s preparation rest and 2 × 90 s working rest, bench/load setup. |
| P4 Single-Leg Calf Raise | 5–6 | 8 lighter + 16 working reps, 30/60 s leg changes and 45/90 s post-round recovery, support/load handling. |
| P5 Glute Bridge Walkout | 3–4 | 1 shortened preparation cycle + 4 working cycles, about 8 s per cycle, 45/90 s recovery and lever selection. |
| P6 Supine Hip Flexion | 5–6 | 6 lighter + 12 working reps at 5 s each, 20/60 s side changes and 45/90 s post-round recovery, band setup. |
| Shared briefing and recording | About 3 | Explain the day's job and capture load/quality notes without adding tests. |
| **Total excluding existing preparation** | **64–75; communicate about 65–75** | Rounded planning envelope. No assumption that Access & Prepare 1 takes a particular number of minutes. |

**Layout:** Start with empty floor bays in separate 10 m lanes. The short hop needs only its stated small distance plus a clear landing/reset area; no runway is used. Clear adjacent athletes and implements from the kettlebell sweep path. After explosiveness, use stable benches and floor spaces for the ordered light/primary work. A coach can demonstrate once for a wave, then observe athletes in turn; no athlete leads extra exercise demos. If there are not enough appropriate DBs/bands/benches, run smaller sequential waves. Do not reorder P1–P6 into arbitrary stations to conceal equipment shortages. No lane width, waiting bay, coach ratio, bar rack or extra floor area is asserted as confirmed.

**Rest and queues:** Peer turns can occupy prescribed recovery only when the elapsed interval actually meets that athlete's clock. Long queues extend the session; short queues do not shorten recovery. This is not a promise that an unspecified full group fits 75 minutes. A 60 min complete session conflicts with the default plan even before preparation. At 90 min, preparation, group delays and any extra load-ramping must still fit; otherwise reduce group size, extend the booking or revise the next delivered prescription while preserving counts and recovery.
