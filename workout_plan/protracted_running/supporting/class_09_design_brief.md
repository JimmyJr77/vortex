# Class 9 stimulus and choice review

The limiting demand is maintaining fast force expression when an implement adds resistance. E1 gives a very small externally loaded takeoff exposure; landmine squat and split-hinge drives offer guided planted-foot force, supported hip thrust addresses hip finish, seated thigh lift addresses leg recovery, and straight-knee heel rise addresses ankle force. The latter five have no flight. Light ankle and offset-trunk work precede familiar challenging strength.

Primary result: Express leg force rapidly against manageable external resistance while keeping the foot and pelvis organized.

Anchor/marker: E1 — Across two sets of two jumps, record secure DB handling, a crisp takeoff and a controlled two-foot landing under the body. Choose the smallest useful load that does not visibly disrupt speed or position; no universal kilograms, velocity target or maximum-load test. If loaded execution is unsuitable, the unloaded route is the correct recorded result.

Prior records: Class 8 prescribed 21 landings and seven primary rounds with a new step-up. No actual response is known. Class 9 reduces jump landings to four, replaces most impact with short resisted rapid-force tasks and retains seven primary rounds. External resistance is a conditional task constraint, not proof of strength gained over eight written classes. Actual response remains unknown.

Choice comparison and novelty: The new default task constraints are a small held jump load, guided triple extension, guided split-hinge force, bench-supported rapid hip finish, a secured seated thigh load and fast straight-knee force without impact. These are openly related resisted/support variants, not six unrelated families. The fast heel-rise action was already a Class 2 contingency, so it is not new for an athlete who actually used that route; that usage is unknown. Simpler substitutes can recur deliberately.

Dosage/audit decisions: Rejected Olympic-lift catches, heavy loaded jumps and loose DB-on-foot drills. Restricted loaded flight to four events and defined secure holds/grounded feet for all other tasks. Kept each overlapping fast hip/ankle task to a few light reps and main strength to seven rounds; slowed challenging sets are separate performances. Landmine mass and bench fit remain delivery checks with practical substitutes.

Conditional progression: A heavier implement is not the goal. Select load for fast controlled execution today, and hold it until actual speed/control and recovery are reported. Do not advance explosive and strength loads together automatically. Class 10 should consolidate repeatable low-cost force tasks, using familiar anchors where comparison is more useful than novelty.
