## Recovery, readiness and delivery

For E2 use two isolated same-leg forward hops instead of the linked pair, 1 × 2 per leg, 20 s between hops and 120 s after each side. For E4 use three isolated low countermovement jumps instead, 20 s between reps and 120 s afterward. E5 may use two isolated short broad jumps instead, same reset/rest.
