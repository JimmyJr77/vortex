# Protracted Running — complete 12-class gym curriculum

**Saved September 13, 2026. Finalized prescriptions: Classes 1–12. Next class: none; written sequence complete. Actual completion and response: unknown.**

This curriculum supports the category **Protracted running**, including **Endurance**, **sustained pace**, and **pacing control**, through complementary strength and explosive gym training. It contains no running, running drills, conditioning finishers or tumbling. The intended contribution is force reserve, ankle spring and control of the stance/swing mechanics relevant to distance running. It cannot supply aerobic running exposure or direct pace-selection practice. A gym quality marker is not a measurement of running economy, endurance or pacing skill.

## Exact seven-category framework

### Protracted running
- Endurance
- sustained pace
- pacing control

### Sprinting
- Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)
- Maximum velocity

### Jumps
- Vertical
- Horizontal
- Lateral
- Force absorption
- Elastic rebound

### Agility
- Deceleration
- Change of direction/reacceleration
- Reactive agility and anticipation

### Object control & receiving
- Tracking
- Catching/receiving
- Controlling/manipulating objects

### Upper-body force generation
- Pushing
- Pulling
- Straight-line projection (such as chest passes)

### Rotational explosiveness
- Upper-body emphasis (rotational throws, punches, and swings)
- Lower-body emphasis (rotational kicks)

Strength, stability, explosiveness and resilience support this framework; they are not additional categories. Body control remains separate. Jump exercise choices in this curriculum support the requested running category and do not create an additional Jumps curriculum.
