# Lower Body Force Generation — progression and workload ledger

All entries are prescriptions, not observed work. Bilateral cycles count once; unilateral doses count each leg. Walkouts are complete out-and-back cycles, with heel steps separately recorded. Landing events and individual foot contacts are distinct. Sprint distance, throws, rapid grounded work and strength are not equivalent stress units. Preparation and existing Access & Prepare 1 are separate.
