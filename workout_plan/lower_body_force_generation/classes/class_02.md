# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 2 of 12

**Week 2: Hip-driven projection** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Initiate and finish hip extension, transferring it into forward projection while the trunk stays organized.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 1 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The paused hip scoop gets the main dose. Retained-load return, near-finish hip drive, supported upward initiation, a small ankle rebound and a grounded lead-leg rise separate complementary jobs. Primary RDL remains the hip anchor; the new loaded bridge replaces unilateral squat work today rather than adding a seventh lift.

**Quality marker:** Within E1 count valid hip-driven releases out of six: visible held hinge, no new dip or arm-dominant lift, feet grounded and forward release. Record ball/setup; do not compare its distance directly with Week 1’s differently started scoop. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

**Delivery:** Keep swing, seated-jump and ball-release areas separate. Seat fit must permit feet planted and a clear floor landing in front; no rebound catches or jumping down from a box. Introduce the continuous swing only when the first prescribed repetitions stay hip-driven. Inspect stable supports, minimum loads, surface grip and all active/waiting/return paths. Keep one athlete active per lane under suitable supervision; no group size or station count is assumed. Three separate 10 m lanes are not a 30 m runway.
