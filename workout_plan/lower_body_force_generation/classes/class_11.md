# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 11 of 12

**Week 11: Connect and control** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Connect a small receiving or lowering action to purposeful leg force, while retaining the ability to stop and reset.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 10 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Two controlled vertical-jump pairs lead. Grounded split-stance release, a small step reversal, rapid floor-to-stand loading, interrupted ankle pairs and a familiar hip finish cover the other force jobs. This is a small integration exposure, not a workload peak or fatigue challenge.

**Quality marker:** In E1 record whether each first reception permits the second controlled upward push and whether the final landing is held. Four attempts are the entire dose; an invalid first landing stops that pair. Do not compare paired-jump output directly with isolated references. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vertical Jump — two linked rises, final stick** | **2 × 2** | Main controlled knee/hip force connection between two upward projections. Each round contains two modest vertical jumps. Use one dip for the first, receive on both feet and make one controlled second rise; then hold the final landing 2 s. Do not rush for short contact time or add a bounce. **Recovery:** 90 s between complete set rounds; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** two separated countermovement jumps per round with a 15 s reset when first reception cannot support a controlled second takeoff. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 2 | **Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — fixed split stance, forward release** | **1 × 1 per leg** | Carry an asymmetric leg push into a free implement without another landing. Use a light ball between thighs and a small fixed split stance. Hinge slightly, drive through the feet to release forward/upward with long arms. No twist, step or catching return; perform one with each lead leg. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 2 total reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a parallel-stance hip scoop at the same total two throws if the split base twists; if containment fails, two unloaded rapid hip hinges with zero throws. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 3 | **Step-Down to Step-Up — quick grounded reversal** | **1 × 1 per leg** | Accept a small controlled lowering then push through the lead foot without flight. Stand with both feet on a low inspected step. Keep the lead foot planted, lower the other heel to a light floor touch in 2 s, then drive back up quickly until both feet join. One complete down-and-up rep per lead leg. Do not bounce from the floor foot. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 2 total reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a shallow supported single-leg squat lower-and-rise at the same per-leg dose if the step height or path is unsuitable. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 4 | **Kettlebell Deadlift — rapid full-foot rise, no swing** | **1 × 3** | Drive a light external load upward through hip/knee extension while retaining whole-foot pressure. Place a light bell between the feet on the floor only if its handle height permits a controlled hinge without rounding. Stand quickly with arms straight and heels down; lower for 3 s to a quiet park and fully reset. No jump, shrug or swing. **Recovery:** 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** three rapid bodyweight hip-hinge returns with heels down and the same reset/rest if handle height or minimum load is unsuitable. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 5 | **Ankle Pogo — two pairs with a full reset** | **1 × 4** | Reconnect a small ankle impulse once, then deliberately reset before repeating. Four pogos total, arranged as two consecutive low hops, stop and rest 15 s, then two more. Keep knees soft, travel negligible and absorb the second and fourth landings. No bonus bounce to restart. **Recovery:** between-rep gaps 0 / 15 / 0 s; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** four isolated supported rapid bilateral calf rises with 10 s resets if ankle rhythm or reception fails; record zero landing events. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 6 | **Glute Bridge — fast grounded rise, settled floor start** | **1 × 3** | Express hip extension near its finish without another lengthened hinge or landing. Bodyweight/light secured pelvic load; settle hips gently on floor, drive up quickly, hold 1 s, lower 2 s. Keep feet down and knees still; finish without back arch. **Recovery:** 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** an unloaded smaller-range fast bridge if load handling or pelvic control is unsuitable. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening, at least 5 technical reps in reserve. Do not chase burn, shaking or pre-fatigue. Reduce load/range when control fades; stop for symptoms.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working sets are challenging at about 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Select load/lever from demonstrated ability, not age or calendar week.
