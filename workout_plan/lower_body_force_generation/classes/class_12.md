## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Squat Jump — settled quarter-squat start** | **2 × 3** | Main knee/hip force from a still start, expressed vertically. Hold a comfortable quarter squat 2 s with hands at hips; jump vertically without another dip, land on both feet with yielding knees and hold 2 s. Keep jump height within a controlled reception. **Recovery:** 90 s between complete set rounds; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a smaller same-start jump; if flight cannot be controlled, a fast nonflight squat rise at the same dose and zero landings. | 2 | **Kettlebell Swing — dead-stop two-hand repetitions** | **1 × 3** | Rapid hip extension moves a retained implement without adding landings. Hike a light kettlebell from a stable parked start, snap hips to a tall finish with a chest-level float, guide the return and park fully after each rep. No squat pull, arm lift or lumbar arch. **Recovery:** 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a very light rapid dumbbell hip hinge with a 2 s lowering and still finish if swing skill or grip is unconfirmed. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 3 | **Calf Raise — rapid supported rise, no flight** | **1 × 3** | Express a quick ankle finish while keeping forefeet continuously grounded. No jump or bouncing heel drop. **Recovery:** 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a smaller unloaded quick heel rise with the same grounded dose. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 4 | **Step-Up — fast grounded drive, rear foot joins** | **1 × 1 per leg** | Let one lead leg supply upward force against a stable support without hand-off to a jump. Use a low inspected immovable step. Front whole foot planted; rise quickly without springing from the trailing foot, bring that foot onto the step, then descend under control in 3 s. | 5 | **Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — hip-driven forward release** | **1 × 2** | Transfer leg/hip extension into a freely released implement rather than a retained swing. Let the ball stop before retrieval. **Recovery:** 20 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** two rapid light-dumbbell quarter-squat rises with heels grounded, 10 s between reps and the same next-section recovery; keep arms fixed and drive the load with the legs; log zero throws. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Squat Rise — supported rapid quarter-range** | **1 × 1 per leg** | Express stance-leg knee extension without flight or a rear foot contributing. Hold stable support, free foot clear; lower to a shallow controlled squat for 2 s, hold 1 s and rise quickly through the whole foot. Use the hand for balance/needed assistance, not a sudden pull. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 2 total reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a supported split-squat rapid rise at the same per-leg dose when the single-leg position cannot stay organized. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening, at least 5 technical reps in reserve. Do not chase burn, shaking or pre-fatigue. Reduce load/range when control fades; stop for symptoms.

Adjust foot distance to stay easy. **Recovery:** 45 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a seated unweighted toe raise through comfortable range. ## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working sets are challenging at about 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Select load/lever from demonstrated ability, not age or calendar week. Keep the stated controlled lowering and complete range; no forced repetitions, maximum testing or conditioning circuit. Check late-set and late-session quality; record reductions instead of forcing nominal completion.
