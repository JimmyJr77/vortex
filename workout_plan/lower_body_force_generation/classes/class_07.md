# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 7 of 12

**Week 7: Independent stance-leg force** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Produce upward and short forward force from each stance leg while controlling the pelvis and receiving on two feet.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 6 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Six low, isolated one-leg vertical takeoffs anchor the session. Grounded lead-biased hip initiation, supported one-leg knee force, ankle force and hip finish support them; two short forward takeoffs provide a small vector transfer. No same-leg landing series is prescribed.

**Quality marker:** In E1 record each takeoff leg, actual hand/arm strategy and whether the two-foot reception is quiet and held for 2 s. An uncontrolled first attempt triggers the grounded replacement for remaining attempts; no extra prerequisite test or symmetry claim is added. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Single-Leg Vertical Takeoff — two-foot floor reception** | **1 × 3 per leg** | Main independent stance-leg upward push with a deliberately broader receiving base. Stand on one leg, make a small controlled dip and project a modest height; bring both feet under the pelvis and receive on two feet, holding 2 s. Three attempts per takeoff leg. No extra preparatory hop. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 60 s, then the other leg; 6 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** two bouts of three bilateral settled-start squat jumps with hands at hips, 10 s between reps and 60 s between bouts, then the same 90 s transition; hold the start 2 s without a second dip and receive on both feet for 2 s. This replaces unilateral takeoffs with bilateral takeoffs and differs from E3 grounded single-leg force. If bilateral receiving also fails, stop the remaining flight work and record a modified session. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 2 | **Kettlebell Swing — staggered dead-stop repetitions** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Start hip-driven force from a parked bell with a lead-biased but two-foot base. Use a shallow stagger and very light bell, hips/pelvis square. Hike, drive one chest-height float and park; reset before the next rep. Complete two per lead leg, without arm lifting or twisting. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a supported single-leg rapid hinge return at the same per-leg dose, with no bell, if parking control or minimum bell load is unsuitable. | 3 | **Single-Leg Squat Rise — supported rapid quarter-range** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Express stance-leg knee extension without flight or a rear foot contributing. Hold stable support, free foot clear; lower to a shallow controlled squat for 2 s, hold 1 s and rise quickly through the whole foot. Use the hand for balance/needed assistance, not a sudden pull. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a supported split-squat rapid rise at the same per-leg dose when the single-leg position cannot stay organized. | 4 | **Bent-Knee Calf Raise — single-leg rapid supported rise** | **1 × 3 per leg** | Produce quick stance-ankle force while holding a modest knee bend. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 5 | **Glute Bridge — single-leg rapid rise with level pelvis** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Express hip extension on each side without the second leg hiding pelvic control. One foot grounded, other thigh comfortably lifted; drive hips up quickly, hold 1 s, lower 2 s without twist. Use bodyweight and a short controllable range. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a kickstand-assisted rapid bridge at the same per-leg dose if the pelvis rotates; record assistance. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Forward Takeoff — two-foot floor reception** | **1 × 1 per leg** | Apply the independent leg push to a short forward rather than upward projection. One modest forward takeoff per leg from a quiet single-leg stance; use a small dip, project into the marked receiving zone and bring both feet to a controlled 2 s reception. No distance target or extra hop. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 60 s, then the other leg; 2 total reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** one supported fast step-up per leg without flight, using the same 60 s side recovery, if a forward reception cannot fit or stay controlled. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening, at least 5 technical reps in reserve. Do not chase burn, shaking or pre-fatigue. Reduce load/range when control fades; stop for symptoms.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |

| 1 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction — light** | **1 × 5 per leg** | Light lateral-hip strengthening to keep the pelvis and knee organized over the force-producing leg. Lie on side, lower leg bent; lift upper leg slightly 2 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s with toes facing forward and pelvis still. Bodyweight is sufficient unless clearly easy. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 30 s, then the other leg; 10 total reps; 45 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a smaller unloaded leg lift with the same per-leg dose. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working sets are challenging at about 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Select load/lever from demonstrated ability, not age or calendar week. Keep the stated controlled lowering and complete range; no forced repetitions, maximum testing or conditioning circuit. Check late-set and late-session quality; record reductions instead of forcing nominal completion.
