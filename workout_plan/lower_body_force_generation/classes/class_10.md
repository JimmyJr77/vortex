# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 10 of 12

**Week 10: Force through the finish** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Complete knee, hip and ankle extension without losing the force-producing base or compensating through the trunk.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 9 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The familiar countermovement jump provides the main upward-force reference. Grounded lead-leg extension, a bench-supported hip finish, rear-ankle output, four fixed-split receptions and a familiar hip reversal give distinct supporting jobs. Walking entries are removed and primary work uses bilateral bases.

**Quality marker:** Record E1’s six attempts under the Week 4 setup when possible: one controlled dip, clear extension and a held quiet reception. Do not chase a locked knee, exaggerated toe point or arched finish; changed setup makes output comparisons descriptive only. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Countermovement Jump — vertical rise and stick** | **2 × 3** | Main coordinated knee/hip push after one controlled countermovement. Use one comfortable dip with arm swing, jump vertically, receive on both feet and hold 2 s. No repeated bouncing, deep-collapse dip or knee tuck for apparent height. **Recovery:** 90 s between complete set rounds; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a smaller countermovement jump or fast nonflight squat rise when reception cannot stay controlled; record removed landings. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 2 | **Step-Up — fast grounded drive, rear foot joins** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Let one lead leg supply upward force against a stable support without hand-off to a jump. Use a low inspected immovable step. Front whole foot planted; rise quickly without springing from the trailing foot, bring that foot onto the step, then descend under control in 3 s. No flight. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a supported fast split-squat rise with both feet grounded and the same per-leg dose if support fit fails. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 3 | **Hip Thrust — bench-supported rapid rise** | **1 × 3** | Express hip extension through the finish with shoulder support allowing a different range from the floor bridge. Use bodyweight first. Support the upper back, not the neck, on a low stable bench that cannot slide; feet flat and knees comfortable. Lower 2 s, drive hips up quickly and hold 1 s with ribs and pelvis aligned. No lumbar arch or head throw. **Recovery:** 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** three rapid floor-bridge rises with the same reset/rest if bench height, friction or shoulder support is unsuitable. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 4 | **Split-Stance Calf Rise — rapid rear-heel finish** | **1 × 3 per leg** | Produce rear-ankle force through the forefoot in a stable forward-oriented stance. Use stable hand support and a short split stance. Front foot stays flat; rear knee softly straight and rear forefoot planted. Rapidly lift the rear heel, hold 1 s and lower 2 s while the pelvis stays still. Three reps per rear leg; no stride or flight. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 6 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** an assisted single-leg straight-knee rapid heel rise at the same per-leg dose if the split stance does not load the rear ankle usefully. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 5 | **Split Squat Jump — fixed stance, same-stance reception** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Complete a modest upward leg drive while retaining a stable front/back foot relationship. From a comfortable fixed split stance make one small dip and jump a modest height. Receive with both feet in the same split stance and hold 2 s; no scissor switch or rear-foot kick. Two attempts per lead leg. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 60 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** two fast grounded supported split-squat rises per lead leg if the split reception cannot be controlled; log removed landings. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 6 | **Kettlebell Swing — continuous two-hand chest-height float** | **1 × 3** | Reapply hip force after the retained implement returns, with no ground landing. Use a light bell and established hinge; three continuous hip-driven swings to chest-level float. Guide the return, keep ribs stacked and park after the third. No arm lift or squat bounce. **Recovery:** 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** three separated parked swings with full 10 s resets if continuous timing is unconfirmed; add actual time. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

**Delivery:** The new rapid hip thrust requires a bench that fits the athlete and cannot slide; the floor-bridge replacement is already fully dosed. Keep split-stance takeoff/receiving inside a marked short area. Rear-heel work stays grounded and outside active lanes. No running entry or object release is needed. Inspect stable supports, minimum loads, surface grip and all active/waiting/return paths. Keep one athlete active per lane under suitable supervision; no group size or station count is assumed. Three separate 10 m lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases need separately verified flight/bounce/roll containment. Replacements occupy the original slot; missing readiness or equipment can change the practice exposure and actual totals.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening, at least 5 technical reps in reserve. Do not chase burn, shaking or pre-fatigue. Reduce load/range when control fades; stop for symptoms.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Band Ankle Eversion — light** | **1 × 5 per leg** | Light outer-ankle strengthening to resist rolling over the foot edge during the push. Heel supported; hand-held light band draws the forefoot inward. Move forefoot outward 2 s, hold 1 s, return 2 s while the knee stays still. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 30 s, then the other leg; 10 total reps; 45 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** gentle hand resistance through a smaller range at the same per-leg dose. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 2 | **Side-Lying Hip Abduction — light** | **1 × 5 per leg** | Light lateral-hip strengthening to keep the pelvis and knee organized over the force-producing leg. Bodyweight is sufficient unless clearly easy. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 30 s, then the other leg; 10 total reps; 60 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a smaller unloaded leg lift with the same per-leg dose. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working sets are challenging at about 2–3 technical reps/cycles in reserve. Select load/lever from demonstrated ability, not age or calendar week. Keep the stated controlled lowering and complete range; no forced repetitions, maximum testing or conditioning circuit. Check late-set and late-session quality; record reductions instead of forcing nominal completion.
