# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 4 of 12

**Week 4: Knee-led upward drive** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Push upward through an organized knee and whole-foot base, then finish through the ankle.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 3 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The countermovement jump leads; a low box adds a defined receiving surface. Grounded knee-to-ankle extension, bent-knee ankle output and front-leg return isolate related contributors, with a small familiar hip-drive dose for continuity. Primary squat receives the main investment.

**Quality marker:** Within E1 record six takeoffs for a single controlled dip, clear upward projection and a quiet 2 s bilateral reception. Record actual dip/arm strategy and surface; a higher box in E2 is not a jump-height measurement. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.
