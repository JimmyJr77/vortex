# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 8 of 12

**Week 8: Hip-to-ankle sequencing** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Connect rapid hip extension through the ankle without compensatory trunk or arm motion.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 7 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The held-hinge leg-driven release leads, with small forward ankle impulses, familiar hip reversals, a stance-leg finish, flexed-knee ankle output and a seated knee/hip start supporting distinct parts of the chain. Landing exposure falls and primary work returns to bilateral bases.

**Quality marker:** In E1 record whether the six listed releases show hip drive preceding a smooth forefoot finish, with no second dip or arm curl. Keep ball, start position and trajectory noted; do not compare distance with the earlier different scoop executions. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

**Delivery:** Verify ball flight, ceiling, bounce and roll containment before using the main release. Keep the short pogo series well inside the first 3 m with remaining space clear. Bench height must fit the athlete for both sit-to-stand and seated calf work; no adjustable bench is presumed. Inspect stable supports, minimum loads, surface grip and all active/waiting/return paths. Keep one athlete active per lane under suitable supervision; no group size or station count is assumed. Three separate 10 m lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases need separately verified flight/bounce/roll containment. Replacements occupy the original slot; missing readiness or equipment can change the practice exposure and actual totals.
