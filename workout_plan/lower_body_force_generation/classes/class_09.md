# Lower Body Force Generation — Class 9 of 12

**Week 9: Controlled entry into force** · One coached class this week.

**Primary effort:** Turn a quiet, short entry into a purposeful upward or forward leg push.

**Baseline:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting and landing technique, qualified supervision and individual load selection. Reviewed Class 8 and its audit/workload, current ledgers and available athlete feedback. No actual performance, completion, symptoms, load tolerance or recovery was supplied. This is a conditional prescription, not an earned progression.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Four walk-in vertical takeoffs anchor the day; two short forward takeoffs change output direction. Grounded lunge return, a stepped leg-driven release, a small step reversal and hip-finish reversal train controlled entry without adding hard approaches. Strength work is reduced while entry constraints change.

**Quality marker:** In E1 record whether the two walking foot placements lead to a stable two-foot plant, clear upward push and a 2 s held reception. A rushed plant, extra hop or unstable reception consumes the attempt and triggers a simpler remaining prescription. Use the listed attempts only; a miss or invalid attempt consumes the dose. Record actual attempts, setup and any substitutions; no bonus tests or comparison across unlike tasks.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

Use light resistance or bodyweight that permits visibly rapid force with control. Begin with E1 while fresh. Each row has a distinct job; supporting work stays small. Rest may be extended to 2–3 minutes when speed, control or attention has not returned. Stop for pain, loss of alignment, stiff/asymmetric landing or clear slowing; rest and regress remaining work only if control returns, otherwise record stopped work. Never make up failed attempts. No drop landings or rotational throws are prescribed.

Exactly two entry foot placements per attempt, no run or preparatory hop. **Recovery:** 90 s between complete set rounds; 15 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a stationary countermovement vertical jump at the same remaining dose if the entry disrupts plant or receiving control; log removed entry steps. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 2 | **Walk-In Broad Jump — step-close forward takeoff** | **1 × 2** | Direct the same modest entry into a short forward leg projection. Take one quiet walking step and close the other foot, then make one modest two-foot forward jump; hold the two-foot landing 2 s. No running, added hop or distance target. **Recovery:** 15 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a stationary broad jump at the same remaining dose if the entry or total travel cannot fit; log removed entry steps. | 3 | **Forward Lunge — rapid return to the starting foot line** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Accept a controlled forward step and drive the front leg to regain the start. Bodyweight: step forward gently, lower under control, then push through the front whole foot to return quickly beside the other foot. Stay grounded, trunk quiet and knee aligned. Two complete returns per moving leg. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a supported stationary split-squat rapid rise at the same per-leg dose if the forward entry cannot stay controlled. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 4 | **Medicine Ball Scoop Toss — walk-step forward release** | **1 × 1 per leg** | Transfer a controlled forward step through the planted leg into a straight release. Start standing with a light ball and long arms. Take one short forward walking step, let the rear foot remain grounded in split stance, then drive legs/hips to release forward. One throw per lead leg; no airborne takeoff, torso twist or catch. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 60 s, then the other leg; 2 total reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** one rapid supported split-stance hip-hinge return per lead leg with no ball, using the same side recovery, if release containment fails. | 5 | **Step-Down to Step-Up — quick grounded reversal** | **1 × 2 per leg** | Accept a small controlled lowering then push through the lead foot without flight. Stand with both feet on a low inspected step. Keep the lead foot planted, lower the other heel to a light floor touch in 2 s, then drive back up quickly until both feet join. Do not bounce from the floor foot. **Recovery:** complete one leg's bout, rest 45 s, then the other leg; 4 total reps; 10 s between individual reps; 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** a shallow supported single-leg squat lower-and-rise at the same per-leg dose if the step height or path is unsuitable. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |
| 6 | **Glute Bridge — controlled lower to rapid reversal** | **1 × 4** | Express hip-finish force after a controlled loaded-position change. Bodyweight, begin in a level bilateral bridge; lower for 2 s until the pelvis lightly meets the floor, then rise rapidly without pausing or arching. Four continuous reps, settling on the floor after the final return. **Recovery:** 90 s before the next exercise/section **Use instead:** four floor-start bridge rises with 10 s full resets and the same 90 s transition if the reversal causes cramp or lumbar compensation; record the extra actual reset time. This replaces remaining work in this slot; follow an explicit replacement dose if stated, otherwise keep the remaining dose/rest. Log changed events or laterality. |

**Delivery:** Mark the whole walking entry and takeoff/receiving corridor within the first 3 m, leaving 7 m clear; shorten steps and projection or use stationary replacements if the actual athlete cannot fit. E1/E2 contain 12 total walking entry foot placements and E4 adds two, for 14 prescribed approach steps. Ordinary reset walks and grounded lunge/step movements are separate unmeasured travel, not sprint metres or jump contacts. No start is outside the lane. Inspect stable supports, minimum loads, surface grip and all active/waiting/return paths. Keep one athlete active per lane under suitable supervision; no group size or station count is assumed. Three separate 10 m lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases need separately verified flight/bounce/roll containment. Replacements occupy the original slot; missing readiness or equipment can change the practice exposure and actual totals.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises
