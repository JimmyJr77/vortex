**Scope:** Twelve weekly coached focus classes, one per week, beginning when scheduled. The request to create this plan “as well” continues the complete-curriculum and collection workflow. Finalize, audit and save each next class sequentially. A missed week resumes the sequence; never double up the work. Dates are not invented.

**Framework mapping:** “Lower Body Force Generation” is a program title, not an additional category. Its primary expression is **Jumps > Vertical / Horizontal**, with **Sprinting > Acceleration (straight-line and multidirectional)** as secondary transfer. Knee, hip and ankle strength support these outcomes. This course emphasizes sagittal force initiation and finish; the existing rebound, approach, agility and rotational-kick curricula remain separate. Do not automatically stack their workloads.
