# Horizontal Jumps — unique drill register

**72 explosive + 24 stabilization/resilience drill entries; no repeated prescribed execution.** Strength lifts may recur. Access & Prepare 1 remains the unchanged existing reference. Variants may share a family; their start/support, action, contraction mode, force application or takeoff/landing relationship must differ meaningfully. A changed name, weight number or repetition target alone is not counted as unique. Class 1's named drill replacements were reserved when selecting later defaults.

The full execution and specific job are in each linked workout and in [the structured register](supporting/drill_uniqueness.json). See [semantic review](full_sequence_audit.md) for the closely related families.
