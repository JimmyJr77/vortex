# Horizontal Jumps — twelve-class prescribed workload

**All figures are prescribed, not observed.** Each class contains 6 explosive, 2 light stabilization/resilience and 6 challenging primary-strength entries. A bilateral landing is one event/two landing-foot contacts; unilateral is one event/one contact. Low-amplitude and initial-drop columns are subsets of landings, not extra contacts. Throws and grounded rapid/swing efforts are separate. Class 12's jump throw contributes to both athlete landings and throws; its hop–step–jump has two unilateral plus one bilateral landing per sequence.

These are not consecutive-day instructions or equal-intensity units. No actual cumulative workload can be calculated from these prescriptions. Access & Prepare 1 exposure/duration, attendance, loads, extra ramps and other sport/tumbling remain unknown. Individual classes and audits specify preparation work, stabilization, resets, per-side dose, sequence counts and entry steps. The clock includes work/rest/ramps/handling and is a one-athlete model; group flow adds unknown time.

For hold accounting, sum separately performed bouts; bilateral simultaneous holds count their elapsed hold time once. Class 1's one-target-foot holds sum both feet. This is prescribed hold time, not a normalized tissue-load score.
