## Replacement identity record

- **E1 Ankle Pogo in Place:** local `ankle-pogo-in-place`, stationary bilateral low-amplitude task; canonical contract and migrations 712/713 available. It removes forward travel.
- **E5 hands-on-hips countermovement Broad Jump to Stick:** proposed execution variant. It removes arm contribution but keeps a continuous dip; neither E2's natural-arm task nor E3's static start. No new canonical ID is assigned.
- **E6 Two-hand Kettlebell Swing:** proposed replacement execution; an exact canonical binding was not verified in this search. Hinge-driven, feet planted, no overhead finish; three complete swings per set.
- **S1 supported shallow single-leg squat:** proposed replacement execution, not an additional slot. **S2 seated one-target-foot doming:** support regression within the researched short-foot family.
- **P1 Double Front-Rack Squat:** local canonical name from migration 447; slug `double-dumbbell-front-squat`, variant `double-dumbbell-front-rack`. Same job with different holding capacity/handling.
- **P4 Dumbbell Glute Bridge:** local name/key `dumbbell-glute-bridge`; migration 539 treats externally loaded floor support as a declared loaded-floor variant, not its unloaded baseline. Record range/support and implement rather than silently calling it the same Hip Thrust.

## Class 2 unique drill entries

- **Seated Broad Jump** — Proposed execution/addition; no exact canonical ID asserted. Distinct job: Project forward from a supported motionless start. Execution: Sit near the edge, feet flat under or slightly ahead of knees; pause 2 s, swing the arms and jump forward to a two-foot 2 s hold.
- **Squat Jump** — Local name/key: `squat-jump`; current canonical publication unverified. Distinct job: Supply rapid leg extension from stillness without the forward-landing demand of the main drill. Execution: Hold a comfortable partial squat 2 s with hands on hips; jump upward without a new dip and stick on two feet.
- **Tall-Kneeling Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss** — Proposed execution/addition; no exact canonical ID asserted. Distinct job: Isolate hip-to-arm forward acceleration while removing ankle/knee takeoff. Execution: Kneel tall on a comfortable stable surface, ball low; hinge slightly at the hips then extend and scoop forward, keeping knees down and ribs controlled.
- **Split-Stance Pogo** — Local name/key: `split-stance-pogo`; current canonical publication unverified. Distinct job: Organize quick ankle pressure through a staggered base before split-position force work. Execution: Keep the chosen staggered stance unchanged; make small two-foot rebounds in place, then settle. Change lead foot for the other bout.
- **Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing** — Proposed execution/addition; no exact canonical ID asserted. Distinct job: Produce a discrete hip impulse from a parked load rather than reuse successive swing momentum. Execution: Hike a light bell, snap the hips to float it below chest height, then guide it back to the floor and fully reset; one hike/swing/park is one rep.
- **Static Split-Squat Jump to Same-Stance Landing** — Proposed execution/addition; no exact canonical ID asserted. Distinct job: Express rapid force from a fixed asymmetrical base without a midair leg switch. Execution: Pause 2 s in a shallow split squat, jump a small amount up/forward, land in the same split stance and hold 2 s.
- **Split Squat Isometric Hold** — Local name/key: `split-squat-isometric-hold`; current canonical publication unverified. Distinct job: Lightly stabilize the knee/pelvis in the settled push position. Execution: Hold a shallow split stance with whole front foot down, rear heel lifted and pelvis level.
- **Calf Isometric Hold — Straight-Knee** — Local name/key: `calf-isometric-hold-straight-knee`; current canonical publication unverified. Distinct job: Maintain controlled forefoot force with knees nearly straight at the end of the push. Execution: Both feet supported, rise modestly onto the forefeet and hold without ankle rolling; light hand support may steady the body.
