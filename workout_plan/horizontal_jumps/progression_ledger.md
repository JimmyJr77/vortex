## Counting conventions

One jump repetition means one takeoff, flight and landing event, including the last pogo and any failed attempt actually attempted. A bilateral landing is one event and two landing-foot contacts. Do not equate a low pogo, a high-intent broad jump, a linked contact and a unilateral takeoff simply because each has one landing. Walking returns and support steps are not plyometric landings.

One repeated-jump sequence here contains exactly two repetitions/landings. One throw is one release, with no planned athlete flight. One walkout cycle is the complete out-and-back action, containing eight heel-reposition steps; these grounded steps are not jumps. A set round includes both sides when prescribed; separately performed left and right sets are two set bouts. Bilateral simultaneous reps are not doubled.
