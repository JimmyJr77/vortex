# Horizontal Jumps — Class 12 of 12

**Primary effort:** Integrate same-leg and opposite-leg contacts into a controlled horizontal finish.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The main task is one integrated hop–step–jump foot sequence from a stationary single-leg start at modest travel. Lateral entry, an elevated same-leg finish and release tasks rehearse organizing different incoming positions into forward force. This is integration, not a maximum-distance test or a claim that the full competition event has been trained.

**Before delivery:** Require actual competence in isolated and linked same-leg/opposite-leg contacts, current recovery and a suitable landing surface. Keep each segment small and do not attempt competition triple-jump distance on the gym floor. If the sequence is not established, revise the task before delivering this class; no earlier written lesson grants eligibility. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: two complete sequences, one starting on each leg. A correct sequence lands the hop on its takeoff leg, the step on the opposite leg and the last jump on both feet; only the final landing holds 2 s. Log each of the three contacts and any sequence error. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stationary Single-leg Hop–Step–Jump** | **2 × 1 = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. continuous reps within the short set; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Two integrated sequences = six landings: four unilateral and two bilateral. No running approach; not three bilateral broad jumps. | Integrate same-leg reuse, opposite-leg transfer and a bilateral final reception into one defined horizontal foot sequence. From one foot, hop to the same foot, step-bound to the opposite foot, then jump from that foot to both feet; hold the final landing. Start the other set on the opposite leg. **Regress/stop:** Shorten each segment and slow entry intent while keeping the sequence; stop for a collapsing intermediate contact or incorrect foot order. |
| 2 | **Lateral Step-in Broad Jump** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four ordinary lateral entry steps, two from each direction; each at most 1 m. This needs verified lane width. | Redirect a small lateral entry into a straight-ahead two-foot push. Take one short lateral step into a parallel two-foot gather, square the pelvis to the forward lane and jump forward; hold the landing 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Shorten/slow the lateral step; stop if the jump drifts sideways or the pelvis rotates through landing. |
| 3 | **Same-leg Forward Hop onto a Low Box** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Receive one-leg forward force with full-foot placement on a raised support without assistance from the free foot. Hop from one foot to the same foot on a very low wide stable box; hold 2 s with the pelvis level, then use a controlled step-down to reset. **Regress/stop:** Lower/bring the box closer; stop for partial-foot contact, free-foot rescue or pelvic collapse. **Entry:** Demonstrated elevated same-leg landing; no narrow or tall box. |
| 4 | **Lateral-entry Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Throw** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four ordinary side-entry steps total; no athlete flight. | Square the hips after a side entry and direct leg-to-arm force forward. Take one short lateral step into a stable bilateral base, then immediately use a shallow dip and light forward scoop release with the pelvis facing the target. **Regress/stop:** Slow the side step and lighten the ball; stop if the throw becomes rotational or entry balance is lost. |
| 5 | **Tall-Kneeling Forward Overhead Medicine Ball Throw** | **1 × 3 = 3 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Express a forward long-lever arm impulse while holding the hip/trunk base steady. Kneel tall with a very light ball above the forehead; release straight forward through a quick two-arm overhead throw with knees grounded and ribs down. **Regress/stop:** Use a lighter ball and smaller arm excursion; stop for back arching or uncomfortable kneeling. |
| 6 | **Forward Medicine Ball Jump Throw** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Small crisp jump with light airborne forward release. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four releases and four bilateral athlete landings are both counted; no return catch. | Coordinate full-body takeoff with an airborne forward release and then recover the feet for landing. With a very light ball low in front, dip and jump modestly forward while scooping the ball forward during flight; land on both feet and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Use smaller flight/lighter ball; stop if the ball enters the athlete's landing zone or release disrupts foot recovery. **Entry:** Familiar jump-release coordination and physically separated athlete/ball landing envelopes. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** Keep the complete hop–step–jump within the first 5 m, with the final controlled finish by 6 m and 4 m clear. Lateral entries require usable width and no neighboring-lane traffic; verify this instead of assuming lane width. The low box must support an entire single foot with margin. For E6 the athlete must land before the ball landing/roll envelope; if those cannot be separated and contained, do not deliver that drill unchanged. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.
