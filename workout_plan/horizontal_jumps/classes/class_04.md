# Horizontal Jumps — Class 4 of 12

**Primary effort:** Synchronize arm action with a staggered two-foot forward push.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The priority is gathering a split starting base into a parallel landing while the arms match leg extension. Elevated landing, one-arm forward release, fore-aft ankle reversal, a switched split landing and a small integrated vertical jump give distinct coordination jobs without returning to Class 1's drill.

**Before delivery:** Confirm split-position takeoff and safe two-foot landing. After Class 3, check actual ankle/knee response rather than scheduling this automatically on the next day. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: eight attempts across the two lead-foot positions; both feet contribute at takeoff, the arms move together, and the final parallel two-foot stance holds 2 s. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split-Stance Broad Jump to Parallel Landing** | **2 × 2 per side = 8 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Convert a staggered bilateral push into aligned forward travel. Begin with one foot modestly ahead, dip once and swing both arms; push off both feet and gather into a parallel two-foot landing held 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Reduce stagger and travel; stop if it becomes a single-leg jump or the landing twists. |
| 2 | **Countermovement Broad Jump onto a Low Box** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Time the forward arm drive while receiving the body on a higher surface. Use a stationary two-foot dip and forward/up arm swing to land both feet fully on a low stable box; stand, then step down under control. **Regress/stop:** Use lower/closer placement; stop for excessive knee tucking, partial-foot landing or an unstable box. **Entry:** Full-foot low-box landing and controlled step-down. |
| 3 | **Single-arm Forward Medicine Ball Push Throw** | **1 × 3 per side = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Couple one arm's forward force with a bilateral leg drive while resisting unwanted rotation. Use a very light ball at one shoulder, feet parallel; dip and extend into a forward push release while keeping hips facing the target and feet planted. **Regress/stop:** Reduce load; stop if trunk rotation or an arm-only strain replaces the coordinated push. |
| 4 | **Two-foot Forward–Backward Line Pogo** | **1 × 6 = 6 reps.** Small quick crossings. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Reverse small fore-aft ankle contacts while maintaining an organized trunk. Make six low two-foot crossings of a flat floor line, alternating forward/backward; each crossing is one landing, then settle. **Regress/stop:** Narrow the crossing distance; stop for deep squatting or loss of rhythm. |
| 5 | **Lunge Switch Jump** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Reorganize an asymmetrical base in flight without a long forward bound. From a shallow split stance, jump modestly and switch legs in flight; hold the opposite split landing 2 s. Reset the stated starting side before each rep. **Regress/stop:** Reduce height and stance length; stop if the feet cross or the landing cannot be held. |
| 6 | **Countermovement Jump — Natural Arms** | **1 × 3 = 3 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Integrate dip-to-arm timing with a small vertical flight before fatigue accumulates in lifting. Dip once, drive the arms up with leg extension, land in the starting area on two feet and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Use modest height; stop if the arms finish after takeoff or landing control fails. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** Keep E1 travel and E2 box landing within the first 6 m, with 4 m clear. Verify box stability, full-foot area and a walking step-down route. Use a flat existing floor marking or a visually designated crossing, never a raised trip line. No simultaneous box work and ball retrieval in the same area. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working load or lever must be **challenging at approximately 2–3 technically sound reps/cycles in reserve**. Keep the same recent actual load when uncertain; do not infer progression from a previous written dose. Controlled technique and secure load handling determine suitability, not age alone. No forced reps, maximal testing or unstable surfaces.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |

| 4 | **Hip Thrust — pelvic dumbbell, upper back on bench** | **1 × 8 = 8 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension near its finish without another large hinge volume. | 6 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 = 3 cycles.** 2–3 technical cycles in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts.** | Load knee-flexor control while holding the pelvis, complementing the hip hinge.
