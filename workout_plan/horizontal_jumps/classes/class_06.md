# Horizontal Jumps — Class 6 of 12

**Primary effort:** Project forward while clearing an obstacle and owning an elevated landing.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The seated box jump is the priority: coordinate a forward/up impulse from support and place both feet fully on a higher receiving surface. A unilateral-to-box task, one hurdle clearance, short linked hurdle contacts and rope singles address different placement/clearance demands; a seated chest pass isolates rapid forward arm force.

**Before delivery:** Confirm stable box/bench geometry and full-foot landing competence. This class is not authorized for an athlete who has only landed on the floor; actual elevated-landing readiness must be checked without extra test repetitions. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: six attempts from a motionless seat, no preliminary rock, full-foot two-foot landing on the low box and a controlled 2 s hold; box height is not a jump-performance score. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Box Jump** | **3 × 2 = 6 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Create forward/up projection from support into a higher receiving surface. Sit still with feet flat, swing the arms and jump forward/up onto a separate low stable box; hold 2 s, stand, then step down and reset. **Regress/stop:** Lower/bring the landing box closer; stop for rocking, foot clipping or knee-tucking to reach a high box. |
| 2 | **Single-leg Takeoff to Two-foot Box Landing** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Apply one leg's propulsion while receiving with both feet on an elevated surface. From one foot near a low box, dip and drive forward/up, then land both feet fully on the box and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Lower/bring the box closer; stop for twisting takeoff or partial-foot landing. |
| 3 | **Low Hurdle Forward Jump to Stick** | **1 × 3 = 3 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Clear a small physical barrier without overreaching the forward landing. Start stationary on two feet, dip and jump over one low hurdle; land beyond it on two feet and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Lower the hurdle and shorten travel; stop for clipping or a feet-first reach that loses the torso. |
| 4 | **Continuous Low Hurdle Hops** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Each rep is one hurdle clearance/landing: four total landings, not four two-hop sequences. | Organize successive forward contacts against an external clearance rhythm. Use two low hurdles, make two linked two-foot hops with no intentional pause, then hold the final landing 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Lower/narrow the hurdle arrangement while retaining two distinct clearances; stop if rushing contact causes clipping or collapse. **Entry:** Demonstrated low linked clearances. |
| 5 | **Seated Medicine Ball Chest Pass** | **2 × 3 = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Supply rapid forward arm force while keeping the leg impact dose unchanged. Sit upright on a stable bench, feet flat; release a light ball forward from chest height with a quick two-hand push, no torso heave. **Regress/stop:** Use a lighter ball; stop for back arching or unsafe containment. |
| 6 | **Jump Rope Power Singles** | **1 × 6 = 6 reps.** Crisp modest jumps; no speed/endurance target. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Practice active foot clearance and an organized ankle return under a predictable rope passage. Use one rope turn per modest two-foot jump, six controlled contacts in place; clear the rope without tucking the knees high. **Regress/stop:** Lower jump height and slow the rope to retain one turn per jump; stop after catches or disrupted landings. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** A separate seated start and landing box are required for E1, each stable and appropriately sized; their quantities are unconfirmed. E2 needs only the low landing box. Keep two hurdles and their final receiving area within the first 6 m and reserve 4 m clear. Rope work needs verified lateral/overhead clearance away from the throw/box station. No athlete jumps down from a box to reset. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working load or lever must be **challenging at approximately 2–3 technically sound reps/cycles in reserve**. Keep the same recent actual load when uncertain; do not infer progression from a previous written dose. Controlled technique and secure load handling determine suitability, not age alone. No forced reps, maximal testing or unstable surfaces.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goblet Squat — dumbbell** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Build bilateral knee/hip extension capacity for the takeoff push. Lower 3 s, stand in about 1 s; keep the whole foot down at a repeatable comfortable depth. Reduce load/range for knee collapse or trunk loss. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension from the flexed-hip position used to organize projection. | 3 | **Split Squat — stationary, two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 per side = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Give each lead leg a challenging force contribution through a stable base. Lower 3 s, rise in about 1 s; retain stance width and pelvic control. Reduce load before balance changes the lift. |
| 4 | **Hip Thrust — pelvic dumbbell, upper back on bench** | **1 × 8 = 8 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension near its finish without another large hinge volume. | 5 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **1 × 10 = 10 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Supply plantarflexion force while the knee remains flexed during plant-to-push. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s to the floor; retain knee angle, secure loads. Reduce load if heel travel shortens or heels bounce. |
| 6 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 = 3 cycles.** 2–3 technical cycles in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts.** | Load knee-flexor control while holding the pelvis, complementing the hip hinge. Each cycle is 2 small outward steps per foot and 2 inward per foot (8 heel placements, about 1 s each); keep slight knee bend and hips level. Shorten the lever for sag or cramping. |
