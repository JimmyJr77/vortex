# Horizontal Jumps — Class 11 of 12

**Primary effort:** Express fast forward force against light external resistance.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** A lightly rear-resisted broad jump is the priority, keeping the natural arm drive while resisting forward body travel. A small dumbbell broad jump removes free arm swing and adds carried mass; grounded landmine extension and a kickstand swing emphasize rapid loaded leg/hip force with fewer impacts. Two different forward release paths complete the external-acceleration jobs.

**Before delivery:** Confirm actual recovery after any drop work. Band condition, comfortable low-pelvis fit, controlled tension and anchor/holder placement must be verified. Loaded jumps require secure handling and a load that permits a small crisp flight; no maximal distance with load. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: four attempts with low, consistent rearward tension, coordinated two-foot takeoff and a controlled two-foot finish held 2 s. Record band/setup and travel separately; do not compare its distance with unresisted jumps. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rear-band-Resisted Broad Jump** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Light consistent resistance and short controlled projection. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Apply forward body impulse against an opposing horizontal force while preserving arm coordination. Use a broad comfortable band low around the pelvis, secured behind by a qualified holder/verified anchor; dip and swing naturally into a short two-foot forward jump, then hold. **Regress/stop:** Reduce tension and travel; stop if the band shifts, rebounds the athlete backward or destabilizes the landing. |
| 2 | **Dumbbell Broad Jump — Arms Fixed at Sides** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Very light load, fast push, modest distance. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Produce forward impulse with carried mass and no free arm swing. Hold very light dumbbells securely at the sides, dip once and make a short forward jump; keep arms quiet and land on two feet with a 2 s hold. **Regress/stop:** Reduce load/travel; stop if grip, arm swinging or a heavy landing dominates. |
| 3 | **Landmine Power Squat — No Flight** | **2 × 3 = 6 efforts.** Rapid extension against light resistance; feet remain planted. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Drive a lightly loaded angled implement rapidly through leg extension without another landing. Hold a light landmine end at chest level with both hands, use a shallow squat and stand rapidly to a tall finish while keeping feet in contact; no separate press. Lower/reset under control. **Regress/stop:** Use less load/range; stop if the anchor moves or the athlete arches/pushes with the arms instead of extending the legs. **Entry:** Secure landmine pivot, suitable bar/handle and verified arc clearance. |
| 4 | **Kickstand Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing** | **1 × 3 per side = 6 efforts.** Crisp hip-driven acceleration; feet stay planted and the bell stays controlled. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Bias fast hip extension toward one lead leg while the rear foot supplies a stable small support. Use a short kickstand stance, park a light bell between reps; hike, snap the hips to float the bell below chest height, then park under control without twisting. **Regress/stop:** Reduce load and narrow the rear offset; stop if pelvis rotation or arm lifting replaces hip drive. **Entry:** Familiar kickstand hinge and bell handling. |
| 5 | **Squat Medicine Ball Chest Throw** | **2 × 2 = 4 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Accelerate a chest-held load forward through a coordinated bilateral leg drive. Hold a light ball at the chest, descend to a comfortable squat and extend promptly into a two-hand forward chest release, keeping feet planted and ribs controlled. **Regress/stop:** Reduce ball load/depth; stop for an arm-only heave or lumbar arching. |
| 6 | **Half-Kneeling Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Use a split grounded hip base and long low-to-forward arm path to express projection. One knee down and opposite foot flat, ball low beside/in front of the lead thigh; extend the hip and scoop straight forward with two hands, without twisting or rising into a jump. **Regress/stop:** Use a lighter ball and smaller hinge; stop for unstable knee/foot support or rotation. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** The band holder/anchor stays behind the start and out of the jump/return path; verify useful band travel without excessive recoil. Short resisted/loaded jumps finish by 5 m and settle by 6 m, leaving 4 m clear. Landmine arc, bell swing and both throw paths require separate inspected envelopes, never simultaneous crossing use. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.
