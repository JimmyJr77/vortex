# Horizontal Jumps — Class 10 of 12

**Primary effort:** Receive a small imposed landing and redirect force forward.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The low drop-to-broad jump is the priority. A grounded snap-down start, a small vertical drop rebound, a received-ball scoop return and supported angled ankle contacts separate eccentric arrival, direction and rapid return. A very small seated single-leg box dose supplies controlled unilateral force without another imposed drop.

**Before delivery:** This class requires demonstrated comfortable low-drop rebounding and actual recovery, not simply basic landing technique. Start from the lowest suitable stable step; no numerical drop height or reactive-strength threshold is presumed. If readiness/setup is absent, revise before delivery. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: four drop/rebound attempts; step off rather than jump off, receive on two feet, project forward without a deliberate pause and hold the final two-foot landing 2 s. Record both contacts per attempt. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Low Drop to Broad Jump** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four attempts = four initial drop landings plus four final jump landings; eight events total. | Redirect an externally imposed downward arrival into forward projection. Step off a very low stable box, receive on both feet and immediately jump forward; use the needed knee/hip bend, then hold the final landing 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Lower the starting step and shorten the forward jump; stop for a stiff/jarring arrival, knee collapse or a deliberate reset between contacts. |
| 2 | **Grounded Snap-Down to Forward Rebound Jump** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. No flight in the initial snap-down: only the forward-jump landing counts unless an extra hop actually occurs. | Create a quick active lowering action and use it for forward force. Start tall with arms raised and heels lightly lifted; snap into a shallow athletic position without leaving the floor, then immediately jump forward and hold the final landing. **Regress/stop:** Reduce lowering speed/depth and travel; stop if an unintended initial hop appears or the athlete loses the foot base. |
| 3 | **Low Drop to Vertical Rebound** | **1 × 2 = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Two attempts = two initial drop and two final landings; four events total. | Separate rapid receiving/return from the forward-direction task. Step off a very low box, land bilaterally and rebound a modest amount upward; control the final two-foot landing. **Regress/stop:** Lower the step and rebound height; stop if impact control or alignment deteriorates. |
| 4 | **Partner-fed Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Return** | **2 × 3 = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Six catches integrated into six rapid return throws, not an extra catch block. | Use a gentle received load to organize an eccentric-to-forward arm/hip impulse. A coach standing offset feeds a very light ball gently to the athlete's low two-hand scoop position; accept it with a small hip/knee bend and promptly throw forward into a clear area, feet planted. **Regress/stop:** Use a slower/lighter feed; stop if catching or containment is unreliable or the torso twists. **Entry:** Competent light-ball receiving and qualified feeder outside the outgoing throw path. |
| 5 | **Wall Ankling Pogo — Bilateral Forward Lean** | **1 × 6 = 6 reps.** Small brisk ankle contacts. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Practice an angled ankle rebound while hand support limits whole-body drift. Hands on a verified fixed wall/support, body in a modest forward lean; make six tiny two-foot ankle rebounds in place with knees soft and trunk aligned. **Regress/stop:** Use less lean/amplitude; stop if the support shifts or the athlete collapses through hips/knees. |
| 6 | **Seated Single-leg Jump onto a Low Box** | **1 × 1 per side = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Supply a controlled one-leg initial impulse into a two-foot elevated finish. Sit still with one foot flat and the other free; push from the supported leg and swing arms to land both feet fully on a very low nearby box; hold and step down. **Regress/stop:** Raise the seat or lower/bring the landing box closer while keeping the same task; stop for rocking, twisting or incomplete foot placement. **Entry:** Demonstrated single-leg seated takeoff and box landing; no attempt to force height. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** Use the lowest suitable stable drop platform and a separate verified seat/landing-box arrangement only where needed. Each forward drop/rebound task finishes by 6 m, leaving 4 m clear. The coach/feed station and waiting athletes stay outside the outgoing ball path. Fixed wall/support integrity and all surfaces must be checked before use. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working load or lever must be **challenging at approximately 2–3 technically sound reps/cycles in reserve**. Keep the same recent actual load when uncertain; do not infer progression from a previous written dose. Controlled technique and secure load handling determine suitability, not age alone. No forced reps, maximal testing or unstable surfaces.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goblet Squat — dumbbell** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Build bilateral knee/hip extension capacity for the takeoff push. Lower 3 s, stand in about 1 s; keep the whole foot down at a repeatable comfortable depth. Reduce load/range for knee collapse or trunk loss. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension from the flexed-hip position used to organize projection. Lower 3 s, stand in about 1 s; hips back, soft knees, bells close. End range before pelvic tuck or back rounding. |
| 3 | **Split Squat — stationary, two dumbbells** | **1 × 6 per side = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Give each lead leg a challenging force contribution through a stable base. Lower 3 s, rise in about 1 s; retain stance width and pelvic control. Reduce load before balance changes the lift. |
| 4 | **Hip Thrust — pelvic dumbbell, upper back on bench** | **1 × 8 = 8 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension near its finish without another large hinge volume. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s; secure the load and bench, finish with ribs/pelvis aligned. Reduce load for lumbar arching. |
| 5 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **1 × 10 = 10 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Supply plantarflexion force while the knee remains flexed during plant-to-push. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s to the floor; retain knee angle, secure loads. Reduce load if heel travel shortens or heels bounce. |
| 6 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 2 = 2 cycles.** 2–3 technical cycles in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts.** | Load knee-flexor control while holding the pelvis, complementing the hip hinge. Each cycle is 2 small outward steps per foot and 2 inward per foot (8 heel placements, about 1 s each); keep slight knee bend and hips level. Shorten the lever for sag or cramping. |

**Preparation, separate from working sets:** **P1:** 5 then 3 easy reps; 60 s between preparation bouts, 120 s before working; **P2:** 4 easy reps; 60 s between preparation bouts, 60 s before working; **P3:** 4 easy reps per side at each ramp load; 60 s between preparation bouts, 60 s before working; **P4:** 4 easy reps; 60 s between preparation bouts, 60 s before working; **P5:** 4 easy reps; 60 s between preparation bouts, 60 s before working; **P6:** 1 easy cycle; 60 s between preparation bouts, 60 s before working. Every preparation set uses the corresponding listed lift, with easy load/lever. Log extra ramps and add their time/exposure. These do not replace Access & Prepare 1.

**Equipment adjustments:** For a goblet holding limit, use the previously documented two-DB front-rack strength replacement instead of the goblet row with the same dose and rest. For an unsuitable hip-thrust bench, use its loaded floor-bridge strength replacement instead with the same dose and rest, recording support/range. Strength may recur under the user's clarified rule; these replacements are not extra exercises. If a new strength support/load setup cannot be made stable, resolve it before lifting. End a set at the technical limit and reduce the next load/lever; do not grind to finish a number.

**Workload:** 24 landing events (24 bilateral, 0 unilateral; 48 landing-foot contacts), including 6 low-amplitude events and 6 initial drop landings; 6 throws and 0 other rapid/ballistic efforts. Primary: 8 rounds / 9 separately performed bouts, 54 conventional reps + 2 walkout cycles. Preparation: 7 rounds / 8 bouts, 28 conventional reps + 1 cycle. Stabilization: 0 dynamic reps + 30 summed hold-seconds across bouts. These are prescriptions excluding unknown Access & Prepare 1 work, not performed totals or equivalent-intensity scores.
