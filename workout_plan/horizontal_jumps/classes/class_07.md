# Horizontal Jumps — Class 7 of 12

**Primary effort:** Carry forward momentum through alternating support legs.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** Three linked alternating bounds are the main task. Same-foot skip hops, short forward unilateral pogos, a walking-entry bound and low-hurdle alternating contacts each constrain a different part of takeoff/receive timing. A step-through chest release couples one forward weight transfer with the arms without another landing.

**Before delivery:** Require actual competence in isolated opposite-leg bounds and low single-leg contacts, with current recovery. Do not progress from written Class 6 alone. Keep linked travel modest enough to stop within the lane. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: two three-contact sequences; alternate the initiating leg between sets. Each landing belongs to the opposite leg from its takeoff, forward travel remains controlled and only the final contact is held 2 s. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Alternating Bounds — Three Linked Contacts** | **2 × 3 = 6 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. continuous reps within the short set; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Six total landings, three on each leg across the two sets. | Transfer impulse repeatedly from one support leg to the other. Begin with a single-leg push and alternate receiving feet for exactly three forward bounds; hold the last landing 2 s. Start the second set from the other leg. **Regress/stop:** Shorten each bound; stop if an intermediate contact collapses or the athlete cannot control the final stop. |
| 2 | **Power Skip for Distance** | **1 × 4 = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four hop landings (two each leg) plus four ordinary setup/transfer steps; at most 4 m. | Coordinate a same-foot hop with the next stepping support before alternating projection. Perform four step-hop cycles traveling forward: land the hop on its takeoff foot, then step onto the other foot for the next hop. **Regress/stop:** Shorten the hop and slow the stepping rhythm; stop if it becomes uncontrolled running bounds. |
| 3 | **Single-leg Forward-Traveling Pogo** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Low amplitude and modest travel. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Keep a small forward-moving ankle rebound organized on each support foot. Make two low consecutive forward same-foot contacts, then settle; repeat on the other side. **Regress/stop:** Reduce travel/amplitude; stop for knee locking, foot reaching or loss of rhythm. |
| 4 | **Walking-entry Opposite-leg Bound to Stick** | **1 × 1 per side = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Two ordinary entry steps total; each entry at most 1 m. | Receive a small amount of entry momentum before transferring to the other leg. Take one walking step onto the takeoff foot, immediately bound forward to the opposite foot and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Shorten/slow the entry; stop for an extra gather step or uncontrolled reach. |
| 5 | **Step-through Medicine Ball Chest Pass** | **2 × 3 = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Six forward step-throughs total; no athlete jump intended. | Match forward weight transfer to a two-arm release. From a staggered stance, step the rear foot forward while driving a light ball from the chest; finish balanced without chasing the throw. **Regress/stop:** Slow/shorten the step and reduce ball load; stop if the body falls through the release. |
| 6 | **Alternating Bounds over Two Low Hurdles** | **1 × 2 = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Two total unilateral landings, one each leg. | Place alternating contacts around a real flight-clearance constraint. Clear two very low hurdles with two alternating forward bounds and hold the final landing; each clearance lands on the opposite foot. **Regress/stop:** Lower/bring the hurdles closer while retaining comfortable alternate contacts; stop for clipping or a rushed landing. **Entry:** Familiar low-hurdle alternating clearance; no new maximum height. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** Every traveling sequence fits within the first 5 m, allowing the final controlled stop by 6 m and 4 m clear. Place two low hurdles only after verifying full spacing and receiving room; their heights are individual setup decisions, not performance targets. Only one athlete travels or retrieves at a time. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.
