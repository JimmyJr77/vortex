# Horizontal Jumps — Class 8 of 12

**Primary effort:** Reapply forward force through the same support leg.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The priority is two linked same-leg hops, then a controlled stop. Single-leg hurdle clearance, a motionless single-leg start, a small fore-aft line reversal and a grounded one-leg throw each isolate a contributor. A split-start jump onto a low box rehearses gathering into a supported two-foot finish.

**Before delivery:** Require actual same-leg hop control and tolerance of linked single-leg contacts. If only isolated or alternating contacts are established, this prescription is conditional and must be revised before delivery; the class number grants no progression. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: eight landings across four two-hop bouts; the same foot takes off and lands twice, the second push remains controlled and the final hold lasts 2 s without free-foot rescue. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Repeated Single-leg Forward Hops — Two Contacts** | **2 × 2 per side = 8 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. continuous reps within the short set; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Eight unilateral landings total, four each leg. | Reapply forward impulse from the same receiving leg. Hop twice forward on the same foot without an intentional pause, then hold the second landing 2 s; alternate side bouts. **Regress/stop:** Shorten each hop; stop for a collapsing intermediate knee/hip or an uncontrolled final contact. |
| 2 | **Single-leg Forward Hurdle Hop to Stick** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Maintain same-leg propulsion and landing around a real clearance constraint. Hop from one foot over a very low hurdle to the same foot and hold 2 s, keeping the receiving knee aligned. **Regress/stop:** Lower/bring the hurdle closer; stop for clipping, reaching or a free-foot touchdown. |
| 3 | **Paused Single-leg Broad Jump to Two-foot Landing** | **1 × 1 per side = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Express a fresh one-leg push without reusing a preceding contact or adding a one-leg finish. Hold a shallow one-leg dip still for 2 s, use the arms and jump forward to two feet; hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Shorten travel and make the start shallower; stop if a re-dip replaces the paused push. |
| 4 | **Single-leg Forward–Backward Line Hops** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Small quick crossings, not distance hops. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Control a small fore-aft reversal through the same foot. Make one forward and one backward crossing of a flat line on the same foot, then settle; repeat on the other foot. **Regress/stop:** Narrow the crossing and lower height; stop if the knee locks or balance dominates. |
| 5 | **Single-leg Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Transmit forward force through one planted leg and a controlled pelvis without a landing. Stand on one foot with a very light ball low in front; use a shallow hip/knee dip to scoop forward, keeping the free foot clear and torso facing ahead. **Regress/stop:** Reduce load/dip; stop for hopping, twisting or loss of foot control. **Entry:** Comfortable one-leg ball handling; power must not be limited by balance. |
| 6 | **Split-Squat Jump onto a Low Box** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Gather a lead-leg-biased push into a symmetrical elevated finish. From a shallow fixed split stance on the floor, dip once and jump forward/up onto a low box with both feet; hold 2 s, then step down. **Regress/stop:** Lower/bring the box closer; stop if the rear leg is trapped, the stance twists or feet land partly off the box. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** Two same-leg hops finish within the first 5 m and settle by 6 m, leaving 4 m clear. A low hurdle and low box require individual fit and separate setups. The one-leg throw must fit the same full containment rules as bilateral throws. Provide a verified hand-support point and comfortable forearm/knee contact surface. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.
