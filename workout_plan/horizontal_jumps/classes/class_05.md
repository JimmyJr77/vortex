# Horizontal Jumps — Class 5 of 12

**Primary effort:** Drive horizontal projection through a decisive hip impulse.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The main task reverses a small backward step into a forward two-foot push. Hip-driven throws, a loaded extension jump, resisted grounded hip extension and low straight-leg bounds separate hip impulse from approach speed. The half-kneeling chest release reinforces a forward force path through a steady pelvis.

**Before delivery:** Confirm the backward-step reversal can be performed at walking pace without stumbling. Straight-leg bounds require familiar low-amplitude alternating contact; the grounded band drill requires a secure comfortable pelvic-band setup. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: six attempts; one backward step and forward gather remain controlled, the jump follows the gather without an extra shuffle, and the two-foot finish holds 2 s. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Backward-step Reversal Broad Jump** | **3 × 2 = 6 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Two entry steps per attempt: 12 total walking entry steps; each entry fits within 2 m, at most 12 m across all entries. | Convert a small rearward load into forward hip-driven projection. From standing, take one short backward step then step forward to gather both feet and immediately jump forward with natural arms; hold the bilateral landing 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Shorten and slow the entry; stop for extra shuffles or an uncontrolled forward pitch. |
| 2 | **Backward Overhead Medicine Ball Throw** | **2 × 3 = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Express hip/knee extension into a free external load without another athlete landing. Face away from the clear throw area, hinge with a light ball between the thighs and drive the hips/legs to release back overhead; keep the feet planted and avoid lumbar arching. **Regress/stop:** Reduce ball load and hinge; do not throw unless the full overhead trajectory and landing/roll fit the verified area. |
| 3 | **Dumbbell Jump Shrug** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Very light load, fast extension and modest height. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Apply rapid hip/knee/ankle extension against a small hanging load. Hold very light bells at the sides, use a shallow hinge/dip and jump a small height while finishing tall; arms stay long, then land on two feet and reset. **Regress/stop:** Reduce load/height; stop if grip, shoulder pulling or landing control limits the leg drive. **Entry:** Demonstrated unloaded jump and secure DB handling. |
| 4 | **Band-resisted Hip Snap — Feet Planted** | **2 × 3 = 6 efforts.** Rapid extension against light resistance; feet remain planted. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Produce fast hip extension against rearward resistance without an impact. With a comfortable broad band low around the pelvis and a verified rear anchor/holder, hinge slightly then snap to tall hips while keeping both feet planted and ribs down. **Regress/stop:** Use less tension and smaller range; stop for lumbar arching, band migration or insecure anchoring. **Entry:** Band condition, pelvic fit and rear anchoring verified; no elastic release toward people. |
| 5 | **Low Straight-Leg Alternating Bounds** | **1 × 6 = 6 reps.** Low amplitude, controlled speed. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Six contacts total, not six left-right pairs; whole route at most 4 m. | Use hip action and ankle stiffness to project with limited knee cycling. Travel forward through six small alternating contacts, three on each foot; keep knees softly unlocked and contact beneath the moving body rather than reaching. Finish under control. **Regress/stop:** Shorten the travel and lower amplitude; stop for jarring locked-knee contact or reaching. |
| 6 | **Half-Kneeling Forward Medicine Ball Chest Pass** | **1 × 3 per side = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Transfer a forward arm impulse through a stable split hip/pelvic base. One knee down, opposite foot flat; with a light ball at chest height, push forward explosively while retaining tall ribs/pelvis and grounded supports. **Regress/stop:** Reduce load; stop if the torso twists or kneeling pressure is uncomfortable. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** The reversal entry occupies at most the first 2 m; jump/hold must finish by 6 m with 4 m clear. The band anchor/holder stays behind the athlete outside any swing or landing path. The backward throw needs verified ceiling and rearward containment; a 10 m lane alone does not establish it. Use a comfortable kneeling contact surface. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.
