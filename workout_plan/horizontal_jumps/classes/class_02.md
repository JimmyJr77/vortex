# Horizontal Jumps — Class 2 of 12

**Primary effort:** Create forward force from a settled start.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The seated forward takeoff is the priority: remove a standing countermovement and coordinate a direct leg push with the arms. The other jobs separate static extension, hip-led release, staggered ankle contact, hip impulse from a parked implement and force from a fixed split position.

**Before delivery:** Confirm the athlete can sit on a stable low bench/box with feet flat, rise without rocking and manage a modest forward landing. Use current comfortable travel; no prior written jump is a readiness pass. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: six seated attempts; feet remain still before the push, no preliminary rock, and the bilateral floor landing holds for 2 s. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Broad Jump** | **3 × 2 = 6 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Project forward from a supported motionless start. Sit near the edge, feet flat under or slightly ahead of knees; pause 2 s, swing the arms and jump forward to a two-foot 2 s hold. **Regress/stop:** Reduce distance and choose a seat height allowing feet flat; stop if rocking or loss of landing replaces the task. |
| 2 | **Squat Jump** | **2 × 2 = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Supply rapid leg extension from stillness without the forward-landing demand of the main drill. Hold a comfortable partial squat 2 s with hands on hips; jump upward without a new dip and stick on two feet. **Regress/stop:** Use a shallower comfortable start and modest height; stop on a re-dip or unstable landing. |
| 3 | **Tall-Kneeling Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss** | **2 × 3 = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Isolate hip-to-arm forward acceleration while removing ankle/knee takeoff. Kneel tall on a comfortable stable surface, ball low; hinge slightly at the hips then extend and scoop forward, keeping knees down and ribs controlled. **Regress/stop:** Use a lighter ball and smaller hinge; no throw if kneeling is uncomfortable or containment is unverified. |
| 4 | **Split-Stance Pogo** | **1 × 4 per side = 8 reps.** Small quick rebounds, not maximum height. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Organize quick ankle pressure through a staggered base before split-position force work. Keep the chosen staggered stance unchanged; make small two-foot rebounds in place, then settle. Change lead foot for the other bout. **Regress/stop:** Reduce amplitude; stop if feet scissor or contacts become deep knee bends. |
| 5 | **Dead-Stop Kettlebell Swing** | **2 × 3 = 6 efforts.** Crisp hip-driven acceleration; feet stay planted and the bell stays controlled. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Produce a discrete hip impulse from a parked load rather than reuse successive swing momentum. Hike a light bell, snap the hips to float it below chest height, then guide it back to the floor and fully reset; one hike/swing/park is one rep. **Regress/stop:** Use a lighter bell; stop for lumbar extension, arm lifting or loss of safe parking. **Entry:** Familiar hinge/swing and secure two-hand handling. |
| 6 | **Static Split-Squat Jump to Same-Stance Landing** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Express rapid force from a fixed asymmetrical base without a midair leg switch. Pause 2 s in a shallow split squat, jump a small amount up/forward, land in the same split stance and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Reduce depth/travel; stop if the stance narrows, knee collapses or landing cannot be held. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** Use a stable seat with feet flat for E1 and a comfortable kneeling surface for E3. Seat takeoff/landing remains within the first 6 m, leaving 4 m clear. No raised-platform drop is intended. Reserve a separate verified swing envelope; the light forward throw requires full ball containment. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.
