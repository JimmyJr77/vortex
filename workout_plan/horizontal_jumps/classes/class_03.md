# Horizontal Jumps — Class 3 of 12

**Primary effort:** Apply forward force from one leg and organize the receiving leg.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The forward same-leg hop is the priority. Low unilateral ankle contacts, an opposite-leg bound, a supported-height takeoff and a small vertical one-leg jump separate the propulsion and receiving tasks; a staggered throw rehearses force from an unequal base without another landing.

**Before delivery:** This class requires demonstrated comfortable single-leg hopping/landing, not just bilateral landing experience. If that is not established, keep the class pending for that athlete and revise the affected slots; do not infer eligibility from Classes 1–2. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: eight prescribed hops, four per leg; take off and land on the same foot, pelvis controlled, knee tracks with the foot and the finish holds 2 s without the free foot touching. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Forward Hop to Stick — Same Leg** | **2 × 2 per side = 8 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Own a single-leg forward push and receive on that same leg. Use one comfortable dip and natural arms, hop forward, absorb through ankle/knee/hip and hold the same-foot landing 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Shorten travel; stop for repeated free-foot touchdown, knee collapse or painful contact. |
| 2 | **Single-Leg Pogo in Place** | **1 × 3 per side = 6 reps.** Low amplitude and quick, not maximum height. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Supply a small ankle-rebound dose on each support leg without long travel. Keep a softly bent knee and make low same-foot rebounds in place; settle after the third. **Regress/stop:** Reduce height; stop for stiff painful contacts or uncontrolled travel. |
| 3 | **Opposite-leg Forward Bound to Stick** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Transfer forward impulse from a pushing leg to a different receiving leg. Push from one foot, swing the opposite thigh forward and land on that opposite foot; hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Shorten the bound and reduce free-leg swing; stop if reaching the landing foot causes a collapse. |
| 4 | **Sprinter Step-Up Jump — Two-foot Floor Landing** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Drive through a forward supported stance leg while coordinating the free knee. Start with one entire foot on a very low stable step, drive up/forward from that leg and land on both feet on the clear floor; hold 2 s and walk to reset. **Regress/stop:** Use a lower step and smaller flight; stop if the rear foot pushes off or the floor landing becomes a drop the athlete cannot control. **Entry:** Suitable low step and familiar jump-off/floor landing. |
| 5 | **Split-Stance Forward Medicine Ball Scoop Toss** | **1 × 3 per side = 6 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Coordinate forward release from a staggered support base without a landing. Hold the lead-foot stance fixed, dip shallowly and extend through the legs into a forward two-hand scoop; no trunk rotation or step. **Regress/stop:** Use a lighter ball and smaller dip; stop if the rear foot slips or the throw becomes a twist. |
| 6 | **Single-leg Vertical Jump to Two-foot Landing** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section.  | Express stance-leg extension without the added forward braking of E1. Dip on one leg, jump a modest height with natural arms, bring both feet into a quiet two-foot landing and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Reduce height; stop if takeoff knee/pelvis control is lost. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** All horizontal single efforts finish within the first 6 m of a 10 m lane with 4 m clear. E4 uses only a genuinely low stable step with a verified floor landing envelope; do not use a large box merely because one exists. Verify a secure hand-support point and comfortable seated foot-work position. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.

## 3. Strength (Stabilization/Resilience) — Exactly 2 Exercises

Light controlled strengthening. Use at least 5 clean reps in reserve/comparable ease on holds; no shaking, burning or pre-fatigue goal. Bodyweight is appropriate where the row says so.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hip Airplane Iso Hold — Hand Supported** | **1 × 1 holds of 12 s per side; 2 holds / 24 prescribed hold-seconds total.** Bodyweight/light resistance, easy control; at least 5 clean reps in reserve or comparable ease. continuous reps within the short set; 30 s between set/side bouts; 45 s before the next row/section.  | Lightly control hip/pelvis rotation around the planted leg for a forward landing. Hinge shallowly on one leg with stable hand support; open the pelvis slightly and hold without rotating the stance knee or foot. **Regress/stop:** Reduce hinge/rotation; stop if balance or hamstring tension dominates. |
| 2 | **Self-resisted Seated Ankle Dorsiflexion** | **1 × 5 per side = 10 reps.** Bodyweight/light resistance, easy control; at least 5 clean reps in reserve or comparable ease. continuous reps within the short set; 30 s between set/side bouts; 45 s before the next row/section.  | Strengthen controlled toe lift for foot placement and early heel acceptance. Sit with the heel planted; press lightly downward on the top of the forefoot with the hand while the foot lifts against it, then return slowly. **Regress/stop:** Reduce hand resistance; keep toes relaxed and stop on discomfort. |

## 4. Strength (Primary) — Exactly 6 Exercises

Working load or lever must be **challenging at approximately 2–3 technically sound reps/cycles in reserve**. Keep the same recent actual load when uncertain; do not infer progression from a previous written dose. Controlled technique and secure load handling determine suitability, not age alone. No forced reps, maximal testing or unstable surfaces.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Squat — stationary, two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 per side = 24 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Give each lead leg a challenging force contribution through a stable base. Lower 3 s, rise in about 1 s; retain stance width and pelvic control. Reduce load before balance changes the lift. |
| 2 | **Romanian Deadlift — two dumbbells** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension from the flexed-hip position used to organize projection. | 3 | **Goblet Squat — dumbbell** | **2 × 6 = 12 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **120 s between working set/side bouts and 120 s before the next preparation.** | Build bilateral knee/hip extension capacity for the takeoff push. Lower 3 s, stand in about 1 s; keep the whole foot down at a repeatable comfortable depth. Reduce load/range for knee collapse or trunk loss. |
| 4 | **Hip Thrust — pelvic dumbbell, upper back on bench** | **1 × 8 = 8 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Load hip extension near its finish without another large hinge volume. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s; secure the load and bench, finish with ribs/pelvis aligned. Reduce load for lumbar arching. |
| 5 | **Bent-Knee Soleus Raise — seated, dumbbells on thighs** | **1 × 10 = 10 reps.** 2–3 technical reps in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts and 90 s before the next preparation.** | Supply plantarflexion force while the knee remains flexed during plant-to-push. Rise 1 s, hold 1 s, lower 2 s to the floor; retain knee angle, secure loads. Reduce load if heel travel shortens or heels bounce. |
| 6 | **Glute Bridge Walkout** | **1 × 3 = 3 cycles.** 2–3 technical cycles in reserve. **90 s between working set/side bouts.** | Load knee-flexor control while holding the pelvis, complementing the hip hinge. Each cycle is 2 small outward steps per foot and 2 inward per foot (8 heel placements, about 1 s each); keep slight knee bend and hips level. Shorten the lever for sag or cramping. |

Loads are held at current demonstrated capacity. The no-repeat request replaces recurring jump tests with recurring strength anchors and movement-demand continuity.
