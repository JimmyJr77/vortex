# Horizontal Jumps — Class 9 of 12

**Primary effort:** Convert a short controlled forward entry into horizontal takeoff.

**Foundation:** Ages 12–14 with established basic lifting/landing technique and qualified supervision. Actual execution, recovery and working loads remain unknown. This is a conditional complete prescription, not evidence that previous work occurred.

**Daily stimulus brief:** The two-step bilateral approach jump is the priority. A one-leg long-jump plant, a walking box entry, height-oriented alternating bounds and a reverse-lunge drive each constrain how the legs organize an incoming body; an overhead forward throw couples a step to a longer arm lever.

**Before delivery:** Require demonstrated controlled entry-to-plant mechanics and sufficient actual receiving control. Use walking-to-brisk entry, not maximum sprint speed. Verify the whole entry/jump/stop fits 10 m. Review [athlete feedback](../athlete_feedback.md), recent/upcoming sport, gym and separate tumbling, and actual Access & Prepare 1 loading. Use recent actual capacity, not the class number, to select demand.

**Performance marker inside E1:** E1: six attempts with exactly two forward entry steps, a controlled two-foot plant, immediate forward takeoff and a two-foot 2 s landing hold; no extra run-up or gather shuffle. Record valid/attempted reps and the specific fault; optional distance only with a verified measurement method. This drill differs from earlier anchors, so its distance cannot be presented as longitudinal improvement over a different drill. No extra testing block.

## 2. Explosiveness — Exactly 6 Exercises

The first row is the day's priority. Other rows have small supporting doses. No fatigue circuit.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Specific job in this workout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-step Approach Broad Jump** | **3 × 2 = 6 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 120 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. 12 entry steps total; each entry at most 2 m. | Redirect a small amount of forward entry momentum through a two-foot takeoff. Take exactly two controlled forward steps into a two-foot plant, swing the arms and jump forward; hold the bilateral finish 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Slow/shorten the entry; stop if braking or an extra shuffle replaces the plant. |
| 2 | **One-step Long Jump to Two-foot Landing** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four ordinary entry steps total, each at most 1 m. | Use a single-foot plant after entry while gathering into a bilateral finish. Take one controlled step onto the takeoff leg, drive the free knee/arms forward and jump from that foot; land on two feet and hold 2 s. **Regress/stop:** Slow the step and shorten travel; stop for a buckling plant or a feet-first reach. |
| 3 | **Walk-in Two-foot Box Jump** | **1 × 2 = 2 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four ordinary walking entry steps total; each two-step entry at most 2 m. | Organize a moving two-foot plant into an elevated full-foot receiving position. Walk two short steps into a two-foot gather, then jump forward/up onto a low box and hold 2 s; step down to reset. **Regress/stop:** Lower the box and slow entry; stop for a rushed plant, clipping or partial-foot landing. |
| 4 | **Alternating Bounds for Height** | **1 × 4 = 4 reps.** Controlled upward projection with modest forward travel. continuous reps within the short set; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four landings, two each leg; at most 4 m. This is not a distance-bound score. | Preserve a useful upward component while carrying forward momentum from one leg to the next. Use four controlled alternating bounds with a tall trunk, upward free-knee drive and short forward travel; hold the final contact. **Regress/stop:** Reduce height/step length; stop for reaching the foot or losing the tall hip position. |
| 5 | **Step-in Forward Overhead Medicine Ball Throw** | **2 × 2 = 4 releases.** Fast release with a light ball; retain the prescribed grounded support. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four ordinary step-ins total; no athlete jump. | Time a forward step with a long-lever two-arm release. Start with a light ball above the forehead, take one short forward step and extend the arms to release ahead while ribs remain controlled; keep a stable finish. **Regress/stop:** Use lighter load and a shorter arm excursion; stop for lumbar arching or throwing outside verified containment. |
| 6 | **Reverse-Lunge Drive Jump to Two-foot Landing** | **1 × 2 per side = 4 reps.** Fast takeoff with controlled travel. 30 s between reps; 90 s between set/side bouts; 90 s before the next row/section. Four ordinary rear entry steps total; no running approach. | Convert a rear-step loading action into a lead-leg drive and balanced finish. Step back into a shallow reverse lunge, drive through the front foot as the rear knee comes forward and make a modest forward/up jump; land on two feet and hold. **Regress/stop:** Shorten the lunge and flight; stop for rear-foot slipping, a twisting push or uncontrolled landing. |

**Execution and accounting:** Perform the written set on one side, then the other with the stated side-bout rest; alternate which side starts successive set rounds. A bilateral landing is one event/two foot contacts; a single-leg landing is one event/one foot contact. Count failed attempts and any accidental extra flight. A sequence's jump count is declared in its row; walking returns are not plyometric reps. No extra practice/test attempts: coached athlete rehearsals consume the listed dose. Keep maximum intent within a distance/height that can be controlled. After a clear decline, extend the next rest 30–60 s; shorten travel/height or reduce the next dose if needed, and stop if quality does not return. Stop for pain, giving way, dizziness or an unsafe station. Record reduced/unperformed work honestly.

**No-repeat rule:** All eight explosive/control drills in this class have different prescribed executions from those in the other classes. Meaningful variations share movement families; changing only load, rep count, name or target distance does not create a new drill. Regress the current drill's effort/range within its declared execution; do not borrow a previous drill to fill a slot. If a prerequisite or required setup cannot be met, defer/rewrite that slot before full delivery, rather than force the work. The coach must not treat a written class as a competency pass.

**Layout:** All two-step entries fit in the first 2 m, with jump and stop completed by 6 m and 4 m kept clear. Single-step entries fit within 1 m. No maximal sprint is prescribed. Use a verified low box/step and fixed leaning support; overhead ball trajectory requires separate ceiling/landing containment. Use one athlete at a time in an active 10 m lane. Verify width, surface, waiting/return paths and coach sightline; retrieve/return only after the lane is clear. Three lanes are not a 30 m runway. Ball releases require a verified flight/bounce/roll envelope and safe retrieval; no throwing wall or catching return is assumed. Choose only an implement that remains fast and controllable. Equipment sizes, quantities and attachment fit remain to be checked; no maximal loading is authorized by availability alone.

Keep the same recent actual load when uncertain; do not infer progression from a previous written dose. Controlled technique and secure load handling determine suitability, not age alone. No forced reps, maximal testing or unstable surfaces.
